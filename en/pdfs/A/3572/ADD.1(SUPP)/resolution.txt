The present volume contains the resolution adopted by the General Assembly
during the period from 10 September 1957, when the eleventh session was resumed
in accordance with Assembly resolution 1119 (XI), to 14 September 1957, when
the session was adjourned.
For the resolutions adopted by the General Assembly during the period from
12 November 1956 to 8 March 1957, see Official Records of the General Assembly,
Eleventh Session, Supplement No. 17 (Aj3572).
