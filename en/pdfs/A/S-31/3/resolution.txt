NOTE
The resolutions and decisions of the General Assembly are identified as follows:
Regular sessions
Until the thirtieth regular session, the resolutions of the General Assembly were
identified by an arabic numeral followed by a roman numeral in parentheses indicating
the session (for example: resolution 3363 (XXX)). When several resolutions were
adopted under the same number, each of them was identified by a capital letter placed
between the two numerals (for example: resolution 3367 A (XXX), resolutions 3411 A
and B (XXX), resolutions 3419 A to D (XXX)). The decisions were not numbered.
Since the thirty-first session, as part of the new system adopted for symbols of
General Assembly documents, resolutions and decisions have been identified by an
arabic numeral, indicating the session, followed by an oblique stroke and another arabic
numeral (for example: resolution 31/1, decision 31/301). When several resolutions or
decisions were adopted under the same number, each of them has been identified by a
capital letter placed after the two numerals (for example: resolution 31/16 A,
resolutions 31/6 A and B, decisions 31/406 A to E).
Special sessions
Until the seventh special session, the resolutions of the General Assembly were
identified by an arabic numeral followed, in parentheses, by the letter “S” and a roman
numeral indicating the session (for example: resolution 3362 (S-VII)). The decisions
were not numbered.
Since the eighth special session, resolutions and decisions have been identified
by the letter “S” and an arabic numeral indicating the session, followed by an oblique
stroke and another arabic numeral (for example: resolution S-8/1, decision S-8/11).
Emergency special sessions
Until the fifth emergency special session, the resolutions of the General Assembly
were identified by an arabic numeral followed, in parentheses, by the letters “ES” and
a roman numeral indicating the session (for example: resolution 2252 (ES-V)). The
decisions were not numbered.
Since the sixth emergency special session, resolutions and decisions have been
identified by the letters “ES” and an arabic numeral indicating the session, followed by
an oblique stroke and another arabic numeral (for example: resolution ES-6/1, decision
ES-6/11).
In each of the series described above, the numbering follows the order of adoption.

II. Decisions

A. Elections and appointments
S-31/2.

Appointment of the members of the Credentials Committee

On 15 July 2020, the General Assembly, in accordance with decisions 74/544 of 27 March 2020, 74/555 of
15 May 2020 and 74/558 of 18 June 2020, decided that, in accordance with rule 28 of the rules of procedure, the
Credentials Committee for the thirty-first special session should have the same membership as the Credentials
Committee of the seventy-fourth regular session of the Assembly, namely, BARBADOS, BOTSWANA, CHINA,
MAURITIUS, NEPAL, the RUSSIAN FEDERATION, SAN MARINO, the UNITED STATES OF AMERICA and URUGUAY.
S-31/3.

Election of the President of the General Assembly

On 15 July 2020, the General Assembly, in accordance with decisions 74/544 of 27 March 2020, 74/555 of
15 May 2020 and 74/558 of 18 June 2020, decided to elect His Excellency Mr. Tijjani Muhammad Bande of Nigeria
as President of the General Assembly at its thirty-first special session.
S-31/4.

Election of the Vice-Presidents of the General Assembly

On 21 July 2020, the General Assembly, in accordance with decisions 74/544 of 27 March 2020, 74/555 of
15 May 2020 and 74/558 of 18 June 2020, decided that the Vice-Presidents of the thirty-first special session shall be
the same as those of the seventy-fourth regular session of the General Assembly.
S-31/5.

Election of the Chairs of the Main Committees of the General Assembly

On 21 July 2020, the General Assembly, in accordance with decisions 74/544 of 27 March 2020, 74/555 of
15 May 2020 and 74/558 of 18 June 2020, decided that the Chairs of the Main Committees of the thirty-first special
session shall be the same as those of the seventy-fourth regular session of the General Assembly.
S-31/8.

President and other officers of the thirty-first special session

At its 1st plenary meeting, on 14 September 2020, the General Assembly, on the proposal of Azerbaijan,2 by a
recorded vote of 153 to none, with 3 abstentions,3 and recalling its decisions S-31/2 and S-31/3 of 15 July 2020 and
S-31/4 and S-31/5 of 21 July 2020:
(a) Decided that, effective upon the opening of its seventy-fifth regular session on 15 September 2020, the
President and Vice-Presidents of the General Assembly and the Chairs of the Main Committees at the thirty-first
special session of the Assembly shall be the same as those at its seventy-fifth regular session;
(b) Also decided that, effective upon the appointment of the members of the Credentials Committee for its
seventy-fifth regular session, the members of the Committee for its seventy-fifth regular session shall also serve as
those for the thirty-first special session.

B. Other decisions
S-31/1.

Member States in arrears under the terms of Article 19 of the Charter of the United Nations

A
On 15 July 2020, the General Assembly, in accordance with decisions 74/544 of 27 March 2020, 74/555 of
15 May 2020 and 74/558 of 18 June 2020, took note of the information contained in document A/S-31/2 concerning
Article 19 of the Charter of the United Nations.
B
At its 2nd plenary meeting, on 3 December 2020, the General Assembly took note of the information contained
in document A/S-31/2/Add.1 concerning Article 19 of the Charter of the United Nations.
S-31/6.

Adoption of the agenda

On 21 July 2020, the General Assembly, in accordance with decisions 74/544 of 27 March 2020, 74/555 of
15 May 2020 and 74/558 of 18 June 2020, decided to adopt the agenda of its thirty-first special session as contained
in document A/S-31/1.
