explanation of vote is related to draft resolution III,
“Situation of human rights in the Islamic Republic of
Iran”. In the previous draft resolutions submitted on the
situation of human rights in the Islamic Republic of
Iran, Chile abstained, having valued the efforts of
President Khatami and his Government to improve
Iran’s situation. However, my country deplores the
violent death of Ms. Zahra Kazemi — the main reason
for the submission of the draft resolution — and we
hope that the judicial system can resolve that matter
shortly.
In addition, we carefully reviewed the reports of
the Working Group on Arbitrary Detention on its visit
to that country in February, and we welcome the
incentive of the standing invitations extended recently
by the Government of Iran. However, such visits
should be seen as means and not an end. In other
words, once the supervision mechanisms enter the
country, the authorities should cooperate so that such
visits can take place in complete transparency, so as to
ensure that the recommendations are fully
implemented. That is why Chile fervently hopes that
conditions will be established in the Islamic Republic
of Iran that will provide for the full enjoyment of
human rights on its territory.
In the coming months, the Government of Chile
will follow closely the development of the situation in
Iran, which will affect our future position in that
regard.
