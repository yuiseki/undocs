Thirdly, the Islamic Republic of Iran believes that

and maintained.

inalienable right of the Palestinian refugees to return to
their homeland.

support those resolutions, submitted under agenda

countries, which always claim to have a perfect human
rights record and condemn the records of others, fail to
join the vast majority of the General Assembly in
supporting the resolutions.

only when justice is ensured, when there is an end to
discrimination and to the occupation of all Palestinian
