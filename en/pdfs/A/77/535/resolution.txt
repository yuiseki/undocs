I. Introduction
1.
At its 3rd plenary meeting, on 16 September 2022, the General Assembly, on the
recommendation of the General Committee, decided to include in the agenda of its
seventy-seventh session the item entitled “Programme budget for 2022” and to allocate
it to the Fifth Committee.
2.
Under that agenda item, the Fifth Committee considered the question of revised
estimates on United Nations activities to mitigate global food insecurity and its
humanitarian impact at its 4th and 8th meetings, on 10 and 21 October 2022,
respectively. Statements and observations made in the course of the Committee’s
consideration of the question are reflected in the relevant summary records.
3.
For its consideration of the question, the Committee had before it the following
documents:
(a) Report of the Secretary-General on revised estimates on United Nations
activities to mitigate global food insecurity and its humanitarian impac t (A/77/325);
(b) Related report of the Advisory Committee on Administrative and
Budgetary Questions (A/77/486).

II. Consideration of draft resolution A/C.5/77/L.3
4.
At its 8th meeting, on 21 October, the Committee had before it a draft resolution
entitled “Revised estimates on United Nations activities to mitigate global food
insecurity and its humanitarian impact” (A/C.5/77/L.3), submitted by the Chair of the
Committee on the basis of informal consultations coordinated by the representative
of Ghana.
5.
At the same meeting, the Committee adopted draft resolution A/C.5/77/L.3
without a vote (see para. 6).

III. Recommendation of the Fifth Committee
6.
The Fifth Committee recommends to the General Assembly the adoption of the
following draft resolution:

Revised estimates on United Nations activities to mitigate global
food insecurity and its humanitarian impact
The General Assembly,
Having considered the report of the Secretary-General 1 and the related report of
the Advisory Committee on Administrative and Budgetary Questions, 2
1.

Takes note of the report of the Secretary-General;

2.
Endorses the conclusions and recommendations contained in the report of
the Advisory Committee on Administrative and Budgetary Questions;
3.
Approves additional resource requirements in the amount of 3,524,600 United
States dollars under the programme budget for 2022, under section 12, Trade and
development (848,600 dollars), section 27, Humanitarian assistance (2,481,700
dollars), and section 36, Staff assessment (194,300 dollars), to be offset by an
equivalent increase of 194,300 dollars under income section 1, Income from staff
assessment;
4.
Authorizes the Secretary-General to enter into commitments in an amount
not exceeding 3,524,600 dollars;
5.
Notes that the use of the commitment authority of 3,524,600 dollars for
2022 will be reported in the financial performance report for 2022.
