I. Introduction
1.
At its 3rd plenary meeting, on 16 September 2022, the General Assembly, on
the recommendation of the General Committee, decided to include in the agenda of
its seventy-seventh session the item entitled “Report of the Human Rights Council”
and to allocate it to both the plenary and the Third Committee, in accordance with its
resolution 65/281.
2.
The Committee held a general discussion and considered a proposal and took
action on the item at its 44th and 54th meetings, on 2 and 17 November 2022,
respectively. An account of the Committee’s consideration of the item is contained in
the relevant summary records. 1
3.
For its consideration of the item, the Committee had before it the report of the
Human Rights Council on its thirty-second, thirty-third and thirty-fourth special
sessions and its forty-ninth and fiftieth sessions (A/77/53) and on its fifty-first session
(A/77/53/Add.1).
4.
At the 44th meeting, on 2 November 2022, the President of the Human Rights
Council made an introductory statement and responded to questions posed and
comments made by the representatives of Costa Rica, Latvia (on behalf of the Nordic
and Baltic countries), Chile, Switzerland, Cameroon, Brazil, Argentina, Cuba, the
Republic of Korea, the United Kingdom of Great Britain and Northern Ireland,
Ethiopia, Morocco, China, Malawi, Nigeria, El Salvador, the Netherlands, Australia
(also on behalf of Canada, Iceland, Liechtenstein, New Zealand and Norway), France,
the Syrian Arab Republic, Angola and Italy.
5.
At the 46th meeting, on 10 November, the representative of the United States of
America made a statement with regard to the draft resolutions before the Committee. 2

resolution entitled “Report of the Human Rights Council” (A/C.3/77/L.53), submitted
by Ecuador and Liberia (on behalf of the States Members of the United Nations that
are members of the Group of African States). Subsequently, the Bolivaria n Republic

draft resolution.
8.

African Group) made a statement.

III. Recommendation of the Third Committee
12. The Third Committee recommends to the General Assembly the adoption of the
following draft resolution:

Report of the Human Rights Council
The General Assembly,
Recalling its resolutions 60/251 of 15 March 2006, by which it established the
Human Rights Council, and 65/281 of 17 June 2011 on the review of the Council,
Recalling also its resolutions 62/219 of 22 December 2007, 63/160 of
18 December 2008, 64/143 of 18 December 2009, 65/195 of 21 December 2010,
66/136 of 19 December 2011, 67/151 of 20 December 2012, 68/144 of 18 December
2013, 69/155 of 18 December 2014, 70/136 of 17 December 2015, 71/174 of
19 December 2016, 72/153 of 19 December 2017, 73/152 of 17 December 2018,
74/132 of 18 December 2019, 75/165 of 16 December 2020 and 76/145 of
16 December 2021,
Having considered the recommendations contained in the report of the Human
Rights Council, 1
Takes note of the report of the Human Rights Council, including the addendum
thereto, and its recommendations.
