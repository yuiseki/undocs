I. Introduction
1.
At its 3rd plenary meeting, on 16 September 2022, the General Assembly, on
the recommendation of the General Committee, decided to include in the agenda o f
its seventy-seventh session the item entitled “Proposed programme budget for 2023 ”
and to allocate it to the Fifth Committee.
2.
The Fifth Committee considered the item at its 5th, 7th, 10th, 12th, 15th, 17th ,
18th, 19th, 20th, 23rd, 24th and resumed 25th meetings, on 12 and 14 Octobe r, 2, 7 ,
15, 21 and 28 November and 5, 8, 14, 16 and 30 December 2022. Statements and
observations made in the course of the Committee’s formal consideration of the item
are reflected in the relevant summary records. 1
3.
At the 5th meeting, on 12 October, introductory statements were made by the
Secretary-General and the Chair of the Advisory Committee on Administrative and
Budgetary Questions.
4.
For its consideration of the item, the Committee had before it the followi n g
documents:
