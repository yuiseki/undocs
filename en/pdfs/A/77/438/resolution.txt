I. Introduction
1.
The item entitled “Observer status for the Digital Cooperation Organization in
the General Assembly” was included in the provisional agenda of the seventy-seventh
session of the General Assembly at the request of Bahrain, Jordan, Kuwait, Morocco,
Nigeria, Oman, Pakistan, Rwanda and Saudi Arabia.
2.
At its 3rd plenary meeting, on 16 September 2022, the General Assembly, on
the recommendation of the General Committee, decided to include the item in its
agenda and to allocate it to the Sixth Committee.
3.
The Sixth Committee considered the item at its 14th and 32nd meetings, on
13 October and 3 November 2022. The views of the representatives who spoke du ring
the Committee’s consideration of the item are reflected in the relevant summary
records. 1
4.
For its consideration of the item, the Committee had before it a letter dated
8 June 2022 from the representatives of those States mentioned in paragraph 1 above
to the United Nations addressed to the Secretary-General (A/77/141).

III. Recommendation of the Sixth Committee
8.
The Sixth Committee recommends to the General Assembly the adoption of the
following draft resolution:

Observer status for the Digital Cooperation Organization in the
General Assembly
The General Assembly,
Wishing to promote cooperation between the United Nations and the Digital
Cooperation Organization,
1.
Decides to invite the Digital Cooperation Organization to participate in the
sessions and the work of the General Assembly in the capacity of observer;
2.
Requests the Secretary-General to take the action necessary to implement
the present resolution.
