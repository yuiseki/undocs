I. Introduction
1.
The Second Committee held a substantive debate on agenda item 18 (see
A/77/443, para. 2). Action on sub-item (c) was taken at the 23rd meeting, on
22 November 2022. An account of the Committee’s consideration of the sub-item is
contained in the relevant summary record. 1

II. Consideration of draft resolutions A/C.2/77/L.35 and
A/C.2/77/L.51
2.
At the 21st meeting, on 10 November 2022, the representative of Pakistan, on
behalf of the States Members of the United Nations that are members of the Group of 77
and China, introduced a draft resolution entitled “Disaster risk reduction”
(A/C.2/77/L.35).
3.
At the 23rd meeting, on 22 November 2022, the Committee had before it a draft
resolution entitled “Disaster risk reduction” (A/C.2/77/L.51), submitted by the
Vice-Chair of the Committee, Ahmed Magdy (Egypt), on the basis of informal
consultations held on draft resolution A/C.2/77/L.35.
4.
At the same meeting, the Committee was informed that A/C.2/77/L.51 had no
programme budget implications.
5.
Also at the same meeting, the Committee adopted draft resolution
A/C.2/77/L.51 (see para. 8).
