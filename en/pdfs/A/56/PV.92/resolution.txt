I request the Rapporteur of the Fifth Committee,

Mr. Wins (Uruguay), Rapporteur of the Fifth
Committee (spoke in Spanish): I have the honour today
to introduce to the General Assembly the reports
prepared by the Fifth Committee during the fifty-sixth
session of the General Assembly. It will be recalled
that the Fifth Committee was assigned 43 agenda items
during the fifty-sixth session.
Throughout the principal part of the fifty-sixth
session, the Fifth Committee focused its attention
primarily on the agenda items and issues with
deadlines. As members will recall, in conformity with
General Assembly resolution 49/233 A, the Committee
will examine all issues relating to the financing of
peacekeeping operations and other peacekeepingrelated matters during the resumed session.
From 8 October to 24 December, the Fifth
Committee held 40 plenary meetings. Intense
negotiations took place, especially on the draft
programme budget for the 2002-2003 biennium, which
often lasted throughout the night in Conference Room
5. I shall now present a brief report on the results of the
work of the Fifth Committee.
The report of the Committee on agenda item 120,
entitled “Financial reports and audited financial
statements, and reports of the Board of Auditors”, is
contained in document A/56/651. In paragraph 7 of its
report, the Committee recommended to the General
Assembly the adoption of a draft resolution, which the
Committee had adopted without a vote.
With regard to agenda items 121 and 128,
entitled, respectively, “Review of the efficiency of the
administrative and financial functioning of the United
Nations” and “Joint Inspection Unit”, the Committee
considered two thematic reports of the Joint Inspection
Unit, entitled, respectively, “Management of buildings:
practices of selected United Nations system
organizations relevant to the renovation of the United
Nations Headquarters” and “Policies and practices in
the use of the services of private management
consulting firms in the organizations of the United
Nations system”. In paragraph 8 of its report contained
in document A/56/652, the Fifth Committee
recommended to the General Assembly the adoption of
two draft resolutions, which the Committee adopted
without a vote.
