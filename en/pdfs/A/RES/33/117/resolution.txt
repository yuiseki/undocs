33/117.

Use of experts and consultants in the
United Nations

The General Assembly,
Recalling its decision of 18 December 1974, taken
at its twenty-ninth session, 68 by which it outlined
principies and guidelines on the use of experts and
consultants in the United Nations,
Recalling further its decision of 17 December 1975,
taken at its thirtieth session, 69 as well as its resolutions 31/205 of 22 December 1976 and 32/203 of
21 December 1977, in which it reaffirmed the aforesaid principies and guidelines and called for their full
and effective implementation,
Taking note of the report of the Secretary-GeneraFº
and the related oral report of the Advisory Committee
on Administrative and Budgetary Questions, 71
Expressing the view that the comparative data contained in the report of the Secretary-General does not
permit the General Assembly to determine whether
the existing deficiencies have been corrected or to
ascertain fully the status of implementation of the
principies and guidelines set by the Assembly,
Taking note of the assurances of the SecretaryGeneral contained in paragraph 22 of bis report 70 and
those of his representative at the 9th meeting of the
Fifth Committee, on 5 October 1978,72
1. Calls upan the Secretary-General to eliminate
the present deficiencies in the implementation of the
principies and guidelines on the use of experts and
consultants and to improve upan the existing procedures, so that a proper evaluation of the existing
practice in this regard can be carried out;
2. Requests the Secretary-General to submit to
the General Assembly at its thirty-fifth session a full
and comprehensive report on the implementation of
the principies and guidelines set by the Assembly;
3. Further requests the Secretary-General to take
into account the views expressed by Member States
during the consideration of this item when implementing the principies and guidelines on the use of experts
and consultants.
