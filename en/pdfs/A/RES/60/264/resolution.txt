60/264. Admission of the Republic of Montenegro to membership
in the United Nations
The General Assembly,
Having received the recommendation of the Security Council of 22 June 2006
that the Republic of Montenegro should be admitted to membership in the United
Nations, 1
Having considered the application for membership of the Republic of
Montenegro, 2
Decides to admit the Republic of Montenegro to membership in the United
Nations.
