Resolution adopted by the General Assembly on 23 November 2005

60/25.

Observer status for the Latin American Integration
Association in the General Assembly

The General Assembly,
Wishing to promote cooperation between the United Nations and the Latin
American Integration Association,
1.
Decides to invite the Latin American Integration Association to
participate in the sessions and the work of the General Assembly in the capacity of
observer;
Requests the Secretary-General to take the necessary action to implement
2.
the present resolution.
