RESOLUTION ADOPTED BY THE GENERAL ASSEMBLY
[on the report of the Fifth Committee (A/49/673/Add.1)]
49/19.

Scale of assessments for the apportionment of the
expenses of the United Nations
B*

The General Assembly,
Reaffirming that the capacity to pay of Member States is the fundamental
criterion for determining the scale of assessments,
Recognizing the obligation of Member States, under Article 17 of the
Charter of the United Nations, to bear the expenses of the Organization as
apportioned by the General Assembly,
Recalling its resolution 48/223 B of 23 December 1993,
Having considered the report of the Committee on Contributions, 1/
1.
Reaffirms the importance of the recommendations of the Committee
on Contributions for the General Assembly in deciding the apportionment of the
expenses of the Organization among Member States in accordance with rule 160
of the rules of procedure of the Assembly;
2.
Resolves that the scale of assessments for the contributions of
Member States to the regular budget of the United Nations for the years 1995,
1996 and 1997 shall be as follows:
