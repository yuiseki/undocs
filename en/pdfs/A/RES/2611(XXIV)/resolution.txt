2611 (XXIV). Administrative and budgetary coordination of the United Nations with the
specialized agencies and the International
Atomic Energy Agency

The General Assembly
1. Takes note of the reports of the Advisory Committee on Administrative and Budgetary Questions on
general co-ordination matters 41 and on the administrative budgets for 1970 of the specialized agencies and
the International Atomic Energy Agency; 42
2. Requests the Secretary-General to refer the report
on general co-ordination matters to the executive
heads of the specialized agencies and the International

and Budgetary Questions contained in chapter III of
