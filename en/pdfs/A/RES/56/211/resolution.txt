56/211. Integrated and coordinated implementation of and followup to the outcomes of the major United Nations
conferences and summits in the economic and social fields
The General Assembly,
Welcoming Economic and Social Council resolution 2001/21 of 26 July 2001,
1.
Decides to examine how best to address the reviews of the
implementation of the outcomes of the major United Nations conferences and
summits of the 1990s, in the economic and social fields, including their format and
periodicity;
2.
Also decides to include in the provisional agenda of its fifty-seventh
session an item entitled “Integrated and coordinated implementation of and followup to the outcomes of the major United Nations conferences and summits in the
economic and social fields”;
3.
Requests the Secretary-General to make available to the Assembly the
report requested by the Economic and Social Council for consideration at its
substantive session of 2002 on the implementation of Council resolution 2001/21.
