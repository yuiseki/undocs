56/243. Scale of assessments for the apportionment of the expenses of
the United Nations
B1
The General Assembly,
Having considered the letter dated 27 December 2001 from the Secretary-General to
the President of the General Assembly,2
1.
Requests the Committee on Contributions to consider the issues raised in the
letter from the Secretary-General2 and to report thereon to the General Assembly at its
fifty-seventh session;
2.
Decides to consider the matter at its fifty-seventh session, taking into account
the views of the Committee thereon.
