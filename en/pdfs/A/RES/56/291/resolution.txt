56/291. Instances for which the United Nations is entitled to
restitution as the result of non-compliance with status-offorces or other agreements
The General Assembly,
Having considered the report of the Secretary-General on instances for which
the United Nations is entitled to restitution as the result of non-compliance with
status-of-forces or other agreements 1 and the related paragraphs in the report of the
Advisory Committee on Administrative and Budgetary Questions, 2
1.
Takes note of the information contained in the report of the SecretaryGeneral;1
2.

Recalls its resolution 55/12 of 1 November 2000;

3.
Requests the Secretary-General to report further to the General Assembly
on the issues raised in his report that relate to the Federal Republic of Yugoslavia.
105th plenary meeting
27 June 2002
