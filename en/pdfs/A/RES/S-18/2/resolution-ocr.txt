Il. RESOLUTIONS ADOPTED WITHOUT REFERENCE
TO A MAIN COMMITTEE

S-18/1. | Admission of the Republic of Namibia to membership in the United
Nations

The General Assembly,

Having received the recommendation of the Security Council of 17 April 1990
that the Republic of Namibia should be admitted to membership in the United
Nations,”

Having considered the application for membership of the Republic of Namibia,’

Decides to admit the Republic of Namibia to membership in the United
Nations.
