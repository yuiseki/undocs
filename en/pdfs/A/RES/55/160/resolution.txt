55/160. Observer status for the Inter-American Development
Bank in the General Assembly
The General Assembly,
Wishing to promote cooperation between the United Nations and the InterAmerican Development Bank,
1.
Decides to invite the Inter-American Development Bank to participate in
the sessions and the work of the General Assembly in the capacity of observer;
2.
Requests the Secretary-General to take the necessary action to implement
the present resolution.
