55/1.

Admission of Tuvalu to membership in the United Nations
The General Assembly,

Having received the recommendation of the Security Council of 17 February
2000 that Tuvalu should be admitted to membership in the United Nations,1
Having considered the application for membership of Tuvalu, 2
Decides to admit Tuvalu to membership in the United Nations.
