Resolution adopted by the General Assembly on 19 October 2009

64/3. Observer status for the International Olympic Committee in
the General Assembly
The General Assembly,
Wishing to promote cooperation between the United Nations and the
International Olympic Committee,
1.
Decides to invite the International Olympic Committee to participate in
the sessions and the work of the General Assembly in the capacity of observer;
2.
Requests the Secretary-General to take the necessary action to implement
the present resolution.
