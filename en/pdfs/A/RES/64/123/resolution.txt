64/123. Observer status for the International Conference on the
Great Lakes Region of Africa in the General Assembly
The General Assembly,
Wishing to promote cooperation between the United Nations and the
International Conference on the Great Lakes Region of Africa,
1.
Decides to invite the International Conference on the Great Lakes Region
of Africa to participate in the sessions and the work of the General Assembly in the
capacity of observer;
Requests the Secretary-General to take the necessary action to implement
2.
the present resolution.
