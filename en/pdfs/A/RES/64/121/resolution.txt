64/121. Observer status for the International Humanitarian
Fact-Finding Commission in the General Assembly
The General Assembly,
Wishing to promote cooperation between the United Nations and the
International Humanitarian Fact-Finding Commission,
1.
Decides to invite the International Humanitarian Fact-Finding Commission
to participate in the sessions and the work of the General Assembly in the capacity
of observer;
Requests the Secretary-General to take the necessary action to implement
2.
the present resolution.
