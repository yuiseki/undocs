Resolution adopted by the General Assembly on 16 December 2009

64/124. Observer status for the Parliamentary Assembly of the
Mediterranean in the General Assembly
The General Assembly,
Wishing to promote cooperation between the United Nations and the
Parliamentary Assembly of the Mediterranean,
1.
Decides to invite the Parliamentary Assembly of the Mediterranean to
participate in the sessions and the work of the General Assembly in the capacity of
observer;
Requests the Secretary-General to take the necessary action to implement
2.
the present resolution.
