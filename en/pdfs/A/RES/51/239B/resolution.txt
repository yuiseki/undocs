The General Assembly,
Recalling paragraph 18 of its resolution 51/239 A of 17 June 1997,
Having considered the report of the Secretary-General on the Lessons
Learned Unit,2 the related statement by the Chairman of the Advisory Committee
on Administrative and Budgetary Questions and the views expressed by Member
States in the Fifth Committee,3
1.

Learned Unit;2
2.

Advisory Committee on Administrative and Budgetary Questions and the vacancy
rates of posts funded from the support account for peacekeeping operations,

category and above and 5.1 per cent for posts in the General Service category;
3.
