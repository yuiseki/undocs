Resolution adopted by the General Assembly on 9 December 2003
[on the report of the Sixth Committee (A/58/525)]

58/86.

Observer status for the East African Community
in the General Assembly

The General Assembly,
Wishing to promote cooperation between the United Nations and the East
African Community,
1.
Decides to invite the East African Community to participate in the
sessions and the work of the General Assembly in the capacity of observer;
2.
Requests the Secretary-General to take the necessary action to implement
the present resolution.
