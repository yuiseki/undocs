The General Assembly,
Having considered the financial report and audited financial statements for the
twelve-month period from 1 July 2002 to 30 June 2003 and the report of the Board
of Auditors on United Nations peacekeeping operations, 2 the related section of the
report of the Advisory Committee on Administrative and Budgetary Questions 3 and
the report of the Secretary-General on the implementation of the recommendations
of the Board of Auditors concerning United Nations peacekeeping operations in
respect of that period, 4

peacekeeping operations for the period from 1 July 2002 to 30 June 2003; 5
2.

3.

contained in the report of the Advisory Committee on Administrative and Budgetary
Questions3 related to the report of the Board of Auditors;
4.
Commends the Board of Auditors for the quality of its report and the
streamlined format thereof;
