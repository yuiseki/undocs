Resolution adopted by the General Assembly on 10 June 2004
[without reference to a Main Committee (A/58/L.63)]

58/213

Further implementation of the Programme of Action for
the Sustainable Development of Small Island Developing
States
B1

The General Assembly,
Recalling its resolution 58/213 A of 23 December 2003, in which it decided to
convene the International Meeting to Review the Implementation of the Programme
of Action for the Sustainable Development of Small Island Developing States from
30 August to 3 September 2004,
Recalling also the offer of the Government of Mauritius to host the
International Meeting,
Taking note of the request by the Government of Mauritius to reschedule the
International Meeting due to logistical considerations,
Decides to convene the International Meeting to Review the
1.
Implementation of the Programme of Action for the Sustainable Development of
Small Island Developing States from 10 to 14 January 2005;
Also decides to hold, if deemed necessary and taking into account
2.
paragraph 7 of its resolution 58/213 A, two days of informal consultations in
Mauritius, on 8 and 9 January 2005, to facilitate the effective preparation of the
International Meeting;
Takes note of the appointment of a Facilitator for the process of informal
3.
consultations, who will report progress thereon to the Bureau of the International
Meeting, when constituted.
90th plenary meeting
10 June 2004
