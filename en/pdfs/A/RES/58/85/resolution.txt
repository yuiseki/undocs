Resolution adopted by the General Assembly on 9 December 2003

58/85.

Observer status for the GUUAM in the General Assembly

The General Assembly,
Wishing to promote cooperation between the United Nations and the GUUAM,
1.
Decides to invite the GUUAM to participate in the sessions and the work
of the General Assembly in the capacity of observer;
2.
Requests the Secretary-General to take the necessary action to implement
the present resolution.
