61/42. Observer status for the OPEC Fund for International
Development in the General Assembly
The General Assembly,
Wishing to promote cooperation between the United Nations and the OPEC
Fund for International Development,
1.
Decides to invite the OPEC Fund for International Development to
participate in the sessions and the work of the General Assembly in the capacity of
observer;
Requests the Secretary-General to take the necessary action to implement
2.
the present resolution.
