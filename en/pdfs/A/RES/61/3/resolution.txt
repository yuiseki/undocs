61/3. Appointment of the Secretary-General of the United
Nations
The General Assembly,
Having considered the recommendation contained in Security Council
resolution 1715 (2006) of 9 October 2006,
Appoints Mr. Ban Ki-moon Secretary-General of the United Nations for a term
of office beginning on 1 January 2007 and ending on 31 December 2011.
