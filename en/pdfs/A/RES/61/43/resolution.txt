Resolution adopted by the General Assembly on 4 December 2006

61/43. Observer status for the Indian Ocean Commission in the
General Assembly
The General Assembly,
Wishing to promote cooperation between the United Nations and the Indian
Ocean Commission,
1.
Decides to invite the Indian Ocean Commission to participate in the
sessions and the work of the General Assembly in the capacity of observer;
2.
Requests the Secretary-General to take the necessary action to implement
the present resolution.
