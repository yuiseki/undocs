72/128.

Observer status for the Fund for the Development
of the Indigenous Peoples of Latin America and
the Caribbean in the General Assembly
The General Assembly,
Wishing to promote cooperation between the United Nations and the Fund for
the Development of the Indigenous Peoples of Latin America and the Caribbean,
1.
Decides to invite the Fund for the Development of the Indigenous Peoples
of Latin America and the Caribbean to participate in the sessions and the work of the
General Assembly in the capacity of observer;
2.
Requests the Secretary-General to take the action necessary to implement
the present resolution.
