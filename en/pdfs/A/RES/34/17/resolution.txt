The General Assembly,
Having considered the report of the Committee of
the Whole of the Economic Commission for Latin
America on its thirteenth special session, 15
Deeply concerned at the magnitude of the damage
caused in Dominica by hurricanes "David" and "Frederic", which resulted in extensive loss of human life
and destruction of the economic and social infrastructure,
1. Endorses the resolution adopted on 19 October
1979 by the Committee of the Whole of the Economic

Dominica;

informed during the next two years of the results
achieved in the implementation of the present
resolution.
