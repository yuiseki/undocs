37/201. New international humanitarian order

The General Assembly,
Recalling its resolution 36/136 of 14 December 1981,
Taking note of the report of the Secretary-General,!7!

Bearing in mind that, as noted by the Secretary-General
in his report, all Governments that provided their views on

the proposal for the promotion of a new international hu-

Recognizing the need for again seeking the views of Gov-

Noting the proposal for the establishment, outside the
framework of the United Nations, of an independent com-
mission on international humanitarian issues, composed of
leading personalities in the humanitarian field or having
wide experience of government or world affairs, !7?

Recognizing further that the deliberations of such a com-
mission, if established, could be useful for further study of
the proposal,

1. Requests Governments that have not yet done so to
communicate their views on the proposal for the promotion
of a new international humanitarian order to the Secretary-
General;

2. Invites the Secretary-General to provide a more com-
prehensive report on the subject to the General Assembly
at its thirty-eighth session;

3. Decides to review at its thirty-eighth session the ques-
tion of a new international humanitarian order.
