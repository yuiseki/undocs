2128 (XX). System of travel and subsistence
allowances to members of organs and subsidiary organs of the United Nations

The General Assembly,
Recalling the provisions of its resolution 1798
(XVII) of 11 December 1962 on the system of travel
and subsistence allowances to members of organs and
subsidiary organs of the United Nations as well as the
provisions of the annex to that resolution,
Having noted the observations and recommendations
of the Advisory Committee on Administrative and
Budgetary Questions in paragraphs 95 to 99 of its
sixth report to the General Assembly at its twentieth
session, 43

1798 ( XVII) sha 11 be amended to read as follows :
"4. Payment of travel expenses shall, in all cases,
