1506 (XV). Question of the publication of a
United Nations juridical yearbook

The General Assembly,
Recalling its resolution 1451 (XIV) of 7 December
1959 by which it decided that a United Nations juridical
yearbook which would include documentary materials

Fifteenth Se11sion

of a legal character relating to the United Nations
should be published,
Considering that encouragement of the development
of international law and of compliance therewith are
essential functions of the United Nations and that the
publication of the said yearbook should constitute a
useful measure to that end,
Taking into account that, in the light of the discussion in the Sixth Committee, further study is required
on the form and contents of the proposed yearbook and
on the financial implications of its publication,
1. Decides to place the question of the publication
of a United Nations juridical yearbook on the provisional agenda of its seventeenth session;
2. Invites Member States to submit to the SecretaryGeneral, not later than 1 June 1962, written comments
or observations on the form and contents of the proposed yearbook.
943rd plenary meeting,
