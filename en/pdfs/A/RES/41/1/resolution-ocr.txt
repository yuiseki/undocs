41/2. Emergency assistance to El Salvador

The General Assembly,

Deeply distressed by the loss of lives, the number of af-
flicted persons and the destruction wrought by the earth-
quake which, on 10 October 1986, struck the city of San
Salvador, capital of El Salvador, and adjoining villages,

Conscious of the efforts of the Government and people of
EI Salvador to save lives and alleviate the sufferings of the
victims of the cataclysm,

Noting the enormous effort that will be required to al-
leviate the grave situation caused by this natural disaster,
especially given the particular circumstances under which
the people of El Salvador are living,

Conscious also of the prompt response being made by
Governments, international agencies, non-governmental
organizations and private individuals to provide emer-
gency disaster relief,
