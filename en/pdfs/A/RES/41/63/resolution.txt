session, in its working groups, continued:
(a) Its detailed consideration of the legal implications
of remote sensing of the Earth from space, with the aim of

(c) Its consideration of matters relating to the defini­

and utilization of the geostationary orbit, including con­

equitable use of the geostationary orbit, without prejudice

ing countries:

through its working group;
