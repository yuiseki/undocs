Rights, the International Covenant on Civil
and Political Rights and the Optional Pro-

The General Assembly,

Having noted the report of the Secretary-General*®
on the status of the International Covenant on Eco-
nomic, Social and Cultural Rights, the International
Covenant on Civil and Political Rights and the Op-
tional Protocol to the International Covenant on Civil
and Political Rights,*®

Convinced that the coming into force of the Inter-
national Covenants on Human Rights will greatly en-
hance the ability of the United Nations to promote and
encourage respect for human rights and fundamental
freedoms for all, without distinction as to race, sex,
language or religion, and will contribute to the attain-
ment of the purposes and principles of the Charter of
the United Nations,

Recalling its resolution 3025 (XXVII) of 18 De-
cember 1972, and in particular the hope expressed
that Member States would find it possible to take ap-
