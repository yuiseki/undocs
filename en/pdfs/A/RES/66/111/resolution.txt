Resolution adopted by the General Assembly on 9 December 2011

66/111. Observer status for the Central European Initiative
in the General Assembly
The General Assembly,
Wishing to promote cooperation between the United Nations and the Central
European Initiative,
1.
Decides to invite the Central European Initiative to participate in the
sessions and the work of the General Assembly in the capacity of observer;
2.
Requests the Secretary-General to take the necessary action to implement
the present resolution.
