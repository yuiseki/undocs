66/112. Observer status for the Intergovernmental Authority
on Development in the General Assembly
The General Assembly,
Wishing to promote cooperation between the United Nations and the
Intergovernmental Authority on Development,
1.
Decides to invite the Intergovernmental Authority on Development to
participate in the sessions and the work of the General Assembly in the capacity of
observer;
Requests the Secretary-General to take the necessary action to implement
2.
the present resolution.
