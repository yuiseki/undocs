66/292. Global Day of Parents
The General Assembly
1.
Decides to proclaim 1 June the Global Day of Parents, to be observed
annually, honouring parents throughout the world;
2.
Invites Member States to celebrate the Global Day of Parents in full
partnership with civil society, particularly involving young people and children;
3.
Requests the Secretary-General to bring the present resolution to the
attention of all Member States, organizations of the United Nations system and civil
society organizations for appropriate observance.
