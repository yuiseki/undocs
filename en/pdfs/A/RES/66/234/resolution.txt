Organization, and commending its contribution to furthering the purposes and
principles of the United Nations,

Decides that staff regulation 1.2 (m) shall be amended to read:

“A conflict of interest occurs when, by act or omission, a staff member’s
personal interests interfere with the performance of his or her official duties
and responsibilities or with the integrity, independence and impartiality
required by the staff member’s status as an international civil servant. When an
actual or possible conflict of interest does arise, the conflict shall be disclosed
by staff members to their head of office, mitigated by the Organization and
resolved in favour of the interests of the Organization”;
13. Welcomes the significant efforts made by the Ethics Office towards the
