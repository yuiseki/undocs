The General Assembly,
Recalling the Declaration on the Granting of Independence to Colonial Countries and Peoples, contained
in its resolution 1514 (XV) of 14 December 1960,
Recalling its resolutions 1654 (XVI) of 27 November 1961, 1810 (XVII) of 17 December 1962, 1956

to Colonial Countries and Peoples tasks relating to
South West Africa, and its resolution 1970 (XVIII)
of 16 December 1963, by which it entrusted to the
Special Committee additional functions relating to information transmitted under Article 73 e of the Charter
of the United Nations,

adoption of the Declaration many Territories are still

the intransigent attitude of the Governments of Portugal
