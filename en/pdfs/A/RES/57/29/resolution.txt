Resolution adopted by the General Assembly

57/29.

Observer status for Partners in Population and
Development in the General Assembly

The General Assembly,
Considering the importance of the work of Partners in Population and
Development,
Taking into account the importance of greater interaction among Partners in
Population and Development, Member States, and relevant bodies, programmes and
agencies of the United Nations in the area of population and development,
Wishing to promote cooperation between the United Nations and Partners in
Population and Development,
1.
Decides to invite Partners in Population and Development to participate
in the sessions and the work of the General Assembly in the capacity of observer;
2.
Requests the Secretary-General to take the necessary action to implement
the present resolution.
