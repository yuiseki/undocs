Resolution adopted by the General Assembly
[on the report of the Fifth Committee (A/57/656/Add.1)]

57/319. Feasibility of consolidating the accounts of the various
peacekeeping operations
The General Assembly,
Recalling paragraph 10 of its resolution 56/293 of 27 June 2002,
Having considered the report of the Secretary-General on the feasibility of
consolidating the accounts of the various peacekeeping operations1 and the related report
of the Advisory Committee on Administrative and Budgetary Questions,2
1.
Takes note of the report of the Secretary-General1 and of the related report of
the Advisory Committee on Administrative and Budgetary Questions;2
2.
Decides to defer consideration of this question until the second part of its
resumed fifty-eighth session, and requests the Secretary-General to provide a
comprehensive report, taking into account the views expressed, questions raised and
information requested by Member States at its fifty-seventh session, including a simulation
of the options proposed.
