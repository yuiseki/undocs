the United Nations and by voluntary 11011-gov:>rnmental

in residual accounts when the Administrator for Residual Affairs of the Agency has completed his task

of Korea";
5. Further reaffirms the action taken at its twelfth
session regarding the arrangements and procedures for
the completion of the residual responsibilities of the
Agency and the liquidation of its accounts.
