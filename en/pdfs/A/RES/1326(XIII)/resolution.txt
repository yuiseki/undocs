1327 (XIII). International collaboration in respect of Non-Self-Governing and Trust Territories in Africa
The General Assembly,
Having regard to the objectives set forth in Chapter
XI, and particularly Article 73 d, and in Chapter XII
of the Charter of the United Nations,
Having regard to its resolution 331 (IV) of 2 December 1949 on international collaboration in connexion
with economic, social and educational conditions in NonSelf-Governing Territories,
Considering that the terms of reference contained in
Economic and Social Council resolution 671 A (XXV)
of 29 April 1958 establishing an Economic Commission
for Africa take fully into account measures to be taken
towards the economic advancement of Non-Self-Governing and Trust Territories in Africa, including its social
aspects, these measures lying within the scope of tJie
Commission's activities,
Considering that many Territories on which information is transmitted by the Administering Members concerned under Article 73 e of the Charter and a majority
of Trust Territories are in Africa, and that the terms
of reference of the Economic Commission for Africa
provide for such Territories to become associate members of the Commission,
1. Welcomes the establishment of the Economic Commission for Africa as an important step towards the
further raising of the levels of living in N on-Self-Governing and Trust Territories in Africa ;

2. Expresses the hope that all African Territories
will apply for associate membership in the Economic
Commission for Africa through 'the Member States
responsible for their administration ;

Noting further that the progress made in certain
Territories towards the removal of practices and the
abolition of laws based on racial grounds has been
limited,
Having regard to the fundamental importance of race
relations, particularly under modern conditions, for the
attainment of the objectives of Chapter XI of the Charter
of the United Nations,
Recognizing the necessity of intensifying the promotion and encouragement of respect for human rights
and fundamental freedoms for all, regardless of race,
sex, language or religion,

1. Reaffenns its resolution 644 (VII), and draws the
particular attention of the Administering Members to
the recommendation contained in paragraph 2 thereof
concerning the examination of existing discriminatory
law~, statutes and ordinances, and their application, with
a. 'Y!ew to the ab?lition of any such discriminatory prov1s1ons and practices ;
2. Urges all Members responsible for the administration of Non-Self-Governing Territories to pay special
and constant attention in the future to the implementation of the terms of its resolution 644 (VII) ;
3. Invites the Administering Members to include in
the annual reports submitted under Article 73 e of the
Charter of the United Nations information on the measures taken by them for the implementation of the present
resolution.
