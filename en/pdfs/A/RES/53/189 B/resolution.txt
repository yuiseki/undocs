RESOLUTION ADOPTED BY THE GENERAL ASSEMBLY

53/189. Implementation of the outcome of the Global Conference on the Sustainable Development of
Small Island Developing States
B*
The General Assembly,
Recalling its resolutions 51/183 of 16 December 1996, 52/202 of 18 December 1997 and 53/189 of
15 December 1998,
Noting the importance of the decision taken at its nineteenth special session1 to convene a two-day special
session in New York in September 1999,
Decides to hold the special session on 27 and 28 September 1999, for a full and comprehensive review and
appraisal of the implementation of the Programme of Action for the Sustainable Development of Small Island
Developing States.2
97th plenary meeting
7 April 1999

*

Consequently, resolution 53/189 of 15 December 1998 becomes resolution 53/189 A.
