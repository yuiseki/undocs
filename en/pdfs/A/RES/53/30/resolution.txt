RESOLUTION ADOPTED BY THE GENERAL ASSEMBLY
[without reference to a Main Committee (A/53/L.46)]
53/30.

Question of equitable representation on and increase in the membership
of the Security Council and related matters

The General Assembly,
Mindful of Chapter XVIII of the Charter of the United Nations and of the importance of reaching
general agreement as referred to in resolution 48/26 of 3 December 1993, determines not to adopt any
resolution or decision on the question of equitable representation on and increase in the membership of
the Security Council and related matters, without the affirmative vote of at least two thirds of the Members
of the General Assembly.
