1145 (XII). Agreement governing the relationship between the United Nations and the
International Atomic Energy Agency

Having considered the application for membership
of the Federation of Malaya,

The General Assembly,

Decides to admit the Federation of Malaya to membership in the United Nations.

1136 (XII). Report of the Committee on arrangements for a conference for the purpose
of reviewing the Charter

Noting that the General Conference and the Board of
Governors of the International Atomic Energy Agency
have approved the above-mentioned Agreement,11

The General Assembly,
Recalling the provisions of its resolution 992 (X)
of 21 November 1955,
Having considered the report of the Committee established by the above resolution,2
1. Decides to keep in being the Committee on arrangements for a conference for the purpose of reviewing the Charter, established by General Assembly resolution 992 (X) and composed of all Members of the
United Nations, and to request the Committee to report
with recommendations, to the General Assembly not
later than at its fourteenth session;

Approves the Agreement governing the relationship
between the United Nations and the International
Atomic Energy Agency, as set forth in the annex to the
present resolution.
