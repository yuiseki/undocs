Account for the United Nations Emergency Force and the
United Nations Disengagement Observer Force, as set
forth in the report of the Secretary-General,2 and referring
to paragraph 5 of the report of the Advisory Committee on
Administrative and Budgetary Questions,3

United Nations Disengagement Observer Force with the
ne�ssary financial resources to enable it to fulfil its
responsibilities under the relevant resolutions of the Secu­
rity Council,

face growing difficulties in meeting the obligations of the
Forces on a current basis, particularly those due to the
Governments of troop-contributing States,

34/7 D of 17 December 1979, 35145 B of I December

tions of the United Nations would aggravate the already
