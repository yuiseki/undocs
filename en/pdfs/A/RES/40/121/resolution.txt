Taking note of the reiterated concern expressed by the
Secretary-General in his report on the work of the Organization, 146 in which he recognizes that the drug problem can

Acknowledging once more that the eradication of this
scourge calls for integrated action which will simultaneously tackle the problems of reduction and control of illicit
