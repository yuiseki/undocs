The General Assembly,

Having considered the report of the Secretary-General
on the financing of the United Nations Observer Group in

Committee on Administrative and Budgetary Questions?

of 7 November 1989, by which the Council established the

period of six months,

Recognizing that the costs of the Group are expenses of
the Organization to be borne by Member States in ac-
cordance with Article 17, paragraph 2. of the Charter of
the United Nations,

Mindful of the fact that tt ts essential to provide the
