11. Confidence-building measures could help to create a political cli­

12. The United Nations should play a central role in orienting,

plied by this process.
13. The freezing and reduction of military expenditures may be

14. The agreements on the freezing and reduction of military bud­

implementation of the security system of the United Nations, and be in­

progress towards general and complete disarmament under effective in­

be complementary to agreements on the limitation of armaments and
disarmament and should not be considered as a substitute for such agree­
