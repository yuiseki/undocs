44/74. United Nations Development Fund for Women

Reaffirming the decisions contained in its resolution

Emphasizing the catalytic role of the United Nations
Development Fund for Women in the United Nations sys-

organizations and financial institutions and its support for
innovative and experimental activities directly benefiting
women in line with national and regional priorities,

Reaffirming those dual priorities of the Fund, which
would better position women for more effective participa-
tion in the development of their countries,

Recognizing the mainstream initiatives of the Fund to
assist national machineries on women, ministries con-
