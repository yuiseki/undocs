(d) Inclusion, on an overall average basis, of the remuneration ad-

(e) Determination of gross salary through reverse application of the

(f) Determination of net salary for staff with neither a dependent

multiplier applicable on | July 1990 will be determined, using if neces-
sary partial classes of post adjustment, so as to arrive at total net emolu-
ments,% which, when compared with the corresponding net emoluments
that would have been applicable on 1 July 1990 on the basis of the cur-

of the movement of the revised post adjustment index.
