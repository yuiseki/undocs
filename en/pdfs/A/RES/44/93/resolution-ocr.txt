44/93. Question of the Turks and Caicos Islands

The General Assembly,

Having considered the question of the Turks and Caicos
Islands,

Having examined the relevant chapters of the report of
the Special Committee on the Situation with regard to the
Implementation of the Declaration on the Granting of In-
dependence to Colonial Countries and Peoples, !®

Recalling its resolution 1514 (XV) of 14 December
1960, containing the Declaration on the Granting of Inde-
pendence to Colonial Countries and Peoples, and all reso-
lutions and decisions of the United Nations relating to the
Turks and Caicos Islands, including in particular General
Assembly resolution 43/40 of 22 November 1988,

Conscious of the need to ensure the full and speedy im-
plementation of the Declaration in respect of the Terri-
tory,

Having heard the statement of the representative of the
United Kingdom of Great Britain and Northern Ireland,
as the administering Power,!?

Noting the stated policy of the Government of the
United Kingdom, the administering Power, that it re-
mains ready to respond positively to the express wish of
the people of the Territory on the question of indepen
dence, !

Noting that the efforts of the administering Power to re
solve the 1986 constitutional crisis in the Territory re
sulted in the drafting of a new Constitution by the admin-
istering Power and the subsequent holding of general
elections in March 1988,

Aware of the special circumstances of the geographical
location and economic conditions of the Turks and Caicos
Islands and bearing in mind the necessity of diversifying
and strengthening further its economy as a matter of pri-
ority in order to promote economic stability and develop a
wider economic base for the Territory,

Noting with concern the vulnerability of the Territory to
drug trafficking and related activities,

Noting the continuing contribution of the United Na-
tions Development Programme to the economic and so
cial development of the Territory,

Recalling the dispatch in 1980 of two United Nations
visiting missions to the Territory,

Mindful that United Nations visiting missions provide
an effective means of ascertaining the situation in the smal}
Territories and considering that the possibility of sending
a further visiting mission to the Turks and Caicos Islands
al an appropriate time should be kept under review.

in accordance with resolution 1514 (XV) and all other

Territory of the possibilities open to them in the exercise
of their right to self-determination and independence;

ritorial Government, to counter problems related to drug

to the development needs of the Turks and Caicos Islands;

11. Requests the Special Committee to continue the ex-
amination of this question at its next session, including the
possible dispatch of a further visiting mission to the Turks
and Caicos Islands at an appropriate time and in consulta-
tion with the administering Power, and to report thereon
to the General Assembly at its forty-fifth session.

the Special Committee on the Situation with regard to the
Implementation of the Declaration on the Granting of In-
dependence to Colonial Countries and Peoples, !6

Anguilla, including in particular General Assembly reso-

Conscious of the need to ensure the full and speedy im-
plementation of the Declaration in respect of the Terri-
tory,

Having heard the statement of the representative of the
United Kingdom of Great Britain and Northern Ireland,
as the administering Power,!’

a priority of the Government of the Territory,

towards independence during its current term of office,

mains ready to respond positively to the express wish of
the people of the Territory on the question of indepen-
dence,!
