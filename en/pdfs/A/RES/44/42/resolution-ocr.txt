Stressing that achieving a comprehensive settlement of

Preoccupied by the increasingly serious situation in the
occupied Palestinian territory as a result of persistent poli-

Aware of the ongoing uprising (intifadah) of the Pales-

rights of the Palestinian people, primarily the right to self-
determination;

3. Reaffirms the following principles for the achieve-
ment of comprehensive peace:

(b) Guaranteeing arrangements for security of all
States in the region, including those named in resolution
181 (ID) of 29 November 1947, within secure and interna-
tionally recognized boundaries:

(c) Resolving the problem of the Palestine refugees in
conformity with Gencral Assembly resolution 194 CUD of
11 December 1948, and subsequent relevant resolutions;

(d) Dismantling the Israeli settlements in the territo-
Ties occupied since 1967:

(e) Guaranteeing freedom of access to Holy Places,

ference on the Middle East, including the establishment of

forts with the parties concerned, and in consultation with

Conference, and to submit progress reports on develop-
