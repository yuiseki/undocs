45/14. Implementation of the Declaration on the

Right of Peoples to Peace

The General Assembly,

Recalling the Declaration on the Right of Peoples to
Peace, adopted in its resolution 39/11 of 12 November
1984,

Stressing that the emerging new positive trends and
events in international relations are becoming increas-
ingly conducive to strengthening universal peace and
security and to implementing the spirit and letter of the
Declaration,

Noting with satisfaction the renaissance of the peace-
keeping and peace-making potential of the United Na-
tions,

Having in mind the Universal Declaration of Human
Rights,’ which emphasizes that recognition of the in-
herent dignity and of the equal and inalienable rights
of all members of the human family is the foundation
of freedom, justice and peace in the world,
