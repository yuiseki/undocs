child

The General Assembly,
Recalling its previous resolutions, as well as Commission on Human Rights and Economic and Social Council
resolutions, on the question of a convention on the rights
of the child,
Reaffirmingthat children's rights require special protection and call for continuous improvement of the situation
of children ali over the world, as well as their development
and education in conditions of peace and security,
Prafoundly concerned that the situation of children in
many parts of the world remains critica! as a result of unsatisfactory social conditions, natural disasters, armed
conflicts, exploitation, illiteracy, hunger and disability and

convinced that urgent and effective national and international action is called for,
Mindful of the important role of the United Nations
Children's Fund and the United Nations in promoting the
well-being of children and their development,
Canvinced of the positive contribution that an international convention on the rights of the child, as a standardsetting accomplishment of the United Nations in the field
of human rights, would make to protecting children's

Noting with appreciatian that the first reading of a full
text of a draft convention on the rights of the child has
been completed by the open-ended working group of the

Bearing in mind that 1989 marks the thirtieth anniversary of the Declaration on the Rights of the Child 81 and
the tenth anniversary of the International Year of the
Child,
Considering that these anniversaries could constitute an
appropriate target date for completion of the work on a
draft convention on the rights of the child and for its adoption by the General Assembly at its forty-fourth session, in
1989,
Bearing in mind the necessity of taking due account of

those rights in the future convention,
l. Welcomes Economic and Social Council resolution

a meeting of the open-ended working group of the Commission on Human Rights for a period of up to two weeks
in November-December 1988, with a view to completing
the second reading of the draft convention on the rights of
the child prior to the forty-fifth session ofthe Commission;
2. Requests the Commission on Human Rights to give
the highest priority to the draft convention on the rights of
the child and to make every effort at its session in 1989 to
complete it and to submit it, through the Economic and
Soc~al Council, to the General Assembly at its forty-fourth
sess10n;
3. Invites ali Member States to offer their active support to the completion of the draft convention on the
rights of the child in 1989, the year of the thirtieth anniversary of the Declaration on the Rights of the Child and
of the tenth anniversarv of the International Year of the
Child;
4. Requests the Secretary-General to provide ali the
support and facilities necessary for the successful completion and adoption of the draft convention on the rights of
