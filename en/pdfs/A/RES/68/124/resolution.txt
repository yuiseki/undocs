Resolution adopted by the General Assembly on 16 December 2013
[on the report of the Sixth Committee (A/68/481)]

68/124. Observer status for the Global Green Growth Institute in
the General Assembly
The General Assembly,
Wishing to promote cooperation between the United Nations and the Global
Green Growth Institute,
1.
Decides to invite the Global Green Growth Institute to participate in the
sessions and the work of the General Assembly in the capacity of observer;
2.
Requests the Secretary-General to take the action necessary to implement
the present resolution.
