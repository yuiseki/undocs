68/123. Observer status for the Pan African Intergovernmental
Agency for Water and Sanitation for Africa in the
General Assembly
The General Assembly,
Wishing to promote cooperation between the United Nations and the Pan
African Intergovernmental Agency for Water and Sanitation for Africa,
1.
Decides to invite the Pan African Intergovernmental Agency for Water
and Sanitation for Africa to participate in the sessions and the work of the General
Assembly in the capacity of observer;
2.
Requests the Secretary-General to take the action necessary to implement
the present resolution.
