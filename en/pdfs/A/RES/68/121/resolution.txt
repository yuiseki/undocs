68/121. Observer status for the International Institute for the
Unification of Private Law in the General Assembly
The General Assembly,
Wishing to promote cooperation between the United Nations and the
International Institute for the Unification of Private Law,
1.
Decides to invite the International Institute for the Unification of Private
Law to participate in the sessions and the work of the General Assembly in the
capacity of observer;
2.
Requests the Secretary-General to take the action necessary to implement
the present resolution.
