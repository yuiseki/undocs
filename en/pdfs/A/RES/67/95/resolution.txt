67/95. Thirtieth anniversary of the Manila Declaration on
the Peaceful Settlement of International Disputes
The General Assembly,
Acknowledging that 15 November 2012 will mark the thirtieth anniversary of
the Manila Declaration on the Peaceful Settlement of International Disputes, as
approved by the General Assembly in its resolution 37/10 of 15 November 1982,
which was adopted without a vote,
Recalling that the Manila Declaration was negotiated on the initiative of
Egypt, Indonesia, Mexico, Nigeria, the Philippines, Romania, Sierra Leone and
Tunisia and on the basis of a text prepared by the Special Committee on the Charter
of the United Nations and on the Strengthening of the Role of the Organization,
Recalling also that the Manila Declaration was the first instrument adopted by
the General Assembly as a result of the work of the Special Committee,
Recalling further that the Manila Declaration is a landmark declaration on the
peaceful settlement of international disputes, building upon the Charter of the
United Nations, in particular its Article 33,
Recalling that the International Court of Justice is the principal judicial organ
of the United Nations,
Recognizes the Manila Declaration on the Peaceful Settlement of
1.
International Disputes as a concrete accomplishment of the Special Committee on
the Charter of the United Nations and on the Strengthening of the Role of the
Organization, and welcomes the thirtieth anniversary of the adoption of the
Declaration;
Reiterates its call to all States to observe and promote in good faith the
2.
Manila Declaration in the peaceful settlement of their international disputes;
Encourages all Member States to commemorate the thirtieth anniversary
3.
of the adoption of the Manila Declaration through appropriate activities.
