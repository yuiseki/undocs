67/102. Observer status for the European Organization
for Nuclear Research in the General Assembly
The General Assembly,
Wishing to promote cooperation between the United Nations and the European
Organization for Nuclear Research,
1.
Decides to invite the European Organization for Nuclear Research to
participate in the sessions and the work of the General Assembly in the capacity of
observer;
Requests the Secretary-General to take the necessary action to implement
2.
the present resolution.
