Bearing in mind Security Council resolution 626 (1988)

which the Council decided to entrust a new mandate to the

seventeen months,

Reaffirming that the costs of the Verification Mission
are expenses of the Organization to be borne by Member
States in accordance with Article 17, paragraph 2, of the

in order to meet the expenditures caused by the Verifica-

applied to meet expenditures of the regular budget of the
United Nations,

Taking into account the fact that the economically more
developed countries are in a position to make relatively
larger contributions and that the economically less devel-
oped countries have a relatively limited capacity to con-
tribute towards such an operation,

permanent members of the Security Council, as indicated
in General Assembly resolution 1874 (S-IV) of 27 June
1963, in the financing of such operations,

fication Mission with the necessary financial resources to

the United Nations Angola Verification Mission IJ in full

Committee for the operation of the Verification Mission
