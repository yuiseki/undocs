2 General Assembly—Forty-sixth Session

46/224. Admission of the Republic of Kazakhstan to
membership in the United Nations

The General Assembly,

Having received the recommendation of the Security
Council of 23 January 1992 that the Republic of Kazakh-
stan should be admitted to membership in the United Na-
tions,°

Having considered the application for membership of
the Republic of Kazakhstan,*

Decides to admit the Republic of Kazakhstan to mem-
bership in the United Nations.

82nd plenary meeting
2 March 1992
