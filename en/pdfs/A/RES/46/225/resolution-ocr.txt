[without reference to a Main Committee (A/46/L.60 and Add.1)]

46/225. Admission of the Republic of Kyrgyzstan to membership in

the United Nations

The General Assembly,

Having received the recommendation of the Security Council of
29 January 1992 that the Republic of Kyrgyzstan should be admitted to
membership in the United Nations, 1/

Having considered the application for membership of the Republic of
Kyrgyzstan, 2/

Decides to admit the Republic of Kyrgyzstan to membership in the United
Nations.
