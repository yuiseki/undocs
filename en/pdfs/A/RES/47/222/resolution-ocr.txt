47/222. Admission of the Slovak Republic to membership

in the United Nations

The General Assembly,

Having received the recommendation of the Security Council of
8 January 1993 that the Slovak Republic should be admitted to membership in
the United Nations, 1/

Having considered the application for membership of the Slovak
Republic, 2/

Decides to admit the Slovak Republic to membership in the United
Nations.
