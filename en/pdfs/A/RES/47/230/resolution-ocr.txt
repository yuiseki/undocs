47/230. Admission of Eritrea to membership in the United Nations

The General Assembly,

Having received the recommendation of the Security Council of
26 May 1993 that Eritrea should be admitted to membership in the United
Nations, 1/

Having considered the application for membership of Eritrea, 2/

Decides to admit Eritrea to membership in the United Nations.
