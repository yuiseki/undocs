47/231. Admission of the Principality of Monaco to membership in the

United Nations

The General Assembly,

Having received the recommendation of the Security Council of 26 May
1993 that the Principality of Monaco should be admitted to membership in the

United Nations, 1/

Having considered the application for membership of the Principality of
Monaco, 2/

Decides to admit the Principality of Monaco to membership in the United
Nations.
