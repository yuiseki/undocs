65/308. Admission of the Republic of South Sudan to membership
in the United Nations
The General Assembly,
Having received the recommendation of the Security Council of 13 July 2011
that the Republic of South Sudan should be admitted to membership in the United
Nations, 1
Having considered the application for membership of the Republic of South
Sudan, 2
Decides to admit the Republic of South Sudan to membership in the United
Nations.
