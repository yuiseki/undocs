65/261. Procurement
The General Assembly,
Having considered the comprehensive report of the Secretary-General on United
Nations procurement activities, 1 and the addenda to the comprehensive report on
procurement governance arrangements within the United Nations 2 and on sustainable
procurement, 3 the related report of the Advisory Committee on Administrative and
Budgetary Questions 4 and the report of the Office of Internal Oversight Services on
the audit of procurement management in the Secretariat, 5
Having also considered the reports of the Joint Inspection Unit on offshoring
in United Nations system organizations 6 and on the environmental profile of the
United Nations system of organizations, 7 as well as the notes by the SecretaryGeneral transmitting his comments and those of the United Nations System Chief
Executives Board for Coordination thereon, 8
Requests the Secretary-General to submit an updated comprehensive
1.
report on United Nations procurement activities for consideration by the General
Assembly at the first part of its resumed sixty-seventh session;
Decides to consider further the reports referenced in the present resolution
2.
at the first part of its resumed sixty-seventh session.
