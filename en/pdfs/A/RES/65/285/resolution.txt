65/285. Review of the implementation of General Assembly
resolution 61/16 on the strengthening of the Economic
and Social Council
The General Assembly,
Recalling the 2005 World Summit Outcome, 1
Recalling also its resolution 61/16 of 20 November 2006 on the strengthening
of the Economic and Social Council,
1.
Takes note of the report of the President of the General Assembly on the
review of the implementation of General Assembly resolution 61/16 on the
strengthening of the Economic and Social Council; 2
2.
Encourages Member States, the Economic and Social Council, the
regional commissions and other entities of the United Nations system to consider
the recommendations contained in the report;
Decides to review further the implementation of resolution 61/16 at its
3.
sixty-seventh session.
105th plenary meeting
29 June 2011
