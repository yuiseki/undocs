65/184. Cooperatives in social development
The General Assembly,
Recalling its resolution 64/136 of 18 December 2009, by which it proclaimed
the year 2012 the International Year of Cooperatives and encouraged all Member
States, as well as the United Nations and all other relevant stakeholders, to take
advantage of the Year as a way of promoting cooperatives and raising awareness of
their contribution to social and economic development,
Decides to convene, at its sixty-sixth session, one plenary meeting of the
1.
General Assembly devoted to the launch of the International Year of Cooperatives
2012, within existing resources;
Also decides to hold, prior to the plenary meeting, an informal,
2.
interactive round-table discussion among Member States, observers, organizations
of the United Nations system, cooperatives and non-governmental organizations in
consultative status with the Economic and Social Council;
Further decides that the President of the General Assembly, in
3.
consultation with Member States, should decide on the theme of the informal
round-table discussion as well as which Member State should chair the discussion;
Decides that one representative of cooperatives, nominated by the
4.
President of the General Assembly in consultation with Member States and relevant
representatives of cooperatives, shall orally present a summary of the informal
round-table discussion to the General Assembly at the beginning of the plenary
meeting;
Invites Member States to consider including representatives of
5.
cooperatives in their delegations or, as appropriate, being represented by
representatives of cooperatives at the plenary meeting of the General Assembly on
this occasion, as well as at the informal round-table discussion, bearing in mind the
principle of gender balance;
Invites all Member States to consider taking action towards establishing
6.
national mechanisms, such as national committees, to prepare for, observe and
follow up on the International Year of Cooperatives, in particular for the purpose of
