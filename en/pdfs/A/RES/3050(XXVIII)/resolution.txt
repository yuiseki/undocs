3050 (XXVIII). Admission of the German Democratic Republic and the Federal Republic of
Germany to membership in the United
Nations

The General Assembly,
Having received the recommendation of the Security
Council of 22 June 1973 that the German Democratic
Republic and the Federal Republic of Germany should
be admitted to membership in the United Nations,1
Having considered separately the application for
membership of the German Democratic Republic 2 and
the application for membership of the Federal Republic of Germany, 8
I. Decides to admit the German Democratic Republic to membership in the United Nations;
2. Decides to admit the Federal Republic of Germany to membership in the United Nations.
