C1

The General Assembly,
Having considered the financial report and audited financial statements for the twelve-month period
from 1 July 1998 to 30 June 1999 and the report of the Board of Auditors on United Nations peacekeeping

Auditors, and endorses the observations and recommendations contained in the report of the Advisory
Committee on Administrative and Budgetary Questions;3
