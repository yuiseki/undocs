69/130. Observer status for the Pacific Community in the
General Assembly
The General Assembly,
Wishing to promote cooperation between the United Nations and the Pacific
Community,
1.
Decides to invite the Pacific Community to participate in the sessions
and the work of the General Assembly in the capacity of observer;
2.
Requests the Secretary-General to take the action necessary to implement
the present resolution.
