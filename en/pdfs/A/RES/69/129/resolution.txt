69/129. Observer status for the Developing Eight Countries
Organization for Economic Cooperation in the
General Assembly
The General Assembly,
Wishing to promote cooperation between the United Nations and the
Developing Eight Countries Organization for Economic Cooperation,
1.
Decides to invite the Developing Eight Countries Organization for
Economic Cooperation to participate in the sessions and the work of the General
Assembly in the capacity of observer;
2.
Requests the Secretary-General to take the action necessary to implement
the present resolution.
