PROBLEM OF EX-PRISONERS OF THE KOREAN WAR
The General Assembly,

Noting that, pending their final disposition, a num-
ber of ex-prisoners of the Korean war remain tempo
rarily in India,

1. Notes with appreciation that the Governments of
Argentina and Brazil have generously offered to resettle
as many of the ex-prisoners as opt to settle in those coun-

tions with regard to arrangements are taking place;

2. Requests the Governments of Member States able
to do so, to assist in bringing about a full solution of this
problem by accepting for resettlement those ex-prisoners
not covered by the present offers ;

3. Requests the Government of India to report to the.
