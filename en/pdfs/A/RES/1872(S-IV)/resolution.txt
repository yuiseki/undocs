General Assembly-Fourth Special Session

RESOLUTION ADOPTED WITHOUT REFERENCE
TO A COMMITIEE

1872 (S-IV). Admission of Kuwait to membership in the United Nations
The General Assembly,
Having received the recommendation of the Security Council of 7 May 1963
that Kuwait should be admitted to membership in the United Nations, 16
Having considered the application for membership of Kuwait, 17
Decides to admit Kuwait to membership in the United Nations.
1203rd plenary meeting,
14 May 1963.
