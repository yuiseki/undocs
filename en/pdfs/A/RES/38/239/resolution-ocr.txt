ANNEX
Amendments to the Pension Scheme Regulations of the

Article 1

RETIREMENT PENSION

Replace “the age of sixty-five” by “the age of sixty’ wherever the term
appears.

In paragraph | (a), replace “five years of service” by “three years of ser-
vice”.

Article H

DISABILITY PENSION

Replace paragraph 2 by the following text:

“2. The amount of the disability pension shall be equal to the amount
of the retirement pension which would have been payable 10 the mem-
ber of the Court concerned had he, at the time of leaving office, com-
pleted the term for which he had been elected, provided that it shall not
be less than one quarter of the annual salary”.

Article HH

In paragraph 3 (6) and (c), replace “the age of sixty-five” by “the age of
sixty”.
