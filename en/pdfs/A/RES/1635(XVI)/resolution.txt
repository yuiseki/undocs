1625 (XVI). Memorial to the late Dag
Hammarskjold
The General Assembly,
Mourning the passing of Mr. Dag Hammarskjold,
Secretary-General of the United Nations,
Desiring to establish an appropriate memorial commemoratmg his service to the United Nations,
Noting with appreciation the hope expressed by the
Ford Foundation, as donor, that the new United Nations library might be considered in some measure an
appropriate remembrance of Mr. Hammarskjold's life,
Decides to dedicate the new library on 16 November
1961 as "The Dag Hammarskjold Library".

16 October 1961.
