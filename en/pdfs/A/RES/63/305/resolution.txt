63/305. Establishment of an ad hoc open-ended working group of
the General Assembly to follow up on the issues contained
in the Outcome of the Conference on the World Financial
and Economic Crisis and Its Impact on Development
The General Assembly,
Recalling its resolution 63/303 of 9 July 2009, by which it endorsed by
consensus the Outcome of the Conference on the World Financial and Economic
Crisis and Its Impact on Development, 1
0F

Recognizing the depth of the current financial and economic crisis and the
urgency of follow-up action,
Reaffirming that this process shall continue to be driven by Member States,
1.
Decides to establish immediately an ad hoc open-ended working group of
the General Assembly to follow up on the issues contained in the Outcome of the
Conference on the World Financial and Economic Crisis and Its Impact on
Development;1
2.
Requests the Ad Hoc Open-ended Working Group to submit a report on
the progress of its work to the General Assembly before the end of the sixty-fourth
session.
