Recalling Security Council resolution 1312 (2000) of 31 July 2000, by which

Mission,
1.

the amount of 17.5 million United States dollars, representing some 1 per cent of the

have paid their assessed contributions in full, and urges all other Member States, in
particular those in arrears, to ensure payment of their outstanding assessed
contributions;
