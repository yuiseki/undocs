63/76. United Nations regional centres for peace and disarmament

2006, and 62/50 of 5 December 2007 regarding the maintenance and revitalization
of the three United Nations regional centres for peace and disarmament,

Regional Centre for Peace and Disarmament in Africa, 1 the United Nations Regional
