63/12. Cooperation between the United Nations and the
Latin American and Caribbean Economic System
The General Assembly,
Recalling its resolution 59/258 of 23 December 2004 on cooperation between
the United Nations and the Latin American Economic System,
Having considered the report of the Secretary-General on cooperation between
the United Nations and regional and other organizations, 1
0F

Bearing in mind the Agreement between the United Nations and the Latin
American Economic System, 2 in which the parties agree to strengthen and expand
their cooperation in matters that are of common concern in the fields of their
respective competence pursuant to their constitutional instruments,
1F

Noting that cooperation between the Latin American and Caribbean Economic
System and the United Nations has been evolving over the last few years and
diversifying with regard to areas of cooperation,
Welcoming the evolution in the treatment of topics relating to the United
Nations system, in close contact with the delegations of the Member States
participating in such deliberations,
Takes note of the holding of the thirty-third regular meeting of the Latin

26 to 28 November 2007;
