Resolution adopted by the General Assembly on 11 December 2008
[on the report of the Sixth Committee (A/63/453)]

63/131. Observer status for the South Centre in the General
Assembly
The General Assembly,
Wishing to promote cooperation between the United Nations and the South
Centre,
1.
Decides to invite the South Centre to participate in the sessions and the
work of the General Assembly in the capacity of observer;
2.
Requests the Secretary-General to take the necessary action to implement
the present resolution.
