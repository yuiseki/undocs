59/53. Observer status for the South Asian Association for
Regional Cooperation in the General Assembly
The General Assembly,
Wishing to promote cooperation between the United Nations and the South
Asian Association for Regional Cooperation,
1.
Decides to invite the South Asian Association for Regional Cooperation
to participate in the sessions and the work of the General Assembly in the capacity
of observer;
Requests the Secretary-General to take the necessary action to implement
2.
the present resolution.
