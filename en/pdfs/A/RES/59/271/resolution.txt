59/271. Report of the Secretary-General on the activities
of the Office of Internal Oversight Services
The General Assembly,
Recalling its resolutions 48/218 B of 29 July 1994 and 54/244 of 23 December
1999,
Having considered the annual report of the Office of Internal Oversight
Services for the period 1 July 2003 to 30 June 2004, 1
1.
Notes with appreciation the work of the Office of Internal Oversight
Services;
2.
Takes note of the annual report of the Office of Internal Oversight
Services;1
3.
Notes the description of the mission of the Office of Internal Oversight
Services, as outlined in its annual report, and in this regard stresses that the mission
of the Office should be in full conformity with its mandate, as approved by the
General Assembly in its resolution 48/218 B;
Notes also the information provided by the Office of Internal Oversight
4.
Services on the economies and savings generated by its recommendations, and
requests the Office to explain its guidelines for measuring the impact of such
economies and savings and to report to the General Assembly thereon in its next
annual report;
Requests the Secretary-General, with regard to paragraph 53 of the
5.
annual report of the Office of Internal Oversight Services, to ensure strict
conformity with the highest standards of quality when recruiting staff to fill
language posts, in accordance with legislative mandates;
Notes with concern the findings of the Office of Internal Oversight
6.
Services on investigations, as reflected in paragraphs 42 to 47 of its annual report,
as well as the fact that some of them reflect serious managerial problems and lack of
control;
