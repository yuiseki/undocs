59/51. Observer status for the Economic Community of West
African States in the General Assembly
The General Assembly,
Wishing to promote cooperation between the United Nations and the Economic
Community of West African States,
1.
Decides to invite the Economic Community of West African States to
participate in the sessions and the work of the General Assembly in the capacity of
observer;
Requests the Secretary-General to take the necessary action to implement
2.
the present resolution.
