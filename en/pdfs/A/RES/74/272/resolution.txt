67/244 B of 12 April 2013, 68/257 of 27 December 2013, 68/267 of 9 April 2014,

United Republic of Tanzania in facilitating the construction pr oject;

requests the Secretary-General to submit a final report to the General Assembly
during its seventy-sixth session;
5.
Recalls paragraphs 10 and 26 of the report of the Advisory Committee,
urges the Secretary-General to intensify his efforts to ensure full implementation of
the project, including the remaining remediation works on the heating, ventilation and
