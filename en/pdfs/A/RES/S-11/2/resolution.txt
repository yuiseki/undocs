RESOLUTIONS ADOPTED WITHOUT REFERENCE TO
A MAIN COMMITTEE

Admission of the Republic of Zimbabwe to membership in the United
Nations

The General Assembly,
Having received the recommendation of the Security Council of 30 July 1980
that the Republic of Zimbabwe should be admitted to membership in the United
Nations,2
Having considered the application for membership of the Republic of Zimbabwe,3
Decides to admit the Republic of Zimbabwe to membership in the United
Nations.
