3528 (XXX). Avoidance of losses on investments
due to currency fluctuations

The General Assembly,
Recognizing that a sound investment policy must take
account of the need to avoid losses due to currency
fluctuations,
Taking into account that the Working Group on
Currency Instability agreed that United Nations investments should be made in sound currencies, as stated
in paragraph 50 of the report of the Working Group,62
Taking into account the contents of paragraph 29
of the report of the Secretary-Ge~eral on the investments of the United Nations Joint Staff Pension Fund,113
Requests the Secretary-General to ensure that, in
making the investments of the United Nations Joint
Staff Pension Fund, all possible care is taken to avoid
