Resolution adopted by the General Assembly on 6 December 2007

62/74. Observer status for the Italian-Latin American Institute
in the General Assembly
The General Assembly,
Wishing to promote cooperation between the United Nations and the ItalianLatin American Institute,
1.
Decides to invite the Italian-Latin American Institute to participate in the
sessions and the work of the General Assembly in the capacity of observer;
2.
Requests the Secretary-General to take the necessary action to implement
the present resolution.
