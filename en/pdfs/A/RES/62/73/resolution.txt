62/73. Observer status for the Regional Centre on Small Arms
and Light Weapons in the Great Lakes Region, the Horn
of Africa and Bordering States in the General Assembly
The General Assembly,
Wishing to promote cooperation between the United Nations and the Regional
Centre on Small Arms and Light Weapons in the Great Lakes Region, the Horn of
Africa and Bordering States,
Decides to invite the Regional Centre on Small Arms and Light Weapons
1.
in the Great Lakes Region, the Horn of Africa and Bordering States to participate in
the sessions and the work of the General Assembly in the capacity of observer;
Requests the Secretary-General to take the necessary action to implement
2.
the present resolution.
