Resolution adopted by the General Assembly on 6 December 2007

62/76. Observer status for the Eurasian Development Bank in the
General Assembly
The General Assembly,
Wishing to promote cooperation between the United Nations and the Eurasian
Development Bank,
1.
Decides to invite the Eurasian Development Bank to participate in the
sessions and the work of the General Assembly in the capacity of observer;
2.
Requests the Secretary-General to take the necessary action to implement
the present resolution.
