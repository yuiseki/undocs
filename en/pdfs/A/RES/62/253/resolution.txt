62/253. Financing of the United Nations Operation in Burundi
The General Assembly,
Having considered the report of the Secretary-General on the financing of the
United Nations Operation in Burundi, 1 the related report of the Advisory Committee
on Administrative and Budgetary Questions 2 and the statements made by the
representative of the Secretary-General and the Chairperson of the Advisory
Committee, 3

Recalling Security Council resolution 1545 (2004) of 21 May 2004, by which
the Council authorized, for an initial period of six months as from 1 June 2004, with

resolutions by which the Council extended the mandate of the Operation, the last of

2000,
