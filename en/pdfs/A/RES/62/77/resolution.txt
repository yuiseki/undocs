62/77. Observer status for the Conference on Interaction and
Confidence-building Measures in Asia in the General
Assembly
The General Assembly,
Wishing to promote cooperation between the United Nations and the
Conference on Interaction and Confidence-building Measures in Asia,
1.
Decides to invite the Conference on Interaction and Confidence-building
Measures in Asia to participate in the sessions and the work of the General
Assembly in the capacity of observer;
Requests the Secretary-General to take the necessary action to implement
2.
the present resolution.
