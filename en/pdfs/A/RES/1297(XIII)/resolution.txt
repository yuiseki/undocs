1295 (XIII). Amendments to the Staff Regulations
of the United Nations
The General Assembly
Resolves that the Staff Regulations of the United Nations shall be modified by the amendments annexed to
the present resolution, with effect from 1 January 1959.
782nd plenary mee.t-ing,
5 December 1958.
ANNEX

Regulation 3.4 (Dependency benefits)
Replace the present text of paragraph ( c) by the following:
"With a view to avoiding duplication of benefits and in
order to achieve equality between staff members who receive
dependency benefits under applicable laws in the form of
governmental grants and staff members who do not receive
such dependency benefits, the Secretary-General shall prescribe conditions under which the dependency allowance for
a child specified in (a) (i) above shall be payable only to the
extent that the dependency benefits enjoyed by the staff member or his spouse under applicable laws amount to less than
such a dependency allowance."
Annex IV, paragraph 2 (Service benefit}
Replace the present text of paragraph ( b) by the following:
"Should such a staff member be granted a probationary
or permanent appointment, or complete five ye:irs of qualifying service and remain on a contract providing for further
service of at least one year, or remain in employment for
more than one year thereafter, he shall lose entitlement to
the service benefit."

1297 (XIII). United Nations International School
The General Assembly,
Having considered the progress report of the Secretary-General on the United Nations International
School19 as well as the report by the Board of Trustees
of the School, 20
Recalling its earlier resolutions on this subject,
namely resolutions 1102 (XI) of 27 February 1957 and
1228 A (XII) of 14 December 1957,
Noting wi,th satisfaction that steps taken by the Secretary-General have resulted in the acquisition, for a
period of up to three years, of premises for the School
in Manhattan, and that the School has been in operation
at these premises since the beginning of the present
academic year,
Noting further the continued need of the School for
suitable permanent premises as near as practicable to
United Nations Headquarters,
Noting also that the Board of Trustees, in furthering
the desired development of the School in Manhattan, is
faced with a consequential deficit of up to $54,000 in
regard to the current academic year,
1. Reaffirms the belief that the provision of adequate
accommodation for the United Nations International
School is in the best interest of the Organization;

2. Hopes that steps will be taken to make the School
available to the children of the greatest possible number
of members of delegations, United Nations staff members and others associated with the United Nations;
3. Expresses appreciation to the Secretary-General
and to the authorities of New York City for their efforts
in finding temporary accommodation for the School ;

$32,700 to the School towards meeting the expected
