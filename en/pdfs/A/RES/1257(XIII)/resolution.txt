1257 (XIIl). United Nations Children's Fund
The General Assembly,
Having considered chapter VI, section 1, of the report
of the Economic and Social Council,1 dealing with the
United N"ations Children's Fund,
Welcoming the emphasis on strengthening co-operation which has developed in the past year with the
Bureau of Social Affairs, the specialized agencies and
the non-governmental organizations,
Recognizing that the impact of the Fund on social
and economic development is steadily increasing,
Recognizing also the increasing opportunities for effective use of aid from the Fund,
1. Expresses the hope that all Governments will contribute to the United Nations Children's Fund as generously as possible;
2. Congra.tulates the Fund on its outstanding achievements.
