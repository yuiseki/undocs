71/154. Observer status for the International Youth Organization
for Ibero-America in the General Assembly
The General Assembly,
Wishing to promote cooperation between the United Nations and the
International Youth Organization for Ibero-America,
1.
Decides to invite the International Youth Organization for Ibero -America
to participate in the sessions and the work of the General Assembly in the capacity
of observer;
2.
Requests the Secretary-General to take the action necessary to implement
the present resolution.
