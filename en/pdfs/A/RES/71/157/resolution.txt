71/157. Observer status for the Central American Bank for
Economic Integration in the General Assembly
The General Assembly,
Wishing to promote cooperation between the United Nations and the Central
American Bank for Economic Integration,
1.
Decides to invite the Central American Bank for Economic Integration to
participate in the sessions and the work of the General Assembly in the capacity of
observer;
2.
Requests the Secretary-General to take the action necessary to implement
the present resolution.
