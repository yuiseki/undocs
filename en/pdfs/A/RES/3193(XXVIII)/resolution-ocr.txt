3192 (XXVIII). Administrative arrangements re-
garding the Fund of the United Nations
Environment Programme

The General Assembly

1. Decides that trust funds established by the Gov-
erning Counci] of the United Nations Environment
Programme shall be administered in accordance with
the Financial Rules of the Fund of the United Nations
Environment Programme;?*

2. Also decides that, notwithstanding regulations
11.1 and 11.4 of the Financial Regulations of the
United Nations, the Executive Director of the United
Nations Environment Programme shall maintain the
accounts of the Fund of the United Nations Environ-

Governing Council of the Programme and to the Gen-
eral Assembly.
