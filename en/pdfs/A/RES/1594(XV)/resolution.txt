1599 (XV). The situation in the Republic of the
Congo
The General Assembly,
Recalling its resolution 1474 (ES-IV) of 20 September 1960 and the Security Council resolutions of
14 July,2 22 July3 and 9 August 1960• and, more particularly, that of 21 February 1961,11 urging the immediate withdrawal and evacuation of all Belgian and
other foreign military and paramilitary personnel and
political advisers not under the United Nations Command, and mercenaries,

2. Decides that all Belgian and other foreign military
and paramilitary personnel and political advisers not
under the United Nations Command, and mercenaries,
shall be completely withdrawn and evacuated;
3. Calls upon all States to exert their influence and
extend their co-operation to effect the implementation
of the present resolution.
