73/213.

Observer status for the New Development Bank
in the General Assembly
The General Assembly,
Wishing to promote cooperation between the United Nations and the New
Development Bank,
1.
Decides to invite the New Development Bank to participate in the sessions
and the work of the General Assembly in the capacity of observer;
2.
Requests the Secretary-General to take the action necessary to implement
the present resolution.
