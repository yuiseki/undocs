70/123. Observer status for the Indian Ocean Rim Association
in the General Assembly
The General Assembly,
Wishing to promote cooperation between the United Nations and the Indian
Ocean Rim Association,
1.
Decides to invite the Indian Ocean Rim Association to participate in the
sessions and the work of the General Assembly in the capacity of observer;
2.
Requests the Secretary-General to take the action necessary to implement
the present resolution.
