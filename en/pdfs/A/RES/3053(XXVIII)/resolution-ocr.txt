for the year 1972 and reports of the Board

A
UNITED NATIONS

The General Assembly

1. Accepts the financial report and accounts of the
United Nations for the year ended 31 December 1972
and the audit opinion of the Board of Auditors;+

2. Concurs in the observations of the Advisory Com-
mittee on Administrative and Budgetary Questions as
set forth in its report;?

remedial action as may be required by the comments
of the Board of Auditors.
