2250 (S-V). Postponement of the United Nations
Conference on the Exploration and Peaceful
Uses of Outer Space

The General Assembly,

Recalling its resolution 2221 (XXI) of 19 De-
cember 1966, by which it was decided that a United
Nations Conference on the Exploration and Peaceful
Uses of Outer Space should be held,

Reaffirming the great importance it attaches to this
Conference,

1. Accepts the unanimous recommendation of the
Committee on the Peaceful Uses of Outer Space ;?

2. Decides that the United Nations Conference on
the Exploration and Peaceful Uses of Outer Space
should be postponed from the planned date of Sep-
