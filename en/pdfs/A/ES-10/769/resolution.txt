Tenth emergency special session
Agenda item 5
Illegal Israeli actions in Occupied East Jerusalem and the
rest of the Occupied Palestinian Territory

Letter dated 18 December 2017 from the Permanent
Representatives of Turkey and Yemen to the United Nations
addressed to the President of the General Assembly
We have the honour, in our capacities as Chair of the Summit of the Organization
of Islamic Cooperation (OIC) and Chair of the Arab Group, respectively, in follow up to the Arab ministerial decision of 9 December 2017 and the OIC Summit decision
of 13 December 2017, and in light of the veto cast today, 18 December 2017, by the
United States of America in the Security Council on a draft resolution regarding the
status of Jerusalem, to request the urgent resumption of the tenth emergency special
session of the General Assembly in accordance with the “Uniting for peace”
procedure pursuant to General Assembly resolution 377 (V) of 1950.
We would be grateful for your consideration of this request and for the
facilitation of the convening of the General Assembly to address this serious matter
as soon as possible.
We should be grateful if you would arrange to have the text of the present letter
distributed as a document of the tenth emergency special session of the General
Assembly, under agenda item 5.
(Signed) Freidun Hadi Sinirlioǧlu
