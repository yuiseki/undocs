Agenda item 136 (continued)
Scale of assessments for the apportionment of the
expenses of the United Nations (A/64/631/Add.6 and
Add.7)
The Acting President: I should like to draw the
attention of the General Assembly to documents
A/64/631/Add.6 and Add.7, in which the SecretaryGeneral informs the President of the General Assembly
that, since the issuance of his communications
contained in documents A/64/631 and addenda 1
through 5, the Dominican Republic and the Marshall
Islands have made the necessary payments to reduce
their arrears below the amount specified in Article 19
of the Charter. May I take it that the General Assembly
duly takes note of the information contained in those
documents?
It was so decided.
Agenda item 63 (continued)
New Partnership for Africa’s Development: progress
in implementation and international support
(a) New Partnership for Africa’s Development:
progress in implementation and international
support
Draft resolution (A/64/L.38/Rev.1)
The Acting President: Members will recall that
the General Assembly held the debate on agenda item

63 and its sub-items (a) and (b), jointly with agenda
item 47, at its 22nd to 24th plenary meetings, on
20 and 21 October 2009.
I now give the floor to the representative of
Yemen to introduce draft resolution A/64/L.38/Rev.1.
Mr. Alsoswa (Yemen): On behalf of the Group of
77 and China, I have the honour to introduce draft
resolution A/64/L.38/Rev.1, entitled “New Partnership
for Africa’s Development: progress in implementation
and international support”.
I am pleased to report that we have reached
consensus on the draft resolution. I would therefore
like to thank all groups for their constructive
engagement in the negotiating process. I would also
like to thank the facilitator, Mr. Nor Eddine Benfreha
of Algeria, as well as the groups’ coordinators, for their
tireless and continuous efforts, which enabled us to
reach consensus.
The Acting President: The Assembly will now
take a decision on draft resolution A/64/L.38/Rev.1. I
should like to announce that, since the publication of
the draft resolution, Croatia, Estonia, Ireland, Israel
and Slovenia have become sponsors.
May I take it that the General Assembly decides
to adopt draft resolution A/64/L.38/Rev.1?
Draft resolution A/64/L.38/Rev.1 was adopted
(resolution 64/258).
The Acting President: May I take it that it is the
wish of the General Assembly to conclude its
consideration of sub-item (a) of agenda item 63, and of
agenda item 63 as a whole?

Agenda item 112 (continued)
Appointments to fill vacancies in subsidiary organs
and other appointments
(a) Appointment of members of the Advisory
Committee on Administrative and Budgetary
Questions
Report of the Fifth Committee (A/64/524/Add.1)

The Acting President: If there is no proposal
under rule 66 of the rules of procedure, I shall take it
that the General Assembly decides not to discuss the
report of the Fifth Committee that is before it today.
It was so decided.
The Acting President: Before we begin to take
action on the recommendation contained in the report
of the Fifth Committee, I should like to advise
representatives that we are going to proceed to take a
decision in the same manner as was done in the Fifth
Committee.
The Fifth Committee recommends in paragraph 3
of its report that the General Assembly appoint
Anupam Ray of India as a member of the Advisory
Committee on Administrative and Budgetary Questions
for a term of office beginning today, 16 March 2010,
and ending on 31 December 2010.
May I take it that it is the wish of the Assembly
to appoint Anupam Ray of India as a member of the
Advisory Committee on Administrative and Budgetary
Questions for a term of office beginning today,
16 March 2010, and ending on 31 December 2010?
It was so decided.
The Acting President: May I take it that it is the
wish of the General Assembly to conclude its
consideration of sub-item (a) of agenda item 112?
It was so decided.
