Social development, including questions relating to
the world social situation and to youth, ageing,
disabled persons and the family: Outcome of the
International Year of Volunteers and its follow-up
Draft resolution (A/57/L.8)
The Acting President: The Assembly shall now
consider draft resolution A/57/L.8. I should like to
announce that, since the introduction of the draft

(Czech Republic)

resolution, the following countries have become
sponsors: Indonesia, Kazakhstan, the Lao People’s
Democratic Republic, Mauritius and Niger.
The Assembly will now take a decision on draft
resolution A/57/L.8, entitled “Follow-up to the
International Year of Volunteers”.
May I take it that the Assembly decides to adopt
draft resolution A/57/L.8?
Draft resolution A/57/L.8 was adopted (resolution
57/106).
The Acting President: We have thus concluded
this stage of our consideration of agenda item 98.
