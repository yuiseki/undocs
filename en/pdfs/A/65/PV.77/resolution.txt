In the absence of the President, Ms. Lucas

The meeting was called to order at 3.10 p.m.
Agenda item 9 (continued)
Report of the Economic and Social Council
Note by the Secretary-General (A/65/768)
The Acting President (spoke in French):
Members will recall that the Assembly considered, in a
joint debate, agenda item 9 and agenda item 13,
“Integrated and coordinated implementation of and
follow-up to the outcomes of the major United Nations
conferences and summits in the economic, social and

related fields”, at its 34th plenary meeting, on
20 October 2010.
The Assembly will now take a decision on the
draft resolution entitled “Revision of the General
Regulations of the World Food Programme”, contained
in the annex to document A/65/768.
May I take it that the Assembly decides to adopt
the draft resolution?
The draft resolution was adopted (resolution
65/266).
The Acting President (spoke in French): The
General Assembly has thus concluded this stage of its
consideration of agenda item 9.
The meeting rose at 3.15 p.m.
