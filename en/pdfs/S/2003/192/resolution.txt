Letter dated 13 February 2003 from the Secretary-General
addressed to the President of the Security Council
I have the honour to refer to Security Council resolution 690 (1991) of
29 April 1991 establishing the United Nations Mission for the Referendum in
Western Sahara (MINURSO).
Following the usual consultations, it is my intention to add Croatia, Mongolia
and Sri Lanka to the list of countries providing military personnel to MINURSO.
I should be grateful if you would bring this matter to the attention of the
members of the Security Council.
(Signed) Kofi A. Annan
