Letter dated 3 December 2003 from the President of the Security
Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 28 November 2003
(S/2003/1142) concerning your decision to appoint Mr. Daudi Ngelautwa
Mwakawago (United Republic of Tanzania) as your Special Representative for
Sierra Leone has been brought to the attention of the members of the Security
Council. They take note of the information and decision contained in your letter.
(Signed) Stefan Tafrov
President of the Security Council
