Letter dated 9 January 2003 from the President of the Security
Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 6 January 2003
(S/2003/27) concerning your proposal to appoint Brigadier-General Franciszek
Gagor of Poland as the next Force Commander of the United Nations Iraq-Kuwait
Observation Mission (UNIKOM) has been brought to the attention of the members
of the Council, who take note of the proposal.
(Signed) Jean-Marc de La Sablière
President of the Security Council
