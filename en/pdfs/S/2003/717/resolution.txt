Letter dated 17 July 2003 from the President of the Security
Council to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 14 July 2003
(S/2003/716) concerning your intention to appoint Lieutenant General Khairuddin
Mat Yusof (Malaysia) to the post of Force Commander of the United Nations
Mission of Support in East Timor (UNMISET) has been brought to the attention of
the members of the Security Council. They take note of the intention contained in
your letter.
(Signed) Inocencio F. Arias
President of the Security Council
