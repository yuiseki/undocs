Letter dated 10 November 2003 from the President of the
Security Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 3 November 2003
(S/2003/1087) concerning the composition of the International Criminal Tribunal for
the Prosecution of Persons Responsible for Serious Violations of International
Humanitarian Law Committed in the Territory of the Former Yugoslavia since 1991
has been brought to the attention of the members of the Security Council. Having
consulted those members, I support your intention to appoint Kevin Horace Parker
as a permanent judge of the Tribunal.
(Signed) Ismael Abraão Gaspar Martins
President of the Security Council
