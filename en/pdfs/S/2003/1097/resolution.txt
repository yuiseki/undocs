Letter dated 14 November 2003 from the President of the Security
Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 11 November 2003
(S/2003/1096) concerning the United Nations Peace-building Support Office in
Guinea-Bissau (UNOGBIS), which will expire on 31 December 2003, has been
brought to the attention of the members of the Security Council. They take note of
your proposal to extend the mandate of UNOGBIS for one year, until 31 December
2004, and the revised mandate of UNOGBIS as indicated in your letter.
(Signed) Ismael Abraão Gaspar Martins
President of the Security Council
