Letter dated 12 February 2003 from the President of the
Security Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 7 February 2003
(S/2003/168) concerning your decision to appoint Mr. Albert Tevoedjre as your
Special Representative for Côte d’Ivoire has been brought to the attention of the
members of the Security Council. They take note of the decision contained in your
letter.
(Signed) Gunter Pleuger
President of the Security Council
