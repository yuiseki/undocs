Letter dated 28 July 2003 from the President of the Security
Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 24 July 2003
(S/2003/761) concerning your intention to appoint Harri Holkeri (Finland) as your
Special Representative and Head of the United Nations Interim Administration
Mission in Kosovo (UNMIK) has been brought to the attention of the members of
the Security Council. They take note of the intention expressed in your letter.
(Signed) Inocencio F. Arias
President of the Security Council
