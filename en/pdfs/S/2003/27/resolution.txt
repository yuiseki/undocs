Letter dated 6 January 2003 from the Secretary-General
addressed to the President of the Security Council
I have the honour to refer to the United Nations Iraq-Kuwait Observation
Mission (UNIKOM).
Major-General Miguel Angel Moreno of Argentina relinquished his post as
Force Commander of UNIKOM on 18 November 2002. After consultation with the
parties concerned, I propose to appoint Brigadier-General Franciszek Gagor of
Poland to succeed him as Force Commander of UNIKOM.
I should be grateful if you would bring this matter to the attention of the
members of the Security Council.
(Signed) Kofi A. Annan
Secretary-General
