Letter dated 7 July 2003 from the Secretary-General addressed to
the President of the Security Council
I have the honour to refer to your letter of 2 July 2003 (S/2003/690) concurring
with my proposal to appoint Ms. Khalida Rachid as a permanent judge of the
International Tribunal for Rwanda. I have received a corresponding letter from the
President of the General Assembly. Accordingly, I have appointed Ms. Rachid as a
permanent judge of the International Tribunal for Rwanda, effective today, for the
remainder of the term of office of Mr. Mansoor Ahmed, which term will expire on
24 May 2007.
(Signed) Kofi A. Annan
