Letter dated 7 March 2003 from the President of the Security
Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 4 March 2003
(S/2003/284) concerning the reporting on the progress of the arrangements for
stand-by units and resources has been brought to the attention of the members of the
Security Council, who have agreed to your proposal to consolidate the reporting in
the annual report to the Special Committee on Peacekeeping Operations.
(Signed) Mamady Traoré
President of the Security Council
