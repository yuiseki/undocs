Letter dated 22 August 2003 from the Secretary-General to the
President of the Security Council
Owing to the untimely and sad death of Mr. Sergio Vieira de Mello, I wish to
inform you that I have, on an interim basis, appointed Mr. Ramiro Lopes da Silva to
serve as my acting Special Representative for Iraq.
I should be grateful if you could bring the present letter to the attention of the
members of the Security Council
(Signed) Kofi A. Annan
