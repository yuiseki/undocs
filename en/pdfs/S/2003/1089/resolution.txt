Letter dated 12 November 2003 from the Secretary-General
addressed to the President of the Security Council
I have the honour to refer to your letter of 10 November 2003 (S/2003/1088),
concurring with my proposal to appoint Kevin Horace Parker as a permanent judge
of the International Criminal Tribunal for the Prosecution of Persons Responsible
for Serious Violations of International Humanitarian Law Committed in the
Territory of the Former Yugoslavia since 1991. I have received a corresponding
letter from the President of the General Assembly. Accordingly, I have appointed
Mr. Parker as a permanent judge of the Tribunal, effective 4 December 2003, for the
remainder of the term of office of Judge David Hunt, whose term will expire on 16
November 2005.
(Signed) Kofi A. Annan
