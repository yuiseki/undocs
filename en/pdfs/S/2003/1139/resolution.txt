Letter dated 21 November 2003 from the President of the Security
Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 14 November 2003
(S/2003/1138) concerning your decision to extend the appointment of Mohamed
Sahnoun as your Special Adviser on Africa until 31 December 2004 has been
brought to the attention of the members of the Security Council. They take note of
the information and decision contained in your letter.
(Signed) Ismael Gaspar Martins
President of the Security Council
