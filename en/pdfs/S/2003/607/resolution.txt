Letter dated 3 June 2003 from the President of the Security
Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 29 May 2003
(S/2003/606) concerning your intention to appoint Brigadier-General Abdul Hafiz
(Bangladesh) to the post of Chief Military Liaison Officer of the United Nations
Mission in Côte d’Ivoire (MINUCI) has been brought to the attention of the
members of the Security Council. They take note of the information and the
intention contained in your letter.
(Signed) Sergey V. Lavrov
President of the Security Council
