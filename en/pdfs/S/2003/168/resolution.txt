Letter dated 7 February 2003 from the Secretary-General
addressed to the President of the Security Council
I have the honour to refer to my briefing to the Security Council on 28 January
2003 in which I indicated my intention to appoint a Special Representative for Côte
d’Ivoire, as well as to the statement by the President of the Security Council on 28
January 2003 (SC/7646) and Council resolution 1464 (2003) of 4 February 2003
relating to my stated intention.
It is my honour to inform you of my decision to appoint Mr. Albert Tevoedjre,
former Member of Parliament and former Minister of Planning, Economic
Restructuring and Promotion of Employment of the Republic of Benin and
previously Deputy Director General of the International Labour Organization, as my
Special Representative for Côte d’Ivoire, effective 6 February 2003.
I should be grateful if you would bring this matter to the attention of the
members of the Security Council.
(Signed) Kofi A. Annan
