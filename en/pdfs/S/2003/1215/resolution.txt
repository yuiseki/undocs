Letter dated 30 December from the President of the Security
Council to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 24 December 2003
(S/2003/1214) concerning your decision to appoint Major General Herbert Joaquin
Figoli Almandos (Uruguay) as the next Force Commander of the United Nations
Peacekeeping Force in Cyprus (UNFICYP) has been brought to the attention of the
members of the Security Council. They take note of the decision contained in that
letter.
(Signed) Stefan Tafrov
President of the Security Council
