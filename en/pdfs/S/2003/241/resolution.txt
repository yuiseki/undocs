Letter dated 3 March 2003 from the President of the Security
Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 26 February 2003
(S/2003/240) concerning your intention to accept the proposal of Argentina and
Chile to include a platoon-sized unit of troops plus two staff officers from Chile in
the Argentine contingent of the United Nations Peacekeeping Force in Cyprus
(UNFICYP) has been brought to the attention of the members of the Security
Council. They take note of the intention expressed in your letter.
(Signed) Mamady Traoré
President of the Security Council
