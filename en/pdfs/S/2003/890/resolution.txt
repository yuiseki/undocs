Letter dated 11 September 2003 from the President of the Security
Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that, as requested, your letter dated 5
September 2003 (S/2003/889), concerning your proposal to extend the mandate of
the United Nations Peace-building Support Office in the Central African Republic
(BONUCA), which expires on 31 December 2003, for an additional year, until 31
December 2004, has been brought to the attention of the members of the Security
Council. They take note of the proposal contained in your letter.
(Signed) Emyr Jones Parry
President of the Security Council
