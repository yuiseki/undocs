Letter dated 18 July 2003 from the President of the Security
Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 15 July 2003
(S/2003/726) concerning your intention to appoint Major General Franciszek Gagor
of Poland to the post of Force Commander of the United Nations Disengagement
Observer Force has been brought to the attention of the members of the Security
Council. They take note of the intention contained in your letter.
(Signed) Inocencio F. Arias
President of the Security Council
