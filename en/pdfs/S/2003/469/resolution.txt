Letter dated 21 April 2003 from the President of the Security
Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 11 April 2003
concerning the United Nations Peace-building Support Office in Liberia (UNOL)
has been brought to the attention of the members of the Security Council. They take
note of the information contained in your letter and approve the revised mandate of
UNOL.
(Signed) Adolfo Aguilar Zinser
President of the Security Council
