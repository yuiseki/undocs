Letter dated 22 August 2003 from the President of the Security
Council to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 22 August 2003
(S/2003/830) concerning your decision to appoint, on an interim basis, Mr. Ramiro
Lopes da Silva to serve as your acting Special Representative for Iraq has been
brought to the attention of the members of the Security Council. They take note of
the appointment indicated in your letter.
(Signed) Mikhail Wehbe
President of the Security Council
