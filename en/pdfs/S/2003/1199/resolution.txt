Letter dated 23 December 2003 from the President of the
Security Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 19 December 2003
(S/2003/1198) concerning your intention to establish a small United Nations
Observer Mission in Bougainville (UNOMB) for a six-month period to replace the
United Nations Political Office in Bougainville (UNPOB) the mandate of which
expires on 31 December 2003, has been brought to the attention of the members of
the Security Council. They take note of your intention, as well as the functions and
the staffing structure of UNOMB, as indicated in your letter.
(Signed) Stefan Tafrov
President of the Security Council
