Letter dated 1 October 2003 from the President of the Security
Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 29 September 2003
(S/2003/926) concerning your intention to appoint Lieutenant-General Daniel
Ishmael Opande (Kenya) as Force Commander of the United Nations Mission in
Liberia (UNMIL) as of 1 October 2003 has been brought to the attention of the
members of the Security Council. They take note of the intention expressed in your
letter.
(Signed) John D. Negroponte
President of the Security Council
