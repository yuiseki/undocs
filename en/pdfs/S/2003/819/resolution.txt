Letter dated 15 August 2003 from the Permanent Representatives
of the United Kingdom of Great Britain and Northern Ireland and
the United States of America to the United Nations addressed to
the President of the Security Council
In view of the letter dated 15 August 2003 addressed to you by the Libyan
Arab Jamahiriya related to the bombing of Pan Am 103 (S/2003/818) and in the
light of the actions and commitments that form the background to the letter, the
Governments of the United Kingdom and the United States of America are prepared
to allow the lifting of the measures set forth by the Council in its resolutions 748
(1992) and 883 (1993) once the necessary sums referred to in the Libyan letter have
been transferred to the agreed escrow account.
In its letter, the Libyan Arab Jamahiriya has pledged before the Council to
cooperate in the international fight against terrorism and to cooperate with any
further requests for information in connection with the Pan Am 103 investigation.
We expect Libya to adhere scrupulously to those commitments.
We should be grateful if you would have the present letter circulated as a
document of the Security Council.
(Signed) Emyr Jones Parry
Permanent Representative
United Kingdom Mission to the
