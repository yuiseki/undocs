NOTE BY THE PRESIDENT OF THE SECURlTY COUNCIL
Following consultations with the members of the Security Council, the
President of the Council made the following statement, on behalf of the Council, at
its 2890th meeting, on 7 November 1989, in connection with the Council's
consideration of the item entitled "Central Pmerica: efforts towards peace":
"The members of the Security Council reaffirm their full support for the
Secretary-General's efforts to assist the Central American Governments in
their efforts to achieve the goals set forth in the Guatemala Agreement of
7 August 1987 and in the Joint Declarations subsequently signed in pursuance
of it. In any consideration of the renewal of the mandate of the United
Nations Observer Group in Central America (ONUCA), they will wish to assure
themselves that the presence of the observer group is continuing to contribute
actively to the achievement of a firm and lasting peace in Central America."
