LETTER DATED 6 AUGUST 1997 FROM THE PRESIDENT OF THE
SECURITY COUNCIL ADDRESSED TO THE SECRETARY-GENERAL

I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 1 August 1997
(S/1997/621) concerning the composition of the military and police components of
the United Nations Transition Mission in Haiti (UNTMIH) has been brought to the
attention of the members of the Council. They take note of the information
contained in your letter and agree with the proposals mentioned therein.

(Signed) John WESTON
President of the Security Council
