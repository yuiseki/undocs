LETTER DATED 12 JUNE 1997 FROM THE PRESIDENT OF THE
SECURITY COUNCIL ADDRESSED TO THE SECRETARY-GENERAL

I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 5 June 1997
(S/1997/449) concerning your decision to appoint Mr. Liviu Bota (Romania) as
your resident Special Representative for Georgia to succeed your current Special
Envoy for Georgia Ambassador Edouard Brunner of Switzerland with effect from
1 July 1997 has been brought to the attention of the members of the Council.
They agree with the proposal contained in your letter.

(Signed) Sergey LAVROV
President of the Security Council
