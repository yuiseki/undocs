LETTER DATED 27 JANUARY 1997 FROM THE PRESIDENT OF THE
SECURITY COUNCIL ADDRESSED TO THE SECRETARY-GENERAL

I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 20 January 1997
(S/1997/76) concerning your intention to appoint Brigadier-General
Boleslaw Izydorczyk (Poland) to succeed Brigadier-General Hasan Abaza (Jordan)
as the next Chief Military Observer of the United Nations Military Observers in
Tajikistan (UNMOT) has been brought to the attention of the members of the
Council. They agree with the intention contained in the letter.

(Signed) Hisashi OWADA
President of the Security Council
