LETTER DATED 25 JULY 1997 FROM THE PRESIDENT OF THE
SECURITY COUNCIL ADDRESSED TO THE SECRETARY-GENERAL

I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 21 July 1997
(S/1997/582) regarding your intention to appoint Brigadier-General
Bernd S. Lubenik (Austria) to succeed Major-General Jorge Barroso de Moura
(Portugal) as Force Commander of the United Nations Mission for the Referendum
in Western Sahara (MINURSO) has been brought to the attention of the members of
the Security Council. They agree with the proposal contained in your letter.

(Signed) Peter OSVALD
President of the Security Council
