LETTER DATED 24 JULY 1997 FROM THE PRESIDENT OF THE SECURITY
COUNCIL ADDRESSED TO THE SECRETARY-GENERAL

I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 21 July 1997
(S/1997/578) concerning the appointment of Mr. William Walker (United States of
America) as the Transitional Administrator for the United Nations Transitional
Administration for Eastern Slavonia, Baranja and Western Sirmium (UNTAES) has
been brought to the attention of the members of the Security Council. They
agree with the intention contained in your letter.

(Signed) Peter OSVALD
President of the Security Council
