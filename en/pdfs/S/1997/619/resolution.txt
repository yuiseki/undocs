LETTER DATED 1 AUGUST 1997 FROM THE SECRETARY-GENERAL
ADDRESSED TO THE PRESIDENT OF THE SECURITY COUNCIL

I have the honour to refer to paragraph 2 of Security Council resolution
1123 (1997) of 30 July 1997, by which the Council decided to establish the
United Nations Transition Mission in Haiti (UNTMIH).
Following the customary consultations, it is my intention to appoint
Brigadier-General J. J. Gagnon (Canada) as Commander of the military element of
UNTMIH.
I should be grateful if you would bring this matter to the attention of the
members of the Security Council.

(Signed)
