LETTER DATED 27 JANUARY 1997 FROM THE SECRETARY-GENERAL
ADDRESSED TO THE PRESIDENT OF THE SECURITY COUNCIL

I have the honour to refer to paragraph 1 of resolution 1094 (1997) of
20 January 1997, by which the Security Council decided to authorize the
attachment to the United Nations Mission for the Verification of Human Rights
and of Compliance with the Comprehensive Agreement on Human Rights in Guatemala
(MINUGUA) of a group of military observers and requisite medical personnel for
the purposes of verification of the Agreement on the definitive ceasefire.
Following the usual consultations, I wish to inform the Security Council of
my intention to appoint Brigadier-General José B. Rodriguez Rodriguez of Spain
as Chief Military Observer of the Military Observer Group.
I should be grateful if you would bring this matter to the attention of the
Security Council.

(Signed)
