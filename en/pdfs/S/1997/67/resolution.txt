LETTER DATED 23 JANUARY 1997 FROM THE PRESIDENT OF THE
SECURITY COUNCIL TO THE SECRETARY-GENERAL

I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 20 January 1997
(S/1997/66) regarding your intention to appoint Major-General W. Hanset of
Belgium to succeed Major-General J. Schoups as the Force Commander of the United
Nations Transitional Administration for Eastern Slavonia, Baranja and Western
Sirmium (UNTAES) has been brought to the attention of the members of the
Security Council. They agree with the intention contained in the letter.

(Signed) Hisashi OWADA
President of the Security Council
