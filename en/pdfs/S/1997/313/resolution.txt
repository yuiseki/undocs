LETTER DATED 15 APRIL 1997 FROM THE PRESIDENT OF THE
SECURITY COUNCIL ADDRESSED TO THE SECRETARY-GENERAL

I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 10 April 1997
(S/1997/312) concerning your intention to appoint Mr. Tuliameni Kalomoh
(Namibia) to succeed Mr. Anthony B. Nyakyi (United Republic of Tanzania) as your
Special Representative for Liberia has been brought to the attention of the
members of the Council. They agree with the intention contained in the letter.

(Signed) António MONTEIRO
President of the Security Council
