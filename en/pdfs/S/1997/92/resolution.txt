LETTER DATED 30 JANUARY 1997 FROM THE PRESIDENT OF THE
SECURITY COUNCIL ADDRESSED TO THE SECRETARY-GENERAL

I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 27 January 1997
(S/1997/91) concerning your intention to appoint Brigadier-General
José B. Rodriguez Rodriguez of Spain as Chief Military Observer of the Military
Observer Group to be attached to the United Nations Mission for the Verification
of Human Rights and of Compliance with the Comprehensive Agreement on Human
Rights in Guatemala (MINUGUA) has been brought to the attention of the members
of the Council. They agree with the intention contained in the letter.

(Signed) Hisashi OWADA
President of the Security Council
