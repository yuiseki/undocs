LETTER DATED 21 JULY 1997 FROM THE SECRETARY-GENERAL ADDRESSED
TO THE PRESIDENT OF THE SECURITY COUNCIL

I have the honour to refer to Security Council resolutions 1037 (1996) of
15 January 1996, 1079 (1996) of 15 November 1996 and 1120 (1997) of 14 July 1997
concerning United Nations Transitional Administration for Eastern Slavonia,
Baranja and Western Sirmium (UNTAES).
With reference to operative paragraph 2 of resolution 1037 (1996), I should
like to inform you that it is my intention to appoint Mr. William Walker (United
States of America) as the Transitional Administrator for UNTAES with effect from
1 August 1997. Mr. Walker will replace Mr. Jacques Paul Klein, who has recently
been appointed Principal Deputy High Representative for Bosnia and Herzegovina.
