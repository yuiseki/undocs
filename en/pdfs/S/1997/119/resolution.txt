LETTER DATED 11 FEBRUARY 1997 FROM THE PRESIDENT OF THE
SECURITY COUNCIL ADDRESSED TO THE SECRETARY-GENERAL

I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 6 February 1997
(S/1997/118) concerning your intention to appoint Mr. Manfred Seitner (Denmark)
as Commissioner of the International Police Task Force has been brought to the
attention of the members of the Council. They agree with the intention
contained in the letter.

(Signed) Njuguna Moses MAHUGU, O.G.W.
President of the Security Council
