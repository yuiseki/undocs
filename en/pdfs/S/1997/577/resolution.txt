LETTER DATED 24 JULY 1997 FROM THE PRESIDENT OF THE SECURITY
COUNCIL ADDRESSED TO THE SECRETARY-GENERAL

I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 21 July 1997
(S/1997/576) concerning an addition to the list of Member States contributing
military personnel to the United Nations Peacekeeping Force in Cyprus has been
brought to the attention of the members of the Security Council. They note the
information contained in the letter and agree with the proposal contained
therein.

(Signed) Peter OSVALD
President of the Security Council
