LETTER DATED 14 APRIL 1997 FROM THE PRESIDENT OF THE
SECURITY COUNCIL ADDRESSED TO THE SECRETARY-GENERAL

(S/1997/307) has been brought to the attention of the members of the Security

Representative in Cambodia for six months.
