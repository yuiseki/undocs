LETTER DATED 17 JUNE 1997 FROM THE PRESIDENT OF THE
SECURITY COUNCIL ADDRESSED TO THE SECRETARY-GENERAL

I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 11 June 1997
(S/1997/466) regarding your intention to appoint Brigadier-General
Bent Sohnemann of Denmark to succeed Brigadier-General Bo Wranker as Force
Commander of the United Nations Preventive Deployment Force (UNPREDEP) has been
brought to the attention of the members of the Security Council. They agree
with the intention contained in the letter.

(Signed) Sergey LAVROV
President of the Security Council
