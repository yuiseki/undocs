LETTER DATED 30 DECEMBER 1997 FROM THE PRESIDENT OF THE
SECURITY COUNCIL ADDRESSED TO THE SECRETARY-GENERAL

I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 26 December 1997
(S/1997/1023) concerning your intention to appoint Mr. Charles F. Dunbar (United
States of America) as your Special Representative for Western Sahara has been
brought to the attention of the members of the Council. They agree with the
intention contained in your letter.

(Signed) Fernando BERROCAL SOTO
President of the Security Council
