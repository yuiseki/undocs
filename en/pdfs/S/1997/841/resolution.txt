LETTER DATED 24 OCTOBER 1997 FROM THE SECRETARY-GENERAL
ADDRESSED TO THE PRESIDENT OF THE SECURITY COUNCIL

I have the honour to refer to the United Nations Iraq-Kuwait Observation
Mission (UNIKOM).
Major-General Gian Giuseppe Santillo (Italy) who has been the Force
Commander of UNIKOM since 1 December 1995, will be relinquishing his post on
30 November 1997. Following consultations with the parties concerned, it is my
intention to appoint Major-General Esa Kalervo Tarvainen of Finland as the next
Force Commander of UNIKOM. A background note on Major-General Tarvainen is
attached.
I should be grateful if you could bring this matter to the attention of the
members of the Security Council.

(Signed)
