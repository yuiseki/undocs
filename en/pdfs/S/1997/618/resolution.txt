LETTER DATED 6 AUGUST 1997 FROM THE PRESIDENT OF THE
SECURITY COUNCIL ADDRESSED TO THE SECRETARY-GENERAL

I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 1 August 1997
(S/1997/617) concerning your decision to appoint Mr. Atsu-Koffi Amega (Togo) and
Mr. Andrew R. Chigovera (Zimbabwe) to be respectively the Chairman and a member
of the investigative team to investigate alleged grave violations of human
rights and international humanitarian law in the Democratic Republic of the
Congo committed since 1 March 1993 has been brought to the attention of the
members of the Council. They take note of the decision contained in your
letter.

(Signed) John WESTON
President of the Security Council
