LETTER DATED 21 JULY 1997 FROM THE SECRETARY-GENERAL
ADDRESSED TO THE PRESIDENT OF THE SECURITY COUNCIL

I have the honour to refer to the composition of the United Nations
Peacekeeping Force in Cyprus (UNFICYP).
The Governments of Austria and Slovenia have proposed that a small number
of Slovene personnel be included in the primarily Austrian unit deployed in the
easternmost sector of the United Nations buffer zone in Cyprus. This is in the
context of cooperation between Austria and neighbouring countries in
peacekeeping.
Having completed the necessary consultations, I propose that Slovenia be
added to the list of Member States contributing military personnel to UNFICYP.
I should be grateful if you could bring this matter to the attention of the
members of the Security Council.

(Signed)
