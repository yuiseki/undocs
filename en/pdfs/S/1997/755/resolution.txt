LETTER DATED 30 SEPTEMBER 1997 FROM THE PRESIDENT OF THE
SECURITY COUNCIL ADDRESSED TO THE SECRETARY-GENERAL

I have the honour to refer to Security Council resolution 1123 (1997) of
30 July 1997, by which the Security Council established the United Nations
Transition Mission in Haiti (UNTMIH), and inform you that the members of the
Security Council agree with your intention to submit in late October 1997 the
report called for by paragraph 7 of resolution 1123 (1997), which was originally
due by 30 September 1997.

(Signed) Bill RICHARDSON
President of the Security Council
