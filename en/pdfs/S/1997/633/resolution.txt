LETTER DATED 8 AUGUST 1997 FROM THE SECRETARY-GENERAL
ADDRESSED TO THE PRESIDENT OF THE SECURITY COUNCIL

I refer to my letter of 1 August 1997 (S/1997/617) in which I informed you
of my decision to appoint two members of the three-man investigative team to the
Democratic Republic of the Congo. I am pleased to inform you, and through you
the members of the Security Council, that I have decided to appoint
Mr. Reed Brody (United States of America) as the third member of the team.

(Signed)
