LETTER DATED 12 AUGUST 1997 FROM THE PRESIDENT OF THE
SECURITY COUNCIL ADDRESSED TO THE SECRETARY-GENERAL

I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 8 August 1997
(S/1997/633) concerning your decision to appoint Mr. Reed Brody (United States
of America) as the third member of the investigative team to investigate alleged
grave violations of human rights and international humanitarian law in the
Democratic Republic of the Congo committed since 1 March 1993 has been brought
to the attention of the members of the Council. They take note of the decision
contained in your letter.

(Signed) John WESTON
President of the Security Council
