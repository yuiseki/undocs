LETTER DATED 3 NOVEMBER 1997 FROM THE PRESIDENT OF THE
SECURITY COUNCIL ADDRESSED TO THE SECRETARY-GENERAL

I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 24 October 1997
(S/1997/841), concerning your intention to appoint Major-General
Esa Kalervo Tarvanien of Finland to succeed Major-General Gian Guiseppe Santillo
(Italy) as the next Force Commander of the United Nations Iraq-Kuwait
Observation Mission (UNIKOM) has been brought to the attention of the members of
the Council. They agree with the proposal contained in your letter.

(Signed) QIN Huasun
President of the Security Council
