LETTER DATED 3 SEPTEMBER 1997 FROM THE PRESIDENT OF THE
SECURITY COUNCIL ADDRESSED TO THE SECRETARY-GENERAL

I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 28 August 1997
(S/1997/680) concerning your decision to appoint Mr. Francis G. Okelo (Uganda)
as your Special Envoy for Sierra Leone has been brought to the attention of the
members of the Council. They welcome the decision contained in your letter.

(Signed) Bill RICHARDSON
President of the Security Council
