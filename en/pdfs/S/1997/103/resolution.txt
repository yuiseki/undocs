LETTER DATED 4 FEBRUARY 1997 FROM THE PRESIDENT OF THE
SECURITY COUNCIL ADDRESSED TO THE SECRETARY-GENERAL

I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 31 January 1997
(S/1997/102) concerning your intention to appoint Mr. Kai Eide (Norway) as your
Special Representative and Coordinator of United Nations Operations in Bosnia
and Herzegovina has been brought to the attention of the members of the Council.
They agree with the intention contained in the letter.

(Signed) Njuguna Moses MAHUGU, O.G.W.
President of the Security Council
