LETTER DATED 1 DECEMBER 1997 FROM THE PRESIDENT OF THE
SECURITY COUNCIL ADDRESSED TO THE SECRETARY-GENERAL

I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 26 November 1997
(S/1997/938) concerning your intention to appoint Ms. Elizabeth Rehn (Finland)
as your Special Representative and Coordinator of United Nations Operations in
Bosnia and Herzegovina has been brought to the attention of the members of the
Council. They agree with the intention contained in your letter. They also
take this opportunity to join you in expressing deep appreciation for the
exemplary way in which Mr. Eide has carried out his important assignment.

(Signed) Fernando BERROCAL SOTO
President of the Security Council
