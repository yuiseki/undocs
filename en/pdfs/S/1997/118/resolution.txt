LETTER DATED 6 FEBRUARY 1997 FROM THE SECRETARY-GENERAL
ADDRESSED TO THE PRESIDENT OF THE SECURITY COUNCIL

I have the honour to refer to Security Council resolution 1088 (1996) of
12 December 1996, by which the Council decided to extend the mandate of the
United Nations Mission in Bosnia and Herzegovina, including the International
Police Task Force (IPTF), for a period terminating on 21 December 1997.
Following the usual consultations, I wish to inform you of my intention to
appoint Mr. Manfred Seitner (Denmark) as Commissioner of IPTF.
I should be grateful if you would bring the above to the attention of the
members of the Security Council.

(Signed)
