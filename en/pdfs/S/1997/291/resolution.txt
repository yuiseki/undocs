LETTER DATED 4 APRIL 1997 FROM THE SECRETARY-GENERAL ADDRESSED
TO THE PRESIDENT OF THE SECURITY COUNCIL

I have the honour to refer to the command of the United Nations Observer
Mission in Georgia (UNOMIG).
The Chief Military Observer of UNOMIG, Major-General Per Källström, will
complete his assignment on 30 April 1997 and will return to the service of his
Government.
Following the usual consultations, it is my intention to appoint
Major-General Harun-Ar-Rashid of Bangladesh to succeed Major-General Källström.
Major-General Harun-Ar-Rashid is expected to take up his functions on
1 May 1997.
I should be grateful if you would bring this matter to the attention of the
members of the Security Council.

(Signed)
