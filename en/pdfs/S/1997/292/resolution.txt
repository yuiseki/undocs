LETTER DATED 8 APRIL 1997 FROM THE PRESIDENT OF THE
SECURITY COUNCIL ADDRESSED TO THE SECRETARY-GENERAL

I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 4 April 1997
(S/1997/291) concerning the command of the United Nations Observer Mission in
Georgia (UNOMIG) and your intention to appoint Major-General Harun-Ar-Rashid of
Bangladesh to succeed Major-General Källström as Chief Military Observer of
UNOMIG has been brought to the attention of the members of the Security Council.
They agree with the intention contained in the letter.

(Signed) António MONTEIRO
President of the Security Council
