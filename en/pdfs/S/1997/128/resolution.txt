LETTER DATED 14 FEBRUARY 1997 FROM THE PRESIDENT OF THE
SECURITY COUNCIL ADDRESSED TO THE SECRETARY-GENERAL

I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 11 February 1997
(S/1997/127) concerning the proposed composition of the group of 155 military
observers and requisite medical personnel attached to the United Nations Mission
for the Verification of Human Rights and of Compliance with the Commitments of
the Comprehensive Agreement on Human Rights in Guatemala (MINUGUA) has been
brought to the attention of the members of the Council. They agree with the
proposal contained in your letter.

(Signed) Njuguna M. MAHUGU, O.G.W.
President of the Security Council
