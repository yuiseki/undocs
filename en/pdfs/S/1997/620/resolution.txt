LETTER DATED 6 AUGUST 1997 FROM THE PRESIDENT OF THE
SECURITY COUNCIL ADDRESSED TO THE SECRETARY-GENERAL

I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 1 August 1997
(S/1997/619) concerning your intention to appoint Brigadier-General J. J. Gagnon
(Canada) as Commander of the military element of the United Nations Transition
Mission in Haiti (UNTMIH) has been brought to the attention of the members of
the Council. They welcome the proposal contained in your letter.

(Signed) John WESTON
President of the Security Council
