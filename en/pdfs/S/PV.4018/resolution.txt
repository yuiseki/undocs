The President: There were 15 votes in favour. The
draft resolution has been adopted unanimously as
resolution 1250 (1999).

The President: The Security Council will now begin
its consideration of the item on its agenda. The Security
Council is meeting in accordance with the understanding
reached in its prior consultations.

I shall now put to the vote the draft resolution
contained in document S/1999/725.

Members of the Council have before them the report
of the Secretary-General on the United Nations operation in
Cyprus, documents S/1999/657 and S/1999/657/Add.1, and
the report of the Secretary-General on his mission of good
offices in Cyprus, document S/1999/707.

In favour:
Argentina, Bahrain, Brazil, Canada, China, France,
Gabon, Gambia, Malaysia, Namibia, Netherlands,
Russian Federation, Slovenia, United Kingdom of
Great Britain and Northern Ireland, United States of
America

Members of the Council also have before them
documents S/1999/724 and S/1999/725, which contain the
texts of two draft resolutions prepared in the course of the
Council’s prior consultations.
It is my understanding that the Security Council is
ready to proceed to the vote on the two draft resolutions
before it. If I hear no objection, I shall put the draft
resolutions to the vote now.
There being no objection, it is so decided.
I shall first put to the vote the draft resolution
contained in document S/1999/724.

2

A vote was taken by show of hands.

The President: There were 15 votes in favour. The
draft resolution has been adopted unanimously as
resolution 1251 (1999).
The Security Council has thus concluded the present
stage of its consideration of the item on its agenda.
The meeting rose at 3.45 p.m.
