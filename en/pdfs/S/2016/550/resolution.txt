Letter dated 20 June 2016 from the Secretary-General addressed
to the President of the Security Council
Pursuant to Security Council resolution 2227 (2015), I have the honour to
transmit herewith a note verbale dated 20 June 2016, through which the Permanent
Mission of France to the United Nations is transmitting to the Council the twelfth
report on actions undertaken by French forces in support of the United Nations
Multidimensional Integrated Stabilization Mission in Mali. The report covers the
period from 1 March to 1 June 2016 (see annex).
I should be grateful if you could have the present letter and its annex brought
to the attention of the members of the Security Council.
(Signed) BAN Ki-moon

Enclosure
Twelfth report on the operational support provided by French
forces to the United Nations Multidimensional Integrated
Stabilization Mission in Mali between 1 March and 1 June 2016
Reference: resolution 2227 (2015), adopted by the Security Council on 29 June 2015
This report, the twelfth since the establishment of the United Nations
Multidimensional Integrated Stabilization Mission in Mali (MINUSMA),
summarizes the actions undertaken between 1 March and 1 June 2016 by French
forces in support of MINUSMA.
Since 1 March 2016, no action has been undertaken in response to an
imminent and serious threat.
