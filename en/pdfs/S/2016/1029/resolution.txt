Note by the President of the Security Council
Following consultations among the members of the Security Council, it has
been agreed to amend the reporting period established in paragraph 6 of resolution
2046 (2012), and subsequently amended in notes by the President of the Security
Council (S/2013/657 and S/2014/613), to six-month intervals, beginning with the
first report under the new timeline due to the Council by 15 May 2017‎.
