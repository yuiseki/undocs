Letter dated 31 October 2016 from the President of the
Security Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 26 October 2016
(S/2016/902) regarding the United Nations Mission in Colombia has been brought
to the attention of the members of the Security Council.
After due consideration, the Security Council takes note of your
recommendation in the above-mentioned letter. The Council notes that the United
Nations Mission in Colombia is authorized, pursuant to Council resolutions 2261
(2016) and 2307 (2016), to verify implementation of the ceasefire protocol signed
on 13 October 2016.
(Signed) Vitaly Churkin
President of the Security Council
