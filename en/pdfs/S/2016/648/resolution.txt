Letter dated 25 July 2016 from the President of the
Security Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 21 July 2016
(S/2016/647) concerning your intention to appoint Major General Mohammad
Humayun Kabir (Bangladesh) as the new Force Commander of the United Nations
Peacekeeping Force in Cyprus (UNFICYP) has been brought to the attention of the
members of the Security Council. They take note of the intention expressed in your
letter.
(Signed) Koro Bessho
