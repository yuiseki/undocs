Letter dated 20 April 2016 from the Secretary-General addressed
to the President of the Security Council
I have the honour to refer to Security Council resolution 1966 (2010), adopted
on 22 December 2010, establishing the International Residual Mechanism for
Criminal Tribunals and, in particular, to article 10, paragraph 2, of the statute of the
Mechanism, annexed thereto, on the appointment of a judge to fill a vacancy in the
roster of judges of the Mechanism.
Article 8, paragraph 1, of the Statute provides that the Mechanism shall have a
roster of 25 independent judges.
In accordance with article 10 of the Statute, the General Assembly elected
25 judges of the Mechanism for a term of four years, effective 1 July 2012. One of
the judges thus elected, Judge Patrick Robinson of Jamaica, resigned with effect
from 17 November 2015, following his election as a Judge of the International
Court of Justice.
Pursuant to article 10, paragraph 2, of the Statute, in the event of a vacancy in
the roster, after consultation with the Presidents of the Security Council and of the
General Assembly, the Secretary-General shall appoint a person meeting the
qualifications of article 9, paragraph 1, of the Statute, for the remainder of the term
of office concerned.
According to article 9, paragraph 1, of the Statute, the judges shall be persons
of high moral character, impartiality and integrity who possess the qualifications
required in their respective countries for appointment to the highest judicial offices.
Particular account shall be taken of experience as judges of the International
Tribunal for the Former Yugoslavia or the International Criminal Tribunal for
Rwanda.
The procedure for identifying a candidate for consideration for appointment
under article 10, paragraph 2, is not prescribed in the statute. In accordance with the
established practice regarding the appointment of a judge to fill a vacancy at the
International Tribunal for the Former Yugoslavia and at the International Criminal
Tribunal for Rwanda, Jamaica, as the State of nationality of the outgoing judge, has
nominated a candidate, Justice Seymour Panton, to replace J udge Robinson for the
remainder of his term of office.
It is my view that Justice Panton meets the qualifications that are prescribed in
article 9, paragraph 1, of the Statute of the Mechanism.
