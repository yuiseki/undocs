Letter dated 24 June 2016 from the Secretary-General addressed
to the President of the Security Council
I have the honour to refer to your letter dated 9 June 2016 ( S/2016/527) in
which you took note of my proposal to reappoint the 25 judges of the International
Residual Mechanism for Criminal Tribunals.
I have also received a corresponding letter from the President of the General
Assembly (see annex I). Accordingly, I am proceeding with the reappointment of all
of the judges of the Mechanism.
Pursuant to paragraph 2 of Security Council resolution 2269 ( 2016), adopted
on 29 February 2016, the judges will be reappointed for a two -year term, with effect
from 1 July 2016 until 30 June 2018.
I should be grateful if you would bring to the attention of the members of the
Security Council the decision to reappoint all of the judges. The present letter
contains a list of the judges (see annex II).
(Signed) BAN Ki-moon

Letter dated 17 June 2016 from the President of the
General Assembly addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to refer to your letter dated 3 June 2016 concerning the
reappointment of the 25 judges of the International Residual Mechanism for
Criminal Tribunals (S/2016/526).
In accordance with article 10, paragraph 3, of the statute of the Mechanism, I
am pleased to inform you that I concur with the proposed reappointment of the 25
judges listed in the annex to your letter, for a term of two years, with effect from
1 July 2016 until 30 June 2018.
(Signed) Mogens Lykketoft
