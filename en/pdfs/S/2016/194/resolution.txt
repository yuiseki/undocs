Letter dated 27 February 2016 from the President of the
Security Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 23 February 2016
(S/2016/193), in which you stated your intention to reappoint Judge Theodor Meron
as President of the International Residual Mechanism for Criminal Tribunals and to
nominate Serge Brammertz for appointment as Prosecutor of the Mechanism, has
been brought to the attention of the members of the Security Council, who have
taken note of it. The members of the Council also note the position ex pressed by the
Russian Federation in a letter dated 27 February 2016 ( S/2016/197).
(Signed) Rafael Darío Ramírez Carreño
President of the Security Council
