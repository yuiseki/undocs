Letter dated 13 May 2016 from the President of the Security Council
addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 11 May 2016
(S/2016/444) concerning your intention to appoint Lieutenant General Johnson
Mogoa Kimani Ondieki (Kenya) as Force Commander of the United Nations
Mission in South Sudan (UNMISS) has been brought to the attention of the
members of the Security Council. They take note of the intention expressed in your
letter.
(Signed) Amr Abdellatif Aboulatta
President of the Security Council
