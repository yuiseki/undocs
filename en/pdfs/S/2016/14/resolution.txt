Letter dated 6 January 2016 from the President of the
Security Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 4 January 2016
(S/2016/13) concerning your intention to appoint Mr. Parfait Onanga -Anyanga
(Gabon) as your Special Representative for the Central African Republic and Head
of the United Nations Multidimensional Integrated Stabilization Mission in the
Central African Republic has been brought to the attention of the members of the
Security Council. They take note of the intention expressed in your letter.
(Signed) Elbio Rosselli
President of the Security Council
