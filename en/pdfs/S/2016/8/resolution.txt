Annex
[Original: French]
The Permanent Mission of France to the United Nations presents its
compliments to the United Nations Secretariat and has the honour to transmit the
following information.
Pursuant to paragraph 27 of Security Council resolution 2227 (2015), France
has the honour to transmit herewith the report on actions undertaken between
1 September and 30 November 2015 by French forces in support of the United
Nations Multidimensional Integrated Stabilization Mission in Mali.
The Permanent Mission of France would be grateful if the Secretary -General
would bring this report to the attention of the members of the Security Council.

Enclosure
[Original: French]

Report on the operational support provided by French forces to
the United Nations Multidimensional Integrated Stabilization
Mission in Mali between 1 September and 30 November 2015
Reference: Security Council resolution 2227 (2015) of 29 June 2015.
This tenth report since the establishment of the United Nations
Multidimensional Integrated Stabilization Mission in Mali (MINUSMA)
summarizes the activities conducted between 1 September and 30 November 2015
by French forces in support of MINUSMA. Since 1 September 2015, no combat
action has been conducted in response to an “imminent and serious threat” to
MINUSMA.
It should be noted, however, that French forces have intensified their support
for the Mission in various areas, as follows:
• Command: The Barkhane force has been providing nine officers to reinforce
understaffed MINUSMA sector headquarters since mid -July 2015;
• Security: The Barkhane force’s liaison and operational support detachments
have worked alongside MINUSMA to help improve the security of populated
areas and transit routes, thereby providing better protection to the population;
• Engineering: The Barkhane force provided engineering support in three ways.
First, its explosive ordnance disposal teams carried out emergency work to
clear explosives from an earthworks site at the MINUSMA camp in Tessalit.
Second, it provided site organization equipment (Bastion wall) and concertina
barbed wire free of charge for the refurbishment of the combat outpost
protecting the entrance to the Tessalit camp. Third, cooperation (exchange of
information, evidence, etc.) on action against improvised explosive devices
was strengthened through the establishment in Gao of a Barkhane laboratory
specializing in action against such devices in November 2015;
• Health: Operation Barkhane continued to provide medical support; on
8 October 2015 a Togolese peacekeeper with life-threatening injuries was
admitted to the French role 2 hospital in Gao, and on 24 November 2015 to
two injured Chadian peacekeepers from the MINUSMA battalion in Tessalit
were taken into the care of Operation Barkhane at the request of the Chadian
doctor and in coordination with the MINUSMA medical emergency
coordinator;
• Operational military support: Operation Barkhane continued to deliver a large
number of operational training sessions to the MINUSMA forces based in
Tessalit and Kidal.
