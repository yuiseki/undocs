Letter dated 11 October 2016 from the President of the
Security Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 7 October 2016
(S/2016/854) concerning your intention to appoint François Louncény Fall (Guinea)
as your Acting Special Representative for Central Africa and Head of t he United
Nations Regional Office for Central Africa has been brought to the attention of the
members of the Security Council. They take note of the intention expressed in your
letter.
(Signed) Vitaly I. Churkin
President of the Security Council
