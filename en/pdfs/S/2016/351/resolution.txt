Letter dated 15 April 2016 from the President of the Security Council
addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 12 April 2016
(S/2016/350) concerning the readiness of the Government of the United Kingdom of
Great Britain and Northern Ireland to deploy a national contingent of up to 70
military personnel to support the United Nations Support Office in Somalia has been
brought to the attention of the members of the Security Council. They take note of
the information contained in your letter and the proposed arrangements expres sed
therein, which are understood to be solely in support of the delivery of the Office ’s
mandated tasks.
(Signed) Liu Jieyi
President of the Security Council
