Letter dated 22 March 2016 from the Secretary-General addressed
to the President of the Security Council
I refer to Security Council resolution 1958 (2010), in which the Council
requested me to report and provide analysis to the Council on an annual basis on the
usage and expenditure of the escrow account referenced in paragraphs 4 and 5 of
that resolution.
In previous years, commencing in 2012, I presented annual reports to the
Council no later than 31 March, that being the date for the closing of the yearly
United Nations accounts. However, the closing of 2015 accounts has been delayed,
and, in consultation with the United Nations Board of Auditors, the time limit for
the preparation of all financial statements for the year ending 31 December 2015 has
been extended to the end of May 2016. In the light of this, and to ensure that the
report includes expenditures as reflected in the financial statements, I would like to
request an extension until 31 May 2016 for the submission of my report to the
Security Council.
I should be grateful if you could bring the present letter to the attention of the
members of the Security Council.
(Signed) BAN Ki-moon
