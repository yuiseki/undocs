Letter dated 26 May 2016 from the President of the
Security Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 24 May 2016
(S/2016/488), concerning your plan outlining the manner in which the Office of the
Special Envoy for Yemen will be strengthened in support of the Yemeni parties and
the peace process as requested in S/PRST/2016/5, has been brought to the attention
of the members of the Security Council. They take note of the information contained
in your letter and the proposed arrangements expressed therein.
(Signed) Amr Abdellatif Aboulatta
President of the Security Council
