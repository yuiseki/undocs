Letter dated 8 March 2016 from the President of the
Security Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 4 March 2016
(S/2016/226) concerning your intention to appoint Mr. Jean Arnault (France) as
your Special Representative for Colombia and Head of the United Na tions Mission
in Colombia has been brought to the attention of the members of the Security
Council. They take note of the intention expressed in your letter.
(Signed) Ismael Abraão Gaspar Martins
President of the Security Council
