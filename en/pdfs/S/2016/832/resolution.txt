Letter dated 30 September 2016 from the President of the
Security Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 13 September 2016
(S/2016/831) concerning your intention to appoint Hubert Hudson Price II
(United States of America) as the Head of the United Nations Support Office in
Somalia has been brought to the attention of the members of the Security Council.
They take note of the intention expressed in your letter.
(Signed) Gerard van Bohemen
President of the Security Council
