Letter dated 17 March 2016 from the President of the
Security Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 15 March 2016
(S/2016/258) concerning your intention to appoint Mr. Nicholas Haysom (South
Africa) as your Special Envoy for the Sudan and South Sudan has been brought to
the attention of the members of the Security Council. They take note of the intention
expressed in your letter.
(Signed) Ismael Abraão Gaspar Martins
President of the Security Council
