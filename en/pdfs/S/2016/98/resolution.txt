Letter dated 1 February 2016 from the President of the
Security Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 28 January 2016
(S/2016/97) concerning your intention to appoint Major General Jai Shanker Menon
(India) as the Head of Mission and Force Commander of the Unit ed Nations
Disengagement Observer Force has been brought to the attention of the members of
the Security Council. They take note of the intention expressed in your letter.
(Signed) Rafael Darío Ramírez Carreño
President of the Security Council
