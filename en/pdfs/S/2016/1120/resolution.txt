Eleventh report of the Secretary-General pursuant to

I. Introduction
1.
The present report is submitted pursuant to paragraph 6 of Security Council
resolution 1956 (2010), in which the Council requested me to provide written
reports on an ongoing basis to the Council every six months about the United
Nations Compensation Fund, with effect from 1 January 2012, evaluating the
continued compliance with the provisions of paragraph 21 of resolutio n 1483
(2003), which requires Iraq to deposit 5 per cent of the proceeds from export sales
of petroleum, petroleum products and natural gas into the Fund. The present
eleventh report covers the developments following the issuance of my tenth report
(S/2016/536) on 14 June 2016.

II. Developments
2.
The overall amount of compensation paid to date by the United Nations
Compensation Commission stands at $47.8 billion, leaving approximately
$4.6 billion outstanding to be paid to Kuwait to settle the last remaining claim.
3.
At the eighty-first session of the Governing Council of the Commission, held
on 2 November 2016, the Council considered a request from the Government of Iraq
for an additional postponement of its deposit requirements. Recognizing that the
extraordinarily difficult security circumstances in Iraq and the unusual budgetary
challenges associated therewith had continued since its adoption of decis ions 272
(2014) and 273 (2015), the Council adopted decision 274 (2016), by which it
granted a further one-year postponement. In adopting the decision, the Council
noted with appreciation the support of the Government of Kuwait for the Iraqi
request. The Council stressed that it was important for Iraq to complete the payment
of the outstanding compensation in a timely manner. In the light of the
postponements, the Council also directed the Commission’s secretariat to explore,
as a matter of importance, future options to ensure the continuation of the payment
of the outstanding compensation and to report back to the Council in advance of its
eighty-second session, in April 2017. The Council also encouraged the Governments
of Iraq and Kuwait to collaborate on future options to be shared with the secretariat
for inclusion in its report to the Council.

4.
Pursuant to decision 274 (2016), the Government of Iraq is to resume deposits
on 1 January 2018 and advise the Governing Council in writing, no later than
31 August 2017, of its proposed arrangements for the resumpt ion. In addition,
payments towards the outstanding amount are scheduled to resume in 2018 in
accordance with the payment mechanism set out in Council decision 267 (2009).
5.
In conclusion, I welcome the most recent decision of the Governing Council
and acknowledge once again the support of the Government of Kuwait for the
request by Iraq. I reiterate my expression of solidarity with the Government and the
people of Iraq. In addition, I echo the sentiments of the Council and stress that it is
important for Iraq to fulfil its obligations and for the Commission’s mandate to be
brought to a conclusion.
