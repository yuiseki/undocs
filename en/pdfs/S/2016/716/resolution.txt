Letter dated 15 August 2016 from the Secretary-General
addressed to the President of the Security Council
In accordance with article 17, paragraph 3, of the Relationship Agreement
between the United Nations and the International Criminal Court, I have the honour
to transmit herewith a letter from the President of the Court, Judge Silvia Fernández
de Gurmendi, dated 13 July 2016 (see annex). The President transmits therewith the
decision on the non-compliance by Djibouti with the request to arrest and surrender
Omar Al-Bashir to the Court and referring the matter to the Security Council and the
Assembly of States Parties to the Rome Statute, issued by Pre-Trial Chamber II of
the Court on 11 July 2016 in the case of The Prosecutor v. Omar Hassan Ahmad
Al Bashir.
I should be grateful if you would bring the present letter and its a nnex to the
attention of the members of the Security Council.
(Signed) BAN Ki-moon

[Original: English and French]
13 July 2016
Excellency,
Pursuant to article 17(3) of the Relationship Agreement between the United
Nations and the International Criminal Court and in accordance with article 87(7) of the
Rome Statute and regulation 109(4) of the Regulations of the Court, and as requested
by Pre-Trial Chamber II, I hereby transmit the decision on the non-compliance by
Djibouti with the request to arrest and surrender Omar Al-Bashir to the Court and
referring the matter to the Security Council and the Assembly of States Parties to the
Rome Statute, in the case of The Prosecutor v. Omar Hassan Ahmad Al Bashir of the
said Chamber, dated 11 July 2016 (see enclosure).
I would be grateful if you could transmit the present letter and the enclosed
decision to the Security Council.
(Signed) Silvia Fernández de Gurmendi
