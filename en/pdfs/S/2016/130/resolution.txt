Letter dated 10 February 2016 from the President of the
Security Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 8 February 2016
(S/2016/129) concerning your intention to appoint Lieutenant General Balla Keïta
(Senegal) as Force Commander of the United Nations Multid imensional Integrated
Stabilization Mission in the Central African Republic (MINUSCA) has been
brought to the attention of the members of the Security Council. They take note of
the intention expressed in your letter.
(Signed) Rafael Darío Ramírez Carreño
President of the Security Council
