Letter dated 6 December 2016 from the Secretary-General
addressed to the President of the Security Council
I have the honour to refer to the United Nations Mission for the Referendum in
Western Sahara (MINURSO), established by the Security Council in resolution 690
(1991) of 29 April 1991 and most recently extended by the Council in resolution
2285 (2016) of 29 April 2016.
Following the usual consultations, I would like to inform you of my intention
to appoint Major General Wang Xiaojun (China) as Force Commander of
MINURSO. He succeeds Major General Muhammad Tayyab Azam (Pakistan), who
completed his assignment on 7 November 2016. I would like to take this
opportunity to express my gratitude to Major General Azam for his d edication and
effective leadership of the MINURSO military component.
I should be grateful if you would bring this matter to the attention of the
members of the Security Council.
(Signed) BAN Ki-moon
