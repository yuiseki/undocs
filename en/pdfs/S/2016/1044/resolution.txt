Letter dated 7 December 2016 from the Secretary-General
addressed to the President of the Security Council
I have the honour to refer to the United Nations Mission in South Sudan
(UNMISS), established by the Security Council in its resolution 1996 (2011), and
most recently extended by the Council in its resolution 2304 (2016).
Following the usual consultations, I would like to inform you of my intention
to appoint Mr. David Shearer (New Zealand) as my Special Representative for
South Sudan and Head of UNMISS. He will succeed Ms. Ellen Margrethe Løj
(Denmark), who completed her assignment on 30 November 2016. I would like to
take this opportunity to express my gratitude to Ms. Løj for her dedication and
effective leadership.
I should be grateful if you would bring this matter to the attention of the
members of the Security Council.
(Signed) BAN Ki-moon
