Letter dated 28 January 2016 from the Secretary-General
addressed to the President of the Security Council
I have the honour to refer to the United Nations Disengagement Observer
Force (UNDOF), which was established by the Security Council in its resolution
350 (1974) and most recently extended by the Council in its resolution 2257 (2015).
Following the usual consultations, I would like to inform you of my intention
to appoint Major General Jai Shanker Menon (India) as the Head of Mission and
Force Commander of UNDOF. Major General Menon succeeds Lieutenant General
Purna Chandra Thapa (Nepal) who will complete his assignment on 7 February
2016. I would like to take this opportunity to express my gratitude to Lieutenant
General Thapa for his dedication and effective leadership of UNDOF.
I should be grateful if you would bring the present letter to the attention of the
members of the Security Council.
(Signed) BAN Ki-moon
