Letter dated 20 May 2016 from the President of the Security Council
addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 18 May 2016
(S/2016/467) concerning your report requested in the statement by the President of
the Security Council (S/PRST/2015/2) has been brought to the attention of the
members of the Council.
The members of the Security Council have taken note of your request to
postpone the submission of your written report that the Council requested in
document S/PRST/2015/2 to coincide with the report requested by the General
Assembly in its resolution 70/262, as well as your suggestion to provide an oral
briefing to the Council, instead of a written report, no later than Decembe r 2016.
(Signed) Amr Abdellatif Aboulatta
President of the Security Council
