Letter dated 8 December 2016 from the President of the
Security Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 6 December 2016
(S/2016/1040) concerning your intention to appoint Major General Wang Xiaojun
(China) as Force Commander of the United Nations Mission for the Referen dum in
Western Sahara (MINURSO) has been brought to the attention of the members of
the Security Council. They take note of the intention expressed in your letter.
(Signed) Román Oyarzun Marchesi
President of the Security Council
