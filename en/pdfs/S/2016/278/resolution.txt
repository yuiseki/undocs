Letter dated 23 March 2016 from the Secretary-General addressed
to the President of the Security Council
I have the honour to refer to Security Council resolution 2271 (2016), adopted
by the Council on 2 March 2016, by which it decided to extend the mandate of the
Panel of Experts on South Sudan until 15 May 2016, as set out in paragraph 18 of
resolution 2206 (2015).
Accordingly, I wish to inform you that, after consulting with the Security
Council Committee established pursuant to resolution 2206 (2015) concerning
South Sudan, I have administratively extended the appointments and contracts of
the five individuals currently serving on the Panel of Experts established by the
same resolution, whose contracts were set to expire on 3 April 2016, until a new
resolution is adopted by the Council regarding the further extension of the mandate
of the Panel.
I should be grateful if you would bring this matter to the attention of the
members of the Security Council.
(Signed) BAN Ki-moon
