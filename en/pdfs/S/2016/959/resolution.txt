Letter dated 11 November 2016 from the Secretary-General
addressed to the President of the Security Council
I have the honour to transmit to you a letter dated 4 November 2016 from
Judge Carmel Agius, President and Judge of the Appeals Chamber of the
International Tribunal for the Former Yugoslavia (see annex).
In his letter, Judge Agius requests the extension of the terms of office of seven
permanent judges and one ad hoc judge of the Tribunal until 30 November 2017 or
until the completion of the cases to which they are or will be assigned, if sooner.
Pursuant to Security Council resolution 2256 (2015) of 22 December 2015 and
General Assembly resolution 70/227 of 23 December 2015, the current term of
office of the judges is set to expire on 31 December 2016. Howe ver, according to
the trial and appeal schedule enclosed with the letter from Judge Agius, the cases to
which the judges are or will be assigned will be completed in November 2017.
Therefore, it is necessary to extend the terms of office of the judges so t hat they
may complete the cases.
In addition, Judge Agius requests a further extension of his term of office until
31 December 2017 so that he may continue to perform the functions required of him
as President in closing the Tribunal.
It falls to the General Assembly and the Security Council to consider and
decide upon these requests. Accordingly, I would be grateful if you would bring the
letter from Judge Agius to the attention of the members of the Security Council.
I also note that, pursuant to Security Council resolution 2256 (2015), the
current term of office of the Prosecutor of the Tribunal, Serge Brammertz, will
expire on 31 December 2016.
According to Article 16, paragraph 4, of the Statute of the Tribunal, the
Prosecutor shall be appointed by the Security Council on nomination by the
Secretary-General.
In this regard, I hereby nominate Serge Brammertz for reappointment as
Prosecutor of the International Tribunal for the Former Yugoslavia until the
completion of the work of the Tribunal.
(Signed) BAN Ki-moon

Letter dated 4 November 2016 from the President of the
International Tribunal for the Former Yugoslavia addressed to the
Secretary-General
I have the honour of referring to Security Council resolution 2256 (2015) of
22 December 2015, in which the Security Council, inter alia, extended the terms of
office of the permanent and ad litem judges of the International Criminal Tribunal
for the Former Yugoslavia until 31 December 2016 or until the completion of the
cases to which they are assigned, if sooner.
By the present letter, I wish to bring to your attention the necessity for a final
extension of the terms of office of the Tribunal’s permanent judges and of an ad hoc
judge. The extensions set out below are sought on the basis of and by reference to
the attached trial and appeal schedules, which contain projections for the completion
of the Tribunal’s remaining substantive trial and appeal cases.
Permanent judges
An extension until 30 November 2017 or until the completion of the cases to
which they are or will be assigned, if sooner, is sought for the following remaining
judges:
Judge Carmel Agius, President (Malta)
Judge Liu Daqun, Vice-President (China)
Judge Alphons Orie (Netherlands)
Judge Fausto Pocar (Italy)
Judge Theodor Meron (United States of America)
Judge Bakone Justice Moloto (South Africa)
Judge Christoph Flügge (Germany)

An extension until 30 November 2017 or until the completion of the cases to

Judge Burton Hall (Bahamas) 1
