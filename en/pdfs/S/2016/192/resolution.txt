Letter dated 26 February 2016 from the President of the Security
Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 23 February 2016
(S/2016/191) concerning your intention to appoint Ms. Elizabeth Spehar (Canada)
as your Special Representative and Head of the United Nations Peacekeeping Force
in Cyprus (UNFICYP) has been brought to the attention of the members of the
Security Council. They take note of the intention expressed in your letter.
(Signed) Rafael Darío Ramírez Carreño
President of the Security Council
