Letter dated 14 December 2016 from the Secretary-General
addressed to the President of the Security Council
The present letter is to inform the Council of the signing, ratification and entry
into force of the final peace agreement between the Government of Colombia and
the Revolutionary Armed Forces of Colombia — People’s Army (FARC-EP).
On 24 November 2016, the two parties signed the revised Final Agreement for
Ending the Conflict and Building a Stable and Lasting Peace. The Final Agreement
was ratified by the Colombian Congress on 30 November, following votes in the
Senate on 29 November and the House of Representatives on 30 November.
The provisions of the Final Agreement pertaining to the bilateral and definitive
ceasefire and cessation of hostilities and the laying down of arms are unchanged
from those set out in the agreement that was signed in Cartagena on 24 September
2016. Those provisions informed my recommendations on the size, operational
aspects and mandate of the United Nations Mission in Colombia set out in my report
of 18 August 2016 (S/2016/729) and approved by the Security Council on
13 September 2016 in its resolution 2307 (2016).
In a joint press statement of 1 December 2016, the Government of Colombia
and FARC-EP confirmed that the Final Agreement had entered into force on
1 December 2016, following its ratification by the Colombian Congress. The parties
confirmed, furthermore, that this date would be considered “D” day, as defined in
the Final Agreement, thus initiating the timetable for the imp lementation of its
provisions relating to the ceasefire, cessation of hostilities and laying down of arms.
The United Nations Mission in Colombia is accordingly proceeding to
implementation of the full range of its mandated tasks, as set out in Security
Council resolutions 2261 (2016) and 2307 (2016).
The signing, ratification and entry into force of the Final Agreement is a
momentous achievement for the parties and for the people of Colombia. The United
Nations Mission in Colombia, along with the parties, is intensifying efforts to meet
the challenging requirements of implementation. My forthcoming report to the
Security Council on the implementation of the Mission’s mandate will elaborate on
progress to date and the next steps in this process.
(Signed) BAN Ki-moon
