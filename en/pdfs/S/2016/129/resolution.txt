Letter dated 8 February 2016 from the Secretary-General
addressed to the President of the Security Council
I have the honour to refer to the United Nations Multidimensional Integrated
Stabilization Mission in the Central African Republic (MINUSCA), established by
Security Council resolution 2149 (2014) and most recently extended until 30 April
2016 by its resolution 2217 (2015).
Following the usual consultations, I would like to inform you of my intention
to appoint Lieutenant General Balla Keïta (Senegal) as Force Commander of
MINUSCA. Lieutenant General Keïta will succeed Major General Martin Chomu
Tumenta (Cameroon), who tragically passed away on 30 November 2015. I would
like to take this opportunity to reiterate my appreciation for the contribution of the
late Major General Tumenta to MINUSCA.
I would be grateful if you could bring the present letter to the attention of the
members of the Security Council.
(Signed) BAN Ki-moon
