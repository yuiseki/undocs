Annex
Letter dated 16 May 2016 from the Chair of the Working Group of
the Security Council on Children and Armed Conflict addressed to
the Secretary-General
At its 55th meeting, on 6 July 2015, the Working Group of the Security
Council on Children and Armed Conflict examined the third report of the SecretaryGeneral on children and armed conflict in Afghanistan ( S/2015/336), covering the
period from 1 September 2010 to 31 December 2014. At its 57th meeting, on
2 March 2016, the Working Group adopted its conclusions on children and armed
conflict in Afghanistan (S/AC.51/2016/1).
In follow-up to the recommendations of the Working Group and subject to and
consistent with applicable international law and relevant Security Council
resolutions, including resolutions 1612 (2005), 1882 (2009), 1998 (2011), 2068
(2012), 2143 (2014), 2250 (2015) and 2255 (2015), I am entrusted, in my capacity
as Chair of the Working Group, to:
(a) Request you to ensure that the country task force on monitoring and
reporting in Afghanistan and other relevant United Nations agencies continue their
engagement and efforts to support the Government of Afghanistan to address
violations and abuses committed against children in armed conflict;
(b) Recognizing the importance of monitoring and reporting of violations
and abuses against children, request you to ensure the continued effectiveness of the
monitoring and reporting mechanism on violations and abuses against children in
armed conflict in Afghanistan, especially in areas most affected by conflict;
(c) Request you to continue to give priority to the child protection activities
and capacity of the United Nations Assistance Mission in Afghanistan a nd to
continue to include in future reports information and analysis on children and armed
conflict in Afghanistan, in line with relevant Security Council resolutions.
(Signed) Ramlan Bin Ibrahim
Chair
Security Council Working Group
