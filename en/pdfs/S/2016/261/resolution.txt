Letter dated 17 March 2016 from the President of the
Security Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 15 March 2016
(S/2016/260) concerning your intention to appoint Mr. Tadamichi Yamamoto
(Japan) as your Special Representative for Afghanistan and Head of the United
Nations Assistance Mission in Afghanistan has been brought to the attention of the
members of the Security Council. They take note of the intention expressed in your
letter.
(Signed) Ismael Abraão Gaspar Martins
President of the Security Council
