Letter dated 7 October 2016 from the Secretary-General
addressed to the President of the Security Council
I have the honour to refer to Security Council resolution 2265 (2016) of
10 February 2016, by which the Council decided to extend until 12 March 2017
the mandate of the Panel of Experts on the Sudan esta blished pursuant to resolution
1591 (2005) and requested that I take the necessary administrative measures in that
regard.
Accordingly, I wish to inform you that, after consulting with the Security Counci l
Committee established pursuant to resolution 1591 (2005) concerning the Sudan,
I have appointed the following experts:


Mr. Thomas Bifwoli Wanjala, transport and customs (Kenya);



Mr. Vincent Darracq, regional (France);



Ms. Daniela Kravetz Miranda, international humanitarian law (Chile);



Mr. Warren Melia, arms (United Kingdom of Great Britain and Northern Ireland);



Mr. Abhai Kumar Srivastav, finance (India)

I have also designated Mr. Wanjala to serve as Coordinator of the Panel of
Experts.
I should be grateful if you would bring this matter to the attention of the
members of the Security Council.
(Signed) BAN Ki-moon
