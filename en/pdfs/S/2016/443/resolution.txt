Letter dated 12 May 2016 from the Secretary-General addressed
to the President of the Security Council
I have the honour to refer to paragraph 12 of Security Council resolution 2278
(2016) concerning Libya, by which the Council decided to extend the mandate of
the Panel of Experts on Libya established pursuant to resolution 1973 (2011) until
31 July 2017.
Accordingly, I wish to inform you that, after consulting with the Security
Council Committee established pursuant to resolution 1970 (2011) concerning
Libya, I have appointed the following experts:
Mr. Naji Abou-Khalil, Lebanon (armed groups/regional)
Mr. Moncef Kartas, Tunisia (arms)
Mr. David McFarland, United Kingdom of Great Britain and Northern Ireland (arms)
Mr. Juan Alberto Pintos Servia, Spain (maritime/transport)
Mr. Steven Spittaels, Belgium (finance)
I have also designated Mr. Spittaels to serve as Coordinator of the Panel of
Experts.
An expert on armed groups will be appointed in due course.
I should be grateful if you would bring the present letter to the attention of the
members of the Security Council.
(Signed) BAN Ki-moon
