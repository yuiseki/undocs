Letter dated 22 April 2016 from the President of the
Security Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 20 April 2016
(S/2016/376) concerning your intention to appoint Justice Seymour Panton
(Jamaica) as a judge of the International Residual Mechanism for Criminal
Tribunals has been brought to the attention of the members of the Security Council.
They take note of the intention expressed in your letter.
(Signed) Liu Jieyi
President of the Security Council
