Letter dated 29 March 2016 from the Secretary-General addressed
to the President of the Security Council
I have the honour to refer to Security Council resolution 2266 (2016), adopted
on 24 February 2016, concerning Yemen. By paragraph 5 of the resolution, the
Council decided to extend the mandate of the Panel of Experts on Yemen until
27 March 2017.
Accordingly, I wish to inform you that, after consulting the Security Council
Committee established pursuant to resolution 2140 (2014), I have appointed the
following experts:
Mr. Ahmed Himmiche, regional (Morocco)
Mr. Gregory Johnsen, armed groups (United States of America)
Mr. Farhan Hyder Sahito, finance (Pakistan)
Mr. Adrian Wilkinson, arms (United Kingdom of Great Britain and
Northern Ireland)
I have designated Mr. Himmiche to serve as Coordinator of the Panel.
An expert on international humanitarian law will be appointed shortly.
(Signed) BAN Ki-moon
