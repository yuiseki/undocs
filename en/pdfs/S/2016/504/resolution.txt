Letter dated 31 May 2016 from the Secretary-General addressed
to the President of the Security Council
I have the honour to refer to Security Council resolution 2278 (2016)
concerning Libya. By paragraph 12 of that resolution, the Council decided to extend
the mandate of the Panel of Experts on Libya until 31 July 2017.
Accordingly, I wish to inform you that, after consulting with the Security
Council Committee established pursuant to resolution 1970 (2011) concerning
Libya, I have appointed the following individual to serve as the sixth expert on the
Panel of Experts:
Mr. Kassim Bouhou, armed groups (France).
(Signed) BAN Ki-moon
