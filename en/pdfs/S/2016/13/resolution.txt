Letter dated 4 January 2016 from the Secretary-General
addressed to the President of the Security Council
I have the honour to refer to the United Nations Multidimensional Integrated
Stabilization Mission in the Central African Republic (MINUSCA), established by
the Security Council in its resolution 2149 (2014) of 10 April 2014 and most
recently extended by the Council in its resolution 2217 (2015) of 28 April 2015.
Following the usual consultations, I would like to inform you of my intention
to appoint Mr. Parfait Onanga-Anyanga (Gabon) as my Special Representative for
the Central African Republic and Head of MINUSCA. Mr. Onanga-Anyanga has
served as my Acting Special Representative for the Central African Republic and
Head of MINUSCA since 16 August 2015.
I would be grateful if you could bring the present letter to the attention of the
members of the Security Council.
(Signed) BAN Ki-moon
