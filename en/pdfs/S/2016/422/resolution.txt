Letter dated 28 April 2016 from the Secretary-General addressed
to the President of the Security Council
Further to my report on the United Nations Peacekeeping Force in Cyprus
(UNFICYP) of 6 January 2016 (S/2016/11), I have the honour to write to you on the
subject of confidence-building measures, and the supporting role played by
UNFICYP in that regard, both of which the Security Council has welcomed (see
resolution 2263 (2016)). As detailed in the aforementioned report, UNFICYP is
lending important assistance, especially in the area of demining.
I am grateful for the strong support of the Security Council in 2015 by
endorsing and subsequently prolonging the inter-mission cooperation arrangement
between UNFICYP and the United Nations Interim Force in Lebanon (UNIFIL) (see
S/2015/495 and S/2015/661). The temporary deployment, under the facilitation of
UNFICYP, of a UNIFIL mine-clearance team from Cambodia from May to
November 2015 allowed for hazardous materials to be cleared, minefields to be
declared as posing no threat and the way to be paved for the opening of crossing
points. Importantly, the initiative helped to build trust between the leaders at a
critical juncture, and confidence among the wider public in the prospects for a
settlement.
There are several remaining known mined areas in Cyprus, including four
within the buffer zone. I therefore propose the establishment of a standing inter mission cooperation arrangement between the two missions, for an initial period of
a year and within the authorized military strength of UNIFIL, fully recognizing that
the unit would remain primarily a UNIFIL asset. This would allow for rapid and
flexible deployment to Cyprus on a short-term and occasional basis, as required,
subject to the operational needs of UNIFIL in its area of operations.
I should be grateful for the concurrence of the Security Council in order for
such an agreement to be formalized, based on the modalities of the successful
deployment in 2014 and 2015 of a Cambodian demining team from UNIFIL.
I should be grateful if you would bring the present letter to the attention of the
members of the Security Council.
(Signed) BAN Ki-moon
