Letter dated 7 June 2016 from the President of the Security Council
addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 3 June 2016
(S/2016/518) concerning your intention to appoint Major General Per Gustaf Lodin
(Sweden) as Chief Military Observer and Head of Mission of the Un ited Nations
Military Observer Group in India and Pakistan, has been brought to the attention of
the members of the Security Council. They take note of the intention expressed in
your letter.
(Signed) François Delattre
President of the Security Council
