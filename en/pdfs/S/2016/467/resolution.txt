Letter dated 18 May 2016 from the Secretary-General addressed
to the President of the Security Council
I refer to my letter to the President of the Security Council of 20 November
2015 (S/2015/909), requesting a postponement of the briefing that the Council
requested in the statement by the President of the Council of 14 Janu ary 2015
(S/PRST/2015/2), and the latter’s reply of 24 November 2015 taking note of it
(S/2015/910). In the same statement, I was also requested to submit a report to the
Council no later than December 2016 on further United Nations peacebuilding
efforts in the aftermath of conflict, including progress towards increasing the
participation of women in peacebuilding, taking into consideration the views of the
Peacebuilding Commission. That report has always been issued as a document of
the General Assembly and of the Security Council.
The General Assembly and the Security Council concluded the 2015 review of
the peacebuilding architecture and adopted substantially ide ntical resolutions on
27 April 2016 (resolutions 70/262 and 2282 (2016), respectively). In its resolution,
the Assembly invited the Secretary-General to report to it at its seventy-second
session, at least 60 days prior to the high-level meeting on “Peacebuilding and
sustaining peace”, on efforts to implement the resolution, including in a number of
specific areas. I would like to propose postponing the submission of the written
report that the Council requested in document S/PRST/2015/2 to coincide with the
report requested by the Assembly in its resolution 70/262. Moreover, I would
suggest that I provide an oral briefing to the Council, instead of a written report, no
later than December 2016, as requested in document S/PRST/2015/2.
The Peacebuilding Support Office will work closely with the members of the
Security Council to reschedule the briefing.
(Signed) BAN Ki-moon
