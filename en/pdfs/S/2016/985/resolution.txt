Enclosure
[Original: English]

Report to the United Nations on Kosovo Force operations
1.
The present report on the Kosovo Force (KFOR) is provided in accordance
with Security Council resolution 1244 (1999) and covers the period from 1 July to
30 September 2016.
2.
As at 30 September 2016, the total number of KFOR troops in th eatre
remained approximately 4,600.
3.

There were no major security incidents during the reporting period.

Security situation and operations
4.
During the reporting period, the security situation in Kosovo remained stable,
and no inter-ethnic incidents occurred. In general, the trend is gradually growing
more positive, as Serbia and Kosovo remain committed to the European Union -led
dialogue process.
5.
During the reporting period, KFOR continued to monitor the situation and to
maintain situational awareness. KFOR also continued to work in close cooperation
and coordination with the Kosovo Police and the European Union Rule of Law
Mission in Kosovo (EULEX). KFOR remained ready to provide immediate
intervention as the third responder, if required. However, there was no requirement
for direct intervention by KFOR during the reporting period.
6.
The mission of KFOR to contribute to a safe and secure environment and
freedom of movement has continued as part of a comprehensive international effort.
Summary
7.
During the reporting period, KFOR continued to contribute to both
maintaining a safe and secure environment and ensuring freedom of movement, in
close coordination with EULEX and the Kosovo Police. The close coordination on
the ground between KFOR, in its capacity as the third responder, and EULEX and
the Kosovo Police continues to be highly effective.
