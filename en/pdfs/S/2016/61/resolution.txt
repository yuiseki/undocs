Letter dated 21 January 2016 from the President of the
Security Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to refer to Security Council resolution 1646 (2005), adopted
on 20 December 2005, in which the Council, in keeping with its resolution 1645
(2005), adopted on the same date, decided that the permanent members listed in
Article 23, paragraph 1, of the Charter of the United Nations should be members of
the Organizational Committee of the Peacebuilding Commission and that, in
addition, the Council should annually select two of its elected members to
participate in the Organizational Committee.
I therefore have the honour to inform you that, following informal
consultations, the members of the Security Council have agreed on the selection of
Angola and Venezuela (Bolivarian Republic of) as the two elected members of the
Council to participate in the Organizational Committee for a term of one year, until
the end of 2016.
(Signed) Elbio Rosselli
President of the Security Council
