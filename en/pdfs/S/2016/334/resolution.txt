Letter dated 8 April 2016 from the Secretary-General addressed to
the President of the Security Council
I have the honour to refer to Security Council resolution 2266 (2016), adopted
by the Council on 24 February 2016, concerning Yemen. In paragraph 5 of the
resolution, the Council decided to extend the mandate of the Panel of Experts on
Yemen until 27 March 2017.
Accordingly, I wish to inform you that, after consulting with the Security
Council Committee established pursuant to resolution 2140 (2014), I have appointed
the following expert in international humanitarian law:
Ms. Dakshinie Ruwanthika Gunaratne (Sri Lanka)
I should be grateful if you would bring this matter to the attention of the
members of the Security Council.
(Signed) BAN Ki-moon
