Letter dated 27 July 2016 from the President of the Security Council
addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 22 July 2016 pursuant to
Security Council resolution 2261 (2016) (S/2016/643) has been brought to the
attention of the members of the Security Council.
The members of the Council have taken note of the request contained in your
letter for an extension until 19 August 2016 of the Council’s deadline for detailed
recommendations on the size and operational aspects of the Unit ed Nations Mission
in Colombia.
(Signed) Koro Bessho
President of the Security Council
