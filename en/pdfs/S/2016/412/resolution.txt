Letter dated 3 May 2016 from the President of the SecurityCouncil
addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 28 April 2016
(S/2016/411) concerning your intention to appoint Modibo Touré (Mali) as your
Special Representative for Guinea-Bissau and Head of the United Nations
Integrated Peacebuilding Office in Guinea-Bissau has been brought to the attention
of the members of the Security Council. They take note of the intention expressed in
your letter.
(Signed) Amr Abdellatif Aboulatta
President of the Security Council
