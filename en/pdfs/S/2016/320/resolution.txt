Letter dated 6 April 2016 from the President of the Security Council
addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 22 March 2016
(S/2016/319) concerning your report pursuant to resolution 1958 (2010) has been
brought to the attention of the members of the Security Council.
The members of the Council have taken note of the request contained in your
letter that the time frame for the issuance of the report be extended, and they look
forward to the submission of the report by 31 May 2016.
(Signed) Liu Jieyi
President of the Security Council
