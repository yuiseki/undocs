Official communiqué of the 4825th (closed) meeting of the
Security Council
Held in private at Headquarters, New York, on Monday, 15 September 2003,
at 4.15 p.m.
In accordance with rule 55 of the provisional rules of procedure of the Security
Council, the following communiqué was issued through the Secretary-General in
place of a verbatim record:
“On 15 September 2003, the Security Council, pursuant to resolution
1353 (2001), annex II, sections A and B, held its 4825th meeting in private
with the potential troop and civilian police-contributing countries to the
proposed United Nations peacekeeping operation in Liberia.
“The Security Council and the potential troop and civilian policecontributing countries heard a briefing under rule 39 of its provisional rules of
procedure by Mr. Jacques Paul Klein, Special Representative of the SecretaryGeneral for Liberia.
“The members of the Council had a constructive exchange of views with
the potential troop and civilian police-contributing countries.”
