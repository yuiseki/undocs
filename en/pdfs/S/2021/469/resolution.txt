Letter dated 14 May 2021 from the Secretary-General addressed
to the President of the Security Council
I have the honour to refer to Security Council resolution 2571 (2021), adopted
on 16 April 2021, by which the Council decided to extend the mandate of the Panel
of Experts on Libya until 15 August 2022.
Accordingly, I wish to inform you that, after consulting with the Security
Council Committee established pursuant to resolution 1970 (2011) concerning Libya,
I have appointed the following experts as of 16 May 2021:
Ms. Alia Aoun, armed groups (Lebanon)
Ms. Marina de Ramon, finance (Spain)
Mr. Georg Kerschischnig, arms/maritime (Austria)
Mr. Yassine Marjane, regional/transport (Morocco)
Ms. Jelena Plamenac, armed groups/international humanitarian law ( Serbia)
Mr. Adrian Wilkinson, arms (United Kingdom of Great Britain and Northern
Ireland)
I have designated Ms. Aoun to serve as Coordinator of the Panel.
I should be grateful if you would bring this matter to the attention of the
members of the Security Council.
(Signed) António Guterres
