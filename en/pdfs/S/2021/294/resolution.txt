Letter dated 25 March 2021 from the President of the
Security Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 23 March 2021
(S/2021/293) concerning your intention to appoint Ms. Joanna Wronecka (Poland) as
the United Nations Special Coordinator for Lebanon has been brought to the attention
of the members of the Security Council. They take note of the intention expressed
therein.
(Signed) Linda Thomas-Greenfield
President of the Security Council
