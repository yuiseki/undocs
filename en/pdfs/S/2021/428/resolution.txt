Letter dated 3 May 2021 from the President of the Security
Council addressed to the Permanent Representatives of the
members of the Security Council
China assumes the presidency of the Security Council for the month of May
2021 in the context of the ongoing coronavirus disease (COVID -19) pandemic.
Taking into consideration the evolution of the pandemic and based on the recent
practices established by the Security Council, the presidency envisages a combination
of informal videoconferences and in-person meetings for the work of the Council in
May.
For the informal videoconferences, the presidency will continue to implement
the agreed working methods set out in the letters from the President of the Security
Council for March and May 2020 (S/2020/253 and S/2020/372).
Subject to the consensus of the Security Council, in-person meetings at United
Nations Headquarters will be conducted in compliance with guidelines and measures
set out in the letter from the President of the Security Council for October 2020 on
working methods (S/2020/966).
The current methods and modalities of work are intended only to respond to the
extraordinary circumstances related to the ongoing COVID-19 pandemic and will not
set a precedent for the future. They may be revised, adapted or suspended, subject to
the agreement of all members of the Security Council.
(Signed) Zhang Jun
President of the Security Council
