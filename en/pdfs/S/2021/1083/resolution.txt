Letter dated 20 December 2021 from the Secretary-General
addressed to the President of the Security Council
I have the honour to refer to your letter dated 16 December 2021 (S/2021/1065)
in which you concurred with my proposal to appoint Ms. Margaret Anne McAuliffe
deGuzman (United States of America) as a judge of the International R esidual
Mechanism for Criminal Tribunals to replace Judge Theodor Meron, who resigned
with effect from 17 November 2021.
I have received a corresponding letter from the President of the General
Assembly dated 17 December 2021 (see annex). Accordingly, I a m proceeding to
appoint Ms. deGuzman as a judge of the Mechanism with immediate effect.
In accordance with article 10, paragraph 2, of the statute of the Mechanism, the
appointment of Ms. deGuzman is effective for the remainder of the term of office that
Judge Meron would have served, that is, until 30 June 2022.
I should be grateful if you would bring this information to the attention of the
members of the Security Council.
(Signed) António Guterres
