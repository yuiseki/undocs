Letter dated 25 October 2021 from the President of the
Security Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 21 October 2021
(S/2021/895) in connection with Security Council resolution 2559 (2020), adopted on
22 December 2020, in which the Council requested an assessment of lessons learned
from the experience of the African Union-United Nations Hybrid Operation in Darfur
(UNAMID), no later than 31 October 2021, has been brought to the attention of the
members of the Council.
The members of the Council have taken note of the information contained in
your letter that the time frame for the submission of the report will be extended, and
they look forward to its submission by December 2021.
(Signed) Martin Kimani
President of the Security Council
