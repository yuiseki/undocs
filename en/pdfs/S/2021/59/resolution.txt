Letter dated 15 January 2021 from the President of the
Security Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 13 January 2021
(S/2021/58) concerning your intention to appoint Mr. Ján Kubiš (Slovakia) as your
Special Envoy on Libya and Head of the United Nations Support Mission in Libya
has been brought to the attention of the members of the Security Council. They take
note of the intention expressed therein.
(Signed) Tarek Ladeb
President of the Security Council
