Letter dated 1 November 2021 from the Secretary-General
addressed to the President of the Security Council
I have the honour to refer to the United Nations Peacekeeping Force in Cyprus
(UNFICYP), which was established by the Security Council in its resolution 186
(1964). Its mandate was most recently extended in resolution 2587 (2021).
I should like to inform you of my intention to appoint Mr. Colin Stewart
(Canada) as my Special Representative and Head of UNFICYP. He succeeds
Ms. Elizabeth Spehar (Canada), who will complete her assignment on 30 November
2021. I should like to take this opportunity to express my gratitude to Ms. Spehar for
her dedication and her effective leadership of UNFICYP.
I should be grateful if you would bring this matter to the attention of the
members of the Security Council.
(Signed) António Guterres
