Letter dated 11 March 2021 from the Secretary-General addressed
to the President of the Security Council
I have the honour to refer to the United Nations Multidimensional Integrated
Stabilization Mission in Mali (MINUSMA), which was established by the Security
Council in its resolution 2100 (2013) of 25 April 2013 and most recently extended by
the Council in its resolution 2531 (2020) of 29 June 2020.
I should like to inform you of my intention to appoint Mr. El-Ghassim Wane
(Mauritania) as my Special Representative and Head of MINUSMA to succeed
Mr. Annadif Khatir Mahamat Saleh (Chad), who will complete his assignment on
31 March 2021. I would like to take this opportunity to express my gratitude to
Mr. Mahamat Saleh for his dedication and effective leadership of MINUSMA.
I should be grateful if you would bring the present letter to the attention of the
members of the Security Council.
(Signed) António Guterres
