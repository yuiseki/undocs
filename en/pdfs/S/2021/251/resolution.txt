Letter dated 11 March 2021 from the Secretary-General addressed
to the President of the Security Council
I have the honour to refer to Security Council resolution 2562 (2021), adopted
on 11 February 2021, by which the Council decided to extend the mandate of the
Panel of Experts on the Sudan until 12 March 2022.
Accordingly, I wish to inform you that, after consulting with the Security
Council Committee established pursuant to resolution 1591 (2005) concerning the
Sudan, I have appointed the following experts as from 13 March 2021:
Ms. Priscilla Ciesay, international humanitarian law (United Kingdom of Great
Britain and Northern Ireland)
Mr. Nikolai Dobronravin, regional issues (Russian Federation)
Mr. Patrick Loots, arms and armed groups (South Africa)
Mr. Rajeev Yadav, finance (India)
In addition, I have appointed Mr. Vincent Darracq, transport and customs expert
(France), to serve on the Panel of Experts until 22 October 2021, when he will reach
the maximum period of service under the applicable rules and regulations of the
United Nations.
I have designated Ms. Ciesay to serve as Coordinator of the Panel.
I should be grateful if you would bring the present letter to the attention of the
members of the Security Council.
(Signed) António Guterres
