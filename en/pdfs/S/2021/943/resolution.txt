Letter dated 10 November 2021 from the President of the
Security Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 5 November 2021
(S/2021/942) concerning your intention to appoint Major General Cornelis Johannes
Matthijssen (Netherlands) as Force Commander of the United Nations
Multidimensional Integrated Stabilization Mission in Mali has been brought to the
attention of the members of the Security Council. They take note of the intention
expressed therein.
(Signed) Juan Ramón de la Fuente Ramírez
President of the Security Council
