Letter dated 22 July 2021 from the President of the Security Council
addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 14 July 2021 (S/2021/674)
concerning your intention to appoint Ms. Fatimata Sanou Touré (Burkina Faso) as a
judge of the International Residual Mechanism for Criminal Tribunals has been
brought to the attention of the members of the Security Council. They take note of
the intention expressed in your letter.
(Signed) Nicolas de Rivière
President of the Security Council
