Letter dated 4 January 2021 from the President of the
Security Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 30 December 2020
(S/2021/11), concerning your intention to appoint Mr. Volker Perthes (Germany) as
your Special Representative for the Sudan and Head of the United Nations Integrated
Transition Assistance Mission in the Sudan, has been brought to the attention of the
members of the Security Council. They take note of the intention expressed in your
letter.
(Signed) Tarek Ladeb
President of the Security Council
