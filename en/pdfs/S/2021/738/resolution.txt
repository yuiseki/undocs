Letter dated 18 August 2021 from the President of the
Security Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 10 August 2021
(S/2021/737) concerning the financing of the Residual Special Court for Sierra Leone
for 2022 has been brought to the attention of the members of the Security Council.
They take note of the intention expressed in your letter, with the understanding
that the requested subvention will be subsequently reimbursed from the voluntary
contributions received by the Court and that the voluntary nature of the funding
arrangement of the Court will not be changed.
(Signed) T. S. Tirumurti
President of the Security Council
