Letter dated 14 July 2021 from the Secretary-General addressed to
the President of the Security Council
I have the honour to refer to Security Council resolution 1966 (2010), adopted
on 22 December 2010, establishing the International Residual Mechanism for
Criminal Tribunals, and, in particular, to article 10, paragraph 2, of the statute of the
Mechanism (annex 1 to the resolution), on the appointment of a person to fill a
vacancy in the roster of judges of the Mechanism.
Article 8, paragraph 1, of the statute provides that the Mechanism shall have a
roster of 25 independent judges.
One of the judges of the Mechanism, Judge Gberdao Gustave Kam (Burkina Faso)
regrettably died on 17 February 2021, leaving a vacancy in the aforementioned roster.
In that regard, article 10, paragraph 2, of the statute provides that, in the event
of a vacancy in the roster, after consultation with the Presidents of the Security
Council and of the General Assembly, the Secretary-General shall appoint a person
meeting the qualifications of article 9, paragraph 1, of the statut e, for the remainder
of the term of office concerned.
Furthermore, article 9, paragraph 1, of the statute specifies that the judges shall
be persons of high moral character, impartiality and integrity who possess the
qualifications required in their respective countries for appointment to the highest
judicial offices. Particular account shall be taken of experience as judges of the
International Tribunal for the Former Yugoslavia or the International Criminal
Tribunal for Rwanda.
However, the procedure for identifying a candidate for consideration for
appointment under article 10, paragraph 2, which is applicable when a vacancy arises
owing to the death of a judge during his or her term of service, is not prescribed in
the statute.
In accordance with the established practice regarding the appointment of a judge
to fill a vacancy at the International Tribunal for the Former Yugoslavia and the
International Criminal Tribunal for Rwanda, which was adopted for the purposes of
filling vacancies in the roster of judges of the Mechanism in 2016, 2018, 2019 and 2020,
the State of the nationality of the judge to be replaced nominates another candidate.
I am pleased to inform you that the Government of Burkina Faso has nominated
Ms. Fatimata Sanou Touré to replace Judge Kam for the remainder of the term of
office, which ends on 30 June 2022.

3.

Summary of qualifications
I am a magistrate de grade exceptionnel (senior member of the national
judiciary) and hold a master’s degree in private judicial law and a diplôme de
magistrat (judicial service diploma). I completed specialist training in development
law at the International Development Law Organization in Italy. I am also involved
in defending and promoting human rights in Africa. In that connection, I was elected
President of the Association des femmes juristes du Burkina Faso (2013–2017) and
Vice- President of the Fondation pour l’étude et la promotion des droits de l’homme
en Afrique, whose President is Halidou Ouédraogo. I am also a founding member of
the African Women Leaders Network for the Promotion of Sexual and Reproductive
Health and Family Planning. I represent human rights organizations on the Economic
and Social Council of Burkina Faso. I participated in the preparation of the
preliminary draft constitution of the Fifth Republic of Burkina Faso (2017) as a
member of the national constitutional commission representing women’s associations
and as deputy rapporteur of that commission. I have represented the African group on
the Women’s Advisory Council of the Organization of Islamic Cooperation since
November 2019.

4.

of the courts of first instance, of the Commercial Court and of the Labour Court.

Chambers and the issuance of court documents;
– handled contentious and non-contentious applications;
– presided over the President’s Court;
– presided over the First Civil Chamber;
– prepared the decisions rendered.
