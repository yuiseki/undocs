Letter dated 16 December 2021 from the President of the
Security Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 13 December 2021
(S/2021/1064) concerning your intention to appoint Ms. Margaret Anne McAuliffe
deGuzman (United States of America) as a judge of the International Residual
Mechanism for Criminal Tribunals has been brought to the attention of the members
of the Security Council. They take note of the intention expressed in your letter.
(Signed) Abdou Abarry
President of the Security Council
