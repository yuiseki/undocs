Letter dated 5 January 2021 from the Secretary-General
addressed to the President of the Security Council
I have the honour to refer to the United Nations Organization Stabilization
Mission in the Democratic Republic of the Congo (MONUSCO), established by
Security Council resolution 1925 (2010) of 28 May 2010 and most recently extended
by resolution 2556 (2020) of 18 December 2020.
I would like to inform you of my intention to appoint Ms. Bintou Keita of
Guinea as Special Representative for the Democratic Republic of the Congo and Head
of MONUSCO. Ms. Keita will succeed Ms. Leila Zerrougui of Algeria, who will
complete her assignment on 5 February 2021. I should like to take this opportunity to
express my gratitude to Ms. Zerrougui for her dedication to the cause of peace in the
Democratic Republic of the Congo.
I should be grateful if you would bring this matter to the attention of the
members of the Security Council.
(Signed) António Guterres
