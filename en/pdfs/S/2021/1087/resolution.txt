Letter dated 23 December 2021 from the President of the
Security Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 21 December 2021
(S/2021/1086), concerning your intention to appoint Major General (retired) Michael
Beary (Ireland) as Head of the United Nations Mission to Support the Hu daydah
Agreement and Chair of the Redeployment Coordination Committee, has been
brought to the attention of the members of the Security Council. They take note of
the intention expressed in your letter.
(Signed) Abdou Abarry
President of the Security Council
