Letter dated 6 August 2021 from the President of the
Security Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 3 August 2021
(S/2021/719) concerning your recommendation that the mandate of the United
Nations Regional Office for Central Africa be extended for three years, from
1 September 2021 to 31 August 2024, has been brought to the attention of the
members of the Security Council. They take note of the information contained in your
letter and the recommendation expressed therein.
(Signed) T.S. Tirumurti
President of the Security Council
