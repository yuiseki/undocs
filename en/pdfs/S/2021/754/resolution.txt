Letter dated 24 August 2021 from the Secretary-General
addressed to the President of the Security Council
I have the honour to refer to the United Nations Mission for the Referendum in
Western Sahara (MINURSO), established by the Security Council in its re solution
690 (1991) of 29 April 1991 and most recently extended by it in its resolution 2548
(2020) of 30 October 2020.
I would like to inform you of my intention to appoint Mr. Alexander Ivanko
(Russian Federation) as my new Special Representative for We stern Sahara and Head
of MINURSO. He succeeds Mr. Colin Stewart (Canada) who completes his
assignment on 26 August 2021. I would like to take this opportunity to express my
gratitude to Mr. Stewart for his dedication and effective leadership of MINURSO.
I should be grateful if you would bring this matter to the attention of the
members of the Security Council.
(Signed) António Guterres
