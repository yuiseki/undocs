Letter dated 25 October 2021 from the Secretary-General
addressed to the President of the Security Council
I have the honour to refer to the United Nations Truce Supervision Organization
(UNTSO), which was established by the Security Council in its resolution 50 (1948).
I should like to inform you of my intention to appoint Major General Patrick
Gauchat (Switzerland) as the Head of Mission and Chief of Staff of UNTSO. He
succeeds Major General Kristin Lund (Norway), who completed her assignment on
22 October 2019. I should like to take this opportunity to express my gratitude to
Major General Lund for her service.
I should be grateful if you would bring this matter to the attention of the
members of the Security Council.
(Signed) António Guterres
