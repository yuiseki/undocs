Letter dated 26 August 2021 from the President of the
Security Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 24 August 2021
(S/2021/754) concerning your intention to appoint Mr. Alexander Ivanko (Russian
Federation) as your Special Representative for Western Sahara and Head of the United
Nations Mission for the Referendum in Western Sahara (MINURSO) has been
brought to the attention of the members of the Security Council. They take note of
the intention expressed therein.
(Signed) T. S. Tirumurti
President of the Security Council
