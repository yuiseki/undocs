Letter dated 8 April 2021 from the President of the
Security Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 6 April 2021 ( S/2021/339)
concerning your intention to appoint Lieutenant General Marcos De Sá Affonso
Da Costa (Brazil) as your Force Commander of the United Nations Organization
Stabilization Mission in the Democratic Republic of the Congo has been brought to
the attention of the members of the Security Council. They take note of the intention
expressed therein.
(Signed) Dang Dinh Quy
President of the Security Council
