Letter dated 6 October 2021 from the Secretary-General
addressed to the President of the Security Council
I am writing in reference to Security Council resolution 2553 (2020),
unanimously adopted on 3 December 2020, in which the Council requested me to
submit a report in September 2021 on my efforts to strengthen the United Nation s
comprehensive approach to security sector reform.
To this end, the Secretariat has initiated extensive consultations in the
framework of the Inter-Agency Security Sector Reform Task Force, co-chaired by the
Department of Peace Operations and the United Nations Development Programme,
as well as with relevant partners, including regional organizations and international
financial institutions. To further inform my report, the Task Force commissioned an
independent review of peace operations with security sector reform mandates to
reflect on good practices and challenges in operationalizing Security Council
resolution 2151 (2014) on security sector reform. This will create a solid foundation
for the implementation of resolution 2553 (2020), in particular as it pertains to
security sector governance and the political dimension of security sector reform.
In the light of the foregoing, I should like to request that the deadline for the
submission of the report to the Security Council be extended to January 202 2. This
will enable the Secretariat to put forth a credible and effective model for United
Nations engagement on security sector reform, on the basis of lessons learned from
past experiences and ongoing consultations.
I remain grateful to the Security Council for recognizing the role of security
sector reform and governance in numerous country-specific and thematic resolutions.
I should be grateful if you would bring the present letter to the attention of the
members of the Security Council.
(Signed) António Guterres
