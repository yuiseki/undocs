Letter dated 12 January 2021 from the Secretary-General
addressed to the President of the Security Council
Pursuant to paragraphs 41 and 62 of Security Council resolution 2531 (2020), I
have the honour to transmit herewith a note verbale dated 23 December 2020 from
the Permanent Mission of France to the United Nations conveying the report on the
actions carried out by French forces in support of the United Nations
Multidimensional Integrated Stabilization Mission in Mali from 1 September to
1 December 2020 (see annex).
I should be grateful if you would bring the present letter and its annex to the
attention of the members of the Security Council.
(Signed) António Guterres

Enclosure
Report No. 30 on the operational support provided by French
forces to the United Nations Multidimensional Integrated
Stabilization Mission in Mali from 1 September to
1 December 2020
References
• Security Council resolution 2531 (2020) of 29 June 2020 (paragraphs 41 and 62)
• Agreement of 12 July 2013 between the Government of the French Republic
and the United Nations on the provision, on a reimbursable basis, of goods and
services in support of the operations of the United Nations Multidimensional
Integrated Stabilization Mission in Mali (MINUSMA), as amended on 11 July
2018
• Technical cooperation and assistance arrangement between the United Nations
Department of Peacekeeping Operations and the French Ministry of Armed
Forces of 26 March 2015.
The present report contains a summary of the support provided by French forces
to MINUSMA from 1 September to 1 December 2020.
During the period covered, the Barkhane force provided MINUSMA with the
operational support described below.
Operations
• None.
Intelligence
• The Barkhane force provided weekly updates.
Engineering
Aeronautical engineering
• In accordance with the agreement indicated in point (b) of the “References”
section above, and pursuant to the letter of assist between France and
MINUSMA of 2013 (amendment No. 9 of 22 September 2020), the French
aeronautical engineering detachment (25th Air Engineer Regiment) has been
maintaining the Gao airfield in operational condition.
• Pursuant to the letter of assist mentioned above, the 25th Air Engineer Regiment
has also been mentoring the Cambodian detachment of MINUSMA in Tessalit
in relation to the maintenance of unpaved airfields.
• The aeronautical engineering officer at the engineering branch of the joint forces
headquarters has been overseeing a project to construct an unpaved (laterite)
runway in Kidal.
Countering improvised explosive devices
• MINUSMA field headquarters, the United Nations Mine Action Service and the
officer of the Barkhane force responsible for counter-improvised explosive
device (IED) efforts have continued to exchange information on a near-daily
basis.

• Twice a week, the level 2 Counter-IED Exploitation Laboratory has been
alternately organizing and participating in information exchange meetings
attended by counter-IED specialists of the Barkhane force (weapons intelligence
teams and explosive ordnance disposal teams), counter-IED specialists of
MINUSMA in Gao, German weapons intelligence and explosive ordnance
disposal teams, Swedish explosive ordnance disposal teams and personnel from
the United Nations Mine Action Service.
• The Counter-IED Exploitation Laboratory has performed resource analyses of
five incidents involving IEDs for MINUSMA. Eight other incidents identified
at the most recent meeting of the counter-IED community hosted by the German
weapons intelligence team at Camp Castor on 14 November 2020 are currently
being analysed.
• As part of their IED Post-Blast Investigation Level 1 training, 12 Explosive
Threat Mitigation Officers from the United Nations Mine Action Service Mali
attended a presentation by the Counter-IED Exploitation Laboratory on its
activities, capacities and analytical reports.
Logistics
Between 1 September and 1 December 2020, the Barkhane force transported a
total of 251 persons and 9.93 tons of cargo, at part load, on board its aircraft. Non e
of the aircraft were refuelled, however.
Health
The Barkhane force role 1 and 2 medical services are able to support MINUSMA
in the event of an influx of injuries or under exceptional circumstances. Bilateral
agreements allow MINUSMA personnel of certain nationalities to have access to
routine and emergency consultations, paramedical care, dental care and surgical
procedures. Such services were rendered exclusively by the role 2 medical service in
Gao.
