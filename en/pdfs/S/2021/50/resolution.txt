Letter dated 14 January 2021 from the President of the
Security Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 6 January 2021
(S/2021/49) concerning your intention to appoint Nicholas Haysom (South Africa) as
your Special Representative for South Sudan and Head of the United Nations Mission
in South Sudan, has been brought to the attention of the members of the Security
Council. They take note of the intention expressed therein.
(Signed) Tarek Ladeb
President of the Security Council
