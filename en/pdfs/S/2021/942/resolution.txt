Letter dated 5 November 2021 from the Secretary-General
addressed to the President of the Security Council
I have the honour to refer to the United Nations Multidimensional Integrated
Stabilization Mission in Mali (MINUSMA), which was established by the Security
Council in its resolution 2100 (2013). Its mandate was most recently extended in
resolution 2584 (2021).
I should like to inform you of my intention to appoint Major General Cornelis
Johannes Matthijssen (Netherlands) as Force Commander of MINUS MA. Major
General Matthijssen will succeed Lieutenant General Dennis Gyllensporre (Sweden),
who completed his tour of duty on 2 October 2021. I should like to take this
opportunity to express my gratitude to Lieutenant General Gyllensporre for his
contribution to MINUSMA.
I should be grateful if you would bring the present letter to the attention of the
members of the Security Council.
(Signed) António Guterres
