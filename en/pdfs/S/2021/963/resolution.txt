Letter dated 17 November 2021 from the Secretary-General
addressed to the President of the Security Council
I have the honour to refer to the United Nations Interim Administration Mission
in Kosovo (UNMIK), which was established by the Security Council in its resolution
1244 (1999).
I should like to inform you of my intention to appoint Ms. Caroline Ziadeh
(Lebanon) as my Special Representative for Kosovo and Head of UNMIK.
Ms. Ziadeh succeeds Mr. Zahir Tanin (Afghanistan), who completed his assignment
on 4 November 2021. I should like to take this opportunity to express my gratitude to
Mr. Tanin for his dedication and effective leadership of UNMIK.
I should be grateful if you would bring the present letter to the attention of the
members of the Security Council.
(Signed) António Guterres
