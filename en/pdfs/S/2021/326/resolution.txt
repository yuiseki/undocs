Letter dated 1 April 2021 from the President of the
Security Council addressed to the Permanent Representatives
of the members of the Security Council
Viet Nam assumes the presidency of the Security Council for the month of April
2021 in the context of the ongoing coronavirus disease (COVID -19) pandemic.
Restrictions put in place by the authorities of the State and city of New York are still
being imposed.
Taking into consideration the evolution of the pandemic and on the basis of the
recent practices established by the Security Council, the presidency en visages that the
majority of discussions of the Security Council during the month will take place
virtually. For such videoconferences, the presidency will continue to implement the
agreed working methods set out in the annex to the letter dated 7 May 202 0 from the
President of the Security Council addressed to the Permanent Representatives of the
members of the Security Council (S/2020/372).
In-person meetings at United Nations Headquarters may be held on the basis of
consensus of the members of the Security Council and will be conducted in
compliance with the guidelines and measures set out in the annexes to the letters from
the President of the Security Council for, respectively, July and October 2020 on
working methods (S/2020/639 and S/2020/966), with particular emphasis on the
critical importance of fully observing public health guidelines, including wearing a
mask at all times, including while delivering remarks.
In its efforts to enhance efficiency and transparency during this unprecedented
time, the presidency also encourages the implementation of the notes by the President
of the Security Council on working methods, which include the note by the President
dated 30 August 2017 (S/2017/507).
The present methods and modalities of work are intended onl y to respond to the
extraordinary circumstances related to the ongoing COVID-19 pandemic and will not
set a precedent for the future. They may be revised, adapted or suspended, subject to
the agreement of all members of the Security Council.
(Signed) Dang Dinh Quy
President of the Security Council
