Letter dated 12 October 2021 from the President of the
Security Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 6 October 2021
(S/2021/870), in connection with Security Council resolution 2553 (2020), in which
the Council requested you to submit a report in September 2021 on your efforts to
strengthen the United Nations comprehensive approach to security sector reform, has
been brought to the attention of the members of the Council.
They have taken note of the request contained in your letter that the time frame
for the submission of the report be extended and they look forward to the report’s
submission by January 2022.

(Signed) Martin Kimani
President of the Security Council
