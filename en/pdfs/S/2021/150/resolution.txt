Letter dated 16 February 2021 from the President of the
Security Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 10 February 2021
(S/2021/149) has been brought to the attention of the members of the Security
Council. In that letter, you expressed your intention to extend the mandate of the
Special Tribunal for Lebanon (Special Tribunal) from 1 March 2021 for a further
period of two years, or until the completion of the cases before the Special Tribunal,
if sooner, or the exhaustion of available funds, if sooner. In your letter, you also
informed the Council of your intention to request, as a temporary measure, a
subvention from the General Assembly for 2021. The members of the Council have
taken note of your intention to extend the mandate of the Special Tribunal.
Bearing in mind that a subvention would be a temporary exceptional measure to
address the current financial situation of the Special Tribunal, the members of the
Council have also taken note of your intention to request the subvention from the
General Assembly for 2021 to cover the anticipated shortfall in contributions from
Lebanon, as well as from the usual donors of the Special Tribunal, with the
understanding that the subvention will be subsequently reimbursed from the voluntary
contributions received by the Special Tribunal, the applicable reporting requirements
to the Assembly will be complied with and the voluntary nature of the funding
arrangement of the Special Tribunal will not be changed.
The members of the Council stress that contributions from Lebanon, as well as
from the donors, should remain a major source of funding for the Special Tribunal
and that additional efforts should be made to avoid reliance on the subvention.
(Signed) Barbara Woodward
President of the Security Council
