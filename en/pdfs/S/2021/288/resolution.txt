Letter dated 19 March 2021 from the Secretary-General addressed
to the President of the Security Council
I have the honour to refer to the United Nations Peacekeeping Force in Cyprus
(UNFICYP), established pursuant to Security Council resolution 186 (1964) of
4 March 1964 and most recently extended pursuant to Council resolution 2561 (2021)
of 29 January 2021.
I would like to inform you of my intention to appoint Major General Ingrid
Gjerde (Norway) as the Force Commander of UNFICYP. She will succeed Major
General Cheryl Pearce (Australia), who completed her assignment on 7 January 2021.
I would like to take this opportunity to express my gratitude to Major General Pearce
for her dedication and effective leadership.
I would be grateful if you could bring this matter to the attention of the members
of the Security Council.
(Signed) António Guterres
