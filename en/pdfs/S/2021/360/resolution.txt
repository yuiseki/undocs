Annex
During a closed videoconference held on 27 July 2020, the Working Group on
Children and Armed Conflict examined the second report of the Secretary-General on
children and armed conflict in Nigeria (S/2020/652), which covers the period from
January 2017 to December 2019 and was introduced by your Special Representative
for Children and Armed Conflict. The Chargé d’affaires a.i. of the Permanent Mission
of Nigeria to the United Nations also addressed the Working Group. The Working
Group adopted its conclusions on children and armed conflict in Nigeria
(S/AC.51/2020/8) on 1 December 2020.
In follow up to the recommendations of the Working Group and subject to and
consistent with applicable international law and relevant Security Council
resolutions, including resolutions 1612 (2005), 1882 (2009), 1998 (2011), 2068
(2012), 2143 (2014), 2225 (2015) and 2427 (2018), I am entrusted, in my capacity as
Chair of the Working Group, to:
(a) Request you to ensure the continued effectiveness of the monitoring and
reporting mechanism on children and armed conflict in Nigeria;
(b) Request you to ensure that the United Nations country task force on
monitoring and reporting continues its ongoing work and advocacy for the release
and reintegration of children associated with armed groups and armed forces and to
continue engagement with the Civilian Joint Task Force to fully implement its action
plan and facilitate the release of any remaining associated children;
(c) Note the negative impact of conflict on children in regions of countries
bordering north-east Nigeria, and invite you in that regard to report on relevant
developments, as appropriate, through the regular reporting to provide a more
comprehensive picture of the situation of children affected by the conflict.
(Signed) Mona Juul
Chair
Working Group on Children and Armed Conflict
