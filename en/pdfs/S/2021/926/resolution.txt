Letter dated 4 November 2021 from the President of the
Security Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 1 November 2021
(S/2021/925) concerning your intention to appoint Mr. Colin Stewart (Canada) as
your Special Representative and Head of the United Nations Peacekeeping Force in
Cyprus has been brought to the attention of the members of the Security Council.
They take note of the intention expressed therein.
(Signed) Juan Ramón de la Fuente Ramírez
President of the Security Council
