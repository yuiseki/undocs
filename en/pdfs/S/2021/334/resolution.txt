Letter dated 6 April 2021 from the Secretary-General addressed to
the President of the Security Council
I have the honour to refer to Security Council resolution 2564 (2021), adopted
on 25 February 2021, by which the Council decided to extend the mandate of the
Panel of Experts on Yemen until 28 March 2022.
Accordingly, I wish to inform you that, after consulting with the Security
Council Committee established pursuant to resolution 2140 (2014), I have appointed
the following experts:
Ms. Dakshinie Ruwanthika Gunaratne, armed groups (Sri Lanka)
Mr. Wolf-Christian Paes, arms (Germany)
Mr. Henry Thompson, regional (United Kingdom of Great Britain and Northern
Ireland)
Ms. Marie-Louise Tougas, international humanitarian law (Canada)
I have designated Ms. Gunaratne to serve as Coordinator of the Panel. One
additional expert will be appointed shortly.
I should be grateful if you would bring this matter to the attention of the
members of the Security Council.
(Signed) António Guterres
