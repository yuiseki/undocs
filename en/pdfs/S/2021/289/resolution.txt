Letter dated 23 March 2021 from the President of the
Security Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 19 March 2021
(S/2021/288), concerning your intention to appoint Major General Ingrid Gjerde
(Norway) as your Force Commander of the United Nations Peacekeeping Force in
Cyprus, has been brought to the attention of the members of the Security Council.
They take note of the intention expressed therein.
(Signed) Linda Thomas-Greenfield
President of the Security Council
