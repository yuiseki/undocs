Letter dated 15 March 2021 from the President of the
Security Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 11 March 2021
(S/2021/261) concerning your intention to appoint Mr. El-Ghassim Wane
(Mauritania) as your Special Representative and Head of the United Nations
Multidimensional Integrated Stabilization Mission in Mali has been brought to the
attention of the members of the Security Council. They take note of the intention
expressed therein.
(Signed) Linda Thomas-Greenfield
President of the Security Council
