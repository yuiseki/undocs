Letter dated 18 March 2021 from the President of the
Security Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 16 March 2021
(S/2021/275) concerning your intention to appoint Mr. Annadif Khatir Mahamat
Saleh (Chad) as your Special Representative for West Africa and the Sahel and Head
of the United Nations Office for West Africa and the Sahel (UNOWAS) has been
brought to the attention of the members of the Security Council. They take note of
the intention expressed therein.
(Signed) Linda Thomas-Greenfield
President of the Security Council
