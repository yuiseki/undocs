Letter dated 6 January 2021 from the President of the
Security Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to refer to Security Council resolution 1646 (2005), adopted
on 20 December 2005, in which the Council, in keeping with its resolution 1645
(2005), adopted on the same date, decided that the permanent members listed in
Article 23, paragraph 1, of the Charter of the United N ations should be members of
the Organizational Committee of the Peacebuilding Commission and that, in addition,
the Council should annually select two of its elected members to participate in the
Organizational Committee.
I therefore have the honour to inform you that, following informal consultations,
the members of the Security Council agreed on the selection of Kenya and Saint
Vincent and the Grenadines as the two elected members of the Council to participate
in the Organizational Committee for a term of one year, until the end of 2021.
(Signed) Tarek Ladeb
President of the Security Council
