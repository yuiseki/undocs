Letter dated 5 January 2021 from the President of the
Security Council addressed to the Permanent Representatives of
the members of the Security Council
Tunisia assumes the presidency of the Security Council on 1 January 2021 in a
context still marked by the restrictions imposed by the authorities of the State and the
City of New York on public and private meetings in connection with the persist ent
coronavirus disease (COVID-19) pandemic.
Against that backdrop, and based on recent practices and modalities developed
by the Security Council to return to United Nations Headquarters, a combination of
informal videoconferences and in-person meetings will continue to be envisaged by
the Security Council in January 2021.
Considering that the members of the Security Council are in agreement that the
decision on convening in-person Council meetings at Headquarters rests with the
Council, and with further guidance from the United Nations medical services, inperson meetings will be held during January as and when it is deemed appropriate
and upon agreement by all Council members.
In-person meetings in the Security Council Chamber or in the Economic and
Social Council Chamber will be held in strict compliance with the health protocols, the
guidelines for physical distancing and the measures laid out in the annexes to the letters
from the President of the Security Council for, respectively, July and October 2020
on working methods (S/2020/639 and S/2020/966), with particular emphasis on the
critical importance of wearing a mask at all times, including while delivering remarks.
For the informal videoconferences of the Security Council, the presidency will
extend and implement the working methods set out in the letter dated 7 May 2020
from the President of the Security Council addressed to the Permanent
Representatives of the members of the Security Council ( S/2020/372).
Tunisia recalls all the letters from the President of the Security C ouncil issued
during the COVID-19 pandemic from March to December 2020 on the issue of
working methods (S/2020/253, S/2020/273, S/2020/372, S/2020/490, S/2020/639,
S/2020/778, S/2020/877, S/2020/966, S/2020/1077 and S/2020/1163) and will
continue to draw on the wisdom contained therein.
The current methods and modalities of work are intended only to respond to the
extraordinary circumstances related to the ongoing COVID-19 pandemic and will not
set a precedent for the future. They will be evaluated at the end of January and m ay
be revised, adapted or suspended, subject to the agreement of all members of the
Security Council.
(Signed) Tarek Ladeb
President of the Security Council
