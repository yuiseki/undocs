Letter dated 29 September 2021 from the Secretary-General
addressed to the President of the Security Council
I am writing pursuant to Security Council resolution 2568 (2021) of 12 March
2021, whereby the Security Council requested me, jointly with the African Union,
and in consultation with the Federal Government of Somalia and donors, to produce
a proposal on the strategic objectives, size and composition of a reconfigured African
Union mission by the end of September 2021. In its resolution, the Council also
requested me to present options for continuing United Nations logistical support from
2022, to include support for the United Nations Assistance Mission in Somalia and to
the Somali security forces alongside a reconfigured African Union mission, by the
end of October 2021.
Since the adoption of the resolution, a series of consultations have taken plac e
at various levels among relevant stakeholders towards fulfilling this request. The
consultations have involved the African Union, the European Union, the Federal
Government of Somalia and the United Nations, among others. Discussions remain
ongoing, including between the African Union and the Federal Government of
Somalia, and require additional time to be completed, prior to developing a final
agreed proposal.
Accordingly, a joint United Nations-African Union proposal on which the
Federal Government of Somalia and partners have been fully consulted should be
submitted to the Security Council by the end of the year.
I would be grateful if you could bring this matter to the attention of the members
of the Security Council.
(Signed) António Guterres
