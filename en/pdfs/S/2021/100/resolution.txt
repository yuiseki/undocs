Letter dated 30 January 2021 from the President of the
Security Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to refer to a letter dated 13 January 2021 from the President
of Colombia, Iván Duque Márquez, by which he requested the Security Council to
add to the mandate of the United Nations Verification Mission in Colombia the task
of supporting the Special Jurisdiction for Peace in verifying compliance with
sentences that the Special Jurisdiction would impose.
In its resolution 2545 (2020), adopted on 25 September 2020, the Security
Council expressed its readiness to consider the addition of that task to the mandate of
the Mission, based on the outcome of the consultation process coordinated by the
Government of Colombia.
In the light of the letter dated 13 January from the President of Colombia, sent
on behalf of the Government of Colombia and with the agreement of the Fuerza
Alternativa Revolucionaria del Común party, the members of the Security Council
request you to provide for their consideration, by 26 February 2021, detailed
recommendations on how that additional task would be carried out and any
implications for the configuration of the Mission.
(Signed) Tarek Ladeb
President of the Security Council
