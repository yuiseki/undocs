Letter dated 30 December 2020 from the Secretary-General
addressed to the President of the Security Council
I have the honour to refer to the United Nations Integrated Transition Assistance
Mission in the Sudan (UNITAMS), which was established by the Security Council in
its resolution 2524 (2020) on 3 June 2020.
In that regard, I should like to inform you of m y intention to appoint Mr. Volker
Perthes (Germany) as my Special Representative for the Sudan and Head of
UNITAMS.
I should be grateful if you would bring this matter to the attention of the
members of the Security Council.
(Signed) António Guterres
