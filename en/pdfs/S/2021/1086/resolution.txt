Letter dated 21 December 2021 from the Secretary-General
addressed to the President of the Security Council
I have the honour to refer to the mandate of the United Nations Mission to
Support the Hudaydah Agreement (UNMHA), established by the Security Council in
its resolution 2452 (2019) and recently extended by its resolution 2586 (2021).
I wish to inform you of my intention to appoint Major General (retired) Michael
Beary (Ireland) as Head of UNMHA and Chair of the Redeployment Coordination
Committee. Major General Beary will replace Lieutenant General (retired) Abhijit
Guha (India), who completed his mission on 2 October 2021. I should like to take this
opportunity to thank Lieutenant General Guha for his dedication and effective
leadership of UNMHA.
I should be grateful if you would transmit the present letter to the members of
the Security Council.
António Guterres
