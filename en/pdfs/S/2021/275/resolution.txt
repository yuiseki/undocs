Letter dated 16 March 2021 from the Secretary-General addressed
to the President of the Security Council
I have the honour to refer to the United Nations Office for West Africa and the
Sahel (UNOWAS), established on 28 January 2016 through an exchange of letters
between the Secretary-General and the President of the Security Council (S/2016/88,
S/2016/89), and its mandate, which was most recently extended from 1 February 2020
to 31 January 2023 through another exchange of letters between the Secretary General and the President of the Security Council ( S/2019/1009, S/2020/85).
I would like to inform you of my intention to appoint Mr. Annadif Khatir
Mahamat Saleh (Chad) as my Special Representative for West Afri ca and the Sahel
and Head of UNOWAS to succeed Mr. Mohamed Ibn Chambas (Ghana), who will
complete his assignment on 7 April 2021.
I would like to take this opportunity to express my gratitude to Mr. Chambas for
his dedication and effective leadership of UNOWAS.
I should be grateful if you could bring the present letter to the attention of the
members of the Security Council.
(Signed) António Guterres
