The Permanent Mission of France to the United Nations presents its
compliments to the Office of the Secretary-General of the United Nations and has the

United Nations Secretariat could bring the present report to the attention of the
members of the Security Council.

Enclosure
Report No. 31 on the operational support provided by French
forces to the United Nations Multidimensional Integrated
Stabilization Mission in Mali from 1 December 2020 to
1 March 2021
References
• Security Council resolution 2531 (2020) of 29 June 2020 (paragraphs 41 and 62).
• Agreement of 12 July 2013 (as amended on 11 July 2018) between the
Government of the French Republic and the United Nations on the provision,
on a reimbursable basis, of goods and services in support of the operations of
the United Nations Multidimensional Integrated Stabilization Mission in Mali
(MINUSMA).
• Technical cooperation and assistance arrangement between the United Nations
Department of Peacekeeping Operations and the French Ministry of Armed
Forces of 26 March 2015.
The thirty-first report contains a summary of the support provided by French
forces to MINUSMA from 1 December 2020 to 1 March 2021.
The Barkhane force provided MINUSMA with the following operational
support during the period covered by the present report:
Operations
None.
Intelligence
In the area of intelligence, during the period under review the Barkhane force
provided the following through FRANIC Bamako:
• Twelve weekly status updates
• Twenty-five special intelligence reports transmitted to MINUSMA
• Nine reports from the Counter-Improvised Explosive Device Exploitation
Laboratory
Military engineering
Aeronautical engineering
• In accordance with the agreement indicated in point b of the “References”
section above, and pursuant to letter of assist France-MINUSMA-2013-12
amendment No. 9 of 22 September 2020, the French aeronautical engineering
detachment (25th Air Engineer Regiment) maintained the Gao (Mali) airfield in
operational condition.
• Pursuant to the letter of assist mentioned above, the 25th Air Engineer Regiment
also mentored the Cambodian detachment of MINUSMA in Tessalit (Mali) in
relation to the maintenance of unpaved airfields.
• The aeronautical engineering officer at the engineering branch of the joint forces
headquarters oversaw a project to construct an unpaved (laterite) runway in
Kidal (Mali) until the end of January 2021.

• The airfield engineering detachment that worked on the construction of the
unpaved runway in Kidal had fully disengaged by the end of January 2021. The

Countering improvised explosive devices
• MINUSMA headquarters, the United Nations Mine Action Service and the
officer of the Barkhane force responsible for efforts to counter-improvised
explosive devices continue to exchange information on a near-daily basis.
• The officer of the Barkhane force responsible for efforts to counter-improvised
explosive devices participated in the first working group to counter improvised
explosive devices, which was held in Bamako on the occasion of the last operational
coordination meeting. In addition, a monthly video teleconference is held between
that officer and the counter-improvised explosive device section of the U2 cell of
MINUSMA headquarters and the United Nations Mine Action Service.
• Owing to the reinforced measures to counter the coronavirus disease (COVID-19),
no more meetings are being held in Gao and no more resources are being accepted
for tests by the Counter-Improvised Explosive Device Exploitation Laboratory.
These exchanges should nevertheless resume in the next few weeks. On 3
December 2020, 12 Explosive Threat Mitigation Officers taking a post-blast
investigation level-1 training course with the United Nations Mine Action Service
in Mali attended a presentation by the Counter-Improvised Explosive Device
Exploitation Laboratory on its activities, capacities and analytical reports.
Logistics
Since 1 December 2020, the Barkhane force has transported a total of 185
persons and 8.22 tons of cargo, at part load, on board its aircraft. None of the aircraft
were refuelled, however.
Health
The Barkhane force role 1 and 2 medical services are able to support MINUSMA
in the event of an influx of injuries or under exceptional circumstances. Bilateral
agreements allow MINUSMA personnel of certain nationalities to have access to
routine and emergency consultations, paramedical care, dental care and surgical
procedures. Such services were rendered exclusively by role 2 in Gao.
