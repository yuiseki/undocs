Letter dated 6 April 2021 from the Secretary-General addressed to
the President of the Security Council
I have the honour to refer to the United Nations Organization Stabilization
Mission in the Democratic Republic of the Congo (MONUSCO), established by
Security Council resolution 1925 (2010) of 28 May 2010, and most recently extended
by resolution 2556 (2020) of 18 December 2020.
I would like to inform you of my intention to appoint Lieutenant General Marcos
De Sá Affonso Da Costa (Brazil) as Force Commander of MONUSCO. He succeeds
Lieutenant General Ricardo Augusto Ferreira Costa Neves (Brazil) who com pleted
his assignment on 31 March 2021. I would like to take this opportunity to express my
gratitude to Lieutenant General Costa Neves for his dedication to MONUSCO.
I would be grateful if you could bring this matter to the attention of the members
of the Security Council.
(Signed) António Guterres
