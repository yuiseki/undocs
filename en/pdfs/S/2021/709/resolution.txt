Letter dated 4 August 2021 from the President of the
Security Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 2 August 2021
(S/2021/708) concerning your intention to appoint Mr. Hans Grundberg (Sweden) as
your Special Envoy for Yemen, at the level of Under-Secretary-General, has been
brought to the attention of the members of the Security Council. They take note of
the intention expressed therein.
(Signed) T. S. Tirumurti
President of the Security Council
