Letter dated 9 March 2021 from the President of the
Security Council addressed to the Permanent Representatives of
the members of the Security Council
The United States assumed the presidency of the Security Council on 1 March
2021 in the context of the ongoing coronavirus disease (COVID -19) pandemic. The
United States plans to hold Council discussions during the month of March 2021
virtually. Video teleconferences will be used to implement the working methods set
out in the annex to the letter dated 7 May 2020 from the President of the Security
Council addressed to the Permanent Representatives of the members of the Security
Council (S/2020/372).
These working methods and guidelines do not set a precedent and are a response
to the current exceptional circumstances during the COVID -19 pandemic. They may
be reviewed, adjusted or discontinued, subject to the agreement of all members of the
Council.
(Signed) Linda Thomas-Greenfield
President of the Security Council
