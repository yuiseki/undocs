Letter dated 5 October 2021 from the President of the
Security Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 1 October 2021
(S/2021/851) concerning your intention to appoint Mr. Staffan de Mistura as your
Personal Envoy for Western Sahara has been brought to the attention of the members
of the Security Council. They take note of the intention expressed in your letter.
(Signed) Martin Kimani
President of the Security Council
