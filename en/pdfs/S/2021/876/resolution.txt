Letter dated 13 October 2021 from the Secretary-General
addressed to the President of the Security Council
I have the honour to transmit herewith a note verbale dated 30 September 2021
from the Permanent Mission of France to the United Nations forwarding two reports
on the activities carried out by French forces in support of the United Nations
Multidimensional Integrated Stabilization Mission in Mali, covering the periods from
1 March to 1 June 2021 and from 1 June to 1 September 2021, respectively (see
annex).
I should be grateful if you would bring the present letter and its annex to the
attention of the members of the Security Council.
(Signed) António Guterres

Annex
[Original: French]
The Permanent Mission of France to the United Nations presents its
compliments to the Executive Office of the Secretary-General and has the honour to
transmit the following information:
Pursuant to paragraphs 41 and 62 of Security Council resolution 2531 (2020)
and paragraphs 43 and 61 of resolution 2584 (2021), France is enclosing herewith the
reports on the actions carried out by French forces in support of the United Nations
Multidimensional Integrated Stabilization Mission in Mali from 1 March to 1 June
2021 and from 1 June to 1 September 2021 (see enclosure).
The Permanent Mission of France to the United Nations should be grateful if
the United Nations Secretariat would bring the present reports to the attention of the
members of the Security Council.

None
Intelligence

• Twelve special intelligence reports transmitted to MINUSMA;
• Eleven reports from the Counter-Improvised Explosive Device Exploitation

• Eleven reports from the weapons intelligence team;
• Twelve other documents of interest.
Engineering
Aeronautical engineering
None
Countering improvised explosive devices
• MINUSMA field headquarters, the United Nations Mine Action Service and the
officer of the Barkhane force responsible for efforts to counter improvised
explosive devices continued to exchange information on a near-daily basis.
• The weapons intelligence team conducted post-blast analyses of nine incidents
involving improvised explosive devices for MINUSMA.
• The Counter-Improvised Explosive Device Exploitation Laboratory performed
resource analyses of six incidents involving improvised explosive devices for
MINUSMA.

Report No. 33 on the operational support provided by French
forces to the United Nations Multidimensional Integrated
Stabilization Mission in Mali from 1 June to 1 September 2021
References
• Security Council resolution 2584 (2021) of 29 June 2021 (paras. 43 and 61).
• Security Council resolution 2531 (2020) of 29 June 2020 (paras. 41 and 62).
• Agreement of 12 July 2013 (as amended on 11 July 2018) between the
Government of the French Republic and the United Nations on the provision,
on a reimbursable basis, of goods and services in support of the operations of
the United Nations Multidimensional Integrated Stabilization Mission in Mali
(MINUSMA).
• Technical cooperation and assistance arrangement between the United Nations
Department of Peacekeeping Operations and the French Ministry of Armed
Forces of 26 March 2015.
The thirty-third report contains a summary of the support provided by French
forces to MINUSMA from 1 June to 1 September 2021.
The Barkhane force provided MINUSMA with the following operational
support during the period covered by the present report:
Operations
Air-land operations
• On 29 August, following an official request from MINUSMA, Barkhane
transported a team of six MINUSMA personnel from Niamey to Dori in a CAS A
CN235 aircraft.
Air operations
• As part of a reassurance mission, intelligence, surveillance and reconnaissance
and fighter support was provided at the Aguelhok camp, which had been
subjected to harassment.
• Four Bateleur missions (fighter reassurance patrols) were carried out for the
benefit of the Ansongo camp.
• Barkhane transported a team of six MINUSMA personnel to Dori by tactical
transport aircraft as part of its monitoring of the Mission’s support to the Joint
Force of the Group of Five for the Sahel.
Intelligence
Barkhane provided the following documents through FRANIC Bamako:
• Nine weekly status updates;
• Ten special intelligence reports transmitted to MINUSMA;
• Nine reports from the Counter-Improvised Explosive Device Exploitation
Laboratory;
• Twenty-five reports from the weapons intelligence team;
• Four other documents of interest.
