Letter dated 12 January 2021 from the President of the
Security Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 5 January 2021
(S/2021/34) concerning your intention to appoint Ms. Bintou Keita (Guinea) as your
Special Representative for the Democratic Republic of the Congo and Head of the
United Nations Organization Stabilization Mission in the Democratic Republic of the
Congo has been brought to the attention of the members of the Security Council. They
take note of the intention expressed therein.
(Signed) Tarek Ladeb
President of the Security Council
