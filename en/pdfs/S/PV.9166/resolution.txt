Official communiqué of the 9166th (closed) meeting of the
Security Council
Held in private at Headquarters, New York, on Wednesday,
26 October 2022, at 3 p.m.
In accordance with rule 55 of the provisional rules of procedure of the Security
Council, the following communiqué was issued through the Secretary -General in
place of a verbatim record:
“At its 9166th meeting, held in private on 26 October 2022, the Security
Council considered the item entitled “Briefing by the President of the
International Court of Justice”.
“The President of the Security Council invited Judge Joan E. Donoghue,
President of the International Court of Justice, to participate in the meeting in
accordance with rule 39 of the Council’s provisional rules of procedure.
“The members of the Council heard a briefing by Judge Donoghue.
“The members of the Council and Judge Donoghue had an exchange of
views.”
