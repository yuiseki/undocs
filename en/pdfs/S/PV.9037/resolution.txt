Consideration of the draft report of the
Security Council to the General Assembly
The President: The Security Council will now
begin its consideration of the item on its agenda.
The Security Council will proceed to the
consideration of its annual report to the General
Assembly for the period from 1 January 2021 to
31 December 2021.
The draft report has been provided to the members
of the Council. The introduction to the draft report
was prepared by the delegation of France, with the
participation of the other members of the Council. The
body of the report was prepared by the Secretariat. I
would like to express my appreciation to France and the
Secretariat for their respective contributions.
I would also like to point out that the draft report was
prepared in accordance with the notes by the President
of 30 August 2017 (S/2017/507) and 27 December 2019
(S/2019/997).
I now give the floor to the representative of France,
whose delegation was responsible for drafting the
introduction to this year’s annual report.
Mr. De Rivière (France) (spoke in French): As
President of the Security Council during the month of
July 2021, France drafted the introduction to this year’s
annual report on the activities of the Council in 2021.
Thanks to the constructive commitment of all
of us, the text was agreed by the January deadline. I
would especially like to thank the elected members
of the Council who were involved in the preparation
of this report but whose mandate ended in December
2021. I also want to thank the Secretariat for its
support, particularly regarding the numerous statistics
provided by the Security Council Affairs Division on
the activities of the Council.
The drafting of the annual report is not only a
mandatory task enshrined in the Charter of the United
Nations, but it is above all an important document for
reviewing the activities of the Security Council during
the previous year and opening a discussion with all
Member States on how the Council has implemented
its mandate. That discussion will take place as soon as
next month in the General Assembly.

As the annual report shows, 2021 was marked by
the coronavirus disease pandemic, first as reflected
in the crises on the Council’s agenda but also in terms
of our working methods, which remained hybrid, with
many meetings held by vide-teleconference. In the
circumstances, I am pleased that the Council was able
to conduct a mission to the Sahel in the autumn. It is
essential to maintain contact with realities on the ground,
and I hope that other field missions can be organized in
the coming months. Also with regard to our working
methods, I would like to point to the importance of
multilingualism, which has suffered greatly during the
pandemic. It is essential to ensure that all the work of
the Council, including its subsidiary bodies and Arria
Formula meetings, benefits from interpretation into the
six official languages of the United Nations. We will
continue to be vigilant about that.
In 2021, the Security Council once again fulfilled
its mandate to maintain international peace and
security by seeking to be ever more inclusive and
productive. I am pleased that we have seen increasing
numbers of women addressing the Security Council,
but there is still a long way to go to achieve parity. The
participation of civil society in our meetings has also
increased significantly, and we value its contribution
to our work.
The Security Council remains above all a
decision-making body. We must never lose sight of that.
In 2021, we adopted 24 presidential statements and 57
resolutions, most of them unanimously. Needless to
say, there are important areas of disagreement among
Council members. That was the case last year, and
it clearly remains the case today. But our collective
responsibility is to show a spirit of compromise and
to scrupulously respect the letter and the spirit of the
Charter. I hope that spirit of compromise will prevail,
because the stakes in terms of peace and security
are enormous.
The President: I thank the representative of
France for his statement and his team for their efforts
in preparing the report.
It is my understanding that the Council is ready to
proceed to the adoption of the draft annual report.
May I take it that the draft report is adopted by
the Council?
There being no objection, it is so decided.
This decision will be reflected in a note by the
President of the Security Council, to be issued as
document S/2022/403.
The meeting rose at 10.10 a.m.
