Letter dated 26 January 2022 from the Secretary-General
addressed to the President of the Security Council
I have the honour to refer to the United Nations Interim Security Force for Abyei
(UNISFA), established by the Security Council in its resolution 1990 (2011) of
27 June 2011 and whose mandate was recently extended by resolution 2609 (2021) of
15 December 2021.
I should like to inform you of my intention to appoint Major General Benjamin
Olufemi Sawyerr (Nigeria) as Force Commander of UNISFA. He succeeds Major
General Kefyalew Amde Tessema (Ethiopia). I should like to take this opportunity to
express my gratitude to Major General Tessema for his dedication and effective
leadership of UNISFA.
I should be grateful if you would bring this matter to th e attention of the
members of the Security Council.
(Signed) António Guterres
