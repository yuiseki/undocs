Letter dated 16 February 2022 from the Secretary-General
addressed to the President of the Security Council
I have the honour to refer to the United Nations Office to the African Union,
which was established on 1 July 2010 pursuant to General Assembly re solution
64/288.
I should like to inform you of my intention to appoint Mr. Parfait
Onanga-Anyanga (Gabon) as my Special Representative to the African Union and
Head of the United Nations Office to the African Union at the level of Under Secretary-General. He will succeed Ms. Hanna Serwaa Tetteh (Ghana). I should like
to take this opportunity to express my gratitude to Ms. Tetteh for her dedicated service
to the Organization and her effective leadership of the Office.
I should be grateful if you would bring this matter to the attention of the
members of the Security Council.
(Signed) António Guterres
