Letter dated 30 November 2022 from the President of the
Security Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 10 August 2022
(S/2022/900) concerning your intention to appoint Ms. Natalia Gherman (Republic of
Moldova) as the Executive Director of the Counter-Terrorism Committee Executive
Directorate has been brought to the attention of the members of the Security Council.
They take note of the intention expressed in your letter, bearing in mind the
report of the Counter-Terrorism Committee on its revitalization (S/2004/124), which
was endorsed by the Security Council in its resolution 1535 (2004).
(Signed) Harold Agyeman
President of the Security Council
