Letter dated 29 June 2022 from the Secretary-General addressed
to the President of the Security Council
I have the honour to refer to the United Nations Mission in South Sudan
(UNMISS), established by the Security Council in its resolution 1996 (2011) and most
recently extended by it in its resolution 2625 (2022).
I would like to inform you of my intention to appoint Lieutenant General Mohan
Subramanian (India) as Force Commander of UNMISS. He will succeed Lieutenant
General Shailesh Tinaikar (India), who completed his assignment on 31 January 2022.
I would like to take this opportunity to express my gratitude to Lieutenant General
Shailesh Tinaikar for his dedication and effective leadership as Force Commander of
UNMISS.
I would be grateful if you could bring this matter to the attention of the members
of the Security Council.
(Signed) António Guterres
