Letter dated 17 January 2022 from the President of the
Security Council addressed to the Permanent Representatives of
the members of the Security Council
Norway assumed the presidency of the Security Council on 1 January 2022.
While alert to the ongoing coronavirus disease (COVID-19) pandemic situation
in New York City, the presidency strives to continue to maintain, to the extent
possible, the regular conduct of business of the Security Council, as foreseen by the
Charter of the United Nations, the provisional rules of procedure and the note by the
President of the Security Council dated 30 August 2017 (S/2017/507), as well as other
relevant notes by the President on working methods.
The presidency plans to hold meetings of the Security Council in person in the
Council Chamber. The presidency strongly encourages the wearing of a mask at all
times, including while delivering remarks; delegations to limit their representation to
1+1, with +1s to be seated in a socially distanced manner; and the vaccination of all
delegates and Secretariat staff attending meetings in the Chamber.
If the COVID-19 pandemic situation deteriorates during the month of January,
then the Council may consider reverting to the working methods outlined in the letter
dated 7 May 2020 from the President of the Security Council addressed to the
Permanent Representatives of the members of the Security Council ( S/2020/372).
Any Member State that participates in a Security Council meeting in accordance
with rules 37 and 39 of the provisional rules of procedure will also observe the above
enhanced safety measures, with participation limited to one representative in the
Chamber. In addition, Member States that are not members of the Council will
continue to be able to participate in open debates through the submission of written
statements.
In order to enhance efficiency and transparency during this extraordinary period,
the presidency encourages the continued full implementation of presidential note
S/2017/507, as well as other relevant notes by the President of the Security Council
on working methods.
The present working methods and guidelines are intended to address exceptional
circumstances. They are not intended to set a precedent for the future and may be
reviewed, adapted or discontinued, subject to the agreement of members of the
Security Council.
(Signed) Mona Juul
President of the Security Council
