Letter dated 17 May 2022 from the Secretary-General addressed
to the President of the Security Council
I have the honour to refer to Security Council resolution 2624 (2022), adopted
on 28 February 2022, by which the Council decided to extend the mandate of the
Panel of Experts on Yemen until 28 March 2023, and requested me to take the
necessary administrative measures to that effect.
Accordingly, I wish to inform you that, after consulting with the Security
Council Committee established pursuant to resolution 2140 (2014), I have appointed
the following experts:
Mr. Debi Prasad Dash, finance (India)
Mr. Cornelius Nagbe, international humanitarian law (Liberia)
Mr. Wolf-Christian Paes, arms (Germany)
I have designated Mr. Dash to serve as the Coordinator of the Panel of Experts.
The expert on armed groups and the expert on regional matters will be appointed
as soon as possible.
I should be grateful if you could bring this matter to the attention of the members
of the Security Council.
(Signed) António Guterres
