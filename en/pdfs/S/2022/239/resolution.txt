Letter dated 13 March 2022 from the Secretary-General addressed
to the President of the Security Council
I have the honour to refer to Security Council resolution 2620 (2022), adopted
on 15 February 2022, by which the Council decided to extend the mandate of the
Panel of Experts on the Sudan until 12 March 2023.
Accordingly, I wish to inform you that, after consulting with the Security
Council Committee established pursuant to resolution 1591 (2005) concerning the
Sudan, I have appointed the following experts:
Ms. Laura Victoria Bernal Moncada, armed groups (Colombia)
Mr. Nikolai Dobronravin, regional issues (Russian Federation)
Ms. Rania El Rajji, international humanitarian law (Lebanon)
Mr. Patrick Loots, arms (South Africa)
Mr. Rajeev Yadav, finance (India)
I have designated Ms. Bernal Moncada to serve as Coordinator of the Panel.
I should be grateful if you would bring the present letter to the attention of the
members of the Security Council.
(Signed) António Guterres
