Letter dated 5 October 2022 from the Secretary-General addressed
to the President of the Security Council
I have the honour to refer to Security Council resolution 2624 (2022), adopted
on 28 February 2022, by which the Council decided to extend the mandate of the
Panel of Experts on Yemen until 28 March 2023 and requested me to take the
necessary administrative measures to that effect.
I wish to inform you that, after consulting the Security Council Committee
established pursuant to resolution 2140 (2014), I have appointed the following expert:
Ms. Lydelle Joubert, armed groups (South Africa)
The expert on regional matters will be appointed as soon as possible.
I should be grateful if you would bring this matter to the attention of the
members of the Security Council.
(Signed) António Guterres
