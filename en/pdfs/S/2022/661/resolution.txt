Letter dated 31 August 2022 from the President of the
Security Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 16 August 2022
(S/2022/660) concerning your intention to seek a subvention from the General
Assembly for the Special Tribunal for Lebanon for 2023 has been brought to the
attention of the members of the Security Council.
Bearing in mind that a subvention would be a temporary exceptional measure to
address the current financial situation of the Special Tribunal, the members of the
Council have taken note of your intention to request the subventio n from the General
Assembly for 2023 to cover the anticipated shortfall in contributions from Lebanon,
as well as from the usual donors of the Special Tribunal, with the understanding that
the subvention will be subsequently reimbursed from the voluntary c ontributions
received by the Special Tribunal, the applicable reporting requirements to the
Assembly will be complied with and the voluntary nature of the funding arrangement
of the Special Tribunal will not be changed.
The members of the Council stress that contributions from Lebanon, as well as
from the donors, should remain a major source of funding for the Special Tribunal
and that additional efforts should be made to avoid reliance on the subvention.
(Signed) Zhang Jun
President of the Security Council
