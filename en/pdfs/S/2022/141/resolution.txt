Letter dated 16 February 2022 from the Secretary-General
addressed to the President of the Security Council
I have the honour to refer to the exchange of letters on 24 and 31 October 2018
(S/2018/955 and S/2018/979) regarding the expansion of the remit of the Special
Envoy for the Sudan and South Sudan to cover the Horn of Africa re gion.
I should like to inform you of my intention to appoint Ms. Hanna Serwaa Tetteh
(Ghana) as my Special Envoy for the Horn of Africa. She will succeed Mr. Parfait
Onanga-Anyanga (Gabon). I should like to take this opportunity to express my
gratitude to Mr. Onanga-Anyanga for his dedication and effective leadership.
I should be grateful if you would bring this matter to the attention of the
members of the Security Council.
(Signed) António Guterres
