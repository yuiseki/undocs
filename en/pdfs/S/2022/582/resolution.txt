Letter dated 28 July 2022 from the Secretary-General addressed to
the President of the Security Council
I have the honour to refer to the United Nations Regional Office for Central
Africa (UNOCA), established following an exchange of letters between the Secretary General and the President of the Security Council in 2009 and 2010 (S/2009/697 and
S/2010/457), the mandate of which was most recently renewed in 2021 following
another such exchange of letters (S/2021/719 and S/2021/720).
I wish to inform you that, following informal consultations with the members
of the Security Council, I have decided to appoint Mr. Abdou Abarry (Niger) as my
Special Representative for Central Africa and Head of UNOCA.
Mr. Abarry will succeed Mr. François Louncény Fall (Guinea), who completed
his mission on 31 May 2022. I would like to take this opportunity to thank Mr. Fall
for his dedication and effective leadership of UNOCA.
I should be grateful if you would circulate the present letter to the members of
the Security Council for their information.
(Signed) António Guterres
