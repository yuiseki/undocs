Letter dated 2 February 2022 from the Secretary-General
addressed to the President of the Security Council
I have the honour to refer to the United Nations Interim Force in Lebanon
(UNIFIL), which was established by the Security Council in its resolutions 425 (1978)
and 426 (1978). Its mandate was subsequently modified in resolution 1701 (2006)
and most recently extended in resolution 2591 (2021).
I should like to inform you of my intention to appoint Major General Aroldo
Lázaro Sáenz (Spain) as Head of Mission and Force Commander of UNIFIL. He will
succeed Major General Stefano Del Col (Italy), who will complete his assignment
shortly. I should like to take this opportunity to express my gratitude to Major General
Del Col for his dedication and effective leadership of UNIFIL.
I should be grateful if you would bring this matter to the attention of the
members of the Security Council.
(Signed) António Guterres
