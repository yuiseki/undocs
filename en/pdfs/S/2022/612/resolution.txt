Letter dated 8 August 2022 from the Secretary-General addressed
to the President of the Security Council
I have the honour to refer to the United Nations Military Observer Group in
India and Pakistan (UNMOGIP) and to Security Council resolution 307 (1971),
adopted on 21 December 1971.
I should like to inform you of my intention to appoint Rear Admiral Guillermo
Pablo Ríos (Argentina) as Head of Mission and Chief Military Observer of
UNMOGIP. He succeeds Major General José Eladio Alcaín (Uruguay), who will
shortly complete his assignment. I should like to take this opportunity to express my
gratitude to Major General Alcaín for his dedication and effective leadership of
UNMOGIP.
I should be grateful if you would bring this matter to the attention of the
members of the Security Council.
(Signed) António Guterres
