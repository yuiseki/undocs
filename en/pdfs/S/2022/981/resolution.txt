Letter dated 16 December 2022 from the Secretary-General
addressed to the President of the Security Council
I have the honour to refer to Security Council resolution 2662 (2022), adopted
on 17 November 2022, by which the Council decided to extend the mandate of the
Panel of Experts on Somalia until 15 December 2023 and requested me to take the
necessary administrative measures to that effect.
Accordingly, I wish to inform you that, after consulting with the Security Council
Committee established pursuant to resolution 751 (1992) concerning Al-Shabaab, I
have appointed the following experts as from 16 December 2022:
Mr. Nikhil Acharya, arms (India)
Ms. Stella Attakpah, finance (Ghana)
Mr. John Peter Hazenberg, armed groups/natural resources (United States of
America)
Mr. Ahmed Himmiche, maritime/regional (Morocco)
Mr. Matthew Rosbottom, armed groups (United Kingdom of Great Britain and
Northern Ireland)
I have also designated Mr. Himmiche to serve as Coordinator of the Panel of
Experts.
The humanitarian expert will be appointed as soon as possible.
I would be grateful if you could bring this matter to the attention of the members
of the Security Council.
(Signed) António Guterres
