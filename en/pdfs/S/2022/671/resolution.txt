Letter dated 31 August 2022 from the Secretary-General
addressed to the President of the Security Council
I have the honour to refer to the United Nations Assistance Mission in
Afghanistan (UNAMA), established by Security Council resolution 1401 (2002) of
28 March 2002. The mandate of UNAMA is defined in Security Council resolutions
1401 (2002) and 1662 (2006) and was renewed by its resolutions 1746 (2007), 1806
(2008), 1868 (2009), 1917 (2010), 1974 (2011), 2041 (2012), 2096 (2013), 2145
(2014), 2210 (2015), 2274 (2016), 2344 (2017), 2405 (2018), 2460 (2019), 2489
(2019) and 2543 (2020). On 17 March 2022, the mandate was adapted and extended
until 17 March 2023 by resolution 2626 (2022).
I would like to inform you of my intention to appoint Ms. Roza Otunbayeva
(Kyrgyzstan) as my Special Representative for Afghanistan and Head of UNAMA.
Ms. Otunbayeva will succeed Ms. Deborah Lyons (Canada).
I would like to take this opportunity to express my gratitude to Ms. Lyons for
her dedication and effective leadership of UNAMA during a period o f critical
developments and challenges.
I should be grateful if you would bring this matter to the attention of the
members of the Security Council.
(Signed) António Guterres
