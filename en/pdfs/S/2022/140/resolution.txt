Letter dated 21 February 2022 from the President of the
Security Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 16 February 2022
(S/2022/139) concerning your intention to appoint Mr. Parfait Onanga-Anyanga
(Gabon) as your Special Representative to the African Union and Head of the United
Nations Office to the African Union, at the level of Under-Secretary-General, has
been brought to the attention of the members of the Security Council. They take note
of the intention expressed therein.
(Signed) Vassily Nebenzia
President of the Security Council
