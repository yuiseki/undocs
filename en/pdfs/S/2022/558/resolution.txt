Letter dated 7 July 2022 from the Secretary-General addressed to
the President of the Security Council
I have the honour to refer to the United Nations Disengagement Observer Force
(UNDOF), which was established by the Security Council in its resolution 350 (1974).
Its mandate was extended most recently in resolution 2639 (2022).
I should like to inform you of my intention to appoint Major General Nirmal
Kumar Thapa (Nepal) as Head of Mission and Force Commander of UNDOF. He will
succeed Lieutenant General Ishwar Hamal (Nepal), who will be completing his
assignment shortly. I should like to take this opportunity to express my gratitude to
Lieutenant General Hamal for his service.
I should be grateful if you would bring this matter to the attention of the
members of the Security Council.
(Signed) António Guterres
