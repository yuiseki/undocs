Letter dated 1 June 2022 from the Secretary-General addressed to
the President of the Security Council
I have the honour to refer to Security Council resolution 2588 (2021), adopted
on 29 July 2021, by which the Council decided to extend the mandate of the Panel of
Experts on the Central African Republic until 31 August 2022, and requested me to
take the necessary administrative measures to that effect.
Accordingly, I wish to inform you that, after consulting with the Security
Council Committee established pursuant to resolution 2127 (2013) concerning the
Central African Republic, I have appointed the following additional expert to serve
on the Panel:
Mr. Ruben de Koning, natural resources/finance (Netherlands)
The expert on armed groups will be appointed as soon as possible.
I would be grateful if you could bring the present letter to the attention of the
members of the Security Council.
(Signed) António Guterres
