I have the honour to transmit herewith a note verbale dated 11 April 2022 from

activities carried out by French forces in support of the United Nations
Multidimensional Integrated Stabilization Mission in Mali from 1 January to

I should be grateful if you would bring the present letter and its annex to the
attention of the members of the Security Council.

Annex
[Original: French]
The Permanent Mission of France to the United Nations presents its
compliments to the Executive Office of the Secretary-General and has the honour to
transmit the following information.
Pursuant to paragraphs 43 and 61 of Security Council resolution 2584 (2021),
France is enclosing herewith the report on the activities carried out by French forces
in support of the United Nations Multidimensional Integrated Stabilization Mission
in Mali (MINUSMA) from 1 January 2022 to 31 March 2022 (see enclosure).
The Permanent Mission of France to the United Nations should be grateful if
the United Nations Secretariat would bring the report to the attention of the members
of the Security Council.

The thirty-fifth report contains a summary of the support provided by French
forces to MINUSMA from 1 January to 31 March 2022.
The Barkhane force provided MINUSMA with the following operational
support during the period covered by the present report:
Operations
Air-land
During the quarter, the Barkhane force transported 127 individuals using aircraft
under its operational control, mostly between Gao and Niamey.
Air
Support to MINUSMA was provided by fighter aircraft flying over its bases,
with radio contact.
