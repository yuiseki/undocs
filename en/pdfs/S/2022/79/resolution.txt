Letter dated 31 January 2022 from the President of the
Security Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 26 January 2022
(S/2022/78) concerning your intention to appoint Major General Benjamin Olufemi
Sawyerr (Nigeria) as Force Commander of the United Nations Interim Security Force
for Abyei has been brought to the attention of the members of the Security Council.
They take note of the intention expressed therein.
(Signed) Mona Juul
President of the Security Council
