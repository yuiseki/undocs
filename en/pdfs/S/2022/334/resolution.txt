Letter dated 11 April 2022 from the Secretary-General addressed
to the President of the Security Council
I am writing with reference to Security Council resolution 2600 (2021), by
which the Council requested that I conduct an assessment of the mandate of the United
Nations Integrated Office in Haiti and convey the findings no later than 15 April 2022.
In this regard, I wish to request an extension of the deadline to 29 April 2022. This
will enable us to finalize consultations on the recommendations.
I would appreciate your bringing the present letter to the attention of the
members of the Security Council and remain grateful to its members for the
importance attributed to United Nations efforts in Haiti.
(Signed) António Guterres
