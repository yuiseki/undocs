Letter dated 22 February 2022 from the President of the
Security Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 17 February 2022
(S/2022/149) concerning your intention to appoint Ms. Valentine Rugwabiza
(Rwanda) as your Special Representative and Head of the United Nations
Multidimensional Integrated Stabilization Mission in the Central African Republic
has been brought to the attention of the members of the Security Council. They take
note of the intention expressed therein.
(Signed) Vassily Nebenzia
President of the Security Council
