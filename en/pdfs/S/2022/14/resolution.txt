Letter dated 6 January 2022 from the Secretary-General
addressed to the President of the Security Council
I have the honour to refer to Security Council resolution 2590 (2021), adopted
on 30 August 2021, by which the Council decided to extend the mandate of the Panel
of Experts on Mali until 30 September 2022 and requested me to take the necessary
administrative measures to that effect.
Accordingly, I wish to inform you that, after consulting with the Security
Council Committee established pursuant to resolution 2374 (2017) concerning Mali,
I have appointed the following experts:
Ms. Lipika Majumdar Roy Choudhury, finance (India)
Mr. Albert Barume Kwokwo, regional (Democratic Republic of the Congo)
Ms. Fatma Hussein Abdelkhalek Saber, humanitarian affairs (Egypt)
Mr. Aurélien Llorca, armed groups (France)
I have designated Ms. Choudhury to serve as the Coordinator of the Panel.
I should be grateful if you would bring this matter to the attention of the
members of the Security Council.
(Signed) António Guterres
