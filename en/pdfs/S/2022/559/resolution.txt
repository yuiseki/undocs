Letter dated 12 July 2022 from the President of the
Security Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 7 July 2022 (S/2022/558)
concerning your intention to appoint Major General Nirmal Kumar Thapa (Nepal) as
Head of Mission and Force Commander of the United Nations Disengagement
Observer Force has been brought to the attention of the members of the Security
Council. They take note of the intention expressed therein.
(Signed) Ronaldo Costa Filho
President of the Security Council
