Letter dated 17 March 2022 from the Secretary-General addressed
to the President of the Security Council
Further to a communication from the Registrar of the International Court of
Justice, and pursuant to paragraph 2 of Article 41 of the Statute of the Court, I have
the honour to transmit herewith the order indicating provision al measures in the case
concerning Allegations of Genocide under the Convention on the Prevention and
Punishment of the Crime of Genocide (Ukraine v. Russian Federation) (see annex).*
(Signed) António Guterres

* The annex is being circulated in the languages of submission only.
