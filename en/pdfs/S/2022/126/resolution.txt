Letter dated 16 February 2022 from the President of the
Security Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 14 February 2022
(S/2022/125), regarding the update on the report on efforts to strengthen the United
Nations comprehensive approach to security sector reform, pursuant to Security
Council resolution 2553 (2020), has been brought to the attention of the members of
the Security Council.
The members of the Council have taken note of the request contained in your
letter that the time frame for the submission of the report be extended and they look
forward to the submission of the report by March 2022.
(Signed) Vassily Nebenzia
President of the Security Council
