Letter dated 31 August 2022 from the Secretary-General addressed
to the President of the Security Council
I have the honour to refer to the United Nations Support Mission in Libya
(UNSMIL), which was established by the Security Council in its resolution 2009
(2011) of 16 September 2011. Since then, the mandate of UNSMIL has been extended
and modified in resolutions 2040 (2012), 2095 (2013), 2144 (2014), 2208 (2015),
2213 (2015), 2273 (2016), 2291 (2016), 2323 (2016), 2376 (2017), 2434 (2018), 2486
(2019), 2509 (2020), 2510 (2020), 2526 (2020), 2542 (2020), 2570 (2021), 2629
(2022) and 2647 (2022), the most recent of which extended the mandate until
31 October 2022.
I would like to inform you of my intention to appoint Abdoulaye Bathily
(Senegal) as my Special Representative for Libya and Head of UNSMIL. He succeeds
Ján Kubiš (Slovakia), who previously served as my Special Envoy on Libya and Head
of UNSMIL. I would like to take this opportunity to express my gratitude to Mr. Kubiš
for his service to the Organization.
I should be grateful if you would bring this matter to the attention of the
members of the Security Council.
(Signed) António Guterres
