Letter dated 21 February 2022 from the President of the
Security Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 16 February 2022
(S/2022/141) concerning your intention to appoint Ms. Hanna Serwaa Tetteh (Ghana)
as your Special Envoy for the Horn of Africa has been brought to the attention of the
members of the Security Council. They take note of the intention expressed therein.
(Signed) Vassily Nebenzia
President of the Security Council
