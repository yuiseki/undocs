Letter dated 14 February 2022 from the Secretary-General
addressed to the President of the Security Council
I am writing to provide an update regarding the report on my efforts to
strengthen the United Nations comprehensive approach to security sector reform,
pursuant to Security Council resolution 2553 (2020).
The Inter-Agency Security Sector Reform Task Force, co-chaired by the
Department of Peace Operations and the United Nations Development Programme,
has incurred delays in the completion of the report. The independent review of peace
operations with security sector reform mandates provided useful insights on good
practices and challenges in operationalizing Security Council resolution 2151 (2014)
on security sector reform. The Task Force deployed every effort to create a consistent
and reliable framework for consultations and to standardize some of the findings of
the independent review, throughout the report on security sector reform requested by
the Security Council.
However, the independent review was delivered with a delay of two months and
an outbreak of the coronavirus disease (COVID-19) within the core drafting team has
severely disrupted the finalization of the report. Therefore, I wish to request another
extension of the deadline for the submission of the report to the Security Council,
until March 2022.
I remain grateful to the Security Council for recognizing the role of security
sector reform and governance in numerous country-specific and thematic resolutions.
I would appreciate your bringing the present letter to the attention of the
members of the Security Council.
(Signed) António Guterres
