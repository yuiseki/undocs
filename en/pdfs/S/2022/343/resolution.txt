Letter dated 21 April 2022 from the President of the Security Council
addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 7 April 2022 (S/2022/300),
in which you updated me on the steps taken by the United Nations to address the
grave threat posed by the Safer oil tanker to the Red Sea and beyond, has been brought
to the attention of the members of the Security Council. The members appreciate the
information provided and welcome your initiative, and the United Nations operational
plan, to urgently address this potential humanitarian, environmental and ecological
disaster before it strikes.
The Security Council, in its deliberations, has indeed repeatedly underscored
the urgency of the matter and has sought to facilitate efforts by the United Nations to
assist in addressing this threat. The members welcome being kep t abreast of ongoing
developments regarding the implementation of the operational plan. They are aware
that, as you state in the letter, the plan envisages the provision of a replacement export
capacity equivalent to the Safer oil tanker within 18 months of the necessary funds
being mobilized and secured and the procurement contract being signed.
The members also underline the necessity of the Houthis’ fulfilling their
obligations in accordance with the memorandum of understanding signed on 5 March,
in order to promptly address the threat posed by the Safer.
The members express their desire for the speedy implementation of the
operational plan. In this regard, they recognize the budgetary requirements set out in
your letter and underscore their desire for all Member States and other potential
donors to fully fund the operational plan, including at the pledging conference in May.
(Signed) Barbara Woodward
President of the Security Council
