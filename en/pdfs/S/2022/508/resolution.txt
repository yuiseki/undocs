Letter dated 22 June 2022 from the Secretary-General addressed
to the President of the Security Council
I have the honour to refer to Security Council resolution 2633 (2022), adopted
on 26 May 2022, by which the Council decided to extend the mandate of the Panel of
Experts on South Sudan until 1 July 2023.
Accordingly, I wish to inform you that, after consulting with the Security
Council Committee established pursuant to resolution 2206 (2015) concerning South
Sudan, I have appointed the following experts:
Mr. Mayank Bubna, humanitarian affairs (India)
Ms. Ivonne Gallegos Palafox, finance (Mexico)
Mr. Michael Gibb, natural resources (Norway)
Mr. Thato Ramoeletsi, arms (Lesotho)
Ms. Valerie Yankey-Wayne, armed groups/regional issues (Ghana)
I have designated Mr. Gibb to serve as Coordinator of the Panel of Experts.
I should be grateful if you would bring this matter to the attention of the
members of the Security Council.
(Signed) António Guterres
