Letter dated 10 August 2022 from the Secretary-General addressed
to the President of the Security Council
I have the honour to refer to the Counter-Terrorism Committee Executive
Directorate, which was established pursuant to Security Council resolution 1535
(2004). Its mandate was extended until 31 December 2025 by the Council in its
resolution 2617 (2021).
I wish to inform you of my intention to appoint Ms. Natalia Gherman (Republic
of Moldova) as the Executive Director of the Counter-Terrorism Committee
Executive Directorate. Ms. Gherman will succeed Ms. Michèle Coninsx (Belgium),
whose appointment ended on 31 December 2021. I should like to take this opportunity
to express my gratitude to Ms. Coninsx for her dedication and effective leadership.
I should be grateful if you would bring the present letter to the attention of the
members of the Security Council.
(Signed) António Guterres
