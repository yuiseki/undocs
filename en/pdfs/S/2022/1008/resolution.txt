Letter dated 28 December 2022 from the Secretary-General
addressed to the President of the Security Council
I have the honour to refer to Security Council resolution 2648 (2022), adopted
on 29 July 2022, by which the Council decided to extend the mandate of the Panel of
Experts on the Central African Republic until 31 August 2023 and requested me to
take the necessary administrative measures to that effect.
Accordingly, I wish to inform you that, after consulting with the Security
Council Committee established pursuant to resolution 2127 (2013) concerning the
Central African Republic, I have appointed an additional expert – Ms. Mariam Yazdani,
regional affairs (Pakistan) – to serve on the Panel.
I would be grateful if you could bring this matter to the attention of the members
of the Security Council.
(Signed) António Guterres
