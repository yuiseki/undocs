Annex
[Original: French]
The Permanent Mission of France to the United Nations presents its
compliments to the Executive Office of the Secretary-General and has the honour to
transmit the following information.
Pursuant to paragraphs 43 and 61 of Security Council resolution 2584 (2021),
France is enclosing herewith the report on the activities carried out by French forces
in support of the United Nations Multidimensional Integrated Stabilization Mission
in Mali from 1 September 2021 to 1 January 2022 (see enclosure).
The Permanent Mission of France should be grateful if the United Nations
Secretariat would bring the report to the attention of the members of the Security
Council.

Enclosure
Report No. 34 on the operational support provided by French forces
to the United Nations Multidimensional Integrated Stabilization
Mission in Mali from 1 September 2021 to 1 January 2022
References
• Security Council resolution 2584 (2021) of 29 June 2021 (paras. 43 and 61).
• Agreement of 12 July 2013 (as amended on 11 July 2018) between the
Government of the French Republic and the United Nations on the provision,
on a reimbursable basis, of goods and services in support of the operations of
the United Nations Multidimensional Integrated Stabilization Mission in Mali
(MINUSMA).
• Technical cooperation and assistance arrangement between the United Nations
Department of Peacekeeping Operations and the French Ministry of Armed
Forces of 26 March 2015.
The thirty-fourth report contains a summary of the support provided by French
forces to MINUSMA from 1 September 2021 to 1 January 2022.
The Barkhane force provided MINUSMA with the following operational
support during the period covered by the present report:
Operations
Air-land
• On 29 August 2021, one person was transported from Niamey to Dori and then
from Dori to Niamey in a CASA CN-235 (1 hour 40 minutes).
• On 2 December 2021, three persons were transported from Niamey to Gao in a
C-130 (1 hour).
• On 4 December 2021, three persons were transported from Ménaka to Gao in a
Mi-8 (1 hour 20 minutes).
• On 27 December 2021, two persons were transported from G ao to Ménaka in a
CASA CN-235.
• On 28 December 2021, one person was transported from Gao to Ménaka in a
CASA CN-235.

Intelligence
Barkhane provided the following documents through FRANIC Bamako during
the period under review:
• Twelve weekly status updates.
• Twelve special intelligence reports transmitted to MINUSMA.
• Eight reports from the Counter-Improvised Explosive Device Exploitation
Laboratory.
• Twenty-seven reports from the weapons intelligence team.
• Four other documents of interest.
Engineering
• Maintenance in operational condition of the airstrip (unpaved runway) in Gao.
• Daily monitoring and regular maintenance of the paved runway in Gao.
• Since 1 September 2021, mentoring of the Cambodian detachment in Tessalit is
no longer included in the letter of assist.
