Letter dated 5 July 2022 from the President of the
Security Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 29 June 2022 ( S/2022/536)
concerning your intention to appoint Lieutenant General Mohan Subramanian (India)
as Force Commander of the United Nations Mission in South Sudan has been brought
to the attention of the members of the Security Council. They take note of the
intention expressed therein.
(Signed) Ronaldo Costa Filho
President of the Security Council
