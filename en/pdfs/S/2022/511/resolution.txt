Letter dated 22 June 2022 from the Secretary-General addressed
to the President of the Security Council
I have the honour to refer to your letter dated 15 June 2022 (S/2022/487) in
which you concurred with my proposal to reappoint the 25 judges of the International
Residual Mechanism for Criminal Tribunals.
I have received a corresponding letter from the President of the General
Assembly (see annex I). Accordingly, I am proceeding with the reappointment of the
25 judges of the Mechanism. A list of the judges is set out in annex II to the present
letter.
In accordance with paragraph 2 of Security Council resolution 2269 (2016),
adopted on 29 February 2016, the judges will be reappointed for a two -year term
commencing on 1 July 2022 and expiring on 30 June 2024.
I will also proceed to appoint Judge Graciela Susana Gatti Santana (Uruguay)
as President of the Mechanism from 1 July 2022 to 30 June 2024.
I should be grateful if you would bring the present letter and its annexes to the
attention of the members of the Security Council.
(Signed) António Guterres

Annex I
Letter dated 17 June 2022 from the President of the
General Assembly addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to refer to your letter dated 10 June 2022 ( S/2022/486)
concerning the nomination of 25 judges of the International Residual Mechanism for
Criminal Tribunals for reappointment for a term of two years, from 1 July 2022 to
30 June 2024.
Pursuant to article 10, paragraph 2, of the statute of the Mechanism and in
accordance with article 9, paragraph 1, of the statute, I have reviewed the
qualifications of the judges and am pleased to inform you that I hereby concur with
the proposed reappointments.
(Signed) Abdulla Shahid
