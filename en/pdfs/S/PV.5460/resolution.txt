Official communiqué of the 5460th (closed) meeting
of the Security Council
Held in private at Headquarters, New York, on Wednesday,
14 June 2006, at 3.45 p.m.
In accordance with rule 55 of the provisional rules of procedure of the Security
Council, the following communiqué was issued through the Secretary-General in
place of a verbatim record:
“At its 5460th meeting, held in private on 14 June 2006, the Security
Council considered the item entitled ‘Reports of the Secretary-General on the
Sudan’.
“In accordance with the decision taken at the 5459th meeting held earlier
on 14 June 2006, the President extended an invitation under rule 39 of the
Council’s provisional rules of procedure to Mr. Luis Moreno-Ocampo,
Prosecutor of the International Criminal Court.
“The members of the Council and Mr. Luis Moreno-Ocampo had an
exchange of views.”
