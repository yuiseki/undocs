Official communiqué of the 8815th (closed) meeting of the
Security Council
Held in private at Headquarters, New York, on Thursday,
8 July 2021, at 11.20 a.m.
In accordance with rule 55 of the provisional rules of procedure of the Security
Council, the following communiqué was issued through the Secretary-General in
place of a verbatim record:
“At its 8815th meeting, held in private on 8 July 2021, the Security
Council considered the item entitled “The question concerning Haiti”.
“The President invited the representative of Haiti to participate in the
meeting in accordance with rule 37 of the Council’s provisional rules of
procedure.
“The President invited Ms. Helen La Lime, Special Representative of the
Secretary-General for Haiti and Head of the United Nations Integrated Office in
Haiti, to participate in the meeting in accordance with rule 39 of the Council’s
provisional rules of procedure.
“The Council heard a briefing by Ms. La Lime.
“Statements were made by the representatives of the United States,
Mexico, Saint Vincent and the Grenadines, Ireland, Niger (speaking on behalf
of the A3+1: Niger, Kenya, Tunisia and Saint Vincent and the Grenadines) ,
Estonia, the United Kingdom, India, Viet Nam, China, Norway, Russian
Federation and by the President, speaking in his capacity as the representative
of France.
“The Council heard a statement by the representative of Haiti.
“Ms. La Lime responded to comments and questions raised.”
