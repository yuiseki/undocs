Official communiqué of the 8959th (closed) meeting of the
Security Council
Held in private at Headquarters, New York, on Friday, 28 January
2022, at 10 a.m.
In accordance with rule 55 of the provisional rules of procedure of the Security
Council, the following communiqué was issued through the Secretary -General in
place of a verbatim record:
“At its 8959th meeting, held in private on 28 January 2022, the Security
Council considered the item entitled ‘The situation in Myanmar ’.
“The President invited the representative of Cambodia to participate in the
meeting in accordance with rule 37 of the Council’s provisional rules of
procedure.
“The President invited Ms. Noeleen Heyzer, Special Envoy of the
Secretary-General for Myanmar; and Mr. Ramesh Rajasingham, Assistant
Secretary-General for Humanitarian Affairs and Deputy Emergency Relief
Coordinator, ad interim, to participate in the meeting in accorda nce with rule 39
of the Council’s provisional rules of procedure.
“The Council heard briefings by His Excellency Mr. Prak Sokhonn,
Deputy Prime Minister and Minister for Foreign Affairs of Cambodia, in his
capacity as Special Envoy of the Association of Southeast Asian Nations Chair
on Myanmar, Ms. Heyzer; and Mr. Rajasingham.
“Statements were made by the representatives of the United Kingdom,
Gabon, Ghana, Albania, Mexico, Kenya, China, France, the Russian Federation,
the United Arab Emirates, India, Brazil, the United States, Ireland and the
President of the Security Council, speaking in her capacity as the representative
of Norway.
“His Excellency Mr. Pram Sokhonn, Ms. Heyzer, and Mr. Rajasingham
responded to comments and questions raised.”
