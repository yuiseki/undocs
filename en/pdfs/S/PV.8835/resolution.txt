Official communiqué of the 8835th (closed) meeting of the
Security Council
Held in private at Headquarters, New York, on Tuesday,
17 August 2021, at 10.00 a.m.
In accordance with rule 55 of the provisional rules of procedure of the Security
Council, the following communiqué was issued through the Secretary -General in
place of a verbatim record:
“At its 8835th meeting, held in private on 17 August 2 021, the Security
Council considered the item entitled “The situation in Myanmar”.
“The President invited the representative of Brunei Darussalam to
participate in the meeting in accordance with rule 37 of the Council’s
provisional rules of procedure.
“The President invited the following briefers to participate in the meeting
in accordance with rule 39 of the Council’s provisional rules of procedure:
Ms. Christine Schraner Burgener, Special Envoy of the Secretary-General for
Myanmar; and Mr. Ramesh Rajasingham, Acting Assistant Secretary-General
for Humanitarian Affairs and Deputy Emergency Relief Coordinator.
“The Council heard briefings by His Excellency Dato Erywan Pehin Yusof,
Foreign Minister II of Brunei Darussalam, in his capacity as Special Envoy of
the ASEAN Chair on Myanmar; Ms. Schraner Burgener; and Mr. Rajasingham.
“Statements were made by the representatives of the United Kingdom,
China, Ireland, the United States, Kenya (speaking on behalf of the three African
members of the Security Council: Kenya, Niger, and Tunisia; and Saint Vincent and
the Grenadines), France, Estonia, Norway, the Russian Federation, Viet Nam,
Mexico and by the President, speaking in his capacity as the representative of India.
“His Excellency Dato Pehin Yusof, Ms. Schraner Burgener, and
Mr. Rajasingham responded to comments and questions raised.”
