Consideration of the draft report of the Security
Council to the General Assembly
The President (spoke in Spanish): The Security
Council will now proceed to the consideration of its
annual report to the General Assembly, the draft of
which has been prepared by the Secretariat.
Members of the Council have received copies of
the draft report of the Security Council to the General
Assembly for the period from 1 August 2003 to 31 July
2004, as circulated by the Assistant Secretary-General
for Political Affairs on 31 August 2004, and a
corrigendum thereto.
I should like to express appreciation to the
Secretariat for the work it has undertaken in preparing
this factual report and in a timely manner.
I now call on Mr. Kalomoh, Assistant SecretaryGeneral for Political Affairs, to make an explanatory
statement.
Mr. Kalomoh (Assistant Secretary-General for
Political Affairs): It gives me great pleasure to present
to the Council the draft annual report of the Security
Council for the period 1 August 2003 to 31 July 2004,
to be submitted to the General Assembly pursuant to
Article 24, paragraph 3, of the United Nations Charter,
which has been prepared by the Secretariat in line with

2

the revised format agreed upon by the Security Council
in 2002.
The format of the draft report before the Council
provides a guide to the activities of the Council in a
concise manner. In this connection, I should like to
note that this is the third report prepared in accordance
with the provisions of the note by the President of the
Security Council of 22 May 2002 (S/2002/199), which
were aimed at enhancing the quality of the report and
accommodating the views expressed in the previous
format.
Of particular interest is the introduction to the
report, which contains an analytical summary of the
work of the Council for the period covered by the
report.
A corrigendum containing changes made by some
Council members is also before the Council.
I believe and hope that Council members will
find the report helpful.
The President (spoke in Spanish): May I take it
that the draft report, as corrected, is adopted by the
Council?
There being no objection, it is so decided.
This decision will be reflected in a note by the
President of the Security Council, to be issued as
document S/2004/743.
The Security Council has thus concluded its
consideration of the item on its agenda.
The meeting rose at 10.20 a.m.
