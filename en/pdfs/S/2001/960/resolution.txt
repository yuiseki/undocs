Letter dated 5 October 2001 from the Secretary-General
addressed to the President of the Security Council
I wish to refer to my report to the Security Council on Guinea-Bissau of 27
September 2001 (S/2001/915), which focused on the latest developments in that
country and on the contributions of the United Nations Peace-building Support
Office in Guinea-Bissau (UNOGBIS) to the country’s peace consolidation efforts.
In that report, I stated that despite some positive developments, the overall
situation in Guinea-Bissau remained dangerously unstable, and I called for the
critical support of the international community.
The authorities of Guinea-Bissau, in recent discussions with my
Representative, have commended the work of UNOGBIS and have requested the
extension of its mandate until the next legislative and presidential elections.
In that light, I propose that the mandate of UNOGBIS, which expires on 31
December 2001, be extended until 31 December 2002.
I should be grateful if you would bring the present letter to the attention of
members of the Security Council.
(Signed) Kofi A. Annan
