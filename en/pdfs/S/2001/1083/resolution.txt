Letter dated 16 November 2001 from the President of the Security
Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 13 November 2001
(S/2001/1082) concerning your intention to appoint General Miguel Angel Moreno
of Argentina as the next Force Commander of the United Nations Iraq-Kuwait
Observation Mission (UNIKOM) has been brought to the attention of the members
of the Security Council. They take note of the intention contained in your letter.
(Signed) Patricia Durrant
President of the Security Council
