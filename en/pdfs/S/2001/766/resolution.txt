Letter dated 2 August 2001 from the Secretary-General addressed
to the President of the Security Council
I have the honour to refer to the command of the United Nations Interim Force
in Lebanon (UNIFIL).
As you know, Major General Seth Kofi Obeng relinquished the command on
15 May 2001 and his Deputy, Brigadier General Ganesan Athamanathan, has been
the Acting Force Commander. Following the usual consultations, it is now my
intention to appoint Major General Lalit Mohan Tewari of India as Force
Commander of UNIFIL.
I should be grateful if you would bring this matter to the attention of the
members of the Security Council.
(Signed) Kofi A. Annan
