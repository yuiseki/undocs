Letter dated 21 November 2001 from the President of the Security
Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 16 November 2001
(S/2001/1097) concerning your decision to continue the activities of the United
Nations Political Office for Somalia for the biennium 2002-2003 has been brought
to the attention of the members of the Security Council. They take note of the
decision contained in your letter.
(Signed) Patricia Durrant
President of the Security Council
