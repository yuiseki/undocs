Letter dated 12 December 2001 from the President of the Security
Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 29 November 2001
(S/2001/1178) concerning your intention to extend the appointment of Mr. Ibrahim
Gambari as your Special Adviser for Special Assignments in Africa until 28
February 2003 has been brought to the attention of the members of the Security
Council. They have taken note of your intention and the information contained in
your letter.
(Signed) Moctar Ouane
President of the Security Council
