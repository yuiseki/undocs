Letter dated 24 April 2001 from the President of the Security
Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 18 April 2001
(S/2001/405) concerning your proposal to include Algeria, Bangladesh, Belgium,
Benin, Bolivia, Burkina Faso, Canada, China, the Czech Republic, Denmark, Egypt,
France, Ghana, India, Jordan, Kenya, the Libyan Arab Jamahiriya, Malawi,
Malaysia, Mali, Morocco, Mozambique, Nepal, the Niger, Nigeria, Pakistan, Peru,
Poland, Romania, the Russian Federation, Senegal, South Africa, Sweden,
Switzerland, Tunisia, Ukraine, the United Kingdom of Great Britain and Northern
Ireland, the United Republic of Tanzania, Uruguay and Zambia in the list of
countries contributing military personnel to the United Nations Organization
Mission in the Democratic Republic of the Congo (MONUC) has been brought to
the attention of the members of the Council. They take note of the proposal
contained therein.
(Signed) Jeremy Greenstock
President of the Security Council
