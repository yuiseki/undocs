Letter dated 4 March 2001 from the Permanent Representative of
the former Yugoslav Republic of Macedonia to the United Nations
addressed to the President of the Security Council
I have been instructed to forward to you, herewith, the letter of His Excellency
Mr. Srgjan Kerim, Minister for Foreign Affairs of the Republic of Macedonia, dated
4 March 2001, and to request the convening of an emergency session of the United
Nations Security Council, at which he will present an action plan of the Government
of the Republic of Macedonia for measures for the cessation of violence and for
lasting stabilization on the border with the Federal Republic of Yugoslavia (Kosovo
section), and for the prevention of a spillover of violence into the Republic of
Macedonia (see annex).
I should be grateful if you would have the present letter and its annex
circulated as a document of the Security Council.
(Signed) Naste Calovski
Ambassador
Permanent Representative

Annex to the letter dated 4 March 2001 from the Permanent
Representative of the former Yugoslav Republic of Macedonia
to the United Nations addressed to the President of the
Security Council
I would like to inform you of the latest very serious incident, which occurred
this morning at 9 a.m., near Tanusevce, in which three soldiers of the Macedonian
Army were killed, two by a landmine and one by a sniper. The soldiers were in a
supply vehicle accompanied by an Organization for Security and Cooperation in
Europe (OSCE) vehicle moving towards the Tanusevce watch tower when they ran
onto the landmine, after which the extremists positioned there carried out the sniper
attack.
At the urgent meeting held today between Mr. Boris Trajkovski, President of
the Republic of Macedonia, Mr. Ljubco Georgievski, Prime Minister of the Republic
of Macedonia, and Mr. Stojan Andov, Speaker of the Parliament of the Republic of
Macedonia, with the Ambassadors of EU and NATO countries and the representative
of OSCE, the President proposed the following immediate actions to be supported
by their respective Governments:
–

Convening of an emergency session of the United Nations Security
Council;

–

Support by NATO and EU countries for immediate action to be
undertaken by KFOR in order to clear the terrain of extremists on the
Kosovo side of the border area near Tanusevce;

–

Public support to be expressed for the right of the Republic of Macedonia
to self-defence and preservation of its territorial integrity and sovereignty
in a situation that endangers the entire region;

–

Concrete implementation of the declaratively expressed concern with the
preservation of the territorial integrity and stability of the Republic of
Macedonia.

Based on the above, I request that an emergency session of the United Nations
Security Council be convened, at which I intend to participate in order to present, on
behalf of the Government of the Republic of Macedonia, an action plan for measures
for the cessation of violence and for lasting stabilization on the border with the
Federal Republic of Yugoslavia (Kosovo section), and for the prevention of a
spillover of violence into the Republic of Macedonia.
(Signed) Srgjan Kerim
