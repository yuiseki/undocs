Letter dated 5 October 2001 from the Secretary-General
addressed to the President of the Security Council
On 18 September 2001, I appointed the following five experts to serve on the
Monitoring Group established pursuant to Security Council resolution 1363 (2001):
Michael Chandler (United Kingdom);
Philippe Graver (France);
Amod Gurung (Nepal);
Ashraf Mohsen Mohamed Mohsen (Egypt);
Daniel Yorks (United States of America).
I was subsequently informed that two of those experts, Ashraf Mohsen and
Daniel Yorks were unable to assume their functions.
Accordingly, in consultation with the Security Council Committee established
pursuant to resolution 1267 (1999), and taking into account the requirements stated
in the above-mentioned resolution, I wish to inform you that I have appointed the
following two experts to serve on the Monitoring Group in New York:
Michael D. Langan (United States of America);
Hassan H. Ali Abaza (Jordan).
(Signed) Kofi A. Annan
