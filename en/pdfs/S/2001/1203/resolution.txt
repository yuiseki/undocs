Letter dated 14 December 2001 from the President of the Security
Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 10 December 2001
(S/2001/1202) concerning your intention to extend the mandate of the United
Nations Political Office in Bougainville for 12 months has been brought to the
attention of the members of the Security Council. They take note of the intention
contained in your letter.
(Signed) Moctar Ouane
President of the Security Council
