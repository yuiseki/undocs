Letter dated 31 October 2001 from the President of the Security
Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 26 October 2001
(S/2001/1029) concerning your proposal to appoint John S. Wolf (United States of
America) as a Commissioner of the United Nations Monitoring, Verification and
Inspection Commission (UNMOVIC) has been brought to the attention of the
members of the Security Council. They take note of the proposal contained in your
letter.
(Signed) Richard Ryan
President of the Security Council
