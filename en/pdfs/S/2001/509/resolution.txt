Letter dated 18 May 2001 from the Secretary-General addressed
to the President of the Security Council
I have the honour to refer to resolutions 1272 (1999) of 25 October 1999 and
1338 (2001) of 31 January 2001, by which the Security Council established the
United Nations Transitional Administration in East Timor (UNTAET) and
authorized its mandate.
Further to my letter of 28 January 2000 concerning the list of contributing
countries and following the usual consultations, I propose that Slovakia be added to
the list of Member States contributing military personnel to UNTAET.
I should be grateful if you could bring this matter to the attention of the
members of the Security Council.
(Signed) Kofi A. Annan
