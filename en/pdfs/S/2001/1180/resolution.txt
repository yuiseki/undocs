Letter dated 5 December 2001 from the Secretary-General
addressed to the President of the Security Council
I have the honour to refer to the United Nations Peace-building Support Office
in Guinea-Bissau (UNOGBIS) and to inform the Security Council of my intention to
appoint Mr. David Stephen, Chief of the United Nations Political Office in Somalia,
as my Representative in Guinea-Bissau and Chief of UNOGBIS as of 1 February
2002.
Mr. Stephen will succeed Mr. Samuel C. Nana-Sinkam, who has done excellent
work in Guinea-Bissau, often in difficult circumstances.
I should be grateful if you would bring this letter to the attention of the
members of the Security Council.
(Signed) Kofi A. Annan
