Letter dated 31 May 2001 from the Secretary-General addressed
to the President of the Security Council
I have the honour to refer to your letter of 30 May 2001 (S/2001/551)
concurring with my proposal to appoint Ms. Andrésia Vaz as a judge of the
International Criminal Tribunal for Rwanda. I have received a corresponding letter
from the President of the General Assembly. Accordingly, I have appointed Ms. Vaz
as a judge of the International Criminal Tribunal for Rwanda, effective today, for the
remainder of the term of office of Judge Kama, which term will expire on 24 May
2003.
(Signed) Kofi A. Annan
