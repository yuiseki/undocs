Letter dated 12 November 2001 from the President of the Security
Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 2 November 2001
(S/2001/1064) concerning your proposal to appoint Li Junhua (China) as a
Commissioner of the United Nations Monitoring, Verification and Inspection
Commission (UNMOVIC) has been brought to the attention of the members of the
Security Council. They take note of the proposal contained in your letter.
(Signed) Patricia Durrant
President of the Security Council
