Letter dated 24 October 2001 from the Secretary-General to the
President of the Security Council
I have the honour to refer to Security Council resolution 1374 (2001), adopted
by the Council on 19 October 2001, in which the mandate of the Monitoring
Mechanism on Sanctions against the União Nacional Para a Independência Total de
Angola (UNITA) established pursuant to Council resolution 1295 (2000) was
extended for a further period of six months, ending on 19 April 2002. In paragraph 7
of that resolution, the Council requested, after consultation with the Security
Council Committee established pursuant to resolution 864 (1993), concerning the
situation in Angola, that I appoint four experts to serve on the Monitoring
Mechanism.
Accordingly, and having consulted with the Committee, I wish to inform you
that I have appointed the following four experts:
Ms. Christine Gordon (United Kingdom of Great Britain and Northern Ireland)
Mr. Wilson Chisala Kalumba (Zambia)
Ambassador Juan Larrain (Chile)
Mr. Ismaila Seck (Senegal)
I have also requested Ambassador Juan Larrain (Chile) to continue to chair the
Monitoring Mechanism.
(Signed) Kofi A. Annan
