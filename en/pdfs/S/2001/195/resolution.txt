Letter dated 6 March 2001 from the President of the Security
Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 1 March 2001
(S/2001/194) concerning your request that Mr. Carl Bildt and Mr. Eduard Kukan
continue their efforts as your Special Envoys for the Balkans until mid-year, and
beyond if required, has been brought to the attention of the members of the Security
Council. They take note of the request contained in your letter.
(Signed) Volodymyr Yu. Yel’chenko
President of the Security Council
