Letter dated 10 September 2001 from the Secretary-General
addressed to the President of the Security Council
I have the honour to refer to the United Nations Mission of Observers in
Prevlaka (UNMOP).
Colonel Graeme Roger Williams, who has been the Chief Military Observer of
UNMOP since 15 July 1998, will relinquish his post on 15 September 2001. For
more than three years, Colonel Williams has given proof of his strong
professionalism in a difficult environment.
Following the usual consultations, it is my intention to appoint Colonel
Rodolfo Sergio Mujica (Argentina) as the next Chief Military Observer of UNMOP.
I should be grateful if you would convey this information to the members of
the Security Council.
(Signed) Kofi A. Annan
