Letter dated 13 July 2001 from the Secretary-General addressed
to the President of the Security Council
I have the honour to refer to the United Nations Military Observer Group in
India and Pakistan (UNMOGIP).
Major-General Manuel L. Saavedra of Uruguay, who has been the Chief
Military Observer of UNMOGIP since 11 July 2000, relinquished his post on
10 July 2001. Following consultations with the parties concerned, it is my intention
to appoint Major-General Hermann K. Loidolt of Austria as the next Chief Military
Observer of UNMOGIP.
I should be grateful if you could bring this matter to the attention of the
members of the Security Council.
(Signed) Kofi A. Annan
