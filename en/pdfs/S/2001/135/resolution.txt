Note by the President of the Security Council
After consultations among the members of the Security Council, it has been
agreed that Curtis A. Ward, Deputy Permanent Representative of Jamaica to the
United Nations, will serve as Chairman of the Working Group of the Security
Council on United Nations Peacekeeping Operations established pursuant to the
eleventh and twelfth paragraphs of the statement made by the President of the
Security Council at the 4270th meeting of the Council, held on 31 January 2001
(S/PRST/2001/3), for a period running until 31 December 2001.
