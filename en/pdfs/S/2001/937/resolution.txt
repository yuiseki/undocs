Letter dated 4 October 2001 from the President of the
Security Council to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 3 October 2001
(A/56/432-S/2001/934) concerning your intention to reappoint Mr. Lakhdar Brahimi
as your Special Representative for Afghanistan has been brought to the attention of
the members of the Security Council. They take note with appreciation of your
intention and the terms of reference for your Special Representative (A/56/432S/2001/934, annex).
(Signed) Richard Ryan
President of the Security Council
