Letter dated 17 September 2001 from the President of the Security
Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 10 September 2001
(S/2001/872) concerning your intention to appoint Colonel Rodolfo Sergio Mujica
(Argentina) as the next Chief Military Observer of the United Nations Mission of
Observers in Prevlaka (UNMOP) has been brought to the attention of the members
of the Security Council. They agree with the intention contained in your letter.
(Signed) Jean-David Levitte
