Letter dated 2 August 2001 from the President of the
Security Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 31 July 2001
(S/2001/760) concerning your intention to appoint Mr. Amos Namanga Ngongi
(Cameroon) as your Special Representative for the Democratic Republic of the
Congo has been brought to the attention of the members of the Security Council.
They take note of the intention contained in your letter.
(Signed) Alfonso Valdivieso
President of the Security Council
