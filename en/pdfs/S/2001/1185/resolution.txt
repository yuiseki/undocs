Letter dated 12 December 2001 from the President of the
Security Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 6 December 2001
(S/2001/1184) concerning your decision to appoint Major General Jin Ha Hwang
(Republic of Korea) as the next Force Commander of the United Nations
Peacekeeping Force in Cyprus has been brought to the attention of the members of
the Security Council. They take note of the intention contained in your letter.
(Signed) Moctar Ouane
President of the Security Council
