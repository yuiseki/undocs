Letter dated 19 April 2001 from the President of the
Security Council to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that members of the Security Council have
considered your report dated 11 April 2001 on the United Nations Office in Angola
(S/2001/351).
They concur with the recommendation contained in paragraph 50 of that report
on the extension of the mandate of the Office until 15 October 2001 and are content
with the objectives for the Office which you set in the report.
(Signed) Jeremy Greenstock
President of the Security Council
