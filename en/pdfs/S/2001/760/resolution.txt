Letter dated 31 July 2001 from the Secretary-General
addressed to the President of the Security Council
I have the honour to refer to the United Nations Mission in the Democratic
Republic of the Congo (MONUC). As you know, Mr. Kamel Morjane (Tunisia), my
Special Representative for the Democratic Republic of the Congo, will shortly take
up another important international assignment.
Consequently, following the usual consultations, it is my intention to appoint
Mr. Amos Namanga Ngongi (Cameroon) as his successor. Mr. Ngongi’s appointment
will take effect in mid-August.
I should be grateful if you would bring this matter to the attention of the
Security Council.
(Signed) Kofi A. Annan
