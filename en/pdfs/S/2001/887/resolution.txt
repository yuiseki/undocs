Letter dated 18 September 2001 from the Secretary-General
addressed to the President of the Security Council
I have the honour to refer to Security Council resolution 1363 (2001) of 30
July 2001 concerning Afghanistan. In paragraph 3 of that resolution, the Council
requested me to establish, in consultation with the Security Council Committee
established pursuant to resolution 1267 (1999), within 30 days of the adoption of the
resolution and for a period running concurrently with the application of the
measures imposed by resolution 1333 (2000), a mechanism: (a) to monitor the
implementation of the measures imposed by resolutions 1267 (1999) and 1333
(2000); (b) to offer assistance to States bordering the territory of Afghanistan under
Taliban control and other States, as appropriate, to increase their capacity regarding
the implementation of the measures imposed by resolutions 1267 (1999) and 1333
(2000); and (c) to collate, assess, verify wherever possible, report and make
recommendations on information regarding violations of the measures imposed by
resolutions 1267 (1999) and 1333 (2000).
Accordingly, I wish to inform you that, in consultation with the Committee and
taking into account the requirements stated in resolution 1363 (2001), I have
appointed the following five experts to serve on the Monitoring Group in New York,
in accordance with paragraph 4 (a) of the resolution:
Mr. Michael Chandler (United Kingdom)
Mr. Philippe Graver (France)
Mr. Amod Gurung (Nepal)
Mr. Ashraf Mohsen Mohamed Mohsen (Egypt)
Mr. Daniel Yorks (United States of America)
I have also selected Mr. Michael Chandler to chair the Monitoring Group.
(Signed) Kofi A. Annan
