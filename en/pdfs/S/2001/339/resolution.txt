Letter dated 6 April 2001 from the President of the Security
Council to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 3 April 2001 concerning
the Panel of experts on the illegal exploitation of natural resources and other forms
of wealth in the Democratic Republic of the Congo (S/2001/338) has been brought
to the attention of the members of the Council. They take note of your
recommendation that the Panel submit its final report to the Council by 16 April
2001.
(Signed) Jeremy Greenstock
President of the Security Council
