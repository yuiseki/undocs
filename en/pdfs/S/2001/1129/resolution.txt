Letter dated 29 November 2001 from the President of the Security
Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 26 November 2001
(S/2001/1128) concerning your intention to establish the Office of the Special
Representative of the Secretary-General for West Africa has been brought to the
attention of the members of the Council. They welcome your intention to establish
the Office of the Special Representative of the Secretary-General for West Africa
and take note of the functions of the office stated in your letter.
(Signed) Patricia Durrant
President of the Security Council
