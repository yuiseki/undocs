Letter dated 22 May 2001 from the President of the Security
Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 18 May 2001
(S/2001/509) concerning your proposal to add Slovakia to the list of Member States
contributing military personnel to the United Nations Transitional Administration in
East Timor has been brought to the attention of the members of the Council. They
take note of the proposal contained therein.
(Signed) James B. Cunningham
President of the Security Council
