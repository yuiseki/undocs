Letter dated 6 August 2001 from the President of the Security
Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 2 August 2001
(S/2001/766) regarding your intention to appoint Major General Lalit Mohan Tewari
of India as Force Commander of the United Nations Interim Force in Lebanon
(UNIFIL) has been brought to the attention of the members of the Council. They
take note of your intention.
(Signed) Alfonso Valdivieso
President of the Security Council
