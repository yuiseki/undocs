Letter dated 16 July 2001 from the Secretary-General addressed
to the President of the Security Council
I have the honour to refer to your letter of 21 June 2001 (S/2001/614), in
which you suggested several practical steps for my consideration with regard to the
implementation of the recommendations contained in my two reports on the
protection of civilians in armed conflict (S/1999/957 and S/2001/331).
I would like to express my appreciation at the continuing commitment of the
Security Council to the protection of civilians in armed conflict. By focusing on the
implementation of those recommendations agreed upon in its resolutions 1265
(1999) and 1296 (2000) or endorsed in its open meeting on 23 April 2001, the
Council helps to ensure that future efforts to bring genuine relief and protection to
civilians in armed conflict will be more effective.
I have taken good note of the Council’s suggestions and agree on the need for
close cooperation and joint strategic planning between the Office for the
Coordination of Humanitarian Affairs and the Department of Peacekeeping
Operations on these pivotal issues. I am pleased to inform you that I have requested
the Office for the Coordination of Humanitarian Affairs to develop operational
procedures, in cooperation with the Department of Peacekeeping Operations and in
close consultation with members of the Executive Committee on Humanitarian
Affairs and the Inter-Agency Standing Committee, which will clarify the
responsibilities for, and facilitate the implementation of, the recommendations and
enhance cooperation between the different parts of the United Nations system.
I shall report regularly to the Security Council on the status of these initiatives,
in particular on progress in developing the modalities for the implementation of the
recommendations, beginning in November 2001. I also welcome the opportunity to
present to the Council a follow-up report on the status of implementation and any
other matters aimed at enhancing the protection of civilians by November 2002, to
be followed by regular implementation reports thereafter.
As requested, I have transmitted my report on the protection of civilians in
armed conflict of 30 March 2001 (S/2001/331) to the President of the General
Assembly.
I should be grateful if you would bring the present letter to the attention of the
members of the Security Council.
(Signed) Kofi A. Annan
