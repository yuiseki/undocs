Letter dated 2 November 2001 from the President of the Security
Council to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 30 October 2001
(S/2001/1041) concerning your intention to appoint William Lacy Swing (United
States of America) to succeed William Eagleton as your Special Representative for
Western Sahara effective from 1 December 2001 has been brought to the attention of
the members of the Security Council. They take note of the intention contained in
your letter.
(Signed) Patricia Durrant
President of the Security Council
