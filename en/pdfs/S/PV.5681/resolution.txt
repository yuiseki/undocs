The situation concerning Iraq
The President: The Security Council will now
begin its consideration of the item on its agenda. The
Council is meeting in accordance with the
understanding reached in its prior consultations.
Members of the Council have before them
photocopies of a letter dated 7 May 2007 from the
Secretary-General addressed to the President of the
Security Council concerning the escrow account
attributable to the United Nations Monitoring,
Verification and Inspection Commission established by
resolution 1284 (1999). That letter will be issued as a
document of the Council under the symbol S/2007/300.
Members of the Council also have before them
the draft of a letter that I, as President of the Security
Council, propose to send to the Secretary-General in
reply to his letter, approving the proposal contained in

2

the Secretary-General’s letter that $694,771 plus
€1,856,754 be transferred from the aforementioned
escrow account to be credited against assessments
issued in respect of the obligations of the Government
of Iraq to the United Nations Industrial Development
Organization and to meet its current obligation with
respect to the regular, peacekeeping, Capital Master
Plan and the International Tribunals for Rwanda and
the former Yugoslavia budgets, respectively.
In the absence of objection, I shall consider that
the Security Council endorses the proposal of the
Secretary-General and agrees that I should send the
letter as drafted to the Secretary-General.
It is so decided.
The reply will be issued as a document of the
Council under the symbol S/2007/301.
The Security Council has thus concluded the
present stage of its consideration of the item on its
agenda.
The meeting rose at 12.10 p.m.
