The United Nations has been seized of the question of South Africa for
the past 45 years. The reason for this is that our people have been subjected
to the policy of apartheid, which the United Nations has determined is a crime
against humanity.

The decisions which have been taken by the Security Council and the
General Assembly on the question of South Africa have been directed at ending
this apartheid crime against humanity and helping to transform our country
into a non-racial democracy. This objective has not yet been achieved. South
Africa continues to be governed by a white minority regime. The overwhelming
majority of our people are still denied the vote. They remain deprived of the
right to determine their destiny.

Representatives of the South African Government will also address the
Council today. However sweet sounding the words they may utter, they
represent the system of white minority rule to which the United Nations is
opposed. They continue to govern our country under a constitution which the

Security Council has declared null and void.
