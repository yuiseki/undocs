Official communiqué of the 5535th (closed) meeting
of the Security Council
Held in private at Headquarters, New York, on Monday,
25 September 2006, at 3 p.m.
In accordance with rule 55 of the provisional rules of procedure of the Security
Council, the following communiqué was issued through the Secretary-General in
place of a verbatim record:
“At its 5535th meeting, held in private on 25 September 2006, the
Security Council considered the item entitled “The situation in Somalia”.
“The President, with the consent of the Council, extended an invitation
under rule 37 of the Council’s provisional rules of procedure to the
representative of Somalia, His Excellency Mr. Ismael Mohamoud Hurreh,
Minister for Foreign Affairs and International Cooperation.
“The President, with the consent of the Council, extended an invitation
under rule 39 of the Council’s provisional rules of procedure to His Excellency
Mr. Raphael Tuju, EGH, MP, Minister for Foreign Affairs of Kenya and
Chairman of the Council of Ministers of the Intergovernmental Authority on
Development.
“Members of the Council, Minister Tuju and Minister Hurreh had an
exchange of views.”
