speak, a member of the Swiss Confederation,
within a customs union with Switzerland.
The third consideration is that Liechtenstein
does not have its own currency: Only Swiss ,::urrency circulates in Liechtenstein.
The fourth consideration is that Liechtenstein
does not have a postal organization; that is in
the hands of Switzerland, which administers the
posts.
The fifth consideration is that the administration of the telegraphs is also in the hands of
