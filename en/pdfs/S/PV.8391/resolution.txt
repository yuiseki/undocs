(S/2018/903)
The President (spoke in Chinese): The Security
Council will now begin its consideration of the item on
its agenda.
Members of the Council have before them document
S/2018/990, which contains the text of a draft resolution
submitted by the United States of America.
I wish to draw the attention of Council members
to document S/2018/903, which contains the report of
the Secretary-General on the situation with respect to
piracy and armed robbery at sea off the coast of Somalia.

The President (spoke in Chinese): The draft
