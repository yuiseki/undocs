Official communiqué of the 9279th (closed) meeting of the
Security Council
Held in private at Headquarters, New York, on Monday,
13 March 2023, at 3 p.m.
In accordance with rule 55 of the provisional rules of procedure of the Security
Council, the following communiqué was issued through the Secretary -General in
place of a verbatim record:
“At its 9279th meeting, held in private on 13 March 202 3, the Security
Council considered the item entitled ‘The situation in Myanmar’.
“The President invited the representative of Indonesia to participate in the
meeting in accordance with rule 37 of the Council’s provisional rules of
procedure.
“The President invited Ms. Noeleen Heyzer, Special Envoy of the
Secretary-General for Myanmar, to participate in the meeting in accordance with
rule 39 of the Council’s provisional rules of procedure.
“The Council heard briefings by Ms. Heyzer, and by Her Excellency
Ms. Retno Marsudi, Minister for Foreign Affairs of Indonesia, in her capacity
as Chair of the Association of Southeast Asian Nations (ASEAN).
“Statements were made by the representatives of the United Kingdom of
Great Britain and Northern Ireland, the United States of America, the Russian
Federation, Switzerland, France, Brazil, China, Gabon, Japan, Ghana, Ecuador,
the United Arab Emirates, Malta, Albania, and by the President, speaking in his
capacity as the representative of Mozambique.
“Ms. Heyzer and Her Excellency Ms. Marsudi responded to comments and
questions raised.”
