Symbols of United Nations documents are composed of capital letters combined with figures. Mention of such a symbol indicates a reference to a United
Nations document.
Documents of the Security Council (symbol S/ . . .) are normally published in
quarterly Supplements
of the Officiul Records of the Security Council. The date
of the document indicates the supplement in which it appears or in which information about it is given.
The resolutions of the Security Council, numbered in accordance with a
system adopted in 1964, are .published in yearly volumes of Resolutions
and
Decisions
of the Security
Council.
The new system, which has been applied
retroactively to resolutions adopted before 1 January 1965, became fully operative
on that date.

Before we take up the item on the agenda I should
like, in my capacity as President of the Council for the
month of December, to discharge a very pleasant
duty, namely, to thank the outgoing President, Ambassador Jorge Illueca of Panama, and at the same
time to congratulate
him for the admirable way in
which he conducted the work of the Security Council
during the month of November. I should like to express
the admiration felt, I am convinced, by all members
of the Council for our colleague from Panama who
discharged his obligations as President of the Council
with so. much distinction
and efficacy. Under his
presidency, thanks to his dedication,
wisdom and
patience, the Council was able to resolve important
and delicate problems and questions.
