and rule 37 of our rules of procedure, it is by invitation of the Security Council
that any Member of the United Nations that is not a member of the Council may
participate, without the right to vote, in the discussion of any question brought
before the Council. It would be well if those who have benefited from that

opportunity and who, by their words, have abused it were to keep that fact in mind.
