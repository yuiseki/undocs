Official communiqué of the 6307th (closed) meeting
of the Security Council
Held in private at Headquarters, New York, on Wednesday,
5 May 2010, at 12 noon
In accordance with rule 55 of the provisional rules of procedure of the Security
Council, the following communiqué was issued through the Secretary-General in
place of a verbatim record:
“On 5 May 2010, the Security Council, pursuant to resolution 1353
(2001), annex II, sections A and B, held its 6307th meeting in private with the
troop- and police-contributing countries to the United Nations Mission in the
Central African Republic and Chad.
“The Security Council and the troop- and police-contributing countries
heard a briefing under rule 39 of its provisional rules of procedure by
Mr. Youssef Mahmoud, Special Representative of the Secretary-General and
Head of the United Nations Mission in the Central African Republic and Chad.
“Members of the Council, Mr. Mahmoud and representatives of
participating contributing countries had an exchange of views.”
