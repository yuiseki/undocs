Letter dated 9 January 2004 from the Secretary-General
addressed to the President of the Security Council
I have the honour to refer to the command of the United Nations
Disengagement Observer Force (UNDOF).
Following the usual consultations, it is my intention to appoint Major General
Bala Nanda Sharma of Nepal as Force Commander of UNDOF. Major General
Sharma will replace Major General Franciszek Gągor, who will relinquish the post
on 16 January 2004.
I should be grateful if you would bring this matter to the attention of the
members of the Security Council.
(Signed) Kofi A. Annan
