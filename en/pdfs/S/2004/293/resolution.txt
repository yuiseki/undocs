Letter dated 8 April 2004 from the Secretary-General addressed to
the President of the Security Council
I have the honour to refer to your letter of 7 April 2004 (S/2004/292)
concurring with my proposal to appoint Sir Charles Michael Dennis Byron as a
permanent judge of the International Tribunal for Rwanda. I have received a
corresponding letter from the President of the General Assembly. Accordingly, I
have appointed Sir Dennis Byron as a permanent judge of the International Tribunal
for Rwanda, effective today, for the remainder of the term of office of Judge Lloyd
George Williams, which term will expire on 24 May 2007.
(Signed) Kofi A. Annan
