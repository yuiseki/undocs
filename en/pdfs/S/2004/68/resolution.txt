Letter dated 19 January 2004 from the Secretary-General to the
President of the Security Council
I have the honour to refer to the command of the United Nations Interim Force
in Lebanon (UNIFIL).
Following the usual consultations, it is my intention to appoint Major General
Alain Pellegrini (France) as Force Commander of UNIFIL. He will replace Major
General Lalit Mohan Tewari, who will relinquish the post on 17 February 2004.
I should be grateful if you would bring this matter to the attention of the
members of the Security Council.
(Signed) Kofi A. Annan
