Letter dated 16 December 2004 from the President of the
Security Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 14 December 2004
(S/2004/974) concerning your decision to appoint Mr. Terje Roed-Larsen of Norway
as your Special Envoy for the implementation of Security Council resolution 1559
(2004) has been brought to the attention of the members of the Council. They took
note of your intention and the information contained in your letter.
(Signed) Abdallah Baali
President of the Security Council
