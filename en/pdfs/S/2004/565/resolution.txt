Letter dated 12 July 2004 from the Secretary-General addressed to
the President of the Security Council
I have the honour to inform the members of the Security Council that it is my
intention to appoint Juan Gabriel Valdés (Chile) as my Special Representative and
Head of the United Nations Stabilization Mission in Haiti.
I should be grateful if you would bring this letter to the attention of the
members of the Security Council.
(Signed) Kofi A. Annan
