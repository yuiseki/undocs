Letter dated 8 January 2004 from the Secretary-General
addressed to the President of the Security Council
I have the honour to refer to Security Council resolution 1284 (1999), which
requires me to consult the Security Council and the Executive Chairman of the
United Nations Monitoring, Verification and Inspection Commission (UNMOVIC)
on the appointment of suitably qualified experts as UNMOVIC Commissioners.
Kostyantyn Gryshchenko has resigned from the College of Commissioners,
and I would like to propose the appointment of Anatoliy Scherba.
I should be grateful if you would bring this matter to the attention of the
Security Council.
(Signed) Kofi A. Annan
