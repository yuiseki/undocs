Letter dated 8 October 2004 from the President of the Security
Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 5 October 2004
(S/2004/809) concerning your intention to appoint Brigadier General Clive Lilley of
New Zealand as the Chief of Staff of the United Nations Truce Supervision
Organization (UNTSO) has been brought to the attention of the members of the
Security Council. They take note of the intention expressed in your letter.
(Signed) Emyr Jones Parry
