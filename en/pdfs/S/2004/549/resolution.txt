Letter dated 9 July 2004 from the President of the Security
Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 6 July 2004
(S/2004/548) concerning your intention to appoint Major-General Rajender Singh
(India) as Force Commander of the United Nations Mission in Ethiopia and Eritrea
(UNMEE), has been brought to the attention of the members of the Security
Council. They take note of the intention expressed in your letter.
(Signed) Mihnea Ioan Motoc
President of the Security Council
