Letter dated 13 July 2004 from the President of the Security
Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 12 July 2004
(S/2004/563) concerning your intention to appoint Ashraf Jehangir Qazi (Pakistan)
as your new Special Representative for Iraq has been brought to the attention of the
members of the Security Council. They take note of the intention expressed in your
letter.
(Signed) Mihnea Ioan Motoc
President of the Security Council
