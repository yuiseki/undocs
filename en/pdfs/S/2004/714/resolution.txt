Letter dated 3 September 2004 from the President of the Security
Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 1 September 2004
(S/2004/713) concerning your intention to designate João Bernardo Honwana
(Mozambique) as your Representative in Guinea-Bissau and Head of the United
Nations Peace-building Support Office in Guinea-Bissau (UNOGBIS) has been
brought to the attention of the members of the Security Council. They take note of
the intention expressed in your letter.
(Signed) Juan Antonio Yañez-Barnuevo
President of the Security Council
