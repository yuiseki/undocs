Letter dated 2 December 2004 from the President of the Security
Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 29 November 2004
(S/2004/941) concerning your intention to add Ecuador, Jordan and the Philippines
to the list of countries that have agreed to provide military personnel to the United
Nations Stabilization Mission in Haiti (MINUSTAH) has been brought to the
attention of the members of the Security Council, who have taken note of your
intention.
(Signed) Abdallah Baali
President of the Security Council
