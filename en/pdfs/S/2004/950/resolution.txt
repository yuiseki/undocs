Note by the President of the Security Council
Following consultations among the members of the Security Council, it was
agreed that Gunter Pleuger, Permanent Representative of Germany to the United
Nations, would serve as the Chairman of the Security Council Committee
established pursuant to resolution 1572 (2004) concerning Côte d’Ivoire from 6 to
31 December 2004.
