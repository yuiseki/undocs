Letter dated 23 August 2004 from the Secretary-General addressed
to the President of the Security Council
I have the honour to refer to Security Council resolution 1558 (2004), adopted
by the Security Council on 17 August 2004, concerning Somalia. In paragraph 3 of
that resolution the Council requested me to re-establish, in consultation with the
Security Council Committee established pursuant to resolution 751 (1992)
concerning Somalia, within 30 days from the date of the adoption of the resolution,
and for a period of six months, the Monitoring Group as referred to in paragraph 2
of resolution 1519 (2003) to undertake the mandate contained in subparagraphs 3 (a)
to (e) of resolution 1558 (2004).
Accordingly, I wish to inform you that, after consultations with the
Committee, I have appointed the following experts:
Mr. John E. Tambi (Sierra Leone)
Mr. Melvin E. Holt, Jr. (United States of America)
Mr. Changsheng Li (China)
Mr. Joel Salek (Colombia)
I should be grateful if you would bring this information to the attention of the
members of the Security Council.
(Signed) Kofi A. Annan
