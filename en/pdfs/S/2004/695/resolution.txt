Letter dated 24 August 2004 from the Secretary-General
addressed to the President of the Security Council
I have the honour to refer to the United Nations Military Observer Group in
India and Pakistan (UNMOGIP).
Major-General Pertti Puonti (Finland), who has been the Chief Military
Observer of UNMOGIP since 9 January 2002, relinquished his post on 28 July 2004.
Following the usual consultations, it is my intention to appoint Major-General
Guido Palmieri (Italy) as the next Chief Military Observer of UNMOGIP.
(Signed) Kofi Annan
