Letter dated 28 May 2004 from the President of the Security
Council to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 24 May 2004
(S/2004/433) concerning your intention to appoint Ms. Carolyn McAskie (Canada)
as your Special Representative for Burundi and Head of the United Nations
Operation in Burundi (ONUB) has been brought to the attention of the members of
the Security Council. They take note of the intention expressed in your letter.
(Signed) Munir Akram
President of the Security Council
