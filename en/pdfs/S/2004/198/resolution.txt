Letter dated 12 March 2004 addressed to the Secretary-General by
the President of the Security Council
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 9 March 2004
(S/2004/197) concerning your intention to add Peru to the list of countries providing
military personnel to the United Nations Peacekeeping Force in Cyprus has been
brought to the attention of the members of the Security Council, who have taken
note of it.
(Signed) Jean-Marc de La Sablière
President of the Security Council
