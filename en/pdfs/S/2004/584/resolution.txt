Letter dated 21 July 2004 from the President of the Security
Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 16 July 2004
(S/2004/583) concerning your nomination of Major-General Derrick Mbuyiselo
Mgwebi (South Africa) as the Force Commander for the United Nations Operation
in Burundi (ONUB) has been brought to the attention of the members of the Security
Council. They take note of the information contained in your letter.
(Signed) Mihnea Ioan Motoc
President of the Security Council
