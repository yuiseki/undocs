Letter dated 14 May 2004 from the President of the Security
Council addressed to the Secretary-General
In reference to your letters dated 10 and 12 May 2004 (S/2004/388 and
S/2004/389), I have the honour to inform you that your nomination of Javier
Rupérez of Spain as the Executive Director of the Counter-Terrorism Committee
Executive Directorate, in compliance with paragraph 3 of resolution 1535 (2004),
has been brought to the attention of the members of the Security Council. They
approve the appointment. Mr. Rupérez should take up his mandated tasks as soon as
possible.
(Signed) Munir Akram
President of the Security Council
