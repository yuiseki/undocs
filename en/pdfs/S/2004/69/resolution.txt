Letter dated 26 January 2004 from the President of the Security
Council to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 19 January 2004
(S/2004/68) concerning your intention to appoint Major General Alain Pellegrini
(France) as Force Commander of the United Nations Interim Force in Lebanon
(UNIFIL) has been brought to the attention of the members of the Security Council.
They take note of the intention expressed in your letter.
(Signed) Heraldo Muñoz
President of the Security Council
