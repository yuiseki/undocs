Letter dated 1 September 2004 from the Secretary-General to the
President of the Security Council
I have the honour to refer to the United Nations Peace-building Support Office
in Guinea-Bissau (UNOGBIS) and to inform the Security Council of my intention to
designate João Bernardo Honwana (Mozambique) as my Representative in GuineaBissau and Head of UNOGBIS as from 15 September 2004.
Mr. Honwana will succeed David Stephen, who served in Guinea-Bissau from
February 2002 until his retirement from the United Nations in April 2004, and
whose excellent work has greatly contributed to the consolidation of peace in that
country.
I should be grateful if you could bring this letter to the attention of the
members of the Security Council.
(Signed) Kofi A. Annan
