Letter dated 28 April 2004 from the President of the Security
Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 23 April 2004
(S/2004/331) concerning your intention to continue the activities of the United
Nations Tajikistan Office of Peace-building for a further period of one year, until
1 June 2005, has been brought to the attention of the members of the Security
Council. They take note of the information contained in your letter and the intention
expressed therein.
(Signed) Gunter Pleuger
President of the Security Council
