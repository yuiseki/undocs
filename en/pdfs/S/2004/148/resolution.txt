Annex
Letter dated 26 February 2004 from the Secretary-General of the
Organization of American States to the Secretary-General of the
United Nations
The Permanent Council of the Organization of American States met this
morning in Special Session, at the request of the Caribbean Community
(CARICOM) Group of States, to consider events in Haiti and approved resolution
CP/RES.862 (1401/04) entitled “Situation in Haiti”. In fulfilment of the mandates
contained in its operative paragraphs 1 and 3, I have enclosed a copy of the said
resolution (see appendix I), as well as the request of CARICOM (CP/INF.4948/04)
(see appendix II), for your information and that of the Security Council.
(Signed) Chaser Gaviria
Secretary-General
