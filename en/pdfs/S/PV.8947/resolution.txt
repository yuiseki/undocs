Official communiqué of the 8947th (closed) meeting of the
Security Council
Held in private at Headquarters, New York, on Wednesday,
12 January 2022, at 3 p.m.
In accordance with rule 55 of the provisional rules of procedure of the Security
Council, the following communiqué was issued through the Secretary -General in
place of a verbatim record:
“On 12 January 2022, the Security Council, pursuant to annex II, sections
A and B of resolution 1353 (2001), held its 8947th meeting in private with the
troop- and police-contributing countries to the United Nations Peacekeeping
Force in Cyprus.
“The President invited Mr. Colin Stewart, Special Representative of the
Secretary-General and Head of the United Nations Peacekeeping Force in
Cyprus, as well as Major General Ingrid Gjerde, Force Commander in the United
Nations Peacekeeping Force in Cyrus, to participate in the meeting in
accordance with rule 39 of the Council’s provisional rules of procedure.
“The Security Council and the troop- and police-contributing countries
heard a briefing by Mr. Stewart and Gen. Gjerde.
“Members of the Council, Mr. Stewart, Gen. Gjerde and representatives of
participating contributing countries had an exchange of views.”
