Official communiqué of the 9172nd (closed) meeting of the
Security Council
Held in private at Headquarters, New York, on Thursday,
27 October 2022, at 4.32 p.m.
In accordance with rule 55 of the provisional rules of procedure of the Security
Council, the following communiqué was issued through the Secretary-General in
place of a verbatim record:
“At its 9172nd meeting, held in private on 27 October 2022, the Security
Council considered the item entitled “Threats to international peace and
security”.
“The President invited the representative of Ukraine to participate in the
meeting in accordance with rule 37 of the Council’s provisional rul es of
procedure.
“The President of the Security Council invited Mr. Rafael Mariano Grossi,
Director General of the International Atomic Energy Agency, to participate in
the meeting in accordance with rule 39 of the Council’s provisional rules of
procedure.
“The members of the Council heard a briefing by Mr. Grossi.
“The members of the Council, the representative of Ukraine and
Mr. Grossi had an exchange of views.”
