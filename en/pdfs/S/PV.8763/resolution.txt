Maintenance of international peace and security

The meeting was called to order at 10.05 a.m.
Expression of thanks to the outgoing President
The President (spoke in Russian): I should like
to take this opportunity to pay tribute, on behalf of
the Council, to His Excellency Mr. Abdou Abarry,
Permanent Representative of the Niger, for his service
as President of the Council for the month of September.
I am sure I speak for all members of the Council in
expressing deep appreciation to Ambassador Abarry
and his team for the great diplomatic skill with which
they conducted the Council’s business last month.

The agenda was adopted.

I wish to draw the attention of Council members to
document S/2020/876, which contains the report of the
Secretary-General pursuant to resolution 2491 (2019).

A vote was taken by show of hands.
