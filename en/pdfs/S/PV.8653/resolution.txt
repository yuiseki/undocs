Official communiqué of the 8653rd (closed) meeting of the
Security Council
Held in private at Headquarters, New York, on Thursday,
31 October 2019, at 10 a.m.
In accordance with rule 55 of the provisional rules of procedure of the Security
Council, the following communiqué was issued through the Secretary-General in
place of a verbatim record:
“At its 8653rd meeting, held in private on 31 October 2019, t he Security
Council considered the item entitled ‘Briefing by the President of the
International Court of Justice’.
“The President of the Security Council invited Judge Abdulqawi Ahmed
Yusuf, President of the International Court of Justice, to participate in the
meeting in accordance with rule 39 of the Council’s provisional rules of
procedure.
“The members of the Council heard a briefing by Judge Yusuf.
“The members of the Council and Judge Yusuf had an exchange of views.”
