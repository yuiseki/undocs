Report of the Secretary-General on South
Sudan (S/2015/296)
The President: The Security Council will now
begin its consideration of the item on its agenda.
Members of the Council have before them document
S/2015/380, which contains the text of a draft resolution
submitted by France, Lithuania, the United Kingdom
of Great Britain and Northern Ireland and the United
States of America.

28/05/2015

I wish to draw the attention of the members of the
Council to document S/2015/296, which contains the
report of the Secretary-General on South Sudan.
The Council is ready to proceed to the vote on the
draft resolution before it. I shall put the draft resolution
to the vote now.
A vote was taken by show of hands.
In favour:
Angola, Chad, Chile, China, France, Jordan,
Lithuania, Malaysia, New Zealand, Nigeria,
Russian Federation, Spain, United Kingdom of
Great Britain and Northern Ireland, United States
of America and Venezuela (Bolivarian Republic of)
The President: The draft resolution received
15 votes in favour. The draft resolution has been
adopted unanimously as resolution 2223 (2015).
The meeting rose at 3.10 p.m.
