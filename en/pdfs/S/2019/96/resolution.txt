Letter dated 30 January 2019 from the President of the
Security Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 28 January 2019
(S/2019/95), concerning your intention to appoint Lieutenant General Michael Anker
Lollesgaard (Denmark) as Chair of the Redeployment Coordination Committee and
Head of the United Nations Mission to Support the Hudaydah Agreement, has been
brought to the attention of the members of the Security Council. They take note of
the intention expressed in your letter.
(Signed) José Singer Weisinger
President of the Security Council
