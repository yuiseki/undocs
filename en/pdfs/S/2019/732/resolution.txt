Letter dated 11 September 2019 from the President of the
Security Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 5 September 2019
(S/2019/731), with reference to Security Council resolution 2479 (2019), in which
the Council requested the Secretary-General and the Chairperson of the African Union
Commission to provide a special report no later than 30 September 2019, has been
brought to the attention of the members of the Security Council.
The members of the Council have taken note of the request contained in your
letter that the time frame for the submission of assessment report be extended, and
they look forward to the submission of the report by 10 October 2019.
(Signed) Vassily A. Nebenzia
President of the Security Council
