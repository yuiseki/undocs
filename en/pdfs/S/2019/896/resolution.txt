Letter dated 19 November 2019 from the Secretary-General
addressed to the President of the Security Council
I have the honour to refer to paragraph 3 of Security Council resolution 2471
(2019), by which the Council decided to extend, until 30 June 2020, the mandate of
the Panel of Experts on South Sudan.
I have the further honour to refer to my letter dated 2 July 2019 ( S/2019/544),
by which I had appointed five experts to serve on the Panel. In that connection, I
regret to inform you that, owing to unforeseen circumstances, the humanitarian affairs
expert was not offered a contract.
Therefore, I wish to further inform you that, after consulting the Security
Council Committee established pursuant to resolution 2206 (2015) concerning South
Sudan, I have appointed Ms. Laura Bernal (Colombia) as the humanitarian affairs
expert.
I should be grateful if you would bring this matter to the attention of the
members of the Security Council.
(Signed) António Guterres
