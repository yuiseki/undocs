Letter dated 5 September 2019 from the Secretary-General
addressed to the President of the Security Council
I have the honour to refer to Security Council resolution 2479 (2019) on the
African Union-United Nations Hybrid Operation in Darfur (UNAMID), in which the
Council requested the Secretary-General and the Chairperson of the African Union
Commission to provide a special report no later than 30 September 2019. The report
was requested in order to present: (a) an assessment of the situation on the ground
and recommendations on the appropriate course of action regarding the drawdown of
UNAMID; and (b) a joint African Union-United Nations political strategy detailing
options for a follow-on mechanism to UNAMID. In view of that request, a joint
African Union-United Nations team is currently visiting Khartoum, from 4 to
9 September 2019, to engage with the new Government of the Sudan and to finalize
the report.
The calendar for the establishment of the new transitional institutions in the
Sudan, following the signature of the constitutiona l declaration on 17 August 2019,
foresees the establishment of the new transitional Government in early September
2019. I therefore wish to request a ten-day extension of the time frame for the
submission of the assessment report, until 10 October 2019, to enable substantive
engagement with the transitional institutions regarding their views, especially on
post-UNAMID arrangements.
I would be grateful if you could bring the present letter to the attention of the
members of the Security Council.
(Signed) António Guterres
