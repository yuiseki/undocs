Letter dated 2 July 2019 from the Secretary-General addressed to
the President of the Security Council
I have the honour to refer to paragraph 3 of Security Council resolution 2471
(2019), by which the Council decided to extend, until 30 June 2020, the mandate of
the Panel of Experts on South Sudan.
Accordingly, I wish to inform you that, after consulting with the Security
Council Committee established pursuant to resolution 2206 (2015) concerning
South Sudan, I have appointed the following experts:
Ms. Carolyn Buff, humanitarian affairs (France)
Mr. Mark Ferullo, natural resources (United States of America)
Mr. Dean Gillespie, arms (South Africa)
Mr. Andrei Kolmakov, finance (Russian Federation)
Mr. Emilio Manfredi, armed groups/regional issues (Italy)
I have designated Mr. Manfredi to serve as Coordinator of the Panel.
I should be grateful if you would bring this matter to the attention of the
members of the Security Council.
(Signed) António Guterres
