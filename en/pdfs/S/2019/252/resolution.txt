Letter dated 20 March 2019 from the President of the
Security Council addressed to the Secretary-General
Further to the letter dated 28 February 2019 from Ambassador Anatolio Ndong
Mba, I have the honour to confirm that the members of the Security Council have
agreed to send a mission to the Sahel from 21 to 25 March 2019. The members have
agreed on the terms of reference of the mission (see annex).
The mission will be co-led by Ambassador Kacou Houadja Léon Adom (Côte
d’Ivoire), Ambassador François Delattre (France) and Ambassador Christoph
Heusgen (Germany).
Following consultations with the members, it has been agreed t hat the
composition of the mission will be as follows:
Ambassador Marc Pecsteen de Buytswerve (Belgium)
Mr. Dianbin Zhang (China)
Ambassador Kacou Houadja Léon Adom (Côte d’Ivoire)
Ambassador José Singer Weisinger (Dominican Republic)
Ambassador Job Obiang Esono Mbengono (Equatorial Guinea)
Ambassador François Delattre (France)
Ambassador Christoph Heusgen (Germany)
Ambassador Dian Triansyah Djani (Indonesia)
Mr. Bader Almunayekh (Kuwait)
Ambassador Gustavo Meza-Cuadra (Peru)
Ambassador Joanna Wronecka (Poland)
Mr. Dmitry A. Polyanskiy (Russian Federation)
Ambassador Jerry Matthews Matjila (South Africa)
Ambassador Stephen Hickey (United Kingdom of Great Britain and Northern Ireland)
Mr. Rodney Hunter (United States of America)
I should be grateful if you could have the present letter and its annex circulated
as a document of the Security Council.
(Signed) François Delattre
President of the Security Council

Annex
Terms of reference of the Security Council mission to the Sahel
1.
The Security Council will carry out a mission to the Sahel from 21 to 25 March
2019.
2.
The mission will be carried out within the framework of Security Council
resolutions 2391 (2017), 2423 (2018) and 2432 (2018), the report of the SecretaryGeneral on the implementation of paragraph 4 of Security Council resolution 2423
(2018) (S/2019/207) and Council statements to the press issued as SC/13584 (2018),
SC/13667 (2019) and SC/13678 (2019), as well as the statement by the Security
Council Committee established pursuant to resolution 2374 (2017) concerning Mali
issued as SC/13638 (2018).
3.
The mission will visit Mali and Burkina Faso for meetings on the
implementation of the Agreement on Peace and Reconciliation in Mali, on the efforts
of the Group of Five for the Sahel and on the situation in Burkina Faso.
4.

The mission will be co-led by France, Germany and Côte d’Ivoire.

5.

The objectives of the mission are as follows:

Implementation of the Agreement on Peace and Reconciliation in Mali
(a) To prepare for the meeting of the Security Council aimed at discussing the
way forward regarding the implementation of the Agreement and t he future of the
United Nations Multidimensional Integrated Stabilization Mission in Mali
(MINUSMA), on the basis of the report of the Secretary-General requested in
paragraph 6 of resolution 2423 (2018);
(b) To assess the implementation of the priority measures referenced in
paragraph 4 of resolution 2423 (2018), as well as of the commitments contained in
the Pact for Peace signed between the Government of Mali and the United Nations,
and to enquire about steps envisaged by the Malian parties for the implementation of
outstanding measures;
(c) To follow up on the provisions of resolution 2423 (2018) expressing the
intent of the Council, in the absence of significant progress by 4 March 2019 (six
months after the presidential inauguration) in the implementation of the priority
measures and commitments mentioned above, to request the Se cretary-General to
provide options for potential significant adaptation of MINUSMA after the expiration
of its current mandate;
(d) To draw the attention of all relevant actors to the provisions of resolution
2423 (2018) expressing the intention of the Council to follow closely the timely
implementation of the road map adopted on 22 March 2018 and to respond with
measures pursuant to resolution 2374 (2017) should the parties not implement the
agreed-upon commitments within the announced time frame, and to further recall, in
this context, the listing decision of 20 December 2018 of the Security Council
Committee established pursuant to resolution 2374 (2017) concerning Mali;
(e) To assess the current situation in central Mali, be informed of the measures
taken by the Government of Mali to develop and implement an integrated response to
this situation and discuss how the international community, including MINUSMA,
can support these ongoing efforts;
(f) To insist on the necessity of ensuring adequate coordination, exchange of
information and, when applicable, support, within their respective mandates and
through existing mechanisms, between MINUSMA, the Malian Defence and Security

Forces, the Joint Force of the Group of Five for the Sahel, the French forces and the
European Union missions in Mali;
(g) To underline Council messages contained in resolutions 2423 (2018) and
2432 (2018) and Council statements to the press issued as SC/13667 (2019) and
SC/13678 (2019), as well as the statement by the Security Council Committee
established pursuant to resolution 2374 (2017) concerning Mali issued as SC/13638
(2018), including those related to the implementation of the Agreement; the
MINUSMA mandate; other security presences in Mali and the Sahel region; the
situation in central Mali; capacities of MINUSMA and the security and safety of
MINUSMA personnel; obligations under international law, human rights policy and
related aspects; environmental issues; and small arms and light weapons;
Group of Five for the Sahel
(h) To assess the level of operationalization of the Joint Force, as well as the
concurrent efforts implemented by the Group of Five in the political and development
fields;
(i) To hear from the Burkinabe rotating presidency of the Group of Five on
its priorities;
(j) To reiterate the intention of the Council to continue to monitor closely the
situation and to discuss international support for the Joint Force, after its full
operationalization and in parallel to its first operational results;
(k) To assess the implementation of the technical agreement between the
United Nations, the European Union and the Group of Five;
(l) To assess the level of operationalization of the compliance framework to
prevent, investigate, address and publicly report violations and abuses of human
rights law and violations of international humanitarian law related to the Joint Force;
to further assess the state of play in the deployment of the police component of the
Joint Force, with a view to ensuring the coordination necessary between the Joint
Force and the criminal justice systems of Group of Five States;
(m) To underline Council messages contained in resolution 2391 (2017) and
Council statement to the press issued as SC/13584 (2018), including those related to
the operationalization and status of the Joint Force; the Groupe de soutien; the
coordination of international support, bilateral support and United Nations support;
as well as obligations under international law and human rights policy;
Burkina Faso
(n) To assess the current security situation in Burkina Faso, including
regarding the evolving threat posed by terrorism and transnational organized crime;
(o) To demonstrate the Council’s interest in contributing to the prevention of
the further destabilization of the security situation in parts of the country that are
subject to cross-border challenges and that have a bearing on peace and security in
the Sahel;
(p) To contribute to the current assessment process led by the United Nations
in order to adapt its support on the ground to the evolving needs of the population, in
accordance with the priorities of the Government of Burkina Faso.
6.
The mission will seek to achieve these objectives through meetings with, among
others:

Burkina Faso

• Members of Burkinabe civil society organizations and representatives of

the protection of children)
• United Nations country team in Burkina Faso and the United Nations Office for
West Africa and the Sahel
7.
The mission will hold a commemorative ceremony to pay tribute to the
peacekeepers who lost their lives while serving in MINUSMA.
8.
The mission will seek to implement lessons learned from previous Security
Council missions, including regarding modalities of association of representatives
from relevant regional organizations.
