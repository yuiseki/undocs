Note by the President of the Security Council
Following consultations among the members of the Security Council, and in line
with the efforts of the United Nations and its Member States to promote the
advancement of women and greater gender inclusivity and equality in their policies
and practices, the members of the Council confirm, as a matter of existing Council
practice, that any reference to a male person in the Council ’s provisional rules of
procedure is deemed not to be limited to male persons. Thus, any such reference also
constitutes a reference to a female person, unless the context clearly indicates
otherwise.
