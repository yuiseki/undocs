Enclosure
Report on the operational support provided by French forces to
the United Nations Multidimensional Integrated Stabilization
Mission in the Central African Republic, from 1 October 2018 to
1 February 2019
Background document:
– Security Council resolution 2387 (2017) of 15 November 2017 (paragraphs 65
and 67) and Security Council resolution 2448 (2018) of 13 December 2018
(paragraphs 69 and 71).
The present report summarizes the support provided by French forces to the
United Nations Multidimensional Integrated Stabilization Mission in the Central
African Republic (MINUSCA) from 1 October 2018 to 1 February 2019.
With regard to operations, under resolution 2448 (2018), French fighter jets
stationed in N’Djamena can provide ad hoc air support to MINUSCA. On 11 January
2019, those jets carried out a show of force mission in support of the joint terminal
attack controllers of the Portuguese contingent of MINUSCA, which were then
operating in the Bambari region of the Central African Republic.
In addition to that operational mission, on 26 November 2018, French fighter
jets conducted a joint close air support training exercise with the joint terminal attack
controllers of the Portuguese contingent, to ensure that the two units are capable of
working together.
With regard to the overall partnership between French forces and MINUSCA,
in October and November 2018, at the request of the commander of the French forces
in Gabon, the French operational support detachment stationed in Bangui prov ided
technical training support to the MINUSCA Gabonese battalion in relation to troop
support and munitions management. The aim was to share good practices and provide
mentoring to the specialists of the Gabonese battalion.
French military air traffic controllers are stationed alongside Central African air
traffic controllers in the control tower during air traffic activity at Bangui -M’Poko
airport. They also work with Central African air traffic controllers to supervise
runway security prior to any air traffic activity with the help of a Rwandan battalion
from MINUSCA.
With regard to aeronautical engineering, the French operational support
detachment carried out temporary repairs (patching) to the taxiway on 9 and
10 December 2018. The taxiway is also used by MINUSCA aircraft when returning
to their parking area. During the week of 22 October 2018, French forces carried out
a technical assessment of the Bouar runway for French aircraft (CASA tactical
medical transport aircraft); the runway is of interest to the Central African Republic,
the European Union Training Mission in the Central African Republic and
MINUSCA.
With regard to logistics, the French operational support detachment is
permanently responsible for processing the cargo of the Gabonese batta lion and
receiving the personnel of that contingent who are in transit. As part of the transfer
for consideration of five armoured forward area vehicles to the Gabonese battalion,
the detachment conducted training for operators on 28 December 2018 and trai ning
for maintenance technicians from 29 January to 1 February 2019.
