Letter dated 29 April 2019 from the Secretary-General addressed
to the President of the Security Council
I have the honour to refer to Security Council resolution 2429 (2018) of 13 July
2018, in which the Council requested the Secretary-General and the Chairperson of
the African Union Commission, in consultation with the African Union-United
Nations Hybrid Operation in Darfur (UNAMID), to provide through a strategic
review by 1 May 2019, an assessment on the implementation of the reconfiguration
of UNAMID and the way forward. In view of this request, a joint African UnionUnited Nations review team visited Khartoum and Darfur in the Sudan and Addis
Ababa from 6 to 19 March 2019, to prepare the assessment report.
The political developments in the Sudan since 11 April 2019, however, have
prompted a careful reconsideration of the proposed way forward, based on the
potential impact on Darfur and on UNAMID of the evolving situation. I would
therefore like to request an extension of one month, until 1 June 2019, for the
submission of the assessment report, to enable the joint African Union-United Nations
review team to factor these developments into the proposal for consideration by the
Security Council.
I should be grateful if you would bring the present letter to the attention of the
members of the Security Council.
(Signed) António Guterres
