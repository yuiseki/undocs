Letter dated 6 September 2019 from the Secretary-General
addressed to the President of the Security Council
I wish to inform you of my intention to appoint Lieutenant General (retired)
Abhijit Guha (India) as the new Chair of the Redeployment Coordination Committee
and Head of the United Nations Mission to Support the Hudaydah Agreement
(UNMHA), at the level of Assistant Secretary-General, pursuant to Security Council
resolutions 2452 (2019) and 2481 (2019).
Lieutenant General Guha succeeds Lieutenant General Michael Lollesgaard,
who served as the Chair of the Redeployment Coordination Committee and Head of
UNMHA from 31 January to 31 July 2019. I am grateful to Lieutenant General
Lollesgaard for his dedicated and exemplary service during that period.
I should be grateful if you would bring this matter to the attention of the
members of the Security Council.
(Signed) António Guterres
