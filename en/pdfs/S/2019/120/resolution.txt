Note by the President of the Security Council
The members of the Security Council have agreed upon a schedule of meetings
on the United Nations Interim Administration Mission in Kosovo (UNMIK), with
concurrent reports of the Secretary-General to the Council. In 2019, the Council
intends to hold briefings on 7 February, in June and in October. As from 2020, the
Council intends to hold briefings twice a year (in April and October) on the issue. The
Council will continue to review this issue, taking into account the situation on the
ground.
