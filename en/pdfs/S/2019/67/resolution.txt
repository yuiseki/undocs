Letter dated 17 January 2019 from the Secretary-General
addressed to the President of the Security Council
Following the usual consultations, I have the honour to inform you of my
intention to appoint Huang Xia (China) as my Special Envoy for the Great Lakes
Region of Africa, replacing Said Djinnit (Algeria).
I take this opportunity to express my deep appreciation to Mr. Djinnit for his
dedicated service over the past several years.
I would be grateful if you could bring the present letter to the attention of the
members of the Security Council.
(Signed) António Guterres
