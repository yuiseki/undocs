Letter dated 24 September 2019 from the Secretary-General
addressed to the President of the Security Council
I have the honour to refer to paragraph 3 of Security Council resolution 2484
(2019), adopted on 29 August 2019, by which the Council decided to extend the
mandate of the Panel of Experts on Mali until 30 September 2020 and requested me
to take the necessary administrative measures to that effect.
Accordingly, I wish to inform you that, after consulting the Security Council
Committee established pursuant to resolution 2374 (2017) concerning Mali, I have
appointed the following experts:
Mr. Albert Kwokwo Barume, regional (Democratic Republic of the Congo)
Mr. Ruben de Koning, finance (Netherlands)
Mr. Aurélien Llorca, armed groups (France)
Ms. Amy Touré, humanitarian affairs (United States of America)
I have designated Mr. Barume to serve as Coordinator of the Panel.
I should be grateful if you would bring this matter to the attention of the
members of the Security Council.
(Signed) António Guterres
