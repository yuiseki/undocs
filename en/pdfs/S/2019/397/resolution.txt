Letter dated 15 May 2019 from the President of the
Security Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 9 May 2019 ( S/2019/396),
with reference to Security Council resolution 2444 (2018), in which the Council
requested that you conduct a technical assessment regarding the arms embargo on
Somalia, with options and recommendations, has been brought to the attention of the
members of the Security Council.
The members of the Council have taken note of the request contained in your
letter for the time frame for the submission of the technical assessment to be extended,
and they look forward to its submission by 30 July 2019.
(Signed) Dian Triansyah Djani
President of the Security Council
