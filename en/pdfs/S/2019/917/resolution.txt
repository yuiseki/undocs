Letter dated 29 November 2019 from the Secretary-General
addressed to the President of the Security Council
I have the honour to refer to the United Nations Organization Stabilization
Mission in the Democratic Republic of the Congo (MONUSCO), establish ed by
Security Council resolution 1925 (2010) and most recently extended by resolution
2463 (2019) for a period of nine months.
I would like to inform you of my intention to appoint Lieutenant General
Ricardo Augusto Ferreira Costa Neves (Brazil) as Force Commander of MONUSCO.
He succeeds Lieutenant General Elias Rodrigues Martins Filho (Brazil), who
completed his assignment on 31 October 2019. I would like to take this opportunity
to express my gratitude to Lieutenant General Martins Filho for his dedication and
effective leadership of the military component of MONUSCO.
I would be grateful if you could bring this matter to the attention of the members
of the Security Council.
(Signed) António Guterres
