Letter dated 17 September 2019 from the Secretary-General
addressed to the President of the Security Council
I have the honour to refer to the United Nations Integrated Office in Haiti
(BINUH), which was established by the Security Council in its resolution 2476 (2019).
I wish to inform you of my intention to appoint Helen Meagher La Lime (United
States of America) as my Special Representative for Haiti and Head of BINUH.
I should be grateful if you would bring this matter to the attention of the
members of the Security Council.
(Signed) António Guterres
