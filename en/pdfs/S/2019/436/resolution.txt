Letter dated 24 May 2019 from the President of the
Security Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 17 May 2019 ( S/2019/435)
concerning your intention to appoint Mr. James Swan (United States of America) as
your Special Representative for Somalia and Head of the United Nations Assistance
Mission in Somalia has been brought to the attention of the members of the Security
Council. They take note of the intention expressed in your letter.
(Signed) Dian Triansyah Djani
President of the Security Council
