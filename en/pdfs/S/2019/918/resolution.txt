Letter dated 3 December 2019 from the President of the
Security Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 29 November 2019
(S/2019/917) concerning your intention to appoint Lieutenant General Ricardo
Augusto Ferreira Costa Neves (Brazil) as Force Commander of the United Nations
Organization Stabilization Mission in the Democratic Republic of the Congo has been
brought to the attention of the members of the Security Council. They take note of
the intention expressed in your letter.
(Signed) Kelly Craft
President of the Security Council
