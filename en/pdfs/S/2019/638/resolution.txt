Letter dated 6 August 2019 from the President of the
Security Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 30 July 2019 (S/2019/637)
concerning the financing of the Residual Special Court for Sierra Leone for 2020 has
been brought to the attention of the members of the Security Council.
I wish to inform you that the members of the Security Council have taken note
of the intentions expressed in your letter on the understanding that the requested
subvention will be subsequently reimbursed from the voluntary contributions
received by the Residual Special Court and that the voluntary nature of the funding
arrangement of the Court will not be changed.
(Signed) Joanna Wronecka
President of the Security Council
