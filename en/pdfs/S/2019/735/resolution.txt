Letter dated 11 September 2019 from the President of the Security
Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 6 September 2019
(S/2019/734), concerning your intention to appoint Lieutenant General (retired)
Abhijit Guha (India) as Chair of the Redeployment Coordination Committee and
Head of the United Nations Mission to Support the Hudaydah Agreement, has been
brought to the attention of the members of the Security Council. They take note of
the intention expressed in your letter.
(Signed) Vassily A. Nebenzia
President of the Security Council
