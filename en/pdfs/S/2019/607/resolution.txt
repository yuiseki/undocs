Letter dated 29 July 2019 from the Secretary-General addressed to
the President of the Security Council
I have the honour to refer to paragraph 3 of Security Council resolution 2478
(2019), by which the Council decided to extend, until 1 August 2020, t he mandate of
the Group of Experts on the Democratic Republic of the Congo.
Accordingly, I wish to inform you that, after consulting with the Security
Council Committee established pursuant to resolution 1533 (2004) concerning the
Democratic Republic of the Congo, I have appointed the following experts:
– Mr. Nelson Alusala, natural resources/finance expert (Kenya )
– Mr. Raymond Debelle, arms (Belgium)
– Ms. Virginie Monchy, humanitarian affairs (France)
– Mr. Emmanuel Ngueyanouba, armed groups (Chad)
– Ms. Sophia Pickles, natural resources/finance expert (United Kingdom of Great
Britain and Northern Ireland)
– Mr. David Zounmenou, armed groups (Benin)
I have designated Mr. Zounmenou to serve as Coordinator of the Group.
I should be grateful if you could bring this matter to the attention of the members
of the Security Council.
(Signed) António Guterres
