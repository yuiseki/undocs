Letter dated 25 July 2019 from the President of the
Security Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 23 July 2019 ( S/2019/596),
concerning your intention to appoint Ms. Rosine Sori-Coulibaly (Burkina Faso) as
your Special Representative for Guinea-Bissau and Head of the United Nations
Integrated Peacebuilding Office in Guinea-Bissau at the level of Assistant SecretaryGeneral, has been brought to the attention of the members of the Security Council.
They take note of the intention expressed in your letter.
(Signed) Gustavo Meza-Cuadra
President of the Security Council
