Letter dated 19 February 2019 from the President of the
Security Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 15 February 2019
(S/2019/164) concerning your intention to appoint Major General Zia Ur Rehman
(Pakistan) as Force Commander of the United Nations Mission for the Referendum
in Western Sahara has been brought to the attention of the members of the Security
Council. They take note of the intention expressed in your letter.
(Signed) Anatolio Ndong Mba
President of the Security Council
