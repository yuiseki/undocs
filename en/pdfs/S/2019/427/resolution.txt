Letter dated 23 May 2019 from the President of the
Security Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 21 May 2019 (S/2019/ 426 )
concerning your intention to appoint Lieutenant General Shailesh Tinaikar (India) as
Force Commander of the United Nations Mission in South Sudan has been brought to
the attention of the members of the Security Council. They take note of the intention
expressed in your letter.
(Signed) Dian Triansyah Djani
President of the Security Council
