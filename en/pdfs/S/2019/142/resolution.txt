Letter dated 12 February 2019 from the Secretary-General
addressed to the President of the Security Council
I have the honour to refer to the United Nations Interim Security Force for Abyei
(UNISFA), established by Security Council resolution 1990 (2011) of 27 June 2011
and most recently extended in resolution 2445 (2018) of 15 November 2018.
Following the usual consultations, I should like to inform you of my intention
to appoint Major General Mehari Zewde Gebremariam (Ethiopia) as Force
Commander of UNISFA. He succeeds Major General Gebre Adhana Woldezgu
(Ethiopia), who will complete his assignment on 23 April 2019. I should like to take
this opportunity to express my gratitude to Major General Woldezgu for his dedication
and effective leadership of UNISFA.
I should be grateful if you would bring the present letter to the attention of the
members of the Security Council.
(Signed) António Guterres
