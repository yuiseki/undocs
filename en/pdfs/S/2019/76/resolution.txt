Letter dated 23 January 2019 from the President of the
Security Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 21 December 2018
(S/2019/75) concerning your intention to appoint Mankeur Ndiaye (Senegal) as your
Special Representative and Head of the United Nations Multidimensional Integra ted
Stabilization Mission in the Central African Republic has been brought to the
attention of the members of the Security Council. They take note of the intention
expressed in your letter.
(Signed) José Singer Weisinger
President of the Security Council
