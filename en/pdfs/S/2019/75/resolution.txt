Letter dated 21 December 2018 from the Secretary-General to the
President of the Security Council
I have the honour to refer to the United Nations Multidimensional Integrated
Stabilization Mission in the Central African Republic (MINUSCA), established by
Security Council resolution 2149 (2014) of 10 April 2014, whose mandate was
recently extended by resolution 2448 (2018) of 13 December 2018.
Following the usual consultations, I wish to inform you of my intention to
appoint Mr. Mankeur Ndiaye (Senegal) as my Special Representative for the Central
African Republic and Head of MINUSCA. Mr. Ndiaye shall succeed Mr. Parfait
Onanga-Anyanga (Gabon), who has been Head of the Mission since August 2015.
I would like to take this opportunity to express my deep gratitude to Mr. Onanga Anyanga for his continued support to the Central African Republic during the cris is
that has marked its recent history. Mr. Onanga-Anyanga has played a crucial role in
the ongoing peace process, as a member of the Panel of Facilitators of the African
Initiative for Peace and Reconciliation. I would like to also thank him for his
dedication, integrity and leadership of MINUSCA.
I should be grateful if you would bring this matter to the attention of the
members of the Security Council.
(Signed) António Guterres
