Letter dated 4 February 2019 from the President of the
Security Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 29 January 2019
(S/2019/107) concerning your intention to appoint Ms. Claudia Hoefer (Germany) as
a judge of the International Residual Mechanism for Criminal Tribunals has been
brought to the attention of the members of the Security Council. They take note of
the intention expressed in your letter.
(Signed) Anatolio Ndong Mba
President of the Security Council
