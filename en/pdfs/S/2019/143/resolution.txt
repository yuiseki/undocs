Letter dated 14 February 2019 from the President of the
Security Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 12 February 2019
(S/2019/142) concerning your intention to appoint Major General Mehari Zewde
Gebremariam (Ethiopia) as Force Commander of the United Nations Interim Security
Force for Abyei has been brought to the attention of the members of the Security
Council. They take note of the intention expressed therein.
(Signed) Anatolio Ndong Mba
President of the Security Council
