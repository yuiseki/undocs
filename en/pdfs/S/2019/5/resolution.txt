Letter dated 2 January 2019 from the Secretary-General
addressed to the President of the Security Council
I have the honour to refer to Security Council resolution 2441 (2018), adopted
on 5 November 2018, by which the Council decided to extend until 15 February 2020
the mandate of the Panel of Experts on Libya established pursuant to resolution 1973
(2011).
Accordingly, I wish to inform you that, after consulting with the Security
Council Committee established pursuant to resolution 1970 (2011) concerning Libya,
I have appointed the following experts:
Mr. Luis Antonio de Alburquerque Bacardit, maritime/transport expert (Spain)
Ms. Amanda Kadlec, armed groups expert (United States of America)
Mr. Moncef Kartas, arms expert (Tunisia)
Ms. Lipika Majumdar Roy Choudhury, finance expert (India)
Mr. Yassine Marjane, armed groups/regional expert (Morocco)
Mr. Adrian Wilkinson, arms expert (United Kingdom of Great Britain and
Northern Ireland)
I have also designated Ms. Majumdar Roy Choudhury to serve as Coordinator
of the Panel of Experts.
I should be grateful if you would bring this matter to the attention of the
members of the Security Council.
(Signed) António Guterres
