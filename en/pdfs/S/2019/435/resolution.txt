Letter dated 17 May 2019 from the Secretary-General addressed
to the President of the Security Council
I have the honour to refer to the United Nations Assistance Mission in Somalia
(UNSOM), which was established by the Security Council in its resolution 2102
(2013), adopted on 2 May 2013. Its mandate was most recently extended in resolution
2461 (2019), adopted on 27 March 2019.
I wish to inform you of my intention to appoint Mr. James Swan (United States
of America) as my Special Representative for Somalia and Head of UNSOM ,
succeeding Mr. Nicholas Haysom (South Africa). I should like to take this opportunity
to reiterate my deep appreciation to Mr. Haysom for his dedicated service with
UNSOM at a challenging time in Somalia.
I should be grateful if you would bring the present letter to the attention of the
members of the Security Council.
(Signed) António Guterres
