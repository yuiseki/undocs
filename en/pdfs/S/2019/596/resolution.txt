Letter dated 23 July 2019 from the Secretary-General addressed to
the President of the Security Council
I have the honour to refer to the United Nations Integrated Peacebuilding Office
in Guinea-Bissau (UNIOGBIS), which was established by the Security Council in its
resolution 1876 (2009) and whose mandate was most recently extended by its
resolution 2458 (2019), in which, inter alia, the Council called for the gradual
drawdown of UNIOGBIS and its exit no later than 31 December 2020.
I wish to inform you of my intention to appoint Ms. Rosine Sori -Coulibaly
(Burkina Faso) as my Special Representative for Guinea-Bissau and Head of
UNIOGBIS at the level of Assistant Secretary-General. She succeeds Mr. José Viegas
Filho (Brazil), who completed his assignment on 18 May 2019.
Following the successful holding of legislative elections in Guinea -Bissau on
10 March 2019 and the appointment of a Prime Minister on 22 June 2019, and in light
of the decision to hold a presidential election on 24 November 2019, the good offices
of and political facilitation by the United Nations will continue to be critical in the
coming months to support the efforts of the country to achieve further progress
towards the full implementation of the Conakry Agreement. The new Special
Representative and Head of UNIOGBIS will also have the responsibility of
implementing the overall mandate of the mission, as well as preparing and managing
its exit no later than 31 December 2020.
I should be grateful if you would bring this matter to the attention of the
Members of the Security Council.
(Signed) António Guterres
