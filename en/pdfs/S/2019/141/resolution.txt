Letter dated 14 February 2019 from the President of the
Security Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 11 February 2019
(S/2019/140) concerning your intention to increase the strength of the guard unit in
Mogadishu to a total of 625 personnel has been brought to the attention of the
members of the Security Council. They take note of the intention expressed in your
letter.
(Signed) Anatolio Ndong Mba
President of the Security Council
