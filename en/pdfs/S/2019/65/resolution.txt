Letter dated 21 January 2019 from the President of the
Security Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to refer to Security Council resolution 1646 (2005), adopted
on 20 December 2005, in which the Council, in keeping with its resolution 1645
(2005), adopted on the same date, decided that the permanent members listed in
Article 23, paragraph 1, of the Charter of the United Nations would be members of
the Organizational Committee of the Peacebuilding Commission and that, in addition,
the Council would annually select two of its elected members to participate in the
Organizational Committee.
I therefore have the honour to inform you that, following informal consultations,
the members of the Security Council have agreed on the selection of Côte d’Ivoire
and Peru as the two elected members of the Council to participate in the
Organizational Committee for a term of one year, until the end of 2019.
(Signed) José Singer Weisinger
President of the Security Council
