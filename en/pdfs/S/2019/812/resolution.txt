Letter dated 11 October 2019 from the President of the
Security Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 17 September 2019
(S/2019/811) concerning your intention to appoint Helen Meagher La Lime (United
States of America) as your Special Representative for Haiti and Head of the United
Nations Integrated Office in Haiti has been brought to the attention of the members
of the Security Council. They take note of the intention expressed in your letter,
recalling Article 101 (3) of the Charter of the United Nations.
(Signed) Jerry Matthews Matjila
President of the Security Council
