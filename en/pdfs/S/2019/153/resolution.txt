Enclosure
Report to the United Nations on Kosovo Force operations
1.
The present report is submitted in accordance with Security Council resolution
1244 (1999) and covers the Kosovo Force (KFOR) activities during the period from
1 October 2018 to 31 December 2018.
2.
As at 31 December 2018, the total number of KFOR troops in theatre remained
approximately 3,800, from 20 North Atlantic Treaty Organization (NATO) and
8 non-NATO troop-contributing nations. In October 2018, Montenegro joined the
KFOR mission, while Estonia withdrew.
3.
During the reporting period, there were no major security incidents and KFOR
was not required to intervene as a third responder.
Security situation update and operations
4.
During the reporting period, there was no significant change to the security
situation in Kosovo, which remained generally stable but fragile, with political
challenges and tensions. Progress is needed in the European Union -facilitated
dialogue between Belgrade and Pristina in order to improve the current situation.
5.
KFOR continued to conduct routine framework operations in the KFOR area of
operations, combined with coordinated and comprehensive engagement activities by
key leaders focused on the northern part of Kosovo due to increased tensi ons. All
events observed during the reporting period occurred peacefully, with no impact on
the KFOR mission. KFOR maintained a flexible force posture to be able to respond
rapidly and effectively, ensuring the maintenance of a safe and secure environment.
6.
On 28 November 2018, KFOR organized the change of command ceremony at
KFOR headquarters, with Major General Lorenzo D’Addario (Italy) assuming
command from Major General Salvatore Cuoci (Italy).
7.
KFOR maintains its close cooperation with other security providers in the
Balkans joint operations area. From 11 to 19 October, KFOR conducted the exercise
Silver Sabre 2018-2, bringing together the European Union, the United Nations and
different agencies in Kosovo, in order to assess and improve capabilitie s and
coordination of security responders in natural disasters and large -scale emergencies.
Summary
8.
During the reporting period, KFOR contributed to a safe and secure environment
as part of a comprehensive international effort. The close coordinati on and
cooperation at the tactical level between KFOR, the European Union Rule of Law
Mission in Kosovo and Kosovo police continue to be effective.
