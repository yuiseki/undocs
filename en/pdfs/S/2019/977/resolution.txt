Letter dated 16 December 2019 from the Secretary-General
addressed to the President of the Security Council
I have the honour to refer to paragraph 29 of Security Council resolution 2498
(2019), by which the Council renewed the mandate of the Panel of Experts on Somalia
until 15 December 2020.
Accordingly, I wish to inform you that, after consulting the Security Council
Committee pursuant to resolution 751 (1992) concerning Somalia, I have appointed
the following experts:
Ms. Natascha Hryckow, maritime/regional (Australia)
Ms. Nazanine Moshiri, armed groups (Islamic Republic of Iran)
Mr. Brian O’Sullivan, armed groups/natural resources (Ireland)
Mr. Matthew Rosbottom, finance (United Kingdom of Great Britain and
Northern Ireland)
Mr. Richard Zabot, arms (France)
I have designated Ms. Hryckow to serve as Coordinator of the Panel.
The remaining expert (humanitarian affairs) will be appointed as soon as
possible.
I should be grateful if you would bring this matter to the attention of the
members of the Security Council.
(Signed) António Guterres
