Letter dated 19 February 2019 from the Secretary-General
addressed to the President of the Security Council
I have the honour to refer to your letter dated 4 February 2019 in which you
concurred with my proposal to appoint Ms. Claudia Hoefer (Germany) as a judge of
the International Residual Mechanism for Criminal Tribunals to replace Judge
Christoph Flügge, who resigned with effect from 7 January 2019.
I have received a corresponding letter from the President of the General
Assembly (see annex). Accordingly, I am proceeding to appoint Ms. Hoefer as a judge
of the Mechanism with immediate effect.
In accordance with article 10, paragraph 2, of the Statute of the Mechanism, the
appointment of Ms. Hoefer is effective for the remainder of the term of office that
Judge Flügge would have served, that is, until 30 June 2020.
I should be grateful if you would bring the present letter and its annex to the
attention of the members of the Security Council.
(Signed) António Guterres

Letter dated 13 February 2019 from the President of the
General Assembly addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to refer to your letter dated 29 January 2019 concerning the
nomination of Ms. Claudia Hoefer (Germany) as a judge of the International Residual
Mechanism for Criminal Tribunals to replace Judge Christoph Flügge for the
remainder of the term of office, which ends on 30 June 2020.
Pursuant to article 10, paragraph 2, of the Statute of the Mechanism and in
accordance with article 9, paragraph 1, of the same Statute, I have reviewed the
qualifications of Ms. Hoefer and I am pleased to infor m you that I hereby concur with
the proposed appointment.
(Signed) María Fernanda Espinosa Garcés
