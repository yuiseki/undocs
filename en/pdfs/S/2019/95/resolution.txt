Letter dated 28 January 2019 from the Secretary-General
addressed to the President of the Security Council
I wish to inform you of my intention to appoint Lieutenant General Michael
Anker Lollesgaard (Denmark) as the new Chair of the Redeployment Coordination
Committee (RCC) and Head of the United Nations Mission to Support the Hudaydah
Agreement (UNMHA), at the level of Assistant Secretary-General, pursuant to
Security Council resolution 2452 (2019).
Lieutenant General Lollesgaard succeeds Major General (retired) Patrick
Cammaert, who led the advance team and has been serving as the Chair of RCC and
Head of UNMHA, pursuant to Security Council resolutions 2451 (2018) and 2452
(2019). I should like to take this opportunity to express my deep appreciation to
General Cammaert for his tireless efforts in laying the groundwork for UNMHA.
I should be grateful if you would bring this matter to the attention of the
members of the Security Council.
(Signed) António Guterres
