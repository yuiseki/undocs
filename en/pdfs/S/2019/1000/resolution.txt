Letter dated 27 December 2019 from the President of the
Security Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 11 December 2019
(S/2019/999) concerning your intention to appoint Lord Iain Bonomy (United
Kingdom of Great Britain and Northern Ireland) as a judge of the International
Residual Mechanism for Criminal Tribunals has been brought to the attention of the
members of the Security Council. They take note of the intention expressed in your
letter.
(Signed) Cherith Norman-Chalet
President of the Security Council
