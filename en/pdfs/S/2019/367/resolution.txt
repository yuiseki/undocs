Letter dated 2 May 2019 from the President of the
Security Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 29 April 2019
(S/2019/366) with reference to Security Council resolution 2429 (2018), in which the
Council requested the Secretary-General and the Chairperson of the African Union
Commission to provide an assessment on the implementation of the reconfiguration
of the African Union-United Nations Hybrid Operation in Darfur (UNAMID) has
been brought to the attention of the members of the Council.
The members of the Security Council have taken note of the request contained
in your letter that the time frame for the submission of the assessment report be
extended and they look forward to the submission by 1 June 2019.
(Signed) Dian Triansyah Djani
President of the Security Council
