Official communiqué of the 4590th (closed) meeting of the
Security Council
Held in private at Headquarters, New York, on Monday,
29 July 2002, at 10.30 a.m.
In accordance with rule 55 of the provisional rules of procedure of the Security
Council, the following communiqué was issued through the Secretary-General in
place of a verbatim record:
“At its 4590th meeting, held in private on 29 July 2002, the Security
Council considered the item entitled ‘The situation in Georgia’.
“In accordance with the relevant provisions of the Charter and rule 37 of
the Council’s provisional rules of procedure, the President invited the Minister
of Special Affairs of Georgia.
“The members of the Council and the Minister of Special Affairs of
Georgia had a constructive discussion.”
