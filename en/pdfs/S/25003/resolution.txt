NOTE BY THE PRESIDENT OF THE SECURITY COUNCIL
Following consultations with the members of the Security Council, the
President of the Council made the following statement, on behalf of the
Council, at its 3153rd meeting, held on 22 December 1992, in connection with
the Council's consideration of the item entitled "The situation in Cambodia":
"The Security Council strongly condemns the illegal detention of
UNTAC personnel by elements of the Party of Democratic Kampuchea and acts
of threat and intimidation against these personnel. It demands that such
actions and any other hostile acts against UNTAC cease immediately, and
that all parties take all action necessary to safeguard the lives and the
security of UNTAC personnel throughout Cambodia.
"The Council urges all the parties to abide scrupulously by their
obligations under the Paris Agreement to cooperate fully with UNTAC and
to respect all the relevant resolutions of the Security Council."
