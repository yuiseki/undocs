Corrigendum

Page 12, fourth paragraph
Should read
We were pleased at the Secretary-General’s initiative to request the support of the North Atlantic Treaty
Organization (NATO) in order to have recourse to aerial power in the defence of UNPROFOR personnel and for the
protection of the safe areas, in conformity with Security Council resolutions. Today NATO’s decision, at the request
of the Secretary-General, to extend the use of aerial power to the territory of Croatia, in defence of UNPROFOR in the
safe area of Bihac, in Bosnia and Herzegovina, seems an equally wise decision. The Serbs, be they from Bosnia or
from Croatia, should not forget that the terms of the decisions of the Security Council and NATO are still valid; nor
should they harbour any doubts about the determination of the United Nations and NATO to apply those decisions
when necessary.
