Official communiqué of the 6443rd (closed) meeting of the
Security Council
Held in private at Headquarters, New York, on Friday,
10 December 2010, at 3 p.m.
In accordance with rule 55 of the provisional rules of procedure of the Security
Council, the following communiqué was issued through the Secretary-General in
place of a verbatim record:
“On 10 December 2010, the Security Council, pursuant to annex II, sections A
and B of resolution 1353 (2001), held its 6443rd meeting in private with the troopand police-contributing countries to the United Nations Mission in the Central
African Republic and Chad.
“The Security Council and the troop- and police-contributing countries heard
briefings under rule 39 of its provisional rules of procedure by Mr. Youssef
Mahmoud, Special Representative of the Secretary-General and Head of the United
Nations Mission in the Central African Republic and Chad and, via video
teleconference, from Major General Elhadji Mouhamedou Kandji, Force
Commander of the United Nations Mission in the Central African Republic and
Chad and Police Commissioner Mamadou Mountaga Diallo, Head of the Police
Component of the United Nations Mission in the Central African Republic and
Chad.
“Members of the Council, Mr. Mahmoud, Major General Kandji, Police
Commissioner Diallo and representatives of participating contributing countries had
an exchange of views.”
