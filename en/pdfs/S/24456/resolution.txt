NOTE BY THE PRESIDENT OF THE SECURITY COUNCIL
At its 3107th meeting, on 17 August 1992, following the adoption of
resolution 772 (1992), in connection with the Council's consideration of the
item entitled, "The question of South Africa", the President of the Security
Council made the following statement on behalf of the Council:
"In connection with the resolution just adopted, I have been
authorized to make the following statement on behalf of the Council:
"It is the understanding of the members of the Council that the
Secretary-General will consult the Council on the number of
observers he has the intention to deploy from time to time."
