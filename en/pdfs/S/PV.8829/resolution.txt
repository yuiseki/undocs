The meeting was called to order at 11.40 a.m.
Expression of thanks to the outgoing President
The President: I should like to take this
opportunity to pay tribute, on behalf of the Council,
to His Excellency Mr. Nicolas de Rivière, Permanent
Representative of France to the United Nations, for
his service as President of the Security Council for the
month of July. I am sure I speak for all members of the
Council in expressing deep appreciation to Ambassador
de Rivière and his team for the great diplomatic skill
with which they conducted the Council’s business
last month.
