The meeting was called to order at 10.10 a.m.
Adoption of the agenda
The agenda was adopted.
The situation in Afghanistan
The President: Under rule 37 of the Council’s
provisional rules of procedure, I invite the
representative of Afghanistan to participate in this
meeting.
The Security Council will now begin its
consideration of the item on its agenda. Members of
the Council have before them document S/2011/630,
which contains the text of a draft resolution submitted
by Germany.
It is my understanding that the Council is ready
to proceed to the vote on the draft resolution before it.

2

I shall put the draft resolution to the vote now.
A vote was taken by show of hands.
In favour:
Bosnia and Herzegovina, Brazil, China,
Colombia, France, Gabon, Germany, India,
Lebanon, Nigeria, Portugal, Russian Federation,
South Africa, United Kingdom of Great Britain
and Northern Ireland and United States of
America.
The President: There were 15 votes in favour.
The draft resolution has been adopted unanimously as
resolution 2011 (2011).
The Security Council has thus concluded the
present stage of its consideration of the item on its
agenda. The Council will remain seized of the matter.
The meeting rose at 10.15 a.m.
