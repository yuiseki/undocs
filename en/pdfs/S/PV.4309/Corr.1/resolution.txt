Insert the following before Adoption of the agenda:
The President: Before I start this morning’s business, I would like members
of the Council to be aware that I have had a request from the delegation of the
Rwandese Republic to observe a minute of silence in remembrance of the victims of
genocide in Rwanda, on the seventh anniversary of that genocide. If there is no
objection from members of the Council, I would like to ask everyone in the room to
stand for a moment to commemorate the victims of genocide in Rwanda in 1994.
The Council observed a minute of silence.
