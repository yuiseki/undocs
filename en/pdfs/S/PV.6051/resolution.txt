The situation in Liberia
Letter dated 12 December 2008 from the
Acting Chairman of the Security Council
Committee established pursuant to resolution
1521 (2003) concerning Liberia addressed to
the President of the Security Council
(S/2008/785)
The President: The Security Council will now
begin its consideration of the item on its agenda. The
Council is meeting in accordance with the
understanding reached in its prior consultations.
Members of the Council have before them
document S/2008/797, which contains the text of a
draft resolution submitted by the United States of
America. I wish to draw the attention of members to
document S/2008/785, containing the text of a letter
dated 12 December 2008 from the Acting Chairman of
the Security Council Committee established pursuant
to resolution 1521 (2003) concerning Liberia addressed

2

to the President of the Security Council, transmitting
the report of the Panel of Experts on Liberia.
It is my understanding that the Council is ready
to proceed to the vote on the draft resolution before it.
Unless I hear any objection, I shall now put the draft
resolution to the vote.
There being no objection, it is so decided.
A vote was taken by show of hands.
In favour:
Belgium, Burkina Faso, China, Costa Rica,
Croatia, France, Indonesia, Italy, Libyan Arab
Jamahiriya, Panama, Russian Federation, South
Africa, United Kingdom of Great Britain and
Northern Ireland, United States of America,
Viet Nam
The President: There were 15 votes in favour.
The draft resolution has been adopted unanimously as
resolution 1854 (2008).
The Security Council has thus concluded the
present stage of its consideration of the item on its
agenda. The Council will remain seized of the matter.
The meeting rose at 10.15 a.m.
