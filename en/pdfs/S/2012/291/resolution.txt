Letter dated 1 May 2012 from the Secretary-General addressed to
the President of the Security Council
I have the honour to inform you of my intention to appoint Mr. Jens Anders
Toyberg-Frandzen of Denmark to serve as my Executive Representative to Sierra
Leone and Head of the United Nations Integrated Peacebuilding Office in the
country.
I should be grateful if you would bring the present letter to the attention of the
members of the Security Council.
(Signed) BAN Ki-moon
