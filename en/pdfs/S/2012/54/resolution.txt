Letter dated 20 January 2012 from the President of the
Security Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 18 January 2012
(S/2012/53) concerning your intention to appoint Major General Paolo Serra (Italy)
as Head of Mission and Force Commander of the United Nations Interim Force in
Lebanon has been brought to the attention of the members of the Security Council.
They take note of the intention and information contained in your letter.
(Signed) Baso Sangqu
President of the Security Council
