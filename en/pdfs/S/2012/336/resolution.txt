Letter dated 17 May 2012 from the President of the
Security Council to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 11 May 2012
(S/2012/335) concerning your intention to appoint a selection panel, in accordance
with article 2, paragraph 5 (d) of the annex to resolution 1757 (2007), has been
brought to the attention of the members of the Security Council. They take note of
the intention and information contained in your letter.
(Signed) Agshin Mehdiyev
President of the Security Council
