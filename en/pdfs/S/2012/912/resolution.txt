Letter dated 7 December 2012 from the Secretary-General
addressed to the President of the Security Council
I have the honour to inform you that Mr. John Everard (United Kingdom of
Great Britain and Northern Ireland), whom I appointed on 27 June 2012 to serve as
expert on regional issues on the Panel of Experts established pursuant to Security
Council resolution 1874 (2009), has withdrawn from the Panel. Accordingly, I wish
to inform you that, in consultation with the Security Council Committee established
pursuant to resolution 1718 (2006), I have appointed Mr. Martin Uden (United
Kingdom of Great Britain and Northern Ireland), to serve on the Panel of Experts
and to carry out the tasks specified in paragraph 26 of resolution 1874 (2009).
I should be grateful if you would bring this information to the attention of the
members of the Security Council.
(Signed) BAN Ki-moon
