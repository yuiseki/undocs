Letter dated 21 June 2012 from the President of the
Security Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 18 June 2012
(S/2012/469) concerning your intention to establish a small Office of the Special
Adviser to the Secretary-General on Yemen for an initial period of 12 months has
been brought to the attention of the members of the Security Council. They take
note of the intention expressed in your letter.
(Signed) Li Baodong
President of the Security Council
