Letter dated 5 June 2012 from the President of the Security Council
to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 1 June 2012
(S/2012/397) concerning your intention to appoint Mr. Parfait Onanga-Anyanga
(Gabon) as your Special Representative for Burundi and Head of the United Nations
Office in Burundi has been brought to the attention of the members of the Security
Council. They take note of the intention expressed in your letter.
(Signed) Li Baodong
President of the Security Council
