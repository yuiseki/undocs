Letter dated 6 December 2012 from the President of the
Security Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 3 December 2012
(S/2012/908) concerning your intention to appoint Major General Delali Johnson
Sakyi (Ghana) as Force Commander of the United Nations Mission in South Sudan
(UNMISS) has been brought to the attention of the members of the Security
Council. They take note of the intention expressed in your letter.
(Signed) Mohammed Loulichki
President of the Security Council
