Letter dated 3 December 2012 from the Secretary-General
addressed to the President of the Security Council
I have the honour to refer to the United Nations Mission in South Sudan
(UNMISS) which was established by the Security Council under its resolution 1996
(2011) and most recently extended by resolution 2057 (2012).
Following the usual consultations, I would like to inform you of my intention
to appoint Major General Delali Johnson Sakyi (Ghana) as Force Commander of
UNMISS. Major General Sakyi will replace Major General Moses Bisong Obi
(Nigeria), whose tour of duty ended on 18 November 2012. I would like to take this
opportunity to express my gratitude to Major General Obi for his contribution to
both the United Nations Mission in the Sudan (UNMIS) and UNMISS.
I would be grateful if you would bring the present letter to the attention of the
members of the Security Council.
(Signed) BAN Ki-moon
