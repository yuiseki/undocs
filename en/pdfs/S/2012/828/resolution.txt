Letter dated 13 November 2012 from the President of the
Security Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 9 November 2012
(S/2012/827) concerning the Strategic Review process in Somalia has been brought
to the attention of the members of the Security Council.
The members have taken note of the request contained in your letter that the
time frame for the issuance of the report be extended, and they look forward to the
submission of the report by 31 January 2013.
(Signed) Hardeep Singh Puri
President of the Security Council
