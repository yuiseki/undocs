Letter dated 8 June 2012 from the President of the
Security Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 6 June 2012
(S/2012/418) concerning your intention to appoint Major General Young-Bum Choi
(Republic of Korea) as Chief Military Observer and Head of Mission of the United
Nations Military Observer Group in India and Pakistan has been brought to the
attention of the members of the Security Council. They take note of the intention
expressed in your letter.
(Signed) Li Baodong
President of the Security Council
