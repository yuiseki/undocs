Letter dated 13 June 2012 from the President of the Security Council
addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 12 April 2012
(S/2012/441) concerning your decision to appoint Mr. Wolfgang Weisbrod-Weber
(Germany) as your Special Representative for Western Sahara and Head of the
United Nations Mission for the Referendum in Western Sahara, has been brought to
the attention of the members of the Security Council. They take note of the intention
expressed in your letter.
(Signed) Li Baodong
President of the Security Council
