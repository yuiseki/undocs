Letter dated 28 November 2012 from the President of the
Security Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 8 November 2012
concerning the financing of the Special Court for Sierra Leone and the Residual
Special Court for Sierra Leone (S/2012/891) has been brought to the attention of the
members of the Security Council.
Having consulted the members of the Security Council, I wish to inform you
that they have taken note with certain reservations of the intention expressed in the
letter with respect to your proposal on alternate means of financing of the Special
Court for Sierra Leone, and that they request the United Nations Secretariat, the
Management Committee and the Registrar and other senior officials of the Special
Court to intensify their efforts to balance the budget and fund the activities of the
Court through voluntary contributions. I wish to further inform you that there is no
agreement with respect to the possible need for alternate means of financing of the
Residual Special Court for Sierra Leone.
(Signed) Hardeep Singh Puri
President of the Security Council
