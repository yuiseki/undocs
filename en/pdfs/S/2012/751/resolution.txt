Letter dated 9 October 2012 from the President of the Security
Council to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 5 October 2012
(S/2012/750), concerning your intention to appoint Mr. Romano Prodi (Italy) as
your Special Envoy for the Sahel, has been brought to the attention of the members
of the Security Council. They take note of the intention expressed in your letter.
(Signed) Gert Rosenthal
President of the Security Council
