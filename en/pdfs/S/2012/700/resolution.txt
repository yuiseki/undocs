Letter dated 11 September 2012 from the President of the
Security Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 10 September 2012
(S/2012/699) concerning your intention to appoint Mr. Tarek Mitri (Lebanon) as
your Special Representative for Libya and Head of the United Nations Support
Mission in Libya has been brought to the attention of the members of the Security
Council. They take note of the intention expressed in your letter.
(Signed) Peter Wittig
President of the Security Council
