Letter dated 6 April 2012 from the Secretary-General addressed to
the President of the Security Council
Further to the briefing by the Joint Special Envoy to the Security Council on
2 April 2012, and with the agreement of the Government of the Syrian Arab
Republic, a United Nations team consisting of staff from the Office of the Joint
Special Envoy, the Department of Peacekeeping Operations and the Department of
Field Support was dispatched to the Syrian Arab Republic to consult with the
Government and engage in technical preparations for the potential deployment of
observers to supervise and monitor a cessation of armed violence under the six-point
plan, should this be achieved. The team, led by Major General Robert Mood
(Norway), arrived in Damascus on 5 April 2012 and started its work.
It has now become clear that in view of the range of work required, it is
necessary to augment the visiting United Nations team with six additional
personnel. To expedite their deployment under tight deadlines, I intend to draw from
United Nations personnel who are already in the region, serving with the United
Nations Truce Supervision Organization (UNTSO). For the time of their service in
the visiting United Nations team in Syria, those officers will not be part of UNTSO
and will not have operational tasks. Rather, they will form part of the United
Nations visiting team and will work solely on tasks related to it, i.e., to consulting
and engaging in technical preparations for the potential deployment of United
Nations observers.
(Signed) BAN Ki-moon
