Letter dated 10 September 2012 from the Secretary-General
addressed to the President of the Security Council
I have the honour to refer to the United Nations Support Mission in Libya
(UNSMIL), established by the Security Council in its resolution 2009 (2011).
Following the usual consultations, I should like to inform you of my intention
to appoint Mr. Tarek Mitri (Lebanon) as my new Special Representative for Libya
and Head of UNSMIL. He will succeed Mr. Ian Martin (United Kingdom of Great
Britain and Northern Ireland), who will complete his assignment on 14 October
2012. I should like to take this opportunity to express my appreciation to Mr. Martin
for his dedicated service during his tenure at UNSMIL.
I should be grateful if you would bring this matter to the attention of the
members of the Security Council.
(Signed) BAN Ki-moon
