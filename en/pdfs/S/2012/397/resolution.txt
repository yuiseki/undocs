Letter dated 1 June 2012 from the Secretary-General to the
President of the Security Council
I have the honour to refer to resolution 2027 (2011) of the Security Council
dated 20 December 2011, in which the Council extended the mandate of the United
Nations Office in Burundi (BNUB) until 15 February 2013.
In that connection, and following the appointment of Ms. Karin Landgren, my
current Special Representative for Burundi and Head of BNUB, as the new Special
Representative for Liberia, I wish to inform you of my intention to appoint
Mr. Parfait Onanga-Anyanga (Gabon) as my new Special Representative for
Burundi and Head of BNUB. Mr. Onanga-Anyanga is currently serving as Director
of the Office of the Deputy Secretary-General.
(Signed) BAN Ki-moon
