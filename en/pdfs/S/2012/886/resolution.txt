Letter dated 27 November 2012 from the President of the
Security Council to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 23 November 2012
(S/2012/885) concerning your intention to appoint Major General Leonard Muriuki
Ngondi (Kenya) as your Force Commander for the United Nations Mission in
Liberia has been brought to the attention of the members of the Security Council.
They take note of the intention expressed in your letter.
(Signed) Hardeep Singh Puri
President of the Security Council
