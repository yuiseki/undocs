draft resolution
The Security Council)
Having examined the question of "Aggressive acts by the Air Force of the
United States of America against the Soviet Union, creating a threat to
universal peace",
Noting that violations of the sovereignty of other states are incompatible
with the principles and purposes of the Charter of the United Nations,
Considering that such actions create a threat to universal peace,
1. Condemns the incursions by United States aircraft into the territory
of other States and regards them as aggressive acts;
2.
Request~ the Government of the United States of America to adopt
immediate measures to halt such actions and to prevent their recurrence.
