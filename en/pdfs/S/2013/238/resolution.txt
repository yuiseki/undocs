Letter dated 19 April 2013 from the President of the
Security Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 15 April 2013
(S/2013/237) concerning the issuance of the report requested in the statement by the
President of the Security Council (S/PRST/2011/19) on the assessment of the United
Nations support for security sector reform has been brought to the attention of the
members of the Council.
The members of the Council have taken note of the fact that there are delays in
the issuance of the aforementioned report and look forward to receiving it as close
to the due date as possible.
(Signed) Eugène-Richard Gasana
President of the Security Council
