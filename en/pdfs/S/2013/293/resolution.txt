Letter dated 16 May 2013 from the President of the Security Council
addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 14 May 2013
(S/2013/292) concerning your intention to appoint Mr. Haile Menkerios (South
Africa) as Head of the United Nations Office to the African Union and your Special
Representative to the African Union at the level of Under-Secretary-General has
been brought to the attention of the members of the Security Council. They take
note of the intention expressed in your letter.
(Signed) Kodjo Menan
President of the Security Council
