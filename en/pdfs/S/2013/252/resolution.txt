Letter dated 29 April 2013 from the President of the
Security Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 25 April 2013
(S/2013/251) concerning your intention to appoint Nicholas Kay (United Kingdom
of Great Britain and Northern Ireland) as your Special Representative for Somalia
and Head of the new Special Political Mission in Somalia has been brought to the
attention of the members of the Security Council. They take note of the intention
expressed in your letter.
(Signed) Eugène-Richard Gasana
President of the Security Council
