Note by the President of the Security Council
Following consultations among the members of the Security Council, it has
been agreed that issues pertaining to the Sudan and South Sudan, including the
African Union/United Nations Hybrid Operation in Darfur (UNAMID), the United
Nations Interim Security Force for Abyei (UNISFA), the United Nations Mission in
South Sudan (UNMISS) and Council resolution 2046 (2012), will, as from
11 November 2013, be considered under the agenda item entitled “Reports of the
Secretary-General on the Sudan and South Sudan”. In addition, members of the
Security Council agreed to amend the reporting period established in paragraph 6 of
resolution 2046 (2012) to one-month intervals.
