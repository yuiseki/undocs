Letter dated 4 April 2013 from the President of the Security Council
addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 2 April 2013
(S/2013/215) concerning the report requested by the Security Council in its
resolution 2088 (2013) has been brought to the attention of the members of the
Council.
The members of the Council have taken note of the request contained in your
letter that the time frame for the issuance of the report be extended, and look
forward to submission of the report by 30 April 2013.
(Signed) Eugène-Richard Gasana
President of the Security Council
