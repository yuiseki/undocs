Letter dated 12 June 2013 from the Secretary-General addressed
to the President of the Security Council
I have the honour to refer to the United Nations Multidimensional Integrated
Stabilization Mission in Mali (MINUSMA), established by Security Council
resolution 2100 (2013) of 25 April 2013.
Following the usual consultations, I would like to inform you of my intention
to appoint Major General Jean Bosco Kazura (Rwanda) as Force Commander of
MINUSMA. Major General Kazura will assume command on 1 July 2013, when, in
accordance with resolution 2100 (2013) and subject to review by the Council, the
authority is scheduled to be transferred from the African-led International Support
Mission in Mali to MINUSMA.
I would be grateful if you would bring the present letter to the attention of the
members of the Security Council.
(Signed) BAN Ki-moon
