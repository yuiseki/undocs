Letter dated 26 August 2013 from the President of the
Security Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 22 August 2013
(S/2013/507) concerning your intention to appoint Major General Imam Edy
Mulyono (Indonesia) as MINURSO Force Commander has been brought to the
attention of the members of the Security Council. They take note of the intention
expressed in your letter.
(Signed) María Cristina Perceval
President of the Security Council
