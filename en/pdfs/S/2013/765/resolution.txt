Letter dated 24 December 2013 from the President of the
Security Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 20 December 2013
(S/2013/764) concerning the deployment of a static United Nations Guard Unit to
strengthen the security of the United Nations Assistance Mission in Somalia
(UNSOM) compound within Mogadishu International Airport, has been brought to
the attention of the Security Council. The Council takes note of the information
contained in your letter and the arrangements proposed therein.
(Signed) Gérard Araud
President of the Security Council
