Letter dated 18 March 2013 from the President of the
Security Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 15 March 2013
(S/2013/166) concerning your intention to appoint Ms. Mary Robinson (Ireland) as
your Special Envoy for the Great Lakes Region has been brought to the attention of
the members of the Security Council. They take note of the intention expressed in
your letter.
(Signed) Vitaly Churkin
President of the Security Council
