Letter dated 15 March 2013 from the Secretary-General addressed
to the President of the Security Council
I have the honour to refer to Security Council resolution 2076 (2012), by
which I was requested to report on options for high-level dialogue between relevant
parties to address short-term and long-term causes underlying the political, security
and humanitarian crises in the eastern Democratic Republic of the Congo, including
the option of the possible designation of a special envoy.
Further to the signature of the Peace, Security and Cooperation Framework for
the Democratic Republic of the Congo and the region on 24 February 2013 in Addis
Ababa, and following the usual consultations, I wish to inform you that it is my
intention to appoint Ms. Mary Robinson (Ireland) as my Special Envoy for the Great
Lakes Region.
I should be grateful if you would bring this letter to the attention of the
members of the Security Council.
(Signed) BAN Ki-moon
