Letter dated 11 January 2013 from the President of the
Security Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 9 January 2013
(S/2013/15) concerning your report pursuant to paragraph 14 of resolution 2065
(2012) regarding the United Nations Integrated Peacebuilding Office in Sierra
Leone (UNIPSIL), has been brought to the attention of the members of the Council.
The members of the Council have taken note of the request contained in your
letter that the time frame for the issuance of the report be extended and that it will
be available on 28 February 2013.
(Signed) Masood Khan
President of the Security Council
