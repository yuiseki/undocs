Letter dated 17 October 2013 from the Secretary-General
addressed to the President of the Security Council
This is to inform you that Mr. Kenichiro Matsubayashi (Japan), whom I
appointed on 26 June 2013 to serve on the Panel of Experts established pursuant to
Security Council resolution 1929 (2010) as the expert on marine transport and
immigration issues, has withdrawn from the Panel. Accordingly, I wish to inform
you that, in consultation with the Security Council Committee established pursuant
to resolution 1737 (2006), I have appointed Mr. Kazuto Suzuki (Japan) to serve on
the Panel of Experts as the expert on international security and export control and to
carry out the tasks specified in paragraph 29 of resolution 1929 (2010).
I should be grateful if you would bring this information to the attention of the
members of the Security Council.
(Signed) BAN Ki-moon
