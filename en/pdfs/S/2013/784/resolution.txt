Letter dated 30 December 2013 from the President of the
Security Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 28 December 2013
(S/2013/776) concerning the facilitation of complementary force and asset
generation in order to temporarily increase the overall force levels of the United
Nations Mission in South Sudan (UNMISS), in accordance with the overall force
levels authorized by the Security Council in its resolution 2132 (2013), has been
brought to the attention of the Council. The Council takes note of the information
contained in your letter and the arrangements proposed therein, and underlines the
importance of consultations by the Secretariat with the troop-contributing countries
affected by these steps.
(Signed) Gérard Araud
President of the Security Council
