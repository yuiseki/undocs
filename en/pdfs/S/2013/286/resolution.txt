Letter dated 15 May 2013 from the President of the Security Council
addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 13 May 2013 concerning
your intention to appoint Mr. Albert Gerard Koenders (Netherlands) as your Special
Representative for Mali and Head of the United Nations Multidimensional
Integrated Stabilization Mission in Mali (MINUSMA) has been brought to the
attention of the members of the Security Council. They take note of the intention
expressed in your letter.
(Signed) Kodjo Menan
President of the Security Council
