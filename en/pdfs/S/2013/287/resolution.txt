Letter dated 14 May 2013 from the Secretary-General addressed
to the President of the Security Council
I have the honour to refer to the United Nations Organization Stabilization
Mission in the Democratic Republic of the Congo (MONUSCO), whose mandate
was established pursuant to Security Council resolution 1925 (2010) and recently
extended pursuant to resolution 2098 (2013) of 28 March 2013.
Following the usual consultations, I wish to inform you of my intention to
appoint Lieutenant-General Carlos Alberto Dos Santos Cruz (Brazil) as Force
Commander of MONUSCO. Lieutenant-General Santos Cruz will replace
Lieutenant-General Prakash Wadhwa (India), whose term came to an end on
31 March 2013. I should like to take this opportunity to express my gratitude to
Lieutenant-General Prakash Wadhwa for his service to MONUSCO.
I should be grateful if you could bring the present letter to the attention of the
members of the Security Council.
(Signed) BAN Ki-moon
