Letter dated 14 June 2013 from the President of the Security Council
addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 12 June 2013 (S/2013/355)
concerning your intention to appoint Major General Jean Bosco Kazura (Rwanda) as
Force Commander of the United Nations Multidimensional Integrated Stabilization
Mission in Mali has been brought to the attention of the members of the Security
Council. They take note of the intention expressed in your letter.
(Signed) Mark Lyall Grant
President of the Security Council
