Letter dated 19 June 2013 from the President of the Security Council
addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 17 June 2013
(S/2013/364) concerning your intention to appoint Jean-Paul Laborde (France) as
the new Executive Director of the Counter-Terrorism Committee Executive
Directorate has been brought to the attention of the members of the Security
Council. They take note of the intention expressed in your letter.
(Signed) Mark Lyall Grant
President of the Security Council
