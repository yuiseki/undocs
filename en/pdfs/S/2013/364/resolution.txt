Letter dated 17 June 2013 from the Secretary-General addressed
to the President of the Security Council
I have the honour to refer to the Counter-Terrorism Committee Executive
Directorate, which was established pursuant to Security Council resolution 1535
(2004) and extended for the period ending 31 December 2013 by the Council in its
resolution 1963 (2010).
Following the usual consultations, I wish to inform you of my intention to
appoint Jean-Paul Laborde (France) as the new Executive Director of the CounterTerrorism Committee Executive Directorate. Mr. Laborde will succeed Mike Smith
(Australia), whose appointment ends on 30 June 2013. I would like to take this
opportunity to express my gratitude to Mr. Smith for his dedication and effective
leadership.
I should be grateful if you would bring the present letter to the attention of the
members of the Security Council.
(Signed) BAN Ki-moon
