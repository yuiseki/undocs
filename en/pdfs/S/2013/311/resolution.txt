Letter dated 6 December 2012 from the Secretary-General
addressed to the President of the Security Council
I have the honour to refer to the United Nations Stabilization Mission in Haiti
(MINUSTAH) established pursuant to Security Council resolution 1542 (2004) and
most recently extended pursuant to resolution 2070 (2012) of 12 October 2012.
Following the usual consultations, it is my intention to appoint Ms. Sandra
Honoré (Trinidad and Tobago) as Special Representative of the Secretary-General
for Haiti and Head of MINUSTAH. She will replace Mr. Mariano Fernández
Amunátegui (Chile) who will complete his mission on 31 January 2013. I should
like to take this opportunity to express my gratitude to Mr. Fernández Amunátegui
for the dedication and effectiveness with which he has led MINUSTAH.
I should be grateful if you would bring this letter to the attention of the members
of the Security Council.
(Signed) BAN Ki-moon
