Letter dated 23 May 2013 from the President of the Security Council
addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 6 December 2012
(S/2013/311) concerning your intention to appoint Ms. Sandra Honoré (Trinidad and
Tobago) as your Special Representative for Haiti and Head of the United Nations
Stabilization Mission in Haiti has been brought to the attention of the members of
the Security Council. They take note of the intention expressed in your letter.
(Signed) Kodjo Menan
President of the Security Council
