Letter dated 27 November 2013 from the President of the
Security Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 21 November 2013
(S/2013/704), concerning the establishment of a guard unit to enable the
implementation of the mandated tasks of the United Nations Support Mission in
Libya (UNSMIL), has been brought to the attention of the Security Council. The
Council takes note of the information contained in your letter and the arrangements
proposed therein.
(Signed) Liu Jieyi
President of the Security Council
