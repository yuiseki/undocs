Letter dated 28 March 2013 from the Secretary-General addressed
to the President of the Security Council
I have the honour to refer to Security Council resolution 2091 (2013)
concerning the Sudan, adopted on 14 February 2013, by which the Council decided
to extend until 17 February 2014 the mandate of the Panel of Experts, originally
appointed pursuant to resolution 1591 (2005) and previously extended by
resolutions 1651 (2005), 1665 (2006), 1713 (2006), 1779 (2007), 1841 (2008), 1891
(2009), 1945 (2010), 1982 (2011) and 2035 (2012), and requested me to take the
necessary administrative measures.
Accordingly, I wish to inform you that I have appointed the following experts
to serve on the Panel of Experts:
Mr. Luis Ángel Benavides Hernández, Mexico (international humanitarian law)
Mr. Issa Maraut, France (regional)
Mr. Guido Potters, the Netherlands (aviation)
Mr. Ghassan Schbley, United States of America (finance)
Mr. Adrian Wilkinson, United Kingdom of Great Britain and Northern Ireland
(arms)
I have also designated Mr. Maraut to serve as Coordinator of the Panel of
Experts.
I should be grateful if you would bring this information to the attention of the
members of the Security Council.
(Signed) BAN Ki-moon
