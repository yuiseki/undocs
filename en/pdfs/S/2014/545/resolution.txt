Letter dated 28 July 2014 from the President of the Security Council
addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 24 July 2014
(S/2014/544) concerning your intention to appoint Major General Martin Chomu
Tumenta (Cameroon) as Force Commander of the United Nations Multidimensional
Integrated Stabilization Mission in the Central African Republic has been brought to
the attention of the members of the Security Council. They take note of the intention
expressed in your letter.
(Signed) Eugène-Richard Gasana
President of the Security Council
