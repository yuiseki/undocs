(Signed) Sylvie Lucas

Annex to the letter dated 4 March 2014 from the President of the
Security Council addressed to the Secretary-General
[Original: English]
At its 41st meeting, on 25 October 2013, the Working Group of the Security
Council on Children and Armed Conflict examined the third report of the SecretaryGeneral on children and armed conflict in the Philippines (S/2013/419), covering
the period from 1 December 2009 to 30 November 2012. At its 43rd meeting, on
14 February 2014, the Working Group adopted its conclusions on children and
armed conflict in the Philippines (S/AC.51/2014/1).
Following the recommendations of the Working Group, approved by the
Security Council, and subject to and consistent with applicable law and relevant
international Security Council resolutions, including Security Council resolutions
1612 (2005), 1882 (2009), 1998 (2011) and 2068 (2012), I am entrusted, in my
capacity as Chair of the Working Group:
(a) To invite you to ensure that the country task force of the United Nations
on monitoring and reporting strengthens, in cooperation with the Government of the
Philippines, its monitoring and reporting activities with respect to all violations and
abuses committed against children in the armed conflict in the Philippines, and the
full and expeditious implementation of the action plan between the Moro Islamic
Liberation Front and the United Nations signed on 1 August 2009;
(b) To request that you encourage the country task force on monitoring and
reporting to renew its efforts to reach out to the New People’s Army, in cooperation
with the Government of the Philippines, with a view to developing an action plan to
end and prevent the recruitment and use of children in violation of applicable
international law, and to address other violations and abuses against children
committed by the New People’s Army in the Philippines.
(Signed) Sylvie Lucas
Chair
Security Council Working Group on Children and Armed Conflict
