Letter dated 22 July 2014 from the President of the
Security Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 18 July 2014
(S/2014/516) concerning your intention to appoint Ms. Ellen Margrethe Løj
(Denmark) as your Special Representative and Head of the United Nations Mission
in South Sudan (UNMISS) has been brought to the attention of the members of the
Security Council. They take note of the intention expressed in your letter.
(Signed) Eugène-Richard Gasana
President of the Security Council
