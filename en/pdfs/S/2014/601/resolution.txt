Letter dated 15 August 2014 from the President of the
Security Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 13 August 2014
(S/2014/600) concerning your reports submitted pursuant to resolution 2048 (2012)
has been brought to the attention of the members of the Security Council.
The members of the Council have taken note of the request contained in y our
letter that your upcoming report be the last one and that further updates be provided
every six months in your regular reports on the United Nations Integrated
Peacebuilding Office in Guinea-Bissau, while oral briefings to the Council will
continue to be provided, as and when required.
(Signed) Mark Lyall Grant
President of the Security Council
