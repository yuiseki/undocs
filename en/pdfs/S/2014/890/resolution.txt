Letter dated 12 December 2014 from the President of the
Security Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 10 December 2014
(S/2014/889) concerning your intention to appoint Mr. Mongi Hamdi (Tunisia) as
your Special Representative for Mali and Head of the United Nations
Multidimensional Integrated Stabilization Mission in Mali (MINUSMA) has been
brought to the attention of the members of the Security Council. They take note of
the intention expressed in your letter.
(Signed) Mahamat Zene Cherif
President of the Security Council
