Letter dated 16 July 2014 from the Secretary-General addressed to
the President of the Security Council
I have the honour to refer to Security Council resolution 2165 (2014), adopted
on 14 July 2014, by which the Council decided to establish a monitoring
mechanism, under my authority, to monitor, with the consent of the relevant
neighbouring countries of the Syrian Arab Republic, the loading of all humanitarian
relief consignments of the United Nations humanitarian agencies and their
implementing partners at the relevant United Nations facilities, and any subsequent
opening of the consignments by the customs authorities of the relevant neighbouring
countries, for passage into the Syrian Arab Republic across the border crossings of
Bab al-Salam, Bab al-Hawa, Al-Ya’rubiyah and Al-Ramtha, and with notification by
the United Nations to the Syrian authorities, in order to confirm the humanitarian
nature of those relief consignments.
I have requested the Office for the Coordination of Humanitarian Affairs to
work on the establishment of the monitoring mechanism, including its expeditious
deployment.
In accordance with your request, I will report to the Security Council on the
implementation of resolution 2165 (2014) within the monthly reporting framework
for resolution 2139 (2014).
I should be grateful if you would bring the present letter to the at tention of the
members of the Security Council.
(Signed) BAN Ki-moon
