Letter dated 16 July 2014 from the President of the
Security Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 14 July 2014
(S/2014/502) concerning your intention to appoint Mr. Said Djinnit (Algeria) as
your Special Envoy for the Great Lakes Region has been brought to the attention of
the members of the Security Council. They take note of the intention expressed in
your letter.
(Signed) Eugène-Richard Gasana
President of the Security Council
