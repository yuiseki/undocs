Letter dated 12 June 2014 from the Secretary-General to the
President of the Security Council
I have the honour to refer to the command of the United Nations Interim Force
in Lebanon (UNIFIL).
Following the usual consultations, it is my intention to appoint Major General
Luciano Portolano (Italy) as Head of Mission and Force Commander of UNIFIL.
Major General Portolano will succeed Major General Paolo Serra, who will
complete his assignment on 24 July 2014. I would like to take this opportunity to
express my gratitude to Major General Serra for his dedicated leadership of
UNIFIL.
I should be grateful if you would bring the present letter to the attention of the
members of the Security Council.
(Signed) BAN Ki-moon
