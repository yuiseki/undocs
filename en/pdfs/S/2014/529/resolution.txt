Letter dated 23 July 2014 from the President of the Security Council
addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 21 July 2014
(S/2014/528) concerning your report pursuant to resolution 2157 (2014) has been
brought to the attention of the members of the Security Council.
The members of the Council have taken note of the request contained in your
letter that the time frame for the issuance of the report be extended by a period of
three months, but they would in turn request that you provide a written update by
12 November 2014, which the Council could consider prior to the expiration of the
mandate of the United Nations Integrated Peacebuilding Office in Guinea-Bissau on
30 November 2014.
(Signed) Eugène-Richard Gasana
President of the Security Council
