Letter dated 16 June 2014 from the President of the Security Council
addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 12 June 2014
(S/2014/413) concerning your intention to appoint Lieutenant General Yohannes
Gebremeskel Tesfamariam (Ethiopia) as the new Force Commander of the United
Nations Mission in South Sudan has been brought to the attention of the members of
the Security Council. They take note of the intention expressed in your letter.
(Signed) Vitaly Churkin
President of the Security Council
