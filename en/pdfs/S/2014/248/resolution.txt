Letter dated 4 April 2014 from the Secretary-General addressed to
the President of the Security Council
I have the honour to refer to Security Council resolution 2141 (2014), by
which the Council decided to extend until 5 April 2015 the mandate of the Panel of
Experts, as specified in paragraph 26 of resolution 1874 (2009), and requested me to
take the necessary administrative measures to that effect.
Accordingly, I wish to inform you that, after consulting with the Security
Council Committee established pursuant to resolution 1718 (2006), I have appointed
the following experts:
Mr. Katsuhisa Furukawa, Japan (nuclear issues)
Mr. Hugh Griffiths, United Kingdom of Great Britain and Northern Ireland (air
transport)
Mr. Chang Guo, China (other weapons of mass destruction and conventional
arms)
Mr. Dmitry Kiku, Russian Federation (customs and export controls)
Mr. Jang-keun Lee, Republic of Korea (weapons of mass destruction arms
control and non-proliferation policy)
Mr. William J. Newcomb, United States of America (finance)
Mr. Neil Watts, South Africa (maritime transport)
I have also reappointed the following expert to serve on the Panel of Experts
for a period of up to seven months, when he will reach the maximum period of
service under the applicable rules and regulations of the United Nations:
Mr. Erik Marzolf, France (missile technology)
I have designated Mr. Marzolf to serve as Coordinator of the Panel of Experts
for the remainder of his term on the Panel.
I should be grateful if you would bring this information to the attention of the
members of the Security Council.
(Signed) BAN Ki-moon
