Letter dated 29 April 2014 from the President of the
Security Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 25 April 2014
(S/2014/306) concerning your intention to appoint Abdoulaye Bathily (Senegal) as
your Special Representative for Central Africa and Head of the United Nations
Regional Office for Central Africa has been brought to the attention of the members
of the Security Council. They take note of the intention expressed in your letter.
(Signed) U. Joy Ogwu
President of the Security Council
