Letter dated 2 September 2014 from the President of the
Security Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 28 August 2014
(S/2014/644) concerning your recommendation of a technical rollover of the
mandate of the United Nations Mission in Liberia for a period of three months , until
30 December 2014, has been brought to the attention of the members of the Security
Council. They take note of the proposal contained in your letter.
(Signed) Samantha Power
President of the Security Council
