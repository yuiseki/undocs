Letter dated 30 June 2014 from the President of the
Security Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 26 June 2014
(S/2014/458) concerning your intention to appoint Major General Delali Johnson
Sakyi (Ghana) as Chief Military Observer and Head of Mission of the United
Nations Military Observer Group in India and Pakistan has been brought to the
attention of the members of the Security Council. They take note of the intention
expressed in your letter.
(Signed) Vitaly Churkin
President of the Security Council
