Letter dated 23 September 2014 from the Secretary-General
addressed to the President of the Security Council
I have the honour to refer to my report on Burundi (S/2014/550) in which I
informed the Security Council of the preparations for the establishment of a
United Nations electoral observer mission, immediately after the end of the mandate
of the United Nations Office in Burundi, pursuant to Council resolution
2137 (2014).
In this regard, I would like to inform you of my intention to deploy
immediately an advance team to commence preparations for the establishment of the
mission. The advance team will ensure that the mission is fully operational on
1 January 2015, in accordance with the expectations o f the Council.
I should be grateful if you would bring the present letter to the attention of the
Members of the Security Council.
(Signed) BAN Ki-moon
