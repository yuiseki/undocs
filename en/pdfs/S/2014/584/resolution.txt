Letter dated 12 August 2014 from the President of the
Security Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 8 August 2014
(S/2014/583) concerning your intention to appoint Bernardino León (Spain) as your
Special Representative and Head of the United Nations Support Mission in Libya
has been brought to the attention of the members of the Security Council. They take
note of the intention expressed in your letter.
(Signed) Mark Lyall Grant
President of the Security Council
