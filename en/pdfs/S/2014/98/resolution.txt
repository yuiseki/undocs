Letter dated 13 February 2014 from the Secretary-General
addressed to the President of the Security Council
I have the honour to refer to Security Council resolution 2127 (2013)
concerning the Central African Republic. By paragraph 59 of the resolution, the
Council requested me to create a Panel of Experts for an initial period of thirteen
months, in consultation with the Security Council Committee established pursuant
to resolution 2127 (2013) concerning the Central African Republic, and to make the
necessary financial and security arrangements to support the work of the Panel.
By paragraph 41 of resolution 2134 (2014), the Council extended the mandate
of the Panel of Experts for a period of one year from the adoption of the resolution
(until 27 January 2015).
Accordingly, I wish to inform you that, after consulting with the Committee, I
have appointed the following experts:
Mr. Ruben de Koning, Netherlands (finance/natural resources);
Mr. Paul-Simon Handy, Cameroon (regional);
Mr. Ahmed Himmiche, Morocco (arms);
Mr. Aurélien Llorca, France (armed groups);
Ms. Carolina Reyes Aragon, Colombia (humanitarian).
I have also designated Mr. Llorca to serve as Coordinator of the Panel of
Experts.
(Signed) BAN Ki-moon
