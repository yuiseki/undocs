Letter dated 2 September 2014 from the Secretary-General
addressed to the President of the Security Council
I have the honour to inform you that Mr. Erik Marzolf (France), whom I
initially appointed on 12 August 2009 and most recently reappointed on 4 April
2014 as missile technology expert on the Panel of Experts established pursuant to
Security Council resolution 1874 (2009), will reach the maximum period of service
under the applicable rules and regulations of the United Nations on 26 September
2014.
Accordingly, in consultation with the Security Council Committee established
pursuant to resolution 1718 (2006), I have appointed Mr. Benoît Camguilhem
(France) to succeed Mr. Marzolf as missile technology expert.
Mr. Marzolf was also appointed, on 4 April 2014, as Coordinator of the Panel
of Experts. In this connection, I have appointed Mr. Hugh Griffiths (United
Kingdom of Great Britain and Northern Ireland) to succeed him as Coordinator.
I should be grateful if you would bring this information to the attention of the
members of the Security Council.
(Signed) BAN Ki-moon
