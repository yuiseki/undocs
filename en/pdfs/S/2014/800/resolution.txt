Letter dated 6 November 2014 from the President of the
Security Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 6 November 2014
(S/2014/799) concerning your intention to appoint Mr. Cassam Uteem (Mauritius)
as your Special Envoy and Head of the United Nations Electoral Observation
Mission in Burundi has been brought to the attention of the members of the Security
Council. They take note of the intention expressed in your letter.
(Signed) Gary Quinlan
President of the Security Council
