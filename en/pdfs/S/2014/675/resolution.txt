Letter dated 17 September 2014 from the President of the
Security Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 15 September 2014
(S/2014/674) concerning your intention to appoint Nicholas Haysom (South Africa)
as your Special Representative for Afghanistan and Head of the United Nations
Assistance Mission in Afghanistan has been brought to the attention of the members
of the Security Council. They take note of the intention expressed in your letter.
(Signed) Samantha Power
President of the Security Council
