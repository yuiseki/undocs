Letter dated 13 March 2014 from the Secretary-General addressed
to the President of the Security Council
I have the honour to refer to Security Council resolution 2136 (2014)
concerning the Democratic Republic of the Congo. By paragraph 5 of that
resolution, the Council requested me to extend the mandate of the Group of Experts
for a period expiring on 1 February 2015.
Accordingly, I wish to inform you that, after consulting with the Committee, I
have appointed the following experts:
Ms. Victry Anya (Nigeria), regional issues;
Mr. Frans Barnard (United Kingdom of Great Britain and Northern Ireland) — arms;
Mr. Zobel Behalal (Cameroon) — natural resources;
Mr. Daniel Fahey (United States of America) — finance;
Mr. Gora Mbaye (Senegal) — customs/aviation;
Ms. Émilie Serralta (France) — armed groups.
I have also designated Mr. Fahey to serve as Coordinator of the Group of
Experts.
(Signed) BAN Ki-moon
