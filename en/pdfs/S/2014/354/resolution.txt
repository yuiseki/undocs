Letter dated 14 May 2014 from the Secretary-General addressed
to the President of the Security Council
I have the honour to refer to the United Nations Operation in Côte d’Ivoire
(UNOCI) established by the Security Council in resolution 1528 (2004) and most
recently extended by resolution 2112 (2013).
Following the usual consultations, I would like to inform you of my intention
to appoint Major General Hafiz Masroor Ahmed (Pakistan) as Force Commander of
UNOCI. Major General Hafiz Masroor Ahmed will replace Major General
Muhammad Iqbal Asi (Pakistan), whose tour of duty ended on 21 April 2014. I
would like to take this opportunity to express my gratitude to Major General Iqbal
Asi for his contribution to UNOCI.
I should be grateful if you would bring the present letter to the attention of the
members of the Security Council.
(Signed) BAN Ki-moon
