Letter dated 24 July 2014 from the Secretary-General addressed to
the President of the Security Council
I have the honour to refer to the United Nations Multidimensional Integrated
Stabilization Mission in the Central African Republic (MINUSCA), e stablished by
the Security Council in its resolution 2149 (2014) of 10 April 2014.
Following the usual consultations, I would like to inform you of my intention
to appoint Major General Martin Chomu Tume nta (Cameroon) as Force Commander
of MINUSCA. Major General Tumenta will commence his appointment on
15 September 2014 at the time of the transfer of authority between the African -led
International Support Mission in the Central African Republic (MISCA) an d
MINUSCA.
I should be grateful if you would bring the present letter to the attention of the
members of the Security Council.
(Signed) BAN Ki-moon
