Letter dated 16 May 2014 from the President of the Security Council
addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 14 May 2014
(S/2014/354) concerning your intention to appoint Major General Hafiz Masroor
Ahmed (Pakistan) as the new Force Commander of the United Nations Operation in
Côte d’Ivoire has been brought to the attention of the members of the Security
Council. They take note of the intention contained in your letter.
(Signed) Oh Joon
