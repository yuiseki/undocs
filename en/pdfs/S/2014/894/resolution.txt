Letter dated 12 December 2014 from the President of the
Security Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 9 December 2014
(S/2014/893), concerning your intention to continue the activities of the United
Nations support team to the Cameroon-Nigeria Mixed Commission, with funding
from the regular budget, has been brought to the attention of the members of the
Security Council. They take note of the information contained in your letter and the
intention expressed therein.
(Signed) Mahamat Zene Cherif
President of the Security Council
