Letter dated 11 March 2014 from the President of the
Security Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 7 March 2014
(S/2014/175) concerning your intention to appoint Lieutenant General Jose Luiz
Jaborandy Jr. (Brazil) as the new Force Commander of the United Nations
Stabilization Mission in Haiti has been brought to the attention of the members of
the Security Council. They take note of the intention expressed in your letter.
(Signed) Sylvie Lucas
President of the Security Council
