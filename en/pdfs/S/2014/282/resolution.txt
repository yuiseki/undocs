Letter dated 16 April 2014 from the Secretary-General to the
President of the Security Council
I have the honour to refer to Security Council resolution 2140 (2014) of
26 February 2014, in which the Council requested that I create for an initial period
of 13 months, in consultation with the Committee established pursuant to the same
resolution, a group of up to four experts (“Panel of Experts”), and also requested
that I make the necessary financial and security arrangements to support the work of
the Panel.
Accordingly, I wish to inform you that I have appointed the following experts
to serve on the Panel:
Ms. Carmela Bühler (Switzerland), international humanitarian law
Mr. Simon David Goddard (United Kingdom of Great Britain and Northern
Ireland), finance
Ms. Alma Abdul-Hadi Jadallah (Jordan), regional issues
Mr. Mohamed Mahmoud Ould Mohamedou (Mauritania), armed groups
I have also designated Mr. Mohamedou to serve as Coordinator of the Panel of
Experts.
I should be grateful if you would bring this information to the attention of the
members of the Security Council.
(Signed) BAN Ki-moon
