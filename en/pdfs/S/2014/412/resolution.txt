Letter dated 16 June 2014 from the President of the Security Council
to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 12 June 2014
(S/2014/411) concerning your intention to appoint Major General Luciano Portolano
(Italy) as the new Head of Mission and Force Commander of the United Nations
Interim Force in Lebanon (UNIFIL) has been brought to the attention of the
members of the Security Council. They take note of the intenti on expressed in your
letter.
(Signed) Vitaly Churkin
President of the Security Council
