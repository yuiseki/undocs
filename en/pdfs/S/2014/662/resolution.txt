Letter dated 11 September 2014 from the President of the
Security Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 9 September 2014
(S/2014/661) concerning your intention to appoint Mohammed Ibn Chambas
(Ghana) as your Special Representative for West Africa and Head of the United
Nations Office for West Africa has been brought to the attention of the members of
the Security Council. They take note of the intention expressed in your letter.
(Signed) Samantha Power
President of the Security Council
