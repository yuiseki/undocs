Letter dated 28 November 2014 from the President of the
Security Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 25 November 2014
(S/2014/860) concerning your intention to appoint Sigrid Kaag (Netherlands) as the
United Nations Special Coordinator for Lebanon has been brought to the attention
of the members of the Security Council. They take note of the intention expressed in
your letter.
(Signed) Gary Quinlan
President of the Security Council
