Letter dated 21 May 2014 from the Secretary-General addressed
to the President of the Security Council
I have the honour to refer to Security Council resolution 2153 (2014)
concerning Côte d’Ivoire. By paragraph 24 of the resolution, the Council decided to
extend the mandate of the Group of Experts until 30 May 2015 and requested me to
take the necessary administrative measures.
Accordingly, I wish to inform you that, after consulting with the Security
Council Committee established pursuant to resolution 1572 (2004) concerning
Côte d’Ivoire, I have appointed the following experts:
Mr. Roman Brühwiler, customs/transport (Switzerland)
Mr. Raymond Debelle, arms (Belgium)
Ms. Sherrone Lobban, finance (Jamaica)
Mr. Roberto Sollazzo, diamonds/natural resources (Italy)
Mr. David Zounmenou, regional (Benin)
I have also designated Mr. Debelle to serve as Coordinator of the Group of
Experts.
(Signed) BAN Ki-moon
