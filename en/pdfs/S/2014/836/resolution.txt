Letter dated 20 November 2014 from the President of the
Security Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 18 November 2014
(S/2014/835) concerning your intention to appoint Major General Birhanu Jula
Gelalcha (Ethiopia) as Force Commander of the United Nations Interim Security
Force for Abyei (UNISFA) has been brought to the attention of the members of the
Security Council. They take note of the intention expressed in your letter.
(Signed) Gary Quinlan
President of the Security Council
