Letter dated 14 July 2014 from the President of the Security Council
addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 10 July 2014
(S/2014/492) concerning your intention to appoint Miguel Trovoada (Sao Tome and
Principe) as your Special Representative in Guinea-Bissau and Head of the United
Nations Integrated Peacebuilding Office in Guinea-Bissau has been brought to the
attention of the members of the Security Council. They take note of the intention
expressed in your letter.
(Signed) Eugène-Richard Gasana
President of the Security Council
