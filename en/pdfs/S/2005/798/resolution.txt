Letter dated 16 December 2005 from the President of the
Security Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 13 December 2005
(S/2005/797), concerning the activities of the Quartet, including the extension of the
mandate of its Special Envoy for Gaza Disengagement, James Wolfensohn, and the
continued support for his office in Jerusalem, has been brought to the attention of
the members of the Security Council. They welcome the proposed arrangements set
out in your letter.
(Signed) Emyr Jones Parry
President of the Security Council
