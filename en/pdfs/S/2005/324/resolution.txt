Letter dated 18 May 2005 from the President of the
Security Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 10 May 2005
(S/2005/323) concerning your intention to continue the activities of the United
Nations Tajikistan Office of Peacebuilding (UNTOP) for a further period of one
year, until 1 June 2006, has been brought to the attention of the members of the
Security Council. They take note of the information contained in your letter and the
intention expressed therein.
(Signed) Ellen Margrethe Løj
President of the Security Council
