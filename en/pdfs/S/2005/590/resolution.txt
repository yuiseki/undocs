Letter dated 15 September 2005 from the President of the
Security Council to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 12 September 2005
(S/2005/589) concerning your intention to appoint Mr. Michael Møller (Denmark)
as your Special Representative in Cyprus and Head of the United Nations
Peacekeeping Force in Cyprus (UNFICYP) has been brought to the attention of the
members of the Security Council. They take note of the intention expressed in your
letter.
(Signed) Lauro L. Baja, Jr.
President of the Security Council
