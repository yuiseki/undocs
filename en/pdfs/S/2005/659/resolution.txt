Note by the President of the Security Council
After consultations among the members of the Security Council, it has been
agreed that Mr. Jean-Marc de La Sablière, Permanent Representative of France to
the United Nations, will serve as Chairman of the Working Group of the Security
Council on Children and Armed Conflict, established pursuant to paragraph 8 of
resolution 1612 (2005) of 26 July 2005.
