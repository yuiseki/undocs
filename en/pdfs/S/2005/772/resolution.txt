Letter dated 2 December 2005 from the Secretary-General
addressed to the President of the Security Council
I have the honour to refer to the United Nations Military Observer Group in
India and Pakistan (UNMOGIP).
Following the usual consultations, it is my intention to appoint Major General
Dragutin Repinc (Croatia) as Chief Military Observer of UNMOGIP. General
Repinc will replace Major General Guido Dante Palmieri (Italy), who relinquished
his post on 13 September 2005.
I should be grateful if you would bring this matter to the attention of the
members of the Security Council.
(Signed) Kofi A. Annan
