Letter dated 25 February 2005 from the Secretary-General
addressed to the President of the Security Council
I have the honour to refer to the United Nations Operation in Côte d’Ivoire
(UNOCI) established pursuant to Security Council resolution 1528 (2004), of
27 February 2004.
I should like to inform you that I intend to appoint Mr. Pierre Schori (Sweden)
as my Special Representative and Head of the United Nations Operation in Côte
d’Ivoire.
I should be grateful if you would bring this letter to the attention of the
members of the Security Council.
(Signed) Kofi A. Annan
