Letter dated 18 July 2005 from the President of the
Security Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 13 July 2005
(S/2005/464), concerning your intention to appoint Alan Doss (United Kingdom of
Great Britain and Northern Ireland) as your Special Representative for Liberia as
from 15 August 2005, has been brought to the attention of the members of the
Security Council. They take note of the information and intention contained in your
letter.
(Signed) Adamantios Th. Vassilakis
President of the Security Council
