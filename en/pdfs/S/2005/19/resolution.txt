Letter dated 11 January 2005 from the President of the Security
Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 6 January 2005
(S/2005/18) concerning your intention to appoint Lieutenant General Joseph
Olorungbon Owonibi (Nigeria) as Force Commander of the United Nations Mission
in Liberia (UNMIL), has been brought to the attention of the members of the
Security Council. They take note of the intention expressed in your letter.
(Signed) César Mayoral
President of the Security Council
