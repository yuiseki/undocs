Letter dated 2 December 2005 from the President of the
Security Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 30 November 2005
(S/2005/758) concerning your recommendation to extend the mandate of the United
Nations Peacebuilding Support Office in the Central African Republic from
1 January to 31 December 2006, and your intention to raise the rank of your
Representative in the Central African Republic to that of Special Representative, at
the Assistant Secretary-General level, has been brought to the attention of the
members of the Security Council. They take note of the recommendation and the
intention expressed in your letter.
(Signed) Emyr Jones Parry
President of the Security Council
