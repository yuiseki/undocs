Letter dated 23 August 2005 from the Secretary-General addressed
to the President of the Security Council
I have the honour to refer to Security Council resolution 1284 (1999), in which
the Council requested that I consult the Executive Chairman of the United Nations
Monitoring, Verification and Inspection Commission (UNMOVIC) and the Council
members on the appointment of suitably qualified experts as UNMOVIC
Commissioners.
Chen Weixiong has informed me of his resignation from the College of
Commissioners. It is my intention to appoint Lu Yongshou in his place.
I should be grateful if you would bring this matter to the attention of the
members of the Security Council.
(Signed) Kofi A. Annan
