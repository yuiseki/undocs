Letter dated 20 December 2005 from the President of the
Security Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 14 December 2005
(S/2005/808) concerning your decision to extend the appointment of Mr. Mohamed
Sahnoun as your Special Adviser on Africa until 31 December 2006 has been
brought to the attention of the members of the Security Council. They take note of
the information and decision contained in your letter.
(Signed) Emyr Jones Parry
President of the Security Council
