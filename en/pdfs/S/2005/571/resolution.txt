Letter dated 9 September 2005 from the President of the Security
Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 6 September 2005
(S/2005/570) concerning your intention to appoint Brigadier General Kurt Mosgaard
(Denmark) to the post of Force Commander of the United Nations Mission for the
Referendum in Western Sahara (MINURSO) has been brought to the attention of the
members of the Security Council. They take note of the intention expressed in your
letter.
(Signed) Lauro L. Baja, Jr.
President of the Security Council
