Letter dated 24 June 2005 from the President of the
Security Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that the Security Council has decided to
approve the proposal contained in your letter of 20 June 2005 (S/2005/406)
concerning the United Nations Monitoring, Verification and Inspection Commission
escrow account established under Security Council resolution 1284 (1999) and
related resolutions, to transfer from this escrow account $200 million to the
Development Fund for Iraq and $20,256,697 to be credited against assessments
issued in respect of the obligations of the Government of Iraq for regular budget,
peacekeeping and Tribunal activities, and the capital master plan of the
Organization.
(Signed) Jean-Marc de La Sablière
President of the Security Council
