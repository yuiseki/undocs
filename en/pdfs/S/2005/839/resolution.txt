Letter dated 28 December 2005 from the President of the
Security Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 22 December 2005
(S/2005/838) concerning your intention to add Mongolia to the list of countries
contributing troops to the United Nations Mission in Liberia (UNMIL) has been
brought to the attention of the members of the Security Council. They take note of
the intention expressed in your letter.
(Signed) Emyr Jones Parry
President of the Security Council
