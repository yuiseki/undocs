Letter dated 25 August 2005 from the President of the Security
Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 23 August 2005
(S/2005/540) concerning your intention to appoint Lu Yongshou (China) as a
Commissioner for the United Nations Monitoring, Verification and Inspection
Commission has been brought to the attention of the members of the Security
Council. They take note of the intention expressed in your letter.
(Signed) Kenzo Oshima
President of the Security Council
