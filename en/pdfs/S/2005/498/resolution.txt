Letter dated 28 July 2005 from the President of the Security
Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 25 July 2005
(S/2005/497) concerning your decision to appoint Peter van Walsum (the
Netherlands) as your Personal Envoy for Western Sahara has been brought to the
attention of the members of the Security Council. They take note of the intention
expressed in your letter.
(Signed) Adamantios Th. Vassilakis
President of the Security Council
