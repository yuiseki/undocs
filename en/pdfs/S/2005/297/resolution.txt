Note by the President of the Security Council
Following consultations among the members of the Security Council on
29 April and 4 May 2005, the members agreed to elect the Chairman and ViceChairmen of the following committee for the period ending 31 December 2005:
Security Council Committee established pursuant to resolution 1591 (2005)
concerning the Sudan
Chairman: Adamantios Th. Vassilakis (Greece)
Vice-Chairmen: Argentina and the Philippines
