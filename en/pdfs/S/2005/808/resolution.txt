Letter dated 14 December 2005 from the Secretary-General
addressed to the President of the Security Council
As you are aware, my Special Adviser, Mr. Mohamed Sahnoun, provides
advice on the actual and potential roles of the United Nations in addressing conflict
situations in Africa, especially in the countries of the Greater Horn of Africa. He has
monitored closely the developments in the region and has participated in
consultations that helped promote and implement negotiated settlements to conflicts.
Mr. Sahnoun has represented me at meetings dealing with regional issues,
including the signing ceremony of the Comprehensive Peace Agreement on the
Sudan held in Kenya. He has also visited Member States and participated on my
behalf in important meetings dedicated to the situation in the Horn of Africa. He
provided me with valuable advice during my recent visit to the Middle East.
I have decided to extend Mr. Sahnoun’s appointment until 31 December 2006
to continue to benefit from his expertise and his efforts, as required.
I should be grateful if you could bring the present letter to the attention of the
members of the Security Council.
(Signed) Kofi A. Annan
