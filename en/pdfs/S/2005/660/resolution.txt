Note by the President of the Security Council
After consultations among the members of the Security Council, it has been
agreed that Mr. Simon Bodéhoussè Idohou, Permanent Representative of Benin to
the United Nations, will serve as Chairman of the Ad Hoc Working Group on
Conflict Prevention and Resolution in Africa, established pursuant to the note by the
President of the Security Council dated 1 March 2002 (S/2002/207), for the period
ending 31 December 2005.
