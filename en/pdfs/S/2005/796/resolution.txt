Letter dated 15 December 2005 from the President of the
Security Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 2 December 2005
(S/2005/795), concerning your proposal that the current mandate of the United
Nations Peacebuilding Support Office in Guinea-Bissau, which expires on
22 December 2005, be revised and extended to 31 December 2006, has been brought
to the attention of the members of the Security Council. They take note of the
information and proposal contained in your letter.
(Signed) Emyr Jones Parry
President of the Security Council
