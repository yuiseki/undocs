Letter dated 29 March 2005 from the Secretary-General addressed
to the President of the Security Council
I have the honour to refer to Security Council resolution 1584 (2005)
concerning Côte d’Ivoire. In paragraph 7 of that resolution, the Council requested
me to appoint, in consultation with the Security Council Committee established
pursuant to resolution 1572 (2004) concerning Côte d’Ivoire, for a period of six
months, no more than three experts having the necessary skills to perform the
mandate set out in the same paragraph.
Accordingly, I wish to inform you that, taking into account the requirements
stated in resolution 1584 (2005), I have appointed the following three experts:
1.

Gilbert Charles Barthe (Switzerland)

2.

Atabou Bodian (Senegal)

3.

Alex Vines (United Kingdom of Great Britain and Northern Ireland)

I have also selected Mr. Bodian to chair the Panel of Experts.
I should be grateful if you would bring this information to the attention of the
members of the Security Council.
(Signed) Kofi A. Annan
