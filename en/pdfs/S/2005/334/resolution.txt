Letter dated 23 May 2005 from the President of the
Security Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 19 May 2005
(S/2005/333) concerning your proposal to appoint Stephen G. Rademaker as a
Commissioner of the United Nations Monitoring, Verification and Inspection
Commission has been brought to the attention of the members of the Security
Council. They agree with the proposal contained in your letter.
(Signed) Ellen Margrethe Løj
President of the Security Council
