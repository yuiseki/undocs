Letter dated 13 May 2005 from the Secretary-General to the
President of the Security Council
I wish to inform the Security Council of my intention to appoint, in accordance
with resolution 1595 (2005), Mr. Detlev Mehlis of Germany as the Commissioner of
the United Nations International Independent Investigation Commission established
following the assassination of Lebanese Prime Minister Rafik Hariri.
Mr. Mehlis is a Senior Public Prosecutor for the State of Berlin and has
substantial experience in investigating serious and complex crimes. During his
30-year legal career he has worked closely with institutions in numerous countries
on matters of transnational criminal law. Mr. Mehlis will proceed to Beirut to take
up his assignment as soon as practicable.
(Signed) Kofi A. Annan
