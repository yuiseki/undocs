Letter dated 17 November 2005 from the President of
the Security Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 14 November 2005
(S/2005/725) concerning your decision to change the title of Geir Pedersen to
Personal Representative of the Secretary-General for Lebanon and to expand his
mandate to include coordination of United Nations political activities for the whole
of Lebanon has been brought to the attention of the members of the Security
Council. They take note of the information and the decision contained in your letter.
(Signed) Andrey I. Denisov
President of the Security Council
