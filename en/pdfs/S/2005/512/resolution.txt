Letter dated 8 August 2005 from the President of the Security
Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 4 August 2005
(S/2005/511) concerning your intention to appoint Mr. Francesco Bastagli (Italy) as
your Special Representative for Western Sahara and Head of the United Nations
Mission for the Referendum in Western Sahara (MINURSO) has been brought to the
attention of the members of the Security Council. They take note of the intention
expressed in your letter.
(Signed) Kenzo Oshima
President of the Security Council
