Letter dated 29 April 2005 from the President of the Security
Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 27 April 2005
(S/2005/279) concerning your intention to appoint François Lonseny Fall (Guinea)
as your Special Representative for Somalia and Head of the United Nations Political
Office for Somalia has been brought to the attention of the members of the Security
Council. They take note of the information and intention contained in your letter.
(Signed) Wang Guangya
President of the Security Council
