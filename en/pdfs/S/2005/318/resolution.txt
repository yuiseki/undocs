Letter dated 17 May 2005 from the President of the Security
Council to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 13 May 2005
(S/2005/317), concerning your intention to appoint, in accordance with resolution
1595 (2005), Mr. Detlev Mehlis of Germany as the Commissioner of the United
Nations International Independent Investigation Commission established following
the assassination of Lebanese Prime Minister Rafik Hariri, has been brought to the
attention of the members of the Security Council. They take note of the intention
expressed in that letter.
(Signed) Ellen Margrethe Løj
President of the Security Council
