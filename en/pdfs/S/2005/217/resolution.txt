Letter dated 31 March 2005 from the President of the Security
Council to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 29 March 2005
(S/2005/216) concerning your decision to appoint Geir O. Pedersen to serve as your
Personal Representative for southern Lebanon has been brought to the attention of
the members of the Security Council. They take note of the information and
decision contained in your letter.
(Signed) Ronaldo Mota Sardenberg
President of the Security Council
