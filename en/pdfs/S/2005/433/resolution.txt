Letter dated 5 July 2005 from the President of the Security
Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 28 June 2005
(S/2005/432) concerning the activities of the Quartet, including the mandate of its
Special Envoy for Gaza Disengagement, has been brought to the attention of the
members of the Security Council. They confirm support for the arrangements
proposed in your letter.
(Signed) Adamantios Th. Vassilakis
President of the Security Council
