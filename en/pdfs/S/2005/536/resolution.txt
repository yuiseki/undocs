Letter dated 19 August 2005 from the President of the Security
Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 8 August 2005
(S/2005/535), concerning the termination of operations relating to letters of credit
raised against the United Nations Iraq Account, has been brought to the attention of
the members of the Security Council. They welcome the arrangements that you
propose in order to balance concern for the timely termination of the oil-for-food
programme against the need for an orderly treatment of the requirements of both
vendor and purchaser. The members of the Council emphasize that it is essential that
the remaining work be done in close consultation with the relevant authorities of the
Government of Iraq. Bearing in mind the timetable envisaged in the note annexed to
your letter, they request you to report orally to the Security Council on the
implementation of the above-mentioned arrangements by mid-October 2005, so as
to allow the members of the Council to assess the progress made, and review the
arrangements.
(Signed) Kenzo Oshima
President of the Security Council
