Letter dated 2 June 2005 addressed to the Secretary-General by
the President of the Security Council
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 27 May 2005
(S/2005/364), in which you stated your intention to appoint Mr. Kai Eide as your
Special Envoy to undertake a comprehensive review of Kosovo, has been brought to
the attention of the members of the Security Council, who have taken note of it.
The President of the Security Council
(Signed) Jean-Marc de La Sablière
