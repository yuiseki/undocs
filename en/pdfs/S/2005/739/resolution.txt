Letter dated 28 November 2005 from the President of the
Security Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 21 November 2005
(S/2005/738) concerning your intention to appoint Lieutenant General Chikadibia
Isaac Obiakor (Nigeria) as Force Commander of the United Nations Mission in
Liberia (UNMIL) has been brought to the attention of the members of the Security
Council. They take note of the intention expressed in your letter.
(Signed) Andrey I. Denisov
President of the Security Council
