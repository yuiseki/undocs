Letter dated 22 July 2005 from the President of the Security
Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 19 July 2005
(S/2005/486) concerning your intention, as requested by Security Council resolution
1603 (2005), to appoint Mr. António Monteiro (Portugal) to the post of High
Representative for the elections in Côte d’Ivoire has been brought to the attention of
the members of the Security Council. They take note of the intention expressed in
your letter.
(Signed) Adamantios Th. Vassilakis
President of the Security Council
