Official communiqué of the 5087th (closed) meeting of the
Security Council
Held in private at Headquarters, New York, on Wednesday,
24 November 2004, at 10 a.m.
In accordance with rule 55 of the provisional rules of procedure of the Security
Council, the following communiqué was issued through the Secretary-General in
place of a verbatim record:
“On 24 November 2004, the Security Council, pursuant to annex II,
sections A and B of resolution 1353 (2001), held its 5087th meeting in private
with the troop-contributing countries to the United Nations Stabilization
Mission in Haiti (MINUSTAH).
“The Security Council and the troop-contributing countries heard a
briefing under rule 39 of its provisional rules of procedure by Mr. Juan Gabriel
Valdés, Special Representative of the Secretary-General and Head of the
United Nations Stabilization Mission in Haiti.
“Representatives of the participating troop-contributing countries,
Mr. Valdés, and members of the Council had a constructive exchange of
views.”
