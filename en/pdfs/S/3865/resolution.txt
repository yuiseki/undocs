The people of Oman are at present the victims of an armed aggression resorted

sovereignty of Oman, a country which has long been independent and whose
independence was reaffirmed in the treaty of Sib which was entered into between
Muscat and Oman on the 25th of September 1920, through the British Government
acting as an intermediary.

permitted to continue, lead to serious consequences. The extreme gravity of the
