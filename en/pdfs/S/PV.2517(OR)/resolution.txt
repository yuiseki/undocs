Documents of the Security Council (symbol S/. . .) are normally published in
quarterly Supplements of the Oflcial Records of the Security Council. The date

The resolutions of the Security Council, numbered in accordance with a

Decisions of the Security Council. The new system, which has been applied
retroactively to resolutions adopted before 1 January 1965, became fully operative
on that date.

Darussalam submitted the application of his country for
membership of the United Nations [S/26353]. Under

decides otherwise, applications for membership shall
be referred by the President of the Council to the Committee on the Admission of new Members. Accordingly, unless I hear a proposal to the contrary, I shall
refer the application of Brunei Darussalam to the Committee for examination and report.
