The question concerning Haiti

The meeting was called to order at 11.20 a.m.
Expression of condolence and a minute of silence
following the passing of His Majesty King Bhumibol
Adulyadej of the Kingdom of Thailand
The President (spoke in Russian): On behalf
of the members of the Security Council, I wish to
express our deepest condolences to the royal family,
the Government and the people of the Kingdom of
Thailand on the passing of His Majesty King Bhumibol
Adulyadej.
I request members of the Security Council to
stand and observe a minute of silence in His Majesty’s
memory.
The members of the Council observed a minute
of silence.
Adoption of the agenda
The agenda was adopted.
The question concerning Haiti
Report of the Secretary-General on the
United Nations Stabilization Mission in Haiti
(S/2016/753)
The President (spoke in Russian): In accordance
with rule 37 of the Council’s provisional rules of
procedure, I invite the representatives of Argentina,
Brazil, Canada, Chile, Colombia, Guatemala and Peru
to participate in this meeting.

13/10/2016

The Security Council will now begin its consideration
of the item on its agenda.
Members of the Council have before them document
S/2016/862, which contains the text of a draft resolution
submitted by Angola, Argentina, Brazil, Canada, Chile,
Colombia, France, Guatemala, Japan, Malaysia, New
Zealand, Peru, Ukraine, the United States of America,
Uruguay and the Bolivarian Republic of Venezuela.
I wish to draw the attention of Council members to
document S/2016/753, which contains the report of the
Secretary-General on the United Nations Stabilization
Mission in Haiti.
The Council is ready to proceed to the vote on the
draft resolution before it. I shall put the draft resolution
to the vote now.
A vote was taken by show of hands.
In favour:
Angola, China, Egypt, France, Japan, Malaysia,
New Zealand, Russian Federation, Senegal, Spain,
Ukraine, United Kingdom of Great Britain and
Northern Ireland, United States of America,
Uruguay and Venezuela (Bolivarian Republic of)
The President (spoke in Russian): The draft
resolution received 15 votes in favour. The draft
resolution has been adopted unanimously as
resolution 2313 (2016).
The meeting rose at 11.25 a.m.
