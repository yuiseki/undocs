Official communiqué of the 8898th (closed) meeting of the
Security Council
Held in private at Headquarters, New York, on Monday,
8 November 2021, at 10 a.m.
In accordance with rule 55 of the provisional rules of procedure of the Security
Council, the following communiqué was issued through the Secretary-General in
place of a verbatim record:
“At its 8898th meeting, held in private on 8 November 2021, the Security
Council considered the item entitled “The situation in Myanmar”.
“The President invited the representative of Brunei Darussalam to
participate in the meeting in accordance with rule 37 of the Council’s
provisional rules of procedure.
“The President invited Mr. Peter Due, Director, Asia-Pacific Division,
Departments of Political and Peacebuilding Affairs and Peace Operations, to
participate in the meeting in accordance with rule 39 of the Council’s
provisional rules of procedure.
“The Council heard briefings by His Excellency Dato Erywan Pehin Yusof,
Foreign Minister II of Brunei Darussalam, in his capacity as Special Envoy of
the ASEAN Chair on Myanmar; and Mr. Due.
“Statements were made by the representatives of the United Kingdom of
Great Britain and Northern Ireland, Norway, Kenya, Ireland, Viet Nam, France,
Niger, Tunisia, the United States of America, Estonia, the Russian Federation,
India, China, Saint Vincent and the Grenadines and the representative of
Mexico, speaking in his capacity as the President of the Security Council.
“His Excellency Dato Pehin Yusof and Mr. Due responded to comments
and questions raised.”
