Letter dated 7 February 2018 from the President of the
Security Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 5 February 2018
(S/2018/102) concerning your intention to appoint Lisa Filipetto (Australia) as Head
of the United Nations Support Office in Somalia has been brought to the attention of
the members of the Security Council. They take note of the intention expressed
therein.
(Signed) Mansour Ayyad Sh. A. Alotaibi
President of the Security Council
