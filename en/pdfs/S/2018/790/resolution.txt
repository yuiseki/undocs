Letter dated 28 August 2018 from the President of the
Security Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 24 August 2018
(S/2018/789) concerning your recommendation to extend the mandate of the United
Nations Regional Office for Central Africa for a period of three years, from
1 September 2018 to 31 August 2021, has been brought to the attention of the
members of the Security Council. They take note of the information contained in your
letter and the recommendation expressed therein.
(Signed) Karen Pierce
President of the Security Council
