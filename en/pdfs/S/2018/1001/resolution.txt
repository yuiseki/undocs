Letter dated 7 November 2018 from the President of the Security
Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 2 November 2018
(S/2018/1000) concerning your intention to appoint Major General Cheryl Pearce of
Australia as Force Commander of the United Nations Peacekeeping Force in Cyprus
has been brought to the attention of the members of the Security Council. They take
note of the intention expressed in your letter. The members of the Council have again
recalled Article 101, paragraph 3, of the Charter of the United Nations.
(Signed) Ma Zhaoxu
President of the Security Council
