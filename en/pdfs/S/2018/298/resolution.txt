Letter dated 3 April 2018 from the President of the Security Council
addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 29 March 2018
(S/2018/297) concerning your intention to appoint Major General Gebre Adhana
Woldezgu (Ethiopia) as Force Commander of the United Nations Interim Security
Force for Abyei has been brought to the attention of the members of the Security
Council. They take note of the intention expressed therein.
(Signed) Gustavo Meza-Cuadra
President of the Security Council
