Letter dated 24 May 2018 from the President of the
Security Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 21 May 2018
(S/2018/498), with reference to Security Council resolution 2366 (2017) establishing
the United Nations Verification Mission in Colombia, has been brought to the
attention of the members of the Council.
The members of the Council have taken note of the request contained in your
letter that the time frame for the submission of the third progress report be extended,
and they look forward to the submission by 20 July 2018.
(Signed) Joanna Wronecka
President of the Security Council
