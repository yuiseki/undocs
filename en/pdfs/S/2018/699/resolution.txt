Letter dated 12 July 2018 from the President of the Security Council
addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 10 July 2018 ( S/2018/698)
concerning your intention to appoint Major General Stefano Del Col (Italy) as Head
of Mission and Force Commander of the United Nations Interim Force in Lebanon
has been brought to the attention of the members of the Security Council. They take
note of the intention expressed in your letter.
(Signed) Olof Skoog
President of the Security Council
