Letter dated 31 July 2018 from the President of the Security Council
addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 27 July 2018 (S/2018/753)
concerning your intention to appoint Ms. Helen Meagher La Lime (United States of
America) as your Special Representative for Haiti and Head of the United Nations
Mission for Justice Support in Haiti (MINUJUSTH) has been br ought to the attention
of the members of the Security Council, who have taken due note of it. The members
of the Council have again recalled Article 101, paragraph 3, of the Charter of the
United Nations.
(Signed) Olof Skoog
President of the Security Council
