Letter dated 24 October 2018 from the Secretary-General
addressed to the President of the Security Council
I have the honour to refer to the appointment of my Special Envoy for the Sudan
and South Sudan effective 1 August 2011 to assist the parties in reaching a negotiated
settlement of the residual Comprehensive Peace Agreement and post -secession issues
after the closure of the United Nations Mission in the Sudan in 2011.
In addition to the recent progress in the South Sudan peace process, the
rapprochement between Eritrea and Ethiopia has created positive momentum for
peace and security in the Horn of Africa and beyond. We expect that the dialogue
initiated between Djibouti and Eritrea will facilitate the resolution of the remaining
challenges in their relationship. As regional leaders embark on efforts to sustain
recent gains and to open a new chapter of cooperation, they will need the support and
assistance of the international community. Our consultation with them has shown that
the interrelated political and security dynamics in the Horn of Africa and the Red Sea
have far-reaching implications for the security and prosperity of the broader region.
In view of the above, I have decided to expand the remit of the Special Envoy
for the Sudan and South Sudan to cover the Horn of Africa region, defined for this
purpose to comprise the members of the Intergovernmental Authority on
Development (IGAD). By doing so, the new responsibilities of the Special Envoy for
the Horn of Africa will be in line with the framework for cooperation between the
United Nations and IGAD signed in Djibouti on 17 November 2015. The Special
Envoy will also work in close collaboration with the African Union High -level
Implementation Panel for Sudan, allowing for closer and streamlined collaboration
among the United Nations, the African Union and IGAD.
In addition to the functions thus far performed by the Special Envoy for the
Sudan and South Sudan, the Special Envoy for the Horn of Africa will have the
following additional functions:
(a) Work closely with, support and assist IGAD and other relevant subregional
and regional organizations in consolidating recent gains in peace and security, and
enhance subregional capacities to address cross-border and cross-cutting issues;
(b) Carry out good offices and special assignments on behalf of the SecretaryGeneral, and, in cooperation with IGAD and other regional and subregional
organizations, as appropriate, enhance subregional capacities for conflict prevention
and mediation;
(c) Complement the capacity of the Department of Political Affairs to advise
the Secretary-General and other senior United Nations officials on matters relating to
