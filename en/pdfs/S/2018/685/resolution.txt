Letter dated 10 July 2018 from the Secretary-General addressed to
the President of the Security Council
I have the honour to refer to Security Council resolution 2418 (2018), adopted
on 31 May 2018, concerning South Sudan, by which the Council decided to extend
until 14 August 2018 the mandate of the Panel of Experts on South Sudan as set out
in paragraph 12 of resolution 2290 (2016).
Accordingly, I wish to inform you that, after consulting with the Security
Council Committee established pursuant to resolution 2206 (2015) concerning South
Sudan, I have administratively extended the appointments and contracts of the
following experts until 14 August 2018:
Mr. Andrews Atta-Asamoah, regional issues (Ghana)
Mr. Andrei Kolmakov, natural resources/finance (Russian Federation)
Ms. Anna Oosterlinck, humanitarian affairs (Belgium)
Mr. Colin Thomas-Jensen, armed groups (United States of America)
I should be grateful if you would bring this matter to the attention of the
members of the Security Council.
(Signed) António Guterres
