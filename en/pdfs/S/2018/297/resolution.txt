Letter dated 29 March 2018 from the Secretary-General addressed
to the President of the Security Council
I have the honour to refer to the United Nations Interim Security Force for Abyei
(UNISFA), established by the Security Council in its resolution 1990 (2011) of
27 June 2011 and most recently extended in resolution 2386 (2017) of 15 November
2017.
Following the usual consultations, I should like to inform you of my intention
to appoint Major General Gebre Adhana Woldezgu (Ethiopia) as Force Commander
of UNISFA. He will succeed Major General Tesfay Gidey Hailemichael (Ethiopia),
who will complete his assignment on 23 April 2018. I should like to take this
opportunity to express my gratitude to Major General Hailemichael for his dedication
and effective leadership of UNISFA.
I should be grateful if you would bring the present letter to the attention of the
members of the Security Council.
(Signed) António Guterres
