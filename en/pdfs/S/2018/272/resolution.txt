Letter dated 27 March 2018 from the Secretary-General addressed
to the President of the Security Council
I have the honour to refer to Security Council resolution 2402 (2018) of
26 February 2018, by which the Council decided to extend the mandate of the Panel
of Experts on Yemen until 28 March 2019.
Accordingly, I wish to inform you that, after consulting with the Security
Council Committee established pursuant to resolution 2140 (2014), I have appointed
the following experts:
Mr. Fernando Carvajal, regional (Mexico)
Mr. Wolf-Christian Paes, arms (Germany)
Ms. Marie-Louise Tougas, international humanitarian law (Canada)
Two additional experts will be appointed shortly.
I should be grateful if you would bring this matter to the attention of the
members of the Security Council.
(Signed) António Guterres
