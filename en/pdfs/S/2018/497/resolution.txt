Enclosure
Report to the United Nations on Kosovo Force operations
1.
The present report is submitted in accordance with Security Council resolution
1244 (1999) and covers the Kosovo Force (KFOR) activities in the period from
1 January to 31 March 2018.
2.
As at 31 March 2018, the total number of KFOR troops in theatre remained
approximately 4,100, from 19 North Atlantic Treaty Organization (NATO) and
8 non-NATO troop-contributing nations.
3.

There was no requirement for KFOR intervention during the reporting period.

Security situation update and operations
4.
During the reporting period, there were no significant changes to the overall
security situation in Kosovo, which remains generally stable, but fragile. There have
been no major disturbances that have significantly affected the safe and secure
environment and freedom of movement.
5.
KFOR conducted routine framework operations across the area of operations,
with joint regional detachments maintaining close contact with the population. KFOR
closely monitored events pertaining to the tenth anniversary celebra tions of Kosovo
and remained poised to adjust its posture when needed. Events held throughout the
10-day period were reported as calm.
6.
Owing to security concerns associated with the murder of political
representative Oliver Ivanovic on 16 January 2018, the Commander of KFOR met
with the Chief of the General Staff of the Serbian Armed Forces in Belgrade on
18 January 2018. KFOR also temporarily increased its patrolling activities in the
Mitrovica North area in order to enhance situational awareness. No s ecurity incidents
were reported.
7.
On 6 March 2018, KFOR organized the International Conference on Women,
Peace and Security in Kosovo at Camp Film City, in Pristina. The conference served
as a platform for international organizations, non-governmental organizations, KFOR
and NATO, as well as Kosovo security organizations, to discuss security and gender related topics.
8.
On 26 March 2018, Serb politician Marko Djuric travelled to Mitrovica for a round table conference on the Serbia internal dialogue on Kosovo. According to the security
organizations in Kosovo, the visit did not seem to comply with the arrangement on
official visits, although Serbia disputes this. Shortly after the start of the meeting, Kosovo
police special operations units detained Mr. Djuric, before returning him to Serbia
approximately two hours later. In unrest associated with the incident, at least 22 civilians
were reported injured. KFOR closely monitored the situation and, on 27 March 2018,
deployed forces near Mitrovica to improve situational awareness, but was not required to
intervene. By 28 March 2018, the situation had returned to normal.
Summary
9.
KFOR continues to contribute to maintaining both a safe and secure
environment and ensuring freedom of movement as part of a comprehensive
international effort. The close coordination on the ground between KFOR (in its
capacity as third responder), the European Union Rule of Law Mission in Kosovo
(EULEX) and Kosovo police continues to be highly effective.
