Letter dated 29 June 2018 from the Secretary-General addressed
to the President of the Security Council
I have the honour to refer to your letter dated 26 June 2018 (S/2018/627)
concurring with my proposal to reappoint 23 judges of the International Residual
Mechanism for Criminal Tribunals.
I have received a corresponding letter from the President of the General
Assembly (see annex I). Accordingly, I am proceeding with the reappointment of the
23 judges of the Mechanism. A list of the judges is set out in annex II to the present
letter.
In accordance with paragraph 2 of Security Council resolution 2269 (2016) of
29 February 2016, the judges will be reappointed for a two -year term commencing on
1 July 2018 and expiring on 30 June 2020.
I will also proceed to reappoint Judge Theodor Meron (United States of
America) as President of the Mechanism from 1 July 2018 to 18 January 2019, and to
appoint Judge Carmel Agius (Malta) as President of the Mechanism from 19 January
2019 to 30 June 2020.
I should be grateful if you would bring the present letter to the attention of the
members of the Security Council.
(Signed) António Guterres

Annex I
Letter dated 27 June 2018 from the President of the
General Assembly addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to refer to your letter dated 22 June 2018 (S/2018/626)
concerning the reappointment of 23 judges of the International Residual Mechanism
for Criminal Tribunals.
Pursuant to article 10, paragraph 3, of the statute of the Mechanism, I am pleased
to inform you that I hereby concur with the proposed reappointment of the 23 judges
listed in the annex of that letter, for a term of two years, from 1 July 2018 to 30 June
2020, in line with the upcoming period of operation of the Mechanism.
(Signed) Miroslav Lajčák
