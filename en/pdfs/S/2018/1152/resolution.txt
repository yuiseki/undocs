Letter dated 21 December 2018 from the President of the
Security Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 19 December 2018
(S/2018/1151), concerning your intention to appoint Mr. Mahandrisoa Edmond
Randrianirina (Madagascar) as a judge of the International Residual Mechanism for
Criminal Tribunals, has been brought to the attention of the members of the Security
Council. They take note of the intention expressed in your letter.
(Signed) Kacou Houadja Léon Adom
President of the Security Council
