Letter dated 31 January 2018 from the President of the
Security Council addressed to the Secretary-General
The ceremony, held on 2 January 2018, at which the national flags of the newly elected members of the Security Council were solemnly installed at the entrance to
the Security Council Chamber, was most warmly received by the incoming Security
Council ambassadors and their delegations. This innovative practice would not have
been possible without your support and the able assistance of the Secretariat. We are
grateful to you and the Protocol and Liaison Service, as well as to the relevant staff
of the Departments of Political Affairs, Safety and Security, and Public Information,
for their assistance in making the ceremony an unquestioned success.
Kazakhstan introduced the flag installation ceremony during its Presidency in
January, which coincided with the start of the new term for incoming non-permanent
Council members. The aim was to recognize the trust that the international
community has placed in these six countries to uphold the ideals of the Charter of the
United Nations and contribute to the maintenance of international peace and security.
The ceremony was also a fitting occasion at which to commend the dedicated efforts
of the outgoing members, which have left their mark on the Council through their
outstanding work. The ceremony followed proper proto col by honouring the
Governments of the new Council members, which had been chosen by the wider
United Nations membership to act on their collective behalf in addressing the main
security challenges facing the world today.
The members of the Security Council are united in the conviction that, with the
continued support of the Secretariat, the flag installation ceremony each January will
become a meaningful annual tradition.
Your personal support in this endeavour would reinforce the important
contribution that the elected Council members make to further the purposes and ideals
for which the United Nations stands.
(Signed) Kairat Umarov
Ambassador
