Letter dated 6 March 2018 from the President of the Security Council
addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 1 March 2018
(S/2018/190) concerning your intention to appoint Judge Elizabeth Ibanda -Nahamya
(Uganda) as a judge of the International Residual Mechanism for Criminal Tribunals
has been brought to the attention of the members of the Security Council. They take
note of the intention expressed in your letter.
(Signed) Karel Jan Gustaaf van Oosterom
President of the Security Council
