Letter dated 25 June 2018 from the Secretary-General addressed
to the President of the Security Council
I have the honour to refer to the United Nations Office to the African Union,
established on 1 July 2010 by the General Assembly in its resolution 64/288.
Following the usual consultations, I should like to inform you of my intention
to appoint Sahle-Work Zewde (Ethiopia) as my Special Representative to the African
Union and Head of the United Nations Office to the African Union, at the level of
Under-Secretary-General.
Ms. Zewde will succeed Haile Menkerios, whose assignment will end on
30 June 2018. I should like to take this opportunity to express my gratitude to
Mr. Menkerios for his dedicated service to the Organization and his effective
leadership of the Office.
I should be grateful if you would bring this matter to the attention of the
members of the Security Council.
(Signed) António Guterres
