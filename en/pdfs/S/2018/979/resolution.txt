Letter dated 31 October 2018 from the President of the
Security Council addressed to the Secretary-General

(S/2018/955) concerning the expansion of the remit of the Special Envoy for Sudan
and South Sudan to cover the Horn of Africa region has been brought to the attention
of the members of the Security Council. They take note of the information contained
in the letter.
