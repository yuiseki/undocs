Letter dated 29 August 2018 from the Secretary-General
addressed to the President of the Security Council
Following the usual consultations, I wish to inform you of my intention to
appoint Jeanine Hennis-Plasschaert (the Netherlands) as my Special Representative
for Iraq and Head of the United Nations Assistance Mission for Iraq. She will succeed
Ján Kubiš (Slovakia), to whom I am grateful for his service.
Ms. Hennis-Plasschaert has over 20 years of political and diplomatic experience
and served as Minister of Defence of the Netherlands from 2012 to 2017. In her new
role, she will have overall authority for the activities of the United Nations in Iraq
and provide leadership, political guidance and high-level operational direction in the
execution of the work of the Mission. She will build on the efforts and achievements
of her predecessor and other United Nations and international efforts.
I would be grateful if you could bring this matter to the attention of the members
of the Security Council.
(Signed) António Guterres
