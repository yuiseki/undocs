Letter dated 14 March 2018 from the Secretary-General addressed
to the President of the Security Council
I have the honour to refer to Security Council resolution 2400 (2018) of
8 February 2018, by which the Council decided to extend the mandate of the Panel of
Experts on the Sudan until 12 March 2019.
Accordingly, I wish to inform you that, after consulting with the Security
Council Committee established pursuant to resolution 1591 (2005) concerning the
Sudan, I have appointed the following experts:
• Ms. Priscilla Ciesay, international humanitarian law (United Kingdom of Great
Britain and Northern Ireland)
• Mr. Vincent Darracq, arms and armed groups (France)
• Mr. Thomas Bifwoli Wanjala, transport and customs (Kenya)
• Mr. Rajeev Yadav, finance (India)
One additional expert will be appointed shortly.
I have designated Mr. Wanjala to serve as Coordinator of the Panel of Experts.
I should be grateful if you would bring this matter to the attention of the
members of the Security Council.
(Signed) António Guterres
