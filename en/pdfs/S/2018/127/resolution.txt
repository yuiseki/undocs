Letter dated 15 February 2018 from the President of the
Security Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 13 February 2018
(S/2018/126) concerning your intention to appoint Martin Griffiths (United Kingdom
of Great Britain and Northern Ireland) as your Special Envoy for Yemen at the
Under-Secretary-General level has been brought to the attention of the members of
the Security Council. They take note of the intention expressed in your letter.
(Signed) Mansour Ayyad Sh. A. Alotaibi
President of the Security Council
