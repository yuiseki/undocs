Letter dated 3 May 2018 from the President of the
Security Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 1 May 2018 ( S/2018/420)
concerning your intention to appoint José Viegas Filho (Brazil) as your Special
Representative for Guinea-Bissau and Head of the United Nations Integrated
Peacebuilding Office in Guinea-Bissau, at the level of Under-Secretary-General, has
been brought to the attention of the members of the Security Council. They take note
of the intention expressed in your letter.
(Signed) Joanna Wronecka
President of the Security Council
