Letter dated 27 June 2018 from the President of the
Security Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 25 June 2018 (S/2018/645)
concerning your intention to appoint Sahle-Work Zewde (Ethiopia) as your Special
Representative to the African Union and Head of the United Nation s Office to the
African Union, at the level of Under-Secretary-General, has been brought to the
attention of the members of the Security Council. They take note of the intention
expressed in your letter.
(Signed) Vassily A. Nebenzia
President of the Security Council
