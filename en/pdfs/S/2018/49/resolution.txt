Letter dated 18 January 2018 from the Secretary-General
addressed to the President of the Security Council
I have the honour to refer to Security Council resolution 2360 (2017), adopted
on 21 June 2017, by which the Council decided to extend the mandate of the Group
of Experts on the Democratic Republic of the Congo until 1 August 2018.
In connection with my letter dated 4 August 2017 (S/2017/683), I wish to inform
you that, after consulting with the Security Council Committee established pursuant
to resolution 1533 (2004) concerning the Democratic Republic of the Congo, I have
appointed Mr. Nelson Alusala, natural resources/finance expert (Kenya), to replace
Mr. Roberto Sollazzo (Italy), who resigned from the Group.
I should be grateful if you would bring this matter to the attention of the
members of the Security Council.
(Signed) António Guterres
