Letter dated 13 December 2018 from the Secretary-General
addressed to the President of the Security Council
I have the honour to refer to Security Council resolution 2444 (2018), adopted
on 14 November 2018, concerning the situation in Somalia. Under paragraph 11 of
the resolution, the Council decided to establish a Panel of Experts on Somalia with
effect until 15 December 2019.
Accordingly, I wish to inform you that, having co nsulted the Security Council
Committee pursuant to resolution 751 (1992) concerning Somalia, I have appointed
the following experts:
Mr. Mohamed Babiker, humanitarian (Sudan)
Mr. Jay Bahadur, regional (Canada)
Ms. Nazanine Moshiri, armed groups (Islamic Republic of Iran)
Mr. Brian O’Sullivan, armed groups/natural resources (Ireland)
Mr. Matthew Rosbottom, finance (United Kingdom of Great Britain and
Northern Ireland)
Mr. Richard Zabot, arms (France)
I have designated Mr. Jay Bahadur to serve as Coordinator of the Panel of
Experts on Somalia.
I should be grateful if you would bring the present letter to the attention of the
members of the Security Council.
(Signed) António Guterres
