Letter dated 29 May 2018 from the Secretary-General addressed
to the President of the Security Council
I have the honour to refer to paragraph 5 (a) of Security Council resolution 1977
(2011), by which the Council requested the Secretary-General to establish, in
consultation with the Security Council Committee established pursuant to resolution
1540 (2004), a group of up to eight experts, acting under the direction and purview
of the Committee, composed of individuals with the appropriate experience and
knowledge to provide the Committee with expertise, to assist the Committee in
carrying out its mandate.
Subsequently, in its resolution 2055 (2012), the Security Council decided to
increase the size of the group of experts to up to nine experts.
Recalling paragraph 5 (a) of resolution 1977 (2011), the Committee, in its report
on recommendations for the structure, methods, modalities, expertise and
representation of the Committee and its group of experts ( S/2011/819, annex),
recommended that the Secretary-General, in consultation with and with the prior
consent of the Committee, appoint a coordinator of the group, who would coordinate
the activities of the other members of the group.
On 21 May 2018, the High Representative for Disarmament Affairs was
informed that the Committee had nominated Raphaël Prenat (France) to serve as
Coordinator of the group of experts.
Accordingly, I wish to inform you that I have appointed Mr. Prenat to that role.
I should be grateful if you would bring this matter to the attention of the
members of the Security Council.
(Signed) António Guterres
