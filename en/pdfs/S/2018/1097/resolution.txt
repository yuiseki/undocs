Letter dated 5 December 2018 from the Secretary-General
addressed to the President of the Security Council
I have the honour to refer to the United Nations Verification Mission in
Colombia established by Security Council resolution 2366 (2017) of 10 July 2017.
The mandate of the Verification Mission was extended by resolution 2435 (2018) of
13 September 2018.
Following the usual consultations, I would like to inform you of my intention to
appoint Mr. Carlos Ruiz Massieu (Mexico) as my Special Representative for
Colombia and Head of the United Nations Verification Mission in Colombia. Mr. Ruiz
Massieu shall succeed Mr. Jean Arnault (France), whose term of office expires on
31 December 2018.
I would like to take this opportunity to express my deep gratitude to Mr. Arnault
for his exemplary service in the cause of peace in Colombia, initially as Delegate of
the Secretary-General to the peace negotiations in Havana, and subsequently as the
Special Representative of the Secretary-General for Colombia and Head of the United
Nations Mission in Colombia and its successor, the Verification Mission.
I should be grateful if you would bring this matter to the attention of the
members of the Security Council.
(Signed) António Guterres
