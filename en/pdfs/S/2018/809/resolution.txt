Letter dated 31 August 2018 from the President of the
Security Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 29 August 2018
(S/2018/808) concerning your intention to appoint Jeanine Hennis-Plasschaert (the
Netherlands) as your Special Representative for Iraq and Head of the United Nations
Assistance Mission for Iraq has been brought to the attention of the members of the
Security Council. They take note of the intention expressed in your letter.
(Signed) Karen Pierce
President of the Security Council
