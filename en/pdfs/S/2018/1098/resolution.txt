Letter dated 7 December 2018 from the President of the
Security Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 5 December 2018
(S/2018/1097) concerning your intention to appoint Mr. Carlos Ruiz Massieu
(Mexico) as your Special Representative for Colombia and Head of the United
Nations Verification Mission in Colombia has been brought to the attention of the
members of the Security Council. They take note of the intention expressed in your
letter.
(Signed) Léon H. Kacou Adom
President of the Security Council
