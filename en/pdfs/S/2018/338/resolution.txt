Letter dated 10 April 2018 from the Secretary-General addressed
to the President of the Security Council
I have the honour to refer to the United Nations Organization Stabilization
Mission in the Democratic Republic of the Congo (MONUSCO), whose mandate was
established pursuant to Security Council resolution 1925 (2010) and extended, most
recently, by resolution 2409 (2018) for a period of one year.
Following the usual consultations, I should like to inform you of my intention
to appoint Lieutenant General Elias Rodrigues Martins Filho (Brazil) as Force
Commander of MONUSCO. Lieutenant General Martins Filho succeeds Lieutenant
General Derrick Mbuyiselo Mgwebi (South Africa), who completed his tour of duty
on 30 January 2018.
I should like to take this opportunity to express my gratitude to Lieutenant
General Mgwebi for his dedication and effective leadershi p of MONUSCO.
I should be grateful if you would bring the present letter to the attention of the
members of the Security Council.
(Signed) António Guterres
