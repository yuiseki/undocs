Enclosure
Report to the United Nations on the operations of the Kosovo Force
1.
The present report is submitted in accordance with Security Council resolution
1244 (1999) and covers the activities of the Kosovo Force (KFOR) during the period
from 1 October to 31 December 2017.
2.
As at 31 December 2017, the total number of KFOR troops in theatre remained
approximately 4,100, from 21 North Atlantic Treaty Organization (NATO) and
8 non-NATO troop-contributing countries.
3.
There were no major security incidents during the reporting period and no
requirement for KFOR intervention.
Security situation update and operations
4.
The reporting period brought no significant changes to the overall security
situation in Kosovo, which remained generally stable but fragile, without major
disturbances of the safe and secure environment and freedom of movement.
5.
During the reporting period, KFOR conducted routine framework operations
across the area of operations, with joint regional detachments maintaining close
contact with the population. In support of the Organization for Security and
Cooperation in Europe, as mandated in resolution 1244 (1999), KFOR contributed to
the effective execution of the local elections on 22 October and 19 November 2017.
KFOR conducted increased presence patrols in the vicinity of the most important
polling stations to address potential security concerns. No major irregularities or
incidents were reported.
6.
KFOR continues its close cooperation with both the Kosovo police and the
European Union Rule of Law Mission (EULEX). One example is the biannual KFOR led Silver Sabre exercise, which was last conducted in October 2017 with other
security providers in Kosovo, namely, EULEX, the Kosovo Security Force, the
Kosovo police and the Emergency Management Agency, in Pristina in the vicinity of
KFOR camps. The exercise focused on the interoperability of participants in planning
and executing civil disturbance management and emergency disaster relief
operations.
7.
In November 2017, as part of the efforts to maintain an effective force posture
in sensitive areas and to ensure an immediate response if required, KFOR conducted
operational rehearsal activities within its normal training cycle in order to verify the
readiness of the operational reserve forces.
Summary
8.
KFOR continues to contribute both to maintaining a safe and secure
environment and to ensuring freedom of movement as part of a comprehensive
international effort. The close coordination at the tactical level between KFOR in i ts
capacity as third responder, EULEX and the Kosovo police continues to be highly
effective.
