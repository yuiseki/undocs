Letter dated 28 August 2018 from the Secretary-General
addressed to the President of the Security Council
I have the honour to refer to the United Nations Multidimensional Integrated
Stabilization Mission in Mali (MINUSMA), whose mandate was established pursuant
to Security Council resolution 2100 (2013) of 25 April 2013 and most recently
extended in Council resolution 2423 (2018) of 28 June 2018.
Following the usual consultations, I would like to inform you of my intention to
appoint Lieutenant General Dennis Gyllensporre (Sweden) as Force Commander of
MINUSMA. Lieutenant General Gyllensporre will succeed Major General Jean -Paul
Deconinck (Belgium), who will complete his tour of duty on 2 October 2018. I would
like to take this opportunity to express my gratitude to Major General Deconinck for
his contribution to MINUSMA.
I should be grateful if you would bring the present letter to the attention of the
members of the Security Council.
(Signed) António Guterres
