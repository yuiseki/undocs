Letter dated 20 August 2018 from the Secretary-General
addressed to the President of the Security Council
I have the honour to refer to Security Council resolution 2428 (2018), by which
the Council decided to extend until 1 July 2019 the mandate of the Panel o f Experts
on South Sudan, as set out in paragraph 18 of Council resolution 2206 (2015) and
paragraph 19 of resolution 2428 (2018).
Accordingly, I wish to inform you that, after consulting with the Security
Council Committee established pursuant to resolution 2206 (2015) concerning South
Sudan, I have appointed the following experts to serve on the Pane l until 1 July 2019:
Mr. Michael Gibb, natural resources (Norway)
Mr. Dean Gillespie, arms (South Africa)
Mr. Andrei Kolmakov, finance (Russian Federation)
Ms. Renifa Madenga, humanitarian affairs (Zimbabwe)
Mr. Emilio Manfredi, armed groups/regional issues (Italy)
I have designated Mr. Gibb to serve as Coordinator of the Panel.
I should be grateful if you would bring this matter to the attention of the
members of the Security Council.
(Signed) António Guterres
