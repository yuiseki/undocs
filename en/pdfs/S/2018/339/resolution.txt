Letter dated 12 April 2018 from the President of the
Security Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 10 April 2018
(S/2018/338) concerning your intention to appoint Lieutenant General Elias
Rodrigues Martins Filho (Brazil) as Force Commander of the United Nations
Stabilization Mission in the Democratic Republic of the Congo (MONUSCO) has
been brought to the attention of the members of the Security Council. They take note
of the intention expressed in your letter.
(Signed) Gustavo Meza-Cuadra
President of the Security Council
