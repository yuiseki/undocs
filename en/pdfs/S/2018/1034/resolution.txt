Enclosure
Report on the operational support provided by French forces to
the United Nations Multidimensional Integrated Stabilization
Mission in the Central African Republic, from 1 January to
1 October 2018
Background documents:
– Security Council resolution 2387 (2017) of 15 November 2017 (paras. 65
and 67)
– Joint procedural document of 11 January 2017 signed by the National Support
Element, the Central African Republic and the United Nations Multidimensional
Integrated Stabilization Mission in the Central African Republic (MINUSCA)
The present report summarizes the support provided by French forces to
MINUSCA from 1 January to 1 October 2018.
With regard to operations, the aerial component of ground support for Operation
Barkhane has the capacity to provide ad hoc air support to MINUSCA. To that end,
Operation Barkhane conducted joint close air support training exe rcises with
Portuguese joint terminal attack controllers from the quick reaction force on 22 March
and 28 September 2018. A show of force was conducted on 13 May 2018 to deter an
ex-Séléka faction from marching into Bangui.
With regard to the overall partnership between French forces and MINUSCA,
since September, French forces have been providing technical training support to the
Gabonese battalion of MINUSCA in the following four areas: health; military police
and gendarmerie; troop support; and ammunition.
Informal discussions take place regularly between the French support
detachment stationed in M’Poko (Bangui) and MINUSCA. There are also discussions
with the Strategic Communication and Public Information Division of MINUSCA.
With regard to engineering support for airport infrastructure, the French support
detachment stationed in M’Poko is currently repairing the taxiway to the Joint Transit
Unit, which is used by MINUSCA aircraft to return to their parking area. French
forces also used their technical expertise to help MINUSCA with the inspection of
the runway at Bouar Airport, which is used by MINUSCA to conduct logistics
operations by air.
With regard to logistics, the French support detachment stationed in M ’Poko
handled the cargo of the Gabonese battalion of MINUSCA and played host to the
Gabonese forces while they were in transit.
French forces based in M’Poko helped to manage the transfer of equipment to
the Gabonese battalion (the forces transferred three trailers ’ worth of field showers
for value in August 2018; carried out technical maintenance of five armoured
vanguard vehicles in preparation for their transfer at the end of November 2018; and
facilitated the logistics of transferring nine Marauder armoured vehicles in September
2018).
With regard to health support, French forces cooperated with the role 1 medical
unit of the Portuguese quick reaction force by making available an ultrasound scanner
through the French role 1 unit and by providing treatment when required. French
forces would also be able to provide support to victims in the event of a mass casualty
incident, if necessary.
