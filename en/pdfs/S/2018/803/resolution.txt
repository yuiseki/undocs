Letter dated 30 August 2018 from the President of the
Security Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 28 August 2018
(S/2018/802) concerning your intention to appoint Lieutenant General Dennis
Gyllensporre (Sweden) as Force Commander of the United Nations Multidimensional
Integrated Stabilization Mission in Mali has been brought to the attention of the
members of the Security Council. They take note of the intention expressed in your
letter.
(Signed) Karen Pierce
President of the Security Council
