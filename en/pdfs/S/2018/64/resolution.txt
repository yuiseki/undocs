Letter dated 24 January 2018 from the President of the
Security Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 19 January 2018
(S/2018/63) concerning the submission of terms of reference pursuant to paragraph 4
of resolution 2379 (2017) has been brought to the attention of the members of the
Security Council.
The members of the Security Council have taken note of the request contained
in your letter that the time frame for the submission of the terms of reference be
extended. They look forward to the submission of the terms of reference by
9 February 2018.
(Signed) Kairat Umarov
President of the Security Council
