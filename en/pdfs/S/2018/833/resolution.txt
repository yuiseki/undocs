Letter dated 11 September 2018 from the President of the
Security Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 7 September 2018
(S/2018/832) concerning your intention to appoint Nicholas Haysom (South Africa)
as your Special Representative for Somalia and Head of the United Nations
Assistance Mission in Somalia has been brought to the attention of the members of
the Security Council. They take note of the intention expressed in your letter.
(Signed) Nikki R. Haley
President of the Security Council
