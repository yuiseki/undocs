Letter dated 27 July 2018 from the Secretary-General addressed to
the President of the Security Council
I have the honour to refer to the United Nations Mission for Justice Support in
Haiti (MINUJUSTH), established pursuant to Security Council resolution 2350
(2017) of 13 April 2017, the mandate of which was most recently extended by the
Council in its resolution 2410 (2018) of 10 April 2018.
Following the usual consultations, I would like to inform you of my intention to
appoint Ms. Helen Meagher La Lime (United States of America) as my Special
Representative for Haiti and Head of MINUJUSTH. Ms. La Lime will succeed
Ms. Susan D. Page, who completed her assignment on 4 May 2018. I would like to
take this opportunity to express my gratitude to Ms. Page for her dedicated service
and effective leadership of MINUJUSTH.
I should be grateful if you would bring this matter to the attention of the
members of the Security Council.
(Signed) António Guterres
