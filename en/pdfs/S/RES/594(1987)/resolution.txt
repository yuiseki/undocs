Decision
On 13 February 1987. after consultations. the President
made the following statement on behalf of the members of
the Council:"
.. The members of the Security Council, mindful of the
sovereignty. independence and territorial integrity of
Lebanon, express their profound concern at the continued
escalation of violence in certain parts of Lebanon.
affecting the civilian population, particularly in and
around Palestinian refugee camps.
.. Deeply alarmed by the tragic suffering undergone by
the civilian population. particularly inside the Palestinian
refugee camps. they call on the parties concerned to
observe an immediate cease-fire and to permit access to
these camps for humanitarian purposes.
