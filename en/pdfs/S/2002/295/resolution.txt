Letter dated 19 March 2002 from the President of the Security
Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 14 March 2002
(S/2002/294) concerning your decision to appoint Ibrahima Fall as your Special
Representative and Head of the Office of the Special Representative of the
Secretary-General for West Africa has been brought to the attention of the members
of the Security Council. They take note of the decision contained in your letter.
(Signed) Ole Peter Kolby
President of the Security Council
