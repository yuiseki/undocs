Letter dated 16 July 2002 from the President of the
Security Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 11 July 2002
(S/2002/772) concerning your intention to appoint Mr. Ibrahima Fall as your Special
Representative for the Great Lakes Region has been brought to the attention of the
members of the Security Council. They take note of the intention contained in your
letter.
(Signed) Jeremy Greenstock
President of the Security Council
