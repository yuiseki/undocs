Report of the Security Council mission to the Great Lakes
region, 27 April-7 May 2002
Addendum
The present addendum contains the annexes to the report of the Security
Council mission to the Great Lakes region.
Annex I contains a non-paper entitled “International conference on peace,
security, democracy and development in the Great Lakes region”, submitted by the
Security Council to its interlocutors during its mission to the Great Lakes region.
Annex II contains a communiqué issued at the conclusion of the joint meeting
of the Political Committee on the Implementation of the Lusaka Ceasefire
Agreement in the Democratic Republic of the Congo and the Security Council
mission to the Great Lakes region, held in Luanda on 2 May 2002.
