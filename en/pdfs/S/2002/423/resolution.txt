Letter dated 15 April 2002 from the Secretary-General to the
President of the Security Council
In accordance with article 4, paragraph 13, of the Peace Agreement of 12
December 2000, the Eritrea-Ethiopia Boundary Commission has transmitted its
decision regarding delimitation to the parties and to the Secretaries-General of the
Organization of African Unity and the United Nations for publication. The decision,
transmitted on 13 April 2002, is annexed to the present letter.*
I should be grateful if you could bring this to the attention of the Council
members.
(Signed) Kofi A. Annan

* The decision is reproduced in the language of submission only.
