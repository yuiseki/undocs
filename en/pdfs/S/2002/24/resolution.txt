Letter dated 4 January 2002 from the President of the Security
Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 31 December 2001
(S/2002/23) concerning your intention to appoint Haile Menkerios as your new
Representative in Liberia and Head of the United Nations Peace-building Support
Office in Liberia (UNOL) as from 1 February 2002 has been brought to the attention
of the members of the Security Council. They take note of the intention contained in
your letter.
(Signed) Jagdish Koonjul
President of the Security Council
