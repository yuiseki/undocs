Letter dated 13 September 2002 from the Secretary-General
addressed to the President of the Security Council
I have the honour to refer to the United Nations Peace-building Support Office
in Liberia (UNOL) and to inform the Security Council of my intention to appoint
Mr. Abou Moussa (Chad) as my new Representative in Liberia and head of UNOL
as soon as possible.
Mr. Moussa, who is currently the Regional Director of the Office of the United
Nations High Commissioner for Refugees in Abidjan, will succeed Mr. Felix
Downes-Thomas, who completed his mission in Liberia in February 2002.
I should be grateful if you would bring the present letter to the attention of the
members of the Security Council.
(Signed) Kofi A. Annan
