Letter dated 13 December 2002 from the Secretary-General
addressed to the President of the Security Council
I have the honour to refer to Security Council resolution S/RES/1284 (1999),
which requires me to consult the Security Council and the Executive Chairman of
the United Nations Monitoring, Verification and Inspection Commission
(UNMOVIC) on the appointment of suitably qualified experts as UNMOVIC
Commissioners.
Ms. Malmi Marjatta Rautio resigned from the College of Commissioners
recently and I would like to propose the appointment of Ms. Olga Pellicer (Mexico),
Professor, Department of International Studies, Autonomous Technological Institute
of Mexico.
I should be grateful if you would bring this matter to the attention of the
Security Council.
(Signed) Kofi A. Annan
