Letter dated 4 April 2002 from the President of the
Security Council addressed to the Secretary-General
In accordance with the provisions of Security Council resolution 689 (1991)
and in the light of your report of 28 March 2002 (S/2002/323), the members of the
Security Council have reviewed the question of the termination or continuation of
the United Nations Iraq-Kuwait Observation Mission (UNIKOM) and its modalities
of operation.
I have the honour to inform you that the members of the Council concur with
your recommendation that UNIKOM be maintained. In accordance with resolution
689 (1991), they have decided to review the question once again by 6 October 2002.
(Signed) Sergey Lavrov
President of the Security Council
