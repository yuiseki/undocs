Letter dated 2 July 2002 from the President of the Security
Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 27 June 2002
(S/2002/719) concerning your decision to appoint Mr. Berhanu Dinka as your
Special Representative for Burundi effective 1 July 2002 has been brought to the
attention of the members of the Security Council. They take note of your decision
and look forward to your proposal for a successor to Mr. Dinka as Special
Representative of the Secretary-General for the Great Lakes region.
(Signed) Jeremy Greenstock
President of the Security Council
