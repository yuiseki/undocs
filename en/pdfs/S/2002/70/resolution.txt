Note by the President of the Security Council
After consultations among the members of the Security Council, it has been
agreed that Martin Belinga-Eboutou, Permanent Representative of Cameroon to the
United Nations, will serve as Chairman of the informal working group of the
Security Council established pursuant to paragraph 3 of the note by the President of
the Security Council dated 17 April 2000 (S/2000/319), until 31 December 2003.
The task of the working group is to develop general recommendations on how to
improve the effectiveness of United Nations sanctions.
