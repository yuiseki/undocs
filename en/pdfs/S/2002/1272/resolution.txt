Letter dated 20 November 2002 from the Secretary-General
addressed to the President of the Security Council
I have the honour to refer to my letter dated 26 September 2002 (S/2002/1106)
by which I forwarded to the Security Council, in accordance with paragraph 1 (c) of
article 12 bis of the Statute of the International Tribunal for Rwanda, the
nominations for permanent judges of that Tribunal which I had received from States
Members of the United Nations and non-member States maintaining permanent
observer missions at United Nations Headquarters within the 60-day period
specified in paragraph 1 (b) of article 12 bis of its Statute.
I also have the honour to refer to the letter dated 11 October 2002 (document
S/2002/1131) of the former President of the Security Council, Martin BelingaEboutou, in which he informed me of the decision of the Council, taken at its 4621st
meeting on 11 October 2002, to extend the deadline for nominations of candidates
for permanent judges of the International Tribunal for Rwanda until 15 November
2002.
Pursuant to paragraph 1 (c) of article 12 bis of the Statute of the International
Tribunal for Rwanda, I have the honour to forward herewith to the Security Council
the 22 nominations which I have received from States Members of the United
Nations and non-member States maintaining permanent observer missions at United
Nations Headquarters within the period specified in paragraph 1 (b) of that same
article, as extended by the Council in the decision taken at its 4621st meeting. The
list of candidates, in alphabetical order, together with the curricula vitae provided in
connection with their nomination, is annexed to the present letter.*
(Signed) Kofi A. Annan

* The material is circulated only to the members of the Security Council.
