Annex
Letter dated 28 February 2002 from the High Representative for
the Implementation of the Peace Agreement on Bosnia and
Herzegovina addressed to the Secretary-General
The Steering Board of the Peace Implementation Council requested me to
inform you that today they accepted the offer made by the EU General Affairs
Council to provide a European Union Police Mission (EUPM) to follow the
International Police Task Force mandate from 1 January 2003.
At the same session, the Steering Board designated Lord Ashdown as the new
High Representative for Bosnia and Herzegovina, and asked the Security Council to
endorse his appointment.
I have enclosed a copy of the Steering Board communiqué of 28 February
2002 for your further reference.
I look forward to seeing you in New York next week.
(Signed) Wolfgang Petritsch
