Note by the President of the Security Council
Following the discussion in the Security Council on the agenda item entitled
“Africa’s food crisis as a threat to peace and security”, held at the 4652nd meeting
of the Council, on 3 December 2002, the attached paper, containing responses to
questions posed at the meeting to Mr. James Morris, Executive Director of the
World Food Programme, is hereby circulated for the attention of the members of the
Council.
