Letter dated 19 December 2002 from the President of the Security
Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 13 December 2002
concerning your proposal to appoint Ms. Olga Pellicer (Mexico) as a Commissioner
of the United Nations Monitoring, Verification and Inspection Commission has been
brought to the attention of the members of the Security Council. They agree with the
proposal contained in your letter.
(Signed) Alfonso Valdivieso
President of the Security Council
