Letter dated 1 May 2002 from the President of the Security
Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 26 April 2002
(S/2002/501) concerning your intention to continue the activities of the United
Nations Tajikistan Office of Peace-building (UNTOP) for a further period of one
year, until 1 June 2003, has been brought to the attention of the members of the
Security Council. They take note with appreciation of the information and of the
intention contained therein.
(Signed) Kishore Mahbubani
President of the Security Council
