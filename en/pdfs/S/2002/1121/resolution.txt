Letter dated 8 October 2002 from the President of the
Security Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 4 October 2002
(S/2002/1120), in which you indicated your intention to appoint Major-General
Robert Gordon (United Kingdom of Great Britain and Northern Ireland) as Force
Commander of the United Nations Mission in Ethiopia and Eritrea (UNMEE), has
been brought to the attention of the members of the Security Council, who have
taken note of your intention.
(Signed) Martin Belinga-Eboutou
President of the Security Council
