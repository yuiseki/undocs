Letter dated 21 January 2002 from the Secretary-General
addressed to the President of the Security Council
I have the honour to inform you that it is my intention to appoint
Michael Steiner (Germany) as my Special Representative and Head of the
United Nations Interim Administration Mission in Kosovo.
I should be grateful if you would bring this matter to the attention of the
members of the Security Council.
(Signed) Kofi A. Annan
