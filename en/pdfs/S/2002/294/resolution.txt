Letter dated 14 March 2002 from the Secretary-General addressed
to the President of the Security Council
Further to my letter of 26 November 2001 (S/2001/1128) to the President of
the Security Council concerning the establishment of the Office of the Special
Representative of the Secretary-General for West Africa, I have the honour to
inform you that I have decided to appoint Ibrahima Fall, currently Assistant
Secretary-General for Political Affairs, as my Special Representative and Head of
the Office of the Special Representative of the Secretary-General for West Africa.
I should be grateful if you would bring the present letter to the attention of the
members of the Security Council.
(Signed) Kofi A. Annan
