Letter dated 16 July 2002 from the President of the Security
Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that, as requested, your recommendation in
your letter dated 11 July 2002 (S/2002/768) to extend the mandate of the United
Nations Office in Angola (UNOA) for a period of one month, until 15 August 2002,
has been brought to the attention of the members of the Security Council. They
concurred with this recommendation.
(Signed) Jeremy Greenstock
President of the Security Council
