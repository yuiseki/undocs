Letter dated 18 October 2002 from the President of the Security
Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 9 October 2002
(S/2002/1174) concerning your intention to extend the mandate of Mr. Ibrahima Fall
as your Special Representative for the Great Lakes region until 31 December 2003
has been brought to the attention of the members of the Security Council. They have
taken note of your intention and look forward to receiving the assessment and
proposals referred to in your letter.
(Signed) Martin Belinga-Eboutou
President of the Security Council
