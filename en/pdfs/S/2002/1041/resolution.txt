Letter dated 18 September 2002 from the President of the Security
Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 13 September 2002
(S/2002/1040) concerning your intention to appoint Mr. Abou Moussa (Chad) as
your Representative in Liberia and head of the United Nations Peace-building
Support Office in Liberia has been brought to the attention of the members of the
Security Council. They take note of the intention contained in your letter.
(Signed) Stefan Tafrov
President of the Security Council
