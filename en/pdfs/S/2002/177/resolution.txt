Letter dated 19 February 2002 from the President of the
Security Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 12 February 2002
(S/2002/176) concerning your intention to appoint Winston A. Tubman as your
Representative in Somalia and Head of the United Nations Political Office for
Somalia has been brought to the attention of the members of the Security Council.
They take note of the intention contained in your letter.
(Signed) Adolfo Aguilar Zínser
President of the Security Council
