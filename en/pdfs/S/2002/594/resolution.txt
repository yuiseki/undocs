Letter dated 29 May 2002 from the President of the Security
Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 23 May 2002
(S/2002/593) concerning your proposal to add Croatia to the list of contributors to
the United Nations Military Observer Group in India and Pakistan has been brought
to the attention of the members of the Council. They take note of the proposal
contained therein.
(Signed) Kishore Mahbubani
