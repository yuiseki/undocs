Letter dated 2 April 2002 from the Secretary-General addressed to
the President of the Security Council
I have the honour to refer to the United Nations Transitional Administration in
East Timor (UNTAET), and to Security Council resolution 1392 (2002) requesting
me to submit further specific proposals for a successor United Nations mission after
independence.
It is my intention, subject to the Council’s approval of the establishment of a
successor mission to UNTAET, to appoint Kamalesh Sharma (India) as my Special
Representative for East Timor and Head of that mission, with effect from 21 May
2002.
Mr. Sharma will succeed Sergio Vieira de Mello, who has served as my Special
Representative and Transitional Administrator with great distinction. I would like to
put on record my sincere appreciation for his exceptional leadership of UNTAET
over the past two and a half years.
I should be grateful if you would bring this matter to the attention of the
members of the Security Council.
(Signed) Kofi A. Annan
