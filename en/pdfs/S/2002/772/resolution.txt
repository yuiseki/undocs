Letter dated 11 July 2002 from the Secretary-General addressed to
the President of the Security Council
I have the honour to refer to my letter to you dated 16 November 2001
(S/2001/1095) and the reply of the President of the Security Council (S/2001/1096)
of 21 November 2001 concerning the mandate of my Special Representative for the
Great Lakes Region, as well as my letter to you dated 27 June 2002 (S/2002/719)
and your reply of 2 July 2002 (S/2002/720) on the reassignment of my then Special
Representative for the Great Lakes Region, Mr. Berhanu Dinka, as my Special
Representative for Burundi.
It is my honour to inform you that it is my intention to appoint Mr. Ibrahima
Fall, former Assistant Secretary-General for Political Affairs, as my Special
Representative for the Great Lakes Region. He will take up his new responsibilities
in mid-July 2002.
I should be grateful if you would bring this matter to the attention of the
members of the Security Council.
(Signed) Kofi A. Annan
