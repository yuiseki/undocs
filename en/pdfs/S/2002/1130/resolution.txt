Letter dated 9 October 2002 from the President of the Security
Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 4 October 2002
(S/2002/1129), in which you recommend that the current mandate of the United
Nations Peace-building Support Office in Liberia, which expires on 31 December
2002, be extended to 31 December 2003, has been brought to the attention of the
members of the Security Council, who have taken note of the recommendation and
information contained therein.
(Signed) Martin Belinga-Eboutou
President of the Security Council
