Letter dated 15 July 2010 from the President of the
Security Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 9 July 2010
(S/2010/384) concerning your intention to seek to negotiate and conclude with the
Government of Sierra Leone an agreement establishing a residual mechanism for the
Special Court for Sierra Leone, and a statute of the residual mechanism, has been
brought to the attention of the members of the Security Council. The members have
taken note of the intention expressed in the letter and agree to the proposed manner
of proceeding.
(Signed) U. Joy Ogwu
President of the Security Council
