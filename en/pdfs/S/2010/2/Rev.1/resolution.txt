Note by the President of the Security Council
Pursuant to paragraph 4 (b) of the note by the President of the Security
Council dated 30 October 1998 (S/1998/1016), and after consultations among the
members of the Council, it was agreed to elect the Chairmen and Vice-Chairmen of
subsidiary bodies for the period ending 31 December 2010 as follows:
Security Council Committee pursuant to resolutions 751 (1992) and 1907 (2009)
concerning Somalia and Eritrea*
Chairman:
Claude Heller (Mexico)
Vice-Chairmen: Lebanon and Nigeria
Security Council Committee established pursuant to resolution 1132 (1997)
concerning Sierra Leone
Chairman:
Nawaf Salam (Lebanon)
Vice-Chairmen: Bosnia and Herzegovina and Brazil
Security Council Committee established pursuant to resolution 1267 (1999)
concerning Al-Qaida and the Taliban and associated individuals and entities
Chairman:
Thomas Mayr-Harting (Austria)
Vice-Chairmen: Brazil and Russian Federation
Security Council Committee established pursuant to resolution 1373 (2001)
concerning counter-terrorism
Chairman:
Ertuğrul Apakan (Turkey)
Vice-Chairmen: France, Gabon and Russian Federation

* The name of the Committee was amended by the Security Council on 2 March 2010.
