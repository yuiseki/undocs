Letter dated 13 October 2010 from the President of the Security
Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 11 October 2010
(S/2010/523) concerning your intention to appoint Major General Muhammad
Khalid (Pakistan) as Force Commander of the United Nations Mission in Liberia has
been brought to the attention of the members of the Security Council. They take
note of the intention contained in your letter.
(Signed) Ruhakana Rugunda
President of the Security Council
