Letter dated 13 August 2010 from the President of the
Security Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 6 August 2010
(S/2010/433) concerning your intention to appoint Mr. Zachary Muburi-Muita
(Kenya) as the Head of the United Nations Office to the African Union at the level
of Assistant Secretary-General has been brought to the attention of the members of
the Security Council. They take note of the intention contained in your letter.
(Signed) Vitaly Churkin
President of the Security Council
