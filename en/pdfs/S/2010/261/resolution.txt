Letter dated 28 May 2010 from the President of the
Security Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 27 May 2010
(S/2010/260) concerning your intention to appoint Ms. Lisa Buttenheim (United
States of America) as your Special Representative in Cyprus and head of the United
Nations Peacekeeping Force in Cyprus (UNFICYP) has been brought to the
attention of the members of the Security Council. They take note of the intention
contained in your letter.
(Signed) Nawaf Salam
President of the Security Council
