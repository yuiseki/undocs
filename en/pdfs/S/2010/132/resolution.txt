Letter dated 10 March 2010 from the President of the
Security Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 8 March 2010
(S/2010/131) concerning your intention to appoint Major General Luiz Guilherme
Paul Cruz (Brazil) as Force Commander of the United Nations Stabilization Mission
in Haiti has been brought to the attention of the members of the Security Council,
who have taken note thereof.
(Signed) Emmanuel Issoze-Ngondet
President of the Security Council
