Letter dated 3 August 2010 from the President of the
Security Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 29 July 2010
(S/2010/416) concerning the issuance of the report requested by the Security
Council in paragraph 27 of its resolution 1888 (2009), under the agenda item
“Women and peace and security”, has been brought to the attention of the members
of the Council.
The members of the Council have taken note of the request contained in your
letter that the time frame for the issuance of the report be extended, and they look
forward to the submission of the report by 1 December 2010.
(Signed) Vitaly Churkin
President of the Security Council
