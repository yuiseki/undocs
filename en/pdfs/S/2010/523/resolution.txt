Letter dated 11 October 2010 from the Secretary-General
addressed to the President of the Security Council
I have the honour to refer to the United Nations Mission in Liberia (UNMIL),
established by Security Council resolution 1509 (2003) of 19 September 2003,
whose mandate was extended by Security Council resolution 1938 (2010) of
15 September 2010.
Following the usual consultations, I would like to inform you of my intention
to appoint Major General Muhammad Khalid (Pakistan) as Force Commander of
UNMIL. Major General Khalid will replace Lieutenant General Sikander Afzal
(Pakistan), whose tour of duty ends on 28 November 2010. I would like to take this
opportunity to express my gratitude to Lieutenant General Afzal for his contribution
to UNMIL.
(Signed) BAN Ki-moon
