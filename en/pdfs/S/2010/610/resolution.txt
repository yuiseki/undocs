(Signed) Mark Lyall Grant

Annex to the letter dated 30 November 2010 from the President
of the Security Council addressed to the Secretary-General
19 November 2010
On 29 April 2010, the Security Council Working Group on Children and
Armed Conflict established by the Security Council in its resolution 1612 (2005)
examined the report of the Secretary-General on children and armed conflict in
Nepal (S/2010/183). At its 27th meeting, on 9 November 2010, the Working Group
adopted its conclusions on children and armed conflict in Nepal (S/AC.51/2010/4).
In follow-up to the recommendations of the Working Group, which were
approved by the Security Council, and subject to and consistent with applicable
international law and relevant Security Council resolutions, including resolutions
1612 (2005) and 1882 (2009), I am entrusted, in my capacity as Chairman of the
Working Group, to commend the efforts undertaken by the United Nations to
contribute actively to the protection of children in Nepal, including the action plan
that was signed with the Government and the Unified Communist Party of NepalMaoist on 16 December 2009, in accordance with the Nepal peace agreement, the
recommendation in the report of the Secretary-General (S/2008/259) and the
conclusions of the Security Council Working Group on Children and Armed Conflict
(S/AC.51/2008/12).
The Working Group requests you to ensure that the United Nations continues
monitoring the compliance of the Unified Communist Party of Nepal-Maoist with
the action plan and with the requirements under resolutions 1612 (2005) and 1882
(2009), with particular attention to whether discharged minors are forced or
otherwise coerced into association with groups that engage in political violence. The
Working Group also kindly requests you to ensure that a child protection capacity is
preserved in phasing out the activities of the United Nations Mission in Nepal, with
a view to facilitating, inter alia, the discharge of all children in the containment
camps.
The Working Group commends the work of the working group of the Children
Associated with Armed Forces or Armed Groups.
(Signed) Claude Heller
Chairman
Security Council Working Group on Children and Armed Conflict
