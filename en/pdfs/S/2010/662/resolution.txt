Letter dated 15 December 2010 from the Secretary-General to the
President of the Security Council
I have the honour to refer to the United Nations Military Observer Group in
India and Pakistan.
Following the usual consultations, it is my intention to appoint Major General
Raul Gloodtdofsky Fernandez (Uruguay) as Chief Military Observer and Head of
Mission of the United Nations Military Observer Group in India and Pakistan.
Major General Gloodtdofsky will replace Major General Kim Moon Hwa (Republic
of Korea), who completed his assignment on 27 November 2010.
I should be grateful if you would bring the present letter to the attention of the
members of the Security Council.
(Signed) BAN Ki-moon
