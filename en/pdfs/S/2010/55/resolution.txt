Letter dated 28 January 2010 from the Secretary-General
addressed to the President of the Security Council
I have the honour to refer to the command of the United Nations
Disengagement Observer Force (UNDOF).
Following the usual consultations, it is my intention to appoint Major General
Natalio C. Ecarma of the Philippines as the Head of Mission and Force Commander
of UNDOF. Major General Ecarma will replace Major General Wolfgang Jilke.
I should be grateful if you would bring this matter to the attention of the
members of the Security Council.
(Signed) BAN Ki-moon
