Letter dated 13 January 2010 from the President of the Security
Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 13 January 2010
(S/2010/23) concerning your intention to immediately deploy Edmond Mulet,
Assistant Secretary-General, Office of Operations, Department of Peacekeeping
Operations, as your Acting Special Representative and Head of the United Nations
Stabilization Mission in Haiti has been brought to the attention of the members of
the Security Council. They take note of the intention expressed in your letter.
(Signed) Zhang Yesui
President of the Security Council
