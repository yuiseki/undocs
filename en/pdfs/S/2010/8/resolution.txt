Letter dated 6 January 2010 from the President of the
Security Council to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 30 December 2009
(S/2010/7), concerning the granting of an additional three months to the mandate of
the Commission of Inquiry into the facts and circumstances of the assassination of
former Pakistani Prime Minister Mohtarma Benazir Bhutto, has been brought to the
attention of the members of the Security Council. They took note of the information
contained in your letter and the intention expressed therein.
(Signed) Zhang Yesui
President of the Security Council
