Letter dated 6 September 2010 from the Secretary-General
addressed to the President of the Security Council
I have the honour to convey to you the attached letter, dated 6 September
2010, sent to me by the Director General of the International Atomic Energy
Agency, Mr. Yukiya Amano (see annex).
I would be most appreciative if you would be so kind as to bring the present
letter and its annex to the attention of the members of the Security Council.
(Signed) BAN Ki-moon
