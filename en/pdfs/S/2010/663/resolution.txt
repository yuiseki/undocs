Letter dated 21 December 2010 from the President of the
Security Council to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 15 December 2010
(S/2010/662) concerning your intention to appoint Major General Raul
Gloodtdofsky Fernandez (Uruguay) as Chief Military Observer and Head of Mission
of the United Nations Military Observer Group in India and Pakistan has been
brought to the attention of the members of the Security Council, who take note of
the intention contained therein.
(Signed) Susan E. Rice
President of the Security Council
