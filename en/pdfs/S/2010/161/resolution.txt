Letter dated 29 March 2010 from the President of the
Security Council to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 26 March 2010
(S/2010/160), in which you informed me of your intention to appoint Mr. Edmond
Mulet (Guatemala) as your Special Representative and Head of the United Nations
Stabilization Mission in Haiti, has been brought to the attention of the members of
the Security Council, who have taken note of it.
(Signed) Emmanuel Issoze-Ngondet
President of the Security Council
