Letter dated 30 December 2010 from the President of the
Security Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 22 December 2010
(S/2010/677) concerning your intention to appoint Ms. Karin Landgren (Sweden) as
your Special Representative in Burundi and Head of the United Nations Office in
Burundi has been brought to the attention of the members of the Security Council,
who take note of the intention contained therein.
(Signed) Susan E. Rice
President of the Security Council
