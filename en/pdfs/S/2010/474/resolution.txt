Letter dated 14 September 2010 from the Secretary-General
addressed to the President of the Security Council
I have the honour to refer to two separate letters dated 14 September 2010
from His Excellency Mr. Madhav Kumar Nepal, Prime Minister of the Federal
Democratic Republic of Nepal (see annex I, enclosure), and the Chairman of the
Unified Communist Party of Nepal (Maoist), Mr. Pushpa Kamal Dahal “Prachanda”
(see annex II), in which they request the extension of the current mandate of the
United Nations Mission in Nepal by four months from 15 September 2010.
I should be grateful if you would bring the present letter and its annexes to the
attention of the members of the Security Council.
(Signed) BAN Ki-moon

Enclosure
I have the honour to refer to my earlier letter of 6 September 2010 (see
S/2010/472) regarding the mandate of the United Nations Mission in Nepal
(UNMIN) and the current status of the peace process in Nepal. As you are kindly
aware, the Government of Nepal has been actively engaged in taking forward the
peace process to its meaningful conclusion.
I am pleased to inform you that, in pursuance of the continuing efforts of the
Government to reach some common acceptable understanding with the Unified
Communist Party of Nepal (Maoist) on matters directly related to the peace process,
we have been able to reach understanding on some core issues of our peace process.
In this new light, I wish to request the renewal of the current mandate of UNMIN
for four months from 15 September 2010. Furthermore, as per the understanding
with the Unified Communist Party of Nepal (Maoist), this will be the final extension
of the mandate.
The Government of Nepal sincerely hopes that with the honest efforts from all
sides, it will be possible to take the ongoing peace process of Nepal to a fruitful
conclusion within that period. We in Nepal encourage UNMIN to play a positive
role in the process ahead.
I am sure that you will give due consideration to the views of the Government
of Nepal.
(Signed) Madhav Kumar Nepal

Annex II
Letter dated 14 September 2010 from the Chairman of the
Unified Communist Party of Nepal (Maoist) addressed to
the Secretary-General
I have the honour to refer to my earlier letter of 6 September 2010 (see
S/2010/473) regarding the mandate of the United Nations Mission in Nepal
(UNMIN). I wish to request again the renewal of the current mandate of the United
Nations Mission in Nepal (UNMIN) for four months from 15 September 2010
In this regard, I am also pleased to inform you that, in pursuance of our
continuing efforts to reach some common acceptable understanding with the
Government of Nepal on matters directly related to the peace process, we have been
able to reach some understandings.
I would like to reiterate the commitment of the Unified Communist Party of
Nepal (Maoist) to taking forward the peace process to its meaningful conclusion.
Our party sincerely hopes that with the honest efforts from all sides, it will be
possible to take the ongoing peace process of Nepal to a fruitful conclusion within
that period.
I am sure that you will give due consideration to the views of our party.
(Signed) Pushpa Kamal Dahal “Prachanda”
