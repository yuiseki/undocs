Letter dated 18 December 2010 from the Permanent
Representative of the Russian Federation to the United Nations
addressed to the President of the Security Council
In accordance with rule 2 of the provisional rules of procedure of the Security
Council I have the honour to request you to convene an emergency meeting of the
Security Council today, on 18 December 2010, to consider the escalating tensions in
the Korean peninsula.
I should be grateful if the present letter is circulated as a document of the
Security Council.
(Signed) Vitaly Churkin
