Official communiqué of the 4226th meeting of the
Security Council
Held in private in the Security Council Chamber at Headquarters
on Friday, 17 November 2000, at 10.40 a.m.
In accordance with rule 55 of the provisional rules of procedure of the Security
Council, the following communiqué was issued by the Secretary-General in place of
a verbatim record:
“At its 4226th meeting, held in private on 17 November 2000, the
Security Council considered the item entitled ‘Briefing by the SecretaryGeneral’.
“The Security Council heard a briefing by the Secretary-General.
“The members of the Council and the Secretary-General had a
constructive discussion.”
