Official communiqué of the 9395th (closed) meeting of the
Security Council
Held in private at Headquarters, New York, on Tuesday, 15 August
2023, at 3 p.m.
In accordance with rule 55 of the provisional rules of procedure of the Security
Council, the following communiqué was issued through the Secretary-General in
place of a verbatim record:
“On 15 August 2023, the Security Council, pursuant to annex II, sections
A and B of resolution 1353 (2001), held its 9395th meeting in private with the
troop- and police-contributing countries to the United Nations Interim Force in
Lebanon.
“The President invited Mr. Alexandre Zouev, Assistant Secretary-General
for Rule of Law and Security Institutions, Department of Peace Operations, to
participate in the meeting in accordance with rule 39 of the Council’s
provisional rules of procedure.
“The Security Council and the troop- and police-contributing countries
heard a briefing by Mr. Zouev.
“Members of the Council, Mr. Zouev and representatives of participating
contributing countries had an exchange of views.”
