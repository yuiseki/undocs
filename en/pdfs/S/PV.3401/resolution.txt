3401st meeting
8 July 1994
Members of the Council have before them document
S/1994/805, which contains the text of a draft resolution
prepared in the course of the Council’s prior
consultations.

The agenda was adopted.
Establishment of an international tribunal for the
prosecution of persons responsible for serious violations
of international humanitarian law committed in the
territory of the former Yugoslavia
Appointment of the Prosecutor
The President: The Security Council will now begin
its consideration of the item on its agenda.
The Security Council is meeting in accordance with
the understanding reached in its prior consultations.

2

It is my understanding that the Council is ready to
adopt the draft resolution before it without a vote. Unless
I hear any objection, I shall take it that the draft
resolution is adopted.
There being no objection, the draft resolution has
been adopted as resolution 936 (1994).
The Security Council has thus concluded the present
stage of its consideration of the item on the agenda.
The meeting rose at 4.30 p.m.
