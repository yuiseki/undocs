LETTER DATED 16 MARCH 1983 FROM THE PERMANENT REPRESENTATIVE
OF
CHAD l-0 THE UNITED NATIONS ADDRESSED TO THE PRESIDENT OF THE
SECURITY COUNCIL

On instructions
from my Government,
I have the honour to request
you to call
an urgent
meeting
of the Security
Council
in order
to consider
the extremely
serious
situation
prevailing
in Chad as a result
of the occupation
of a part of
Chad territory
by Libya
and of repeated
acts of aggression
by that country
against
the people
of Chad.
Indeed,
since 1973, Libya
has not only been in military
occupation
of that
part of Chad territory
commonly
known as "Bande d'Aouzou",
but has also been openly
intervening
in the internal
affairs
of Chad, in flagrant
violation
of the United
Nations
Charter
and of the relevant
resolutions
of the General
Assembly.
