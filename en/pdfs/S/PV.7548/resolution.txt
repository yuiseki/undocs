Official communiqué of the 7548th (closed) meeting of the
Security Council
Held in private at Headquarters, New York, on Wednesday,
4 November 2015, at 10 a.m.
In accordance with rule 55 of the provisional rules of procedure of the
Security Council, the following communiqué was issued through the Secretary General in place of a verbatim record:
“At its 7548th meeting, held in private on 4 November 2015, the Security
Council considered the item entitled ‘Briefing by the President of the
International Court of Justice’.
“The President of the Security Council invited Judge Ronny Abraham,
President of the International Court of Justice, to participate in the meeting in
accordance with rule 39 of the Council’s provisional rules of procedure.
“The members of the Council heard a briefing by Judge Abraham.
“The members of the Council and Judge Abraham had an exchange of
views”.
