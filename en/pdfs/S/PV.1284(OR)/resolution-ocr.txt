Symbols of United Nations documents are composed of capital letters com~
bined with figures. Mention of such a symbol indicates a reference to a United
Nations document.

Documents of the Security Council (symbol S/...) are normally published in
quarterly Supplements of the Official Records of the Security Council. The date
of the document indicates the supplement in which it appears or in which informa-
tion about it is given.

The resolutions of the Security Council, numbered in accordance with a system
adopted in 1964, are published in yearly volumes of Resolutions and Decisions of
the Security Council. The new system, which has been applied retroactively to
resolutions adopted before 1 January 1965, became fully operative on that date.
