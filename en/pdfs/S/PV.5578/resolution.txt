The meeting was called to order at 10.20 a.m.
Adoption of the agenda
The agenda was adopted.
Consideration of the draft report of the Security
Council to the General Assembly
The President (spoke in Arabic): The Security
Council will now proceed to the consideration of its
annual report to the General Assembly, for the period
from 1 August 2005 to 31 July 2006.
The introduction to the draft report was prepared
by the delegation of France and the factual contents
were prepared by the Secretariat. I should like to
express my appreciation to the delegation of France
and to the Secretariat for the work they have
undertaken in preparing their respective contributions.
I should like to point out that the format of the
draft report now before us corresponds to the
provisions initially set out in the note by the President
of 22 May 2002 (S/2002/199) and now incorporated in
the note by the President of 19 July 2006 (S/2006/507).
I now call on Mr. Tuliameni Kalomoh, Assistant
Secretary-General for Political Affairs, to make an
explanatory statement.
Mr. Kalomoh (Assistant Secretary-General for
Political Affairs): I have the great pleasure to present
the draft annual report of the Security Council for the

2

period 1 August 2005 to 31 July 2006, to be submitted
to the General Assembly pursuant to Article 24,
paragraph 3, and Article 15, paragraph 1, of the Charter
of the United Nations. The factual information
contained in the draft report before the Council was
prepared by the Secretariat in accordance with the
provisions of the note by the President of the Security
Council of 22 May 2002, contained in document
S/2002/199, and now incorporated in the note by the
President of 19 July 2006, contained in document
S/2006/507.
The draft report before the Council provides a
guide to the activities of the Council in a concise and
comprehensive manner. Of particular interest is the
introduction to the report, which was prepared by the
delegation of France. The Secretariat wishes to express
its appreciation for the introduction by the delegation
of France.
The President (spoke in Arabic): May I take it
that the draft report is adopted by the Council?
There being no objection, it is so decided.
This decision will be reflected in a note by the
President of the Security Council to be issued as
document S/2006/942.
The Security Council has thus concluded its
consideration of the item on its agenda.
The meeting rose at 10.25 a.m.
