Official communiqué of the 5527th (closed) meeting
of the Security Council
Held in private at Headquarters, New York, on Monday,
18 September 2006, at 9.35 a.m.
In accordance with rule 55 of the provisional rules of procedure of the Security
Council, the following communiqué was issued through the Secretary-General in
place of a verbatim record:
“On 18 September 2006, the Security Council, pursuant to resolution
1353 (2001), annex II, sections A and B, held its 5527th meeting in private
with the troop-contributing countries to the United Nations Mission in the
Sudan.
“A briefing was made pursuant to rule 39 of the Council’s provisional
rules of procedure by Mr. Jan Pronk, Special Representative of the SecretaryGeneral for the Sudan.”
