The meeting was called to order at 12.10 p.m.
Adoption of the agenda
The agenda was adopted.
International Residual Mechanism for Criminal
Tribunals
The President (spoke in Arabic): The Security
Council will now begin its consideration of the item on
its agenda.
The Council has before it the text of a statement by
the President on behalf of the Council on the subject
of today’s meeting. I thank Council members for their
valuable contributions to this statement.
In accordance with the understanding reached
among the members of the Council, I shall take it
that the members of the Security Council agree to the
statement, which will be issued as a document of the
Security Council under the symbol S/PRST/2022/2.
Before adjourning the meeting, as this is the last
scheduled meeting of the Council for the month of
March, I would like to express the sincere appreciation
of the delegation of the United Arab Emirates to the
members of the Council and to the secretariat of the
Council for all the support they have given to us.
Indeed, it has been a busy and challenging month, in
which we grappled with several important issues on the
Council’s agenda. We have been able to reach consensus

31/03/2022

several times and have delivered achievements that we
can build upon. We must continue our efforts to fulfil
the mandate of the Security Council and to speak in
one voice.
What we achieved this month would not have been
possible without the hard work, support and positive
contributions of every delegation and the representatives
of the Secretariat, as well as that of the interpreters and
administrative and security staff.
As we end our presidency, I know I speak on behalf
of the Council in wishing the delegation of the United
Kingdom good luck in the month of April.
I shall now give the floor to those members of the
Council who wish to make statements.
Mr. Polyanskiy (Russian Federation) (spoke in
Arabic): As this is the last meeting of your presidency,
I would like to thank and congratulate you, Madam
President, at the end of your Council presidency.
It is no exaggeration to say that we had a very
busy month. Nevertheless, your presidency was very
successful. Your credible stewardship of the Council
was very effective, professional and balanced. Those
are the characteristics that distinguish the diplomacy of
the sisterly United Arab Emirates. We also wish every
success in April to our colleagues from the delegation
of the United Kingdom.
The meeting rose at 12.15 p.m.
