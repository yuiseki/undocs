Letter dated 22 October 2009 from the President of the
Security Council to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 19 October 2009
(S/2009/546) concerning your intention to appoint Lieutenant General Sikander
Afzal (Pakistan) as Force Commander of the United Nations Mission in Liberia
(UNMIL) has been brought to the attention of the members of the Security Council.
They take note of the intention contained in your letter.
(Signed) Le Luong Minh
President of the Security Council
