Letter dated 7 May 2009 from the President of the Security Council
addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 5 May 2009
(S/2009/233) concerning your intention to add the Philippines to the list of countries
that have agreed to provide military personnel to the United Nations Military
Observer Group in India and Pakistan (UNMOGIP) has been brought to the
attention of the members of the Security Council. They take note of the intention
expressed in your letter.
(Signed) Vitaly I. Churkin
President of the Security Council
