Letter dated 3 November 2009 from the Secretary-General
addressed to the President of the Security Council
I have the honour to convey the attached letter dated 26 October 2009, from
Hoshyar Zebari, Minister for Foreign Affairs of the Republic of Iraq, regarding the
attacks which took place in Baghdad on 19 August and 25 October 2009 (see
annex).

members of the Security Council, as requested by Foreign Minister Zebari.
(Signed) BAN Ki-moon

Annex
Letter dated 26 October 2009 from the Minister for Foreign
Affairs of Iraq addressed to the Secretary-General
[Original: Arabic]
I refer to the letter of 30 August 2009 addressed to you by the Prime Minister
regarding the terrorist attack of 19 August 2009 on Baghdad, which targeted Iraqi
Government institutions, and to our letter of 25 September 2009, also addressed to
you and with regard to the same subject, in which we conveyed the desire of the
Iraqi Government for a high-level international envoy to be dispatched to assess the
extent of foreign involvement in terrorist acts committed in Iraq and their impact on
its stability and security.
However, before either you or the Security Council could take any practical
measures, Baghdad was once again the target of two simultaneous terrorist attacks
on 25 October 2009. The attacks, which killed and injured a large number of
innocent civilians, targeted Government institutions in the same manner and at the
same time of day as the terrorist attacks of 19 August 2009. This confirms our
previous assertion that the aim of those criminal actions is to paralyse the Iraqi State
and its institutions and to abort the democratic political process under way in Iraq,
thereby gravely undermining its security and stability and having an adverse affect
on regional security and stability. These attacks should be investigated because they
are a threat to international peace and security and because the security situation in
Iraq, which is regarded as the forward battleground in the global war against
terrorism, is of great concern to the international community, as evidenced by the
relevant Security Council resolutions.
We should therefore like to reiterate Iraq’s request for a high-level
international envoy to be appointed to assess the extent of foreign involvement in
terrorist acts committed in Iraq. We further reiterate that organized attacks of such
size and complexity could not have been planned, funded and executed without
significant support from outside parties.
While bringing this matter to your attention, we request that it should also be
brought to the attention of the Security Council as soon as possible, so that
appropriate action to deter terrorism and its sponsors may be taken.
(Signed) Hoshyar Zebari
Minister for Foreign Affairs of Iraq
