Letter dated 30 January 2009 from the President of the
Security Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 27 January 2009
(S/2009/55) concerning your intention to appoint Mr. Joseph Mutaboba (Rwanda) as
your Representative in Guinea-Bissau and Head of the United Nations
Peacebuilding Support Office in Guinea-Bissau (UNOGBIS) has been brought to the
attention of the members of the Security Council. They take note of the intention
expressed in your letter.
(Signed) Jean-Maurice Ripert
