Letter dated 22 May 2009 from the President of the
Security Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 21 May 2009
(S/2009/262), concerning the activities of the High-level Coordinator undertaken to
fulfil the mandate outlined in paragraph 14 of resolution 1284 (1999), has been
brought to the attention of the members of the Security Council.
The members of the Security Council take note of the information contained in
your letter and the proposal made therein.
(Signed) Vitaly I. Churkin
President of the Security Council
