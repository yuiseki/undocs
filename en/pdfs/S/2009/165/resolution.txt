Letter dated 30 March 2009 from the President of the
Security Council to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 26 March 2009
(S/2009/164) concerning your intention to appoint Major General Floriano Peixoto
Vieira Neto (Brazil) as Force Commander of the United Nations Stabilization
Mission in Haiti (MINUSTAH) has been brought to the attention of the members of
the Security Council. They take note of the intention expressed in your letter.
(Signed) Abdurrahman Mohamed Shalgham
President of the Security Council
