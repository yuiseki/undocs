Letter dated 7 December 2009 from the Secretary-General
addressed to the President of the Security Council
I have the honour to refer to the command of the United Nations Interim Force
in Lebanon (UNIFIL).
Following the usual consultations, I intend to appoint Major General Alberto
Asarta Cuevas (Spain) as Head of Mission and UNIFIL Force Commander. Major
General Asarta Cuevas will replace Major General Claudio Graziano, who will
relinquish his post on 28 January 2010.
I should be grateful if you would bring this letter to the attention of the
members of the Security Council.
(Signed) BAN Ki-moon
