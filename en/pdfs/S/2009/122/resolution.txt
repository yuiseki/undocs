Letter dated 3 March 2009 from the President of the
Security Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 27 February 2009
(S/2009/121) concerning your intention to appoint Major General Elhadji
Mouhamedou Kandji (Senegal) as Force Commander of the United Nations Mission
in the Central African Republic and Chad has been brought to the attention of the
members of the Security Council. They take note of the intention expressed in your
letter.
(Signed) Ibrahim O. A. Dabbashi
President of the Security Council
