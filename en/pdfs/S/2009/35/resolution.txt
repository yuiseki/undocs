Letter dated 14 January 2009 from the President of the
Security Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 9 January 2009
(S/2009/34) concerning your intention to appoint Major General Robert Mood of
Norway as the Head of Mission and Chief of Staff of the United Nations Truce
Supervision Organization (UNTSO) has been brought to the attention of the
members of the Security Council. They take note of the intention expressed in your
letter.
(Signed) Jean-Maurice Ripert
President of the Security Council
