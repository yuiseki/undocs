Letter dated 29 May 2009 from the President of the
Security Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 26 May 2009
(S/2009/279) concerning your intention to appoint Sahle-Work Zewde (Ethiopia) as
your new Special Representative in the Central African Republic and head of the
United Nations Peacebuilding Support Office in the Central African Republic,
which is to be succeeded by the United Nations Integrated Peacebuilding Office in
the Central African Republic, has been brought to the attention of the members of
the Security Council. They take note of the intention expressed in your letter.
(Signed) Vitaly I. Churkin
President of the Security Council
