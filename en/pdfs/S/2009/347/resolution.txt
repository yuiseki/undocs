Letter dated 8 July 2009 from the President of the
Security Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 6 July 2009
(S/2009/346) concerning your intention to appoint Ad Melkert (the Netherlands) as
your Special Representative for Iraq and Head of the United Nations Assistance
Mission in Iraq has been brought to the attention of the members of the Security
Council. They take note of the intention expressed in your letter.
(Signed) Ruhakana Rugunda
President of the Security Council
