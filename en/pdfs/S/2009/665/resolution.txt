Letter dated 21 December 2009 from the President of the
Security Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 15 December 2009
(S/2009/664) concerning your intention to let the United Nations Political Office for
Somalia (UNPOS) continue to carry out its tasks during the biennium 2010-2011 has
been brought to the attention of the members of the Security Council. They have
taken note of the intention expressed in the letter and the information contained
therein.
(Signed) Michel Kafando
President of the Security Council
