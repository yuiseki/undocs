Letter dated 8 January 2009 addressed to the Secretary-General
by the President of the Security Council
I have the honour to inform you that your letter of 5 January 2009 (S/2009/17)
concerning your intention to appoint Mr. Michael von der Schulenburg (Germany)
as Executive Representative of the Secretary-General for the United Nations
Integrated Peacebuilding Office in Sierra Leone (UNIPSIL) as well as Resident
Representative of the United Nations Development Programme and United Nations
Resident Coordinator, has been brought to the attention of the members of the
Security Council. They have taken note of the intention expressed in your letter.

(Signed) Jean-Maurice Ripert
President of the Security Council
