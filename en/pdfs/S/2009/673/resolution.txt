Letter dated 24 December 2009 from the President of the
Security Council to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 18 December 2009
(S/2009/672) concerning your intention to extend the appointment of Major General
Fernand Marcel Amoussou as the Force Commander of the United Nations
Operation in Côte d’Ivoire until 31 March 2010 has been brought to the attention of
the members of the Security Council, who have taken due note of your intention.
(Signed) Michel Kafando
President of the Security Council
