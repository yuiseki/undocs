Letter dated 30 January 2009 from the President of the Security
Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 27 January
2009 (S/2009/57) concerning your intention to appoint Karin Landgren (Sweden) to
the post of Representative of the Secretary-General in Nepal and Head of the United
Nations Mission in Nepal (UNMIN) has been brought to the attention of the
members of the Security Council. They have taken note of the intention expressed in
your letter.
(Signed) Jean-Maurice Ripert
President of the Security Council
