Letter dated 27 July 2009 from the President of the
Security Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 22 July 2009
(S/2009/386) concerning the composition of the International Tribunal for the
Prosecution of Persons Responsible for Serious Violations of International
Humanitarian Law Committed in the Territory of the Former Yugoslavia since 1991
has been brought to the attention of the members of the Security Council. Having
consulted those members, I support your intention to appoint Mr. Guy Delvoie,
Mr. Howard Morrison and Sir Burton Hall as permanent judges of the International
Tribunal.
(Signed) Ruhakana Rugunda
President of the Security Council
