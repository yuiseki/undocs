NOTE BY THE PRESIDENT OF THE SECURITY COUNCIL

After consultations among members of the Security Council, the President of
the Council made the following statement, on behalf of the Council, at its
3275th meeting, on 14 September 1993, in connection with the Council's
consideration of the item entitled "The situation in Croatia":

"The Security Council expresses its profound concern at the reports
from the Secretariat of recent military hostilities in Croatia, in
particular the escalation of the means employed, and the grave threat they
pose to the peace process in Geneva and overall stability in the former
Yugoslavia.

"The Council reaffirms its respect for the sovereignty and territorial
integrity of the Republic of Croatia, and calls on both sides to accept
UNPROFOR's proposal of an immediate cease-fire. It calls on the Croatian
Government to withdraw its armed forces to positions occupied before
9 September 1993, on the basis of that proposal, and calls on the Serbian
forces to halt all provocative military actions."
