Security Council

Distr.: General
27 March 2023
Original: English

Belarus, China, Democratic People’s Republic of Korea, Eritrea,
Nicaragua, Russian Federation, Syrian Arab Republic and
Bolivarian Republic of Venezuela: draft resolution
The Security Council,
Condemning in the strongest terms the act of sabotage on the Nord Stream 1 and
2 gas pipelines in September 2022,
Expressing serious concern over the consequences to the environment caused
by the gas leak resulting from the explosions,
Emphasizing that in addition to damage to the natural environment this act of
sabotage created massive danger to the navigation in the Baltic Sea as well as long term economic loss to a number of countries,
Stressing that acts of sabotage on underwater cross-country gas pipelines pose
a threat to international peace and security,
Urging to engage concerned parties into the national investigations conducted
by some Member States,
Emphasizing the importance of conducting an efficient, impartial, transparent,
inclusive and thorough international investigation into all circumstances of the
sabotage on the Nord Stream 1 and 2 gas pipelines,
Stressing that all responsible for organizing, sponsoring and carrying out t his
act of sabotage must be brought to account,
1.
Requests the Secretary-General to establish an international independent
investigation Commission (“the Commission”) to conduct comprehensive,
transparent and impartial international investigation of all aspects of the act of
sabotage on the Nord Stream 1 and 2 gas pipelines, including identification of its
perpetrators, sponsors, organizers and accomplices;
2.
Decides that the Commission shall be composed of impartial and
internationally respected, experienced experts who shall be selected by the SecretaryGeneral and shall be furnished with an adequate number of experienced and impartial
staff;
3.
Requests the Secretary-General to report to the Council within 30 days the
recommendations for the proposed specific modalities for the establishment of the
Commission;
