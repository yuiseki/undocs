Letter dated 9 May 2023 from the Secretary-General addressed to
the President of the Security Council
I have the honour to refer to the United Nations Multidimensional Integrated
Stabilization Mission in the Central African Republic (MINUSCA), which was
established by the Security Council in its resolution 2149 (2014). Its mandate was
most recently extended, until 15 November 2023, by the Council in its resolution
2659 (2022).
Following the usual consultations, I should like to inform you of my in tention
to appoint Major General Humphrey Nyone (Zambia) as Force Commander of
MINUSCA. He will succeed Lieutenant General Daniel Sidiki Traoré (Burkina Faso),
who completed his assignment in January 2023. I should like to take this opportunity
to thank Lieutenant General Traoré for his dedication and effective leadership of
MINUSCA.
I should be grateful if you would bring this matter to the attention of the
members of the Security Council.
(Signed) António Guterres
