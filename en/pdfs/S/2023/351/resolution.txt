Letter dated 12 May 2023 from the President of the
Security Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 9 May 2023 ( S/2023/350),
in which you expressed your intention to appoint Major General Humphrey Nyone
(Zambia) as Force Commander of the United Nations Multidimensional Integrated
Stabilization Mission in the Central African Republic, has been brought to the
attention of the members of the Security Council, who have taken due note of it.
(Signed) Pascale Baeriswyl
President of the Security Council
