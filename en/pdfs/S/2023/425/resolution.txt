Letter dated 12 June 2023 from the President of the
Security Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 7 June 2023 ( S/2023/424)
concerning your intention to appoint Mr. Kaha Imnadze (Georgia) as your Special
Representative and Head of the United Nations Regional Centre for Preventive
Diplomacy for Central Asia has been brought to the attention of the members of the
Security Council. They take note of the intention expressed in your letter.
(Signed) Lana Zaki Nusseibeh
President of the Security Council
