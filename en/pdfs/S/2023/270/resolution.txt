Letter dated 12 April 2023 from the President of the
Security Council addressed to the Secretary-General
The Security Council took note of the letter from the Minister for Foreign
Affairs of Colombia dated 14 February 2023 (S/2023/112, annex) in which he updated
the Council on the progress made by the Government of Colombia in its talks with
various groups and in which he requested the expansion of the mandate of the United
Nations Verification Mission in Colombia to support the effort s, in particular in the
verification of ceasefires.
The members of the Security Council request that you provide for their
consideration, within the next 60 days, detailed recommendations and options,
including implications for the configuration of the M ission, taking into account the
opinions of all relevant Colombian stakeholders and of Council members, for the
possible role that the United Nations could play in this regard.
(Signed) Vassily Nebenzia
President of the Security Council
