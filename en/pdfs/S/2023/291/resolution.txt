Letter dated 25 April 2023 from the President of the
Security Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 19 April 2023
(S/2023/290) concerning your intention to appoint Mr. Leonardo Santos Simão of
Mozambique as your Special Representative for West Africa and the Sahel and Head
of the United Nations Office for West Africa and the Sahel has been brought to the
attention of the members of the Security Council. They take note of the intention
expressed therein.
(Signed) Vassily Nebenzia
President of the Security Council
