Letter dated 2 May 2023 from the President of the Security Council
addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 27 April 2023
(S/2023/313) concerning your intention to appoint Ms. Catriona Laing (United
Kingdom of Great Britain and Northern Ireland) as your Special Representative for
Somalia and Head of the United Nations Assistance Mission in Somalia has been
brought to the attention of the members of the Security Council. They take note of
the intention expressed therein.
(Signed) Pascale Baeriswyl
President of the Security Council
