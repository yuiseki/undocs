Letter dated 15 February 2023 from the Secretary-General
addressed to the President of the Security Council
I have the honour to refer to the United Nations Support Office in Somalia
(UNSOS), which was established by the Security Council in its resolution 2245
(2015). Its mandate was most recently extended in resolution 2670 (2022).
I should like to inform you of my intention to appoint Ms. Aisa Kirabo Kacyira
(Rwanda) as Head of UNSOS. Ms. Kacyira will replace Ms. Lisa Filipetto (Australia).
I should like to take this opportunity to express my gratitude to Ms. Filipetto for her
dedication and effective leadership of UNSOS.
I should be grateful if you would bring this matter to the attention of the
members of the Security Council.
(Signed) António Guterres
