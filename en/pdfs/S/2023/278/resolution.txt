Letter dated 17 April 2023 from the President of the
Security Council addressed to the Chair of the
Peacebuilding Commission
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 11 April 2023
(S/2023/277) concerning the provisional programme of work of the Peacebuilding
Commission for 2023 has been brought to the attention of the members of the Secu rity
Council. They take note of the substantive scope and areas of action contained therein,
including the request to continue to receive from the Secretariat the advance unedited
versions of the reports of the Secretary-General to the Security Council that are
specified in the letter.
(Signed) Vassily Nebenzia
President of the Security Council
