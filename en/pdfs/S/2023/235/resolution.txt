Letter dated 27 March 2023 from the Secretary-General addressed
to the President of the Security Council
I have the honour to refer to resolution 2675 (2023), adopted on 15 February
2023, by which the Security Council decided to extend the mandate of the Panel of
Experts on Yemen until 15 December 2023 and requested me to take the necessary
administrative measures to that effect.
Accordingly, I wish to inform you that, after consulting with the Security
Council Committee established pursuant to resolution 2140 (2014), I have appointed
the following experts:
Ms. Alessia Chiocchetti, international humanitarian law (Italy)
Mr. Debi Prasad Dash, finance (India)
Mr. Raymond Debelle, arms (Belgium)
Ms. Lydelle Joubert, armed groups (South Africa)
I have designated Mr. Dash to serve as the Coordinator of the Panel.
The regional expert will be appointed as soon as possible.
I should be grateful if you would bring this matter to the attention of the
members of the Security Council.
(Signed) António Guterres
