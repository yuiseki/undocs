Letter dated 27 April 2023 from the Secretary-General addressed
to the President of the Security Council
I have the honour to refer to the United Nations Assistance Mission in Somalia
(UNSOM), which was established by the Security Council in its resolution 2102
(2013) of 2 May 2013. Its mandate was most recently extended in resolution 2657
(2022) of 31 October 2022.
I should like to inform you of my intention to appoint Ms. Catriona Laing
(United Kingdom of Great Britain and Northern Ireland) as my Special
Representative for Somalia and Head of UNSOM.
I should like to take this opportunity to express my g ratitude to my former
Special Representative, James Swan (United States of America), for his dedication
and steadfast leadership of UNSOM during his tenure.
I should be grateful if you would bring this matter to the attention of the
members of the Security Council.
(Signed) António Guterres
