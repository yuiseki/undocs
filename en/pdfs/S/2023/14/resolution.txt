Letter dated 29 December 2022 from the Secretary-General
addressed to the President of the Security Council
I have the honour to refer to the United Nations Organization Stabilization
Mission in the Democratic Republic of the Congo (MONUSCO), which was
established by the Security Council in its resolution 1925 (2010). Its mandate was
most recently extended in resolution 2666 (2022).
I should like to inform you of my intention to appoint Lieutenant General Otávio
Rodrigues de Miranda Filho (Brazil) as Force Commander for MONUSCO. He
succeeds Lieutenant General Marcos de Sá Affonso Da Costa (Brazil), who will
complete his assignment on 28 February 2023 and to whom I would like to express
my gratitude for his dedication to MONUSCO.
I should be grateful if you would bring this matter to the attention of the
members of the Security Council.
(Signed) António Guterres
