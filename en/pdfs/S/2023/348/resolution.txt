Letter dated 11 May 2023 from the President of the Security Council
addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 8 May 2023 ( S/2023/347)
concerning your intention to appoint Ms. Lydia N. Mugambe Ssali (Uganda) as a
judge of the International Residual Mechanism for Criminal Tribunals has been
brought to the attention of the members of the Security Council. They take note of
the intention expressed in your letter.
(Signed) Pascale Baeriswyl
President of the Security Council
