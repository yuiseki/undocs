Letter dated 15 March 2023 from the President of the
Security Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 10 March 2023
(S/2023/200), concerning your intention to appoint Major General Md Fakhrul Ahsan
(Bangladesh) as Force Commander of the United Nations Mission for the Referendum
in Western Sahara, has been brought to the attention of the members of the Security
Council. They take note of the intention expressed therein.
(Signed) Pedro Comissário Afonso
President of the Security Council
