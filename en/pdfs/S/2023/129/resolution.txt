Letter dated 21 February 2023 from the President of the Security
Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 15 February 2023
(S/2023/128) concerning your intention to appoint Ms. Aisa Kirabo Kacyira
(Rwanda) as Head of the United Nations Support Office in Somalia has been brought
to the attention of the members of the Security Council. They take note of the
intention expressed therein.
(Signed) Vanessa Frazier
President of the Security Council
