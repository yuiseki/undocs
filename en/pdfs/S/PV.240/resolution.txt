sure that India and Pakistan, having submitted

both belong, will be able to come to an understanding and maintain the friendliest relations

.. peace-Ioving peoples ".

Council should, therefore, heed the cry of a people
in arms which is not represented here and thereby serve the interests of the two dominions of

they so prefer, not to belong to either India or
Pakistan, but to remain an independent State.
