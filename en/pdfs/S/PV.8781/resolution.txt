Consideration of the draft report of the Security Council to the General Assembly

The meeting was called to order at 10.15 a.m.
Adoption of the agenda
The agenda was adopted.
Consideration of the draft report of the
Security Council to the General Assembly
The President (spoke in Chinese): The Security
Council will now begin its consideration of the item on
its agenda.
The Security Council will now proceed to the
consideration of its annual report to the General
Assembly, for the period from 1 January 2020 to
31 December 2020.
The draft report has been provided to the members
of the Council. The introduction to the draft report
was prepared by the delegation of the Niger, with the
participation of the other members of the Council. The
body of the report was prepared by the Secretariat. I
should like to express my appreciation to the Niger and
to the Secretariat for their respective contributions.
I should like to point out that the draft report was
prepared in accordance with the notes by the President
of 30 August 2017 (S/2017/507) and 27 December 2019
(S/2019/997).
I now give the floor to the representative of the
Niger, whose delegation was responsible for drafting
the introduction to this year’s annual report.
Mr. Abarry (Niger) (spoke in French): At the
outset, allow me to thank you, Mr. President, for
organizing this meeting to allow the Security Council
to adopt the annual report on its activities, the drafting
of whose introduction my country, the Niger, had the
honour to coordinate. I would also like to thank all of the
members of the Council and all those who participated
in, and contributed to, our discussions with a view to
finalizing the Council’s report for the year 2020.
I extend special thanks to the Secretariat, in
particular the Security Council Affairs Division, which
throughout the drafting of the report spared no effort
to provide us with the necessary technical advice.
Lastly, I express my sincere thanks to the delegations of

Belgium, the Dominican Republic, Germany, Indonesia
and South Africa — which were members of the Council
last year — for their valuable contributions.
Looking back over the course of 2020, the first
thing that comes to mind is the coronavirus disease
pandemic and how it upended our lives and ways of
working. The Security Council was not spared, insofar
as, in mid-March 2020, under the wise leadership of
Ambassador Zhang Jun, its members had to turn
to virtual meetings in order to make it possible for
the Council to exercise its mandate of safeguarding
international peace and security. The Council’s
proceedings were therefore unique, leading to the
holding of video-teleconferences, with eventually
the holding of some hybrid meetings that included
in-person gatherings.
The draft annual report before the Council, which
provides a summary of the activities and decisions of the
Council for the past year, is the outcome of our collective
efforts. As such, we hope it will meet with the approval
of all members. We also hope that this document, which
we will adopt in the next few moments, will provide
the States Members of the United Nations and other
interested organizations with useful information they
might need in the course of their activities.
In conclusion, I would like to pay tribute to all the
experts on my team, who were able, in the allotted time
frame, to skilfully complete the important task given to
us by the Council.
The President (spoke in Chinese): I thank the
representative of the Niger and his team for their efforts
in preparing the draft report.
It is my understanding that the Council is ready to
proceed to the adoption of the draft annual report.
May I take it that the draft report is adopted by
the Council?
There being no objection, it is so decided.
This decision will be reflected in a note by the
President of the Security Council, to be issued as
document S/2021/500.
The meeting rose at 10.20 a.m.
