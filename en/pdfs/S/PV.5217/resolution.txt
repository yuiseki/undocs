Official communiqué of the 5217th (closed) meeting of the
Security Council
Held in private at Headquarters, New York, on Wednesday,
29 June 2005, at 10.35 a.m.
In accordance with rule 55 of the provisional rules of procedure of the Security
Council, the following communiqué was issued through the Secretary-General in
place of a verbatim record:
“At its 5217th meeting, held in private on 29 June 2005, the Security
Council considered the item entitled ‘Reports of the Secretary-General on the
Sudan’.
“In accordance with the decision taken at the 5216th meeting, the
President extended an invitation under rule 39 of the Council’s provisional
rules of procedure to Mr. Luis Moreno-Ocampo, Prosecutor of the
International Criminal Court.
“The members of the Council and Mr. Luis Moreno-Ocampo, Prosecutor
of the International Criminal Court, had an exchange of views after the
briefing.”
