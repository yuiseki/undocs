Letter dated 12 May 2011 from the Secretary-General addressed to
the President of the Security Council
I have the honour to refer to the United Nations Stabilization Mission in Haiti
(MINUSTAH), established pursuant to resolution 1542 (2004) of 30 April 2004 and
recently extended pursuant to resolution 1944 (2010) of 14 October 2010.
Following the customary consultations, it is my intention to appoint
Mr. Mariano Fernández (Chile) as my Special Representative and Head of
MINUSTAH. He will replace Mr. Edmond Mulet (Guatemala), who will complete
his assignment on 31 May 2011. I would like to take this opportunity to express my
appreciation to Mr. Mulet for his dedication and effective leadership of
MINUSTAH.
I should be grateful if you would bring this letter to the attention of the
members of the Security Council.
(Signed) BAN Ki-moon
