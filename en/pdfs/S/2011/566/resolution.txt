Letter dated 13 September 2011 from the Secretary-General
addressed to the President of the Security Council
By its resolution 1786 (2007) of 28 November 2007, the Security Council
appointed Serge Brammertz as Prosecutor of the International Tribunal for the
Former Yugoslavia. Pursuant to that resolution and the statute of the International
Tribunal, the term of office of Mr. Brammertz will come to an end on 31 December
2011.
According to article 16, paragraph 4, of the statute of the International
Tribunal, the Prosecutor is appointed by the Security Council on nomination by the
Secretary-General and shall serve a four-year term.
In this regard, I hereby nominate Serge Brammertz and request that the
Security Council reappoint him as Prosecutor of the International Tribunal for the
Former Yugoslavia. The appointment should be effective 1 January 2012 for a fouryear term, subject to an earlier termination by the Security Council in view of the
expectation that the International Tribunal will complete its work before the expiry
of the four-year term.
(Signed) BAN Ki-moon
