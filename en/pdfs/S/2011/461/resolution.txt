Letter dated 22 July 2011 from the Secretary-General addressed to
the President of the Security Council
I have the honour to refer to the command of the United Nations Interim
Security Force for Abyei (UNISFA), which the Security Council established under
its resolution 1990 of 27 June 2011.
Following the usual consultations, I intend to appoint Lieutenant General
Tadesse Werede Tesfay (Ethiopia) as Head of Mission and UNISFA Force
Commander.
I should be grateful if you would bring the present letter to the attention of the
members of the Security Council.
(Signed) BAN Ki-moon
