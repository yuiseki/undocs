Letter dated 26 July 2011 from the President of the
Security Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 22 July 2011
(S/2011/459) concerning your intention to appoint Major General Abdul Hafiz
(Bangladesh) as Force Commander of the United Nations Mission for the
Referendum in Western Sahara (MINURSO) has been brought to the attention of the
members of the Security Council. They take note of the intention expressed in your
letter.
(Signed) Peter Wittig
President of the Security Council
