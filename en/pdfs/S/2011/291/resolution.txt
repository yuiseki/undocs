Letter dated 6 May 2011 from the Secretary-General addressed to
the President of the Security Council
I have the honour to refer to the Security Council’s presidential statement of
14 December 2010 (S/PRST/2010/26), in which the Council welcomed the
extension of the mandate of the United Nations Integrated Peacebuilding Office in
the Central African Republic (BINUCA) for a period of one year until 31 December
2011.
In that connection, following the appointment of Ms. Sahle-Work Zewde as
Director-General of the United Nations Office in Nairobi, I would like to inform you
of my intention to appoint Ms. Margaret Vogt of Nigeria as my new Special
Representative for the Central African Republic and Head of BINUCA. Ms. Vogt is
currently a Deputy Director in the Department of Political Affairs.
I would appreciate it if you would bring this letter to the attention of the
members of the Security Council.
(Signed) BAN Ki-moon
