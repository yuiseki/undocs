Letter dated 18 July 2011 from the President of the
Security Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 14 July 2011
(S/2011/431) concerning your proposal to add Thailand to the list of contributors to
the mission of the United Nations Military Observer Group in India and Pakistan
(UNMOGIP) has been brought to the attention of the members of the Security
Council. They take note of the proposal contained in your letter.
(Signed) Peter Wittig
President of the Security Council
