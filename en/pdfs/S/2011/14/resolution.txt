Letter dated 12 January 2011 from the President of the
Security Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 10 January 2011
(S/2011/13) concerning your intention to appoint Major General Chao Liu (China)
as Force Commander of the United Nations Peacekeeping Force in Cyprus has been
brought to the attention of the members of the Security Council. They take note of
the intention expressed in your letter.
(Signed) Ivan Barbalić
President of the Security Council
