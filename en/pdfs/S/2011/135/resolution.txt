Letter dated 14 March 2011 from the President of the
Security Council to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 11 March 2011
(S/2011/134) concerning your intention to appoint Major General Gnakoudè Béréna
(Togo) as Force Commander of the United Nations Operation in Côte d’Ivoire has
been brought to the attention of the members of the Security Council, who take note
of that intention, as expressed therein.
(Signed) Li Baodong
President of the Security Council
