Letter dated 13 May 2011 from the President of the
Security Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 12 May 2011
(S/2011/301) concerning your intention to appoint Mr. Mariano Fernández (Chile)
as your Special Representative for Haiti and Head of the United Nations
Stabilization Mission in Haiti (MINUSTAH) has been brought to the attention of the
members of the Security Council, who have taken note thereof.
(Signed) Gérard Araud
President of the Security Council
