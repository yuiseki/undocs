Letter dated 21 June 2011 from the Secretary-General addressed
to the President of the Security Council
I have the honour to draw your attention to resolution 1973 (2011), by which
the Security Council requested me to create for an initial period of one year, in
consultation with the Security Council Committee established pursuant to resolution
1970 (2011) concerning the Libyan Arab Jamahiriya, a group of up to eight experts,
which would be placed under the direction of the Committee and would carry out
the tasks listed in paragraph 24 of the resolution.
Accordingly, I wish to inform you that, in addition to the seven experts whom
I have already appointed and following consultations with the Committee, I have
appointed the following expert to serve on the group of experts:
Mr. Simon Dilloway, United Kingdom of Great Britain and Northern Ireland
(finance)
The group of experts now has eight members, its maximum authorized
complement.
I should be grateful if you would bring this letter to the attention of the
members of the Security Council.
(Signed) BAN Ki-moon
