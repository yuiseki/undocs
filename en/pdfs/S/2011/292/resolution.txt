Letter dated 10 May 2011 from the President of the
Security Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 6 May 2011 concerning
your intention to appoint Ms. Margaret Vogt (Nigeria) as your Special
Representative for the Central African Republic and Head of the United Nations
Integrated Peacebuilding Office in the Central African Republic (BINUCA), has
been brought to the attention of the members of the Security Council. They take
note of the intention contained in your letter.
(Signed) Gérard Araud
President of the Security Council
