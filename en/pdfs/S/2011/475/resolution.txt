Letter dated 29 July 2011 from the President of the
Security Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 27 July 2011
(S/2011/474) concerning your decision to appoint Mr. Haile Menkerios (South
Africa) as your Special Envoy for the Sudan and South Sudan has been brought to
the attention of the members of the Security Council. They take note of the decision
contained in your letter.
(Signed) Peter Wittig
President of the Security Council
