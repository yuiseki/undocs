Letter dated 19 September 2011 from the President of the
Security Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 16 September 2011
(S/2011/587) concerning your intention to appoint Mr. Ian Martin (United Kingdom
of Great Britain and Northern Ireland) as your Special Representative and Head of
UNSMIL and Mr. Georg Charpentier (Finland) as your Deputy Special
Representative and Resident Coordinator ad interim has been brought to the
attention of the members of the Security Council. They take note of the intention
expressed in your letter.
(Signed) Nawaf Salam
President of the Security Council
