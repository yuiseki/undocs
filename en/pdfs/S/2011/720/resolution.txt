Letter dated 15 November 2011 from the Secretary-General
addressed to the President of the Security Council
I have the honour to refer to Security Council resolution 2002 (2011). In
paragraph 6, the Council requested the Secretary-General to re-establish, for a
period of 12 months, the Monitoring Group on Somalia and Eritrea, consisting of
eight experts, drawing, as appropriate, on the expertise of the members of the
Monitoring Group established pursuant to resolution 1916 (2010), and consistent
with resolution 1907 (2009), in order to fulfil its expanded mandate.
I wish to inform you that, after consulting with the Security Council
Committee pursuant to resolutions 751 (1992) and 1907 (2009) concerning Somalia
and Eritrea, I have appointed Ms. Kristèle Younès (Lebanon) to serve as
humanitarian expert for the Somalia component of the Monitoring Group, replacing
Ms. Samira Bouslama (Tunisia), whom I appointed on 24 August 2011.
I should be grateful if you would bring the present letter to the attention of the
members of the Security Council.
(Signed) BAN Ki-moon
