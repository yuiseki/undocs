Letter dated 25 March 2011 from the President of the
Security Council to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 23 March 2011
(S/2011/187) concerning your intention to appoint Major General Luiz Eduardo
Ramos Pereira (Brazil) as Force Commander of the United Nations Stabilization
Mission in Haiti has been brought to the attention of the members of the Security
Council. They take note of the intention expressed in your letter.
(Signed) Li Baodong
President of the Security Council
