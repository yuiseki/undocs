Letter dated 9 August 2011 from the President of the
Security Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 5 August 2011
(S/2011/510) concerning your intention to request the United Nations Interim
Security Force for Abyei (UNISFA) to undertake a reconnaissance mission along the
border between Sudan and South Sudan as soon as possible has been brought to the
attention of the members of the Security Council. They take note of the intention
expressed in your letter.
(Signed) H. S. Puri
President of the Security Council
