Letter dated 16 September 2011 from the Secretary-General
addressed to the President of the Security Council
I have the honour to refer to the United Nations Support Mission in Libya
(UNSMIL), which was established by Security Council resolution 2009 (2011).
Following the usual consultations, I should like to inform you of my intention
to appoint Mr. Ian Martin (United Kingdom of Great Britain and Northern Ireland)
as my Special Representative and Head of UNSMIL. In addition, I intend to appoint
Mr. Georg Charpentier (Finland) as my Deputy Special Representative and Resident
Coordinator ad interim.
I should be grateful if you would bring this letter to the attention of the
members of the Security Council.
(Signed) BAN Ki-moon
