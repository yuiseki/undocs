Letter dated 11 October 2011 from the President of the
Security Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 7 October 2011
(S/2011/631) concerning your intention to appoint Farid Zarif (Afghanistan) as your
Special Representative for Kosovo and Head of the United Nations Interim
Administration Mission in Kosovo has been brought to the attention of the members
of the Security Council. They take note of the intention expressed in your letter.
(Signed) U. Joy Ogwu
President of the Security Council
