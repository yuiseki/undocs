Letter dated 11 March 2011 from the Secretary-General addressed
to the President of the Security Council
I have the honour to refer to the letter from the President of the Security
Council dated 30 August 2010 (S/2010/457), in which the Council welcomed my
intention to establish a United Nations Regional Office for Central Africa in
Libreville, Gabon.
In that connection, I wish to inform you of my intention to appoint Mr. Abou
Moussa of Chad as my new Special Representative for Central Africa and Head of
the United Nations Regional Office for Central Africa. Mr. Moussa is currently
serving as my Deputy Special Representative in Côte d’Ivoire.
I should be grateful if you would bring the present letter to the attention of the
members of the Security Council.
(Signed) BAN Ki-moon
