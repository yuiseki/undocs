Letter dated 27 June 2011 from the Secretary-General addressed
to the President of the Security Council
I have the honour to refer to Security Council resolution 1985 (2011), by
which the Council decided to extend until 12 June 2012 the mandate of the Panel of
Experts established in paragraph 26 of resolution 1874 (2009), and requested me to
take the necessary administrative measures to that effect.
Consequently, I wish to inform you that, following consultations with the
Security Council Committee established pursuant to resolution 1718 (2006), I have
renewed the mandates of the following experts:
– Mr. John Everard (United Kingdom of Great Britain and Northern Ireland):
regional questions;
– Mr. George A. Lopez (United States of America): finances;
– Mr. Erik Marzolf (France): missile technology;
– Mr. Duk Ho Moon (Republic of Korea): proliferation of weapons of mass
destruction;
– Mr. Alexander Vilnin (Russian Federation): customs;
– Mr. Xiaodong Xue (China): export control, nuclear materials;
– Mr. Takehiko Yamamoto (Japan): nuclear questions.
I have appointed Mr. John Everard Coordinator of the Panel of Experts.
I should be grateful if you would bring this information to the attention of the
members of the Security Council.
(Signed) BAN Ki-moon
