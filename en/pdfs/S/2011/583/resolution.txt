Note by the President of the Security Council
Following consultations among the members of the Security Council, it has
been agreed that the Secretary-General’s report on the implementation of resolutions
1820 (2008), 1888 (2009) and 1960 (2010) requested in paragraph 18 of resolution
1960 (2010) shall be submitted by January 2012.
In addition, the review of the mandates of the Special Representative and the
Team of Experts, requested in paragraph 28 of resolution 1888 (2009), shall be
conducted by January 2012.
