Letter dated 17 May 2011 from the Secretary-General addressed to
the President of the Security Council
Pursuant to article 17, paragraph 1, of the Relationship Agreement between the
United Nations and the International Criminal Court, I have the honour to transmit
herewith the attached communication dated 12 May 2011 from the Registrar of the
Court (see annex). It is a notification of the “Decision informing the United Nations
Security Council and the Assembly of the States Parties to the Rome Statute about
Omar Al-Bashir’s recent visit to Djibouti”, issued by Pre-Trial Chamber I of the
International Criminal Court on 12 May 2011 in the case The Prosecutor v. Omar
Hassan Ahmad Al Bashir. The Pre-Trial Chamber directed the Registrar to notify the
Security Council of its decision in this case.
I should be grateful if you would bring this letter and its enclosures to the
attention of the members of the Security Council.
(Signed) BAN Ki-moon
