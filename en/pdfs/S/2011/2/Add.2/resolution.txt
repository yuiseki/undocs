Note by the President of the Security Council
Pursuant to paragraph 4 (b) of the note by the President of the Security
Council dated 30 October 1998 (S/1998/1016), and after consultations among the
members of the Council, it was agreed to elect the Chairmen and Vice-Chairmen of
subsidiary bodies for the period ending 31 December 2011 as follows:
Security Council Committee pursuant to resolutions 1267 (1999) and 1989 (2011)
concerning Al-Qaida and associated individuals and entities 1
Chairman:
Peter Wittig (Germany)
Vice-Chairmen: Brazil and Russian Federation
Security Council Committee established pursuant to resolution 1988 (2011) 2
Chairman:
Peter Wittig (Germany)
Vice-Chairmen: Brazil and Russian Federation

__________________
1 The name of the Committee was amended by the Security Council on 30 June 2011.
2 Resolution 1988 (2011) was adopted on 17 June 2011.
