Letter dated 21 March 2011 from the Secretary-General addressed
to the President of the Security Council
This is to inform you that Mr. Young-wan Song of the Republic of Korea,
whom I appointed on 8 July 2010 to serve on the Panel of Experts established
pursuant to Security Council resolution 1874 (2009), has withdrawn from the Panel
of Experts. Accordingly, I wish to inform you that, in consultation with the Security
Council Committee established pursuant to resolution 1718 (2006), I have appointed
Mr. John Everard of the United Kingdom of Great Britain and Northern Ireland to
serve on the Panel of Experts and carry out tasks as specified in paragraph 26 of the
resolution.
I should be grateful if you would bring this information to the attention of the
members of the Security Council.
(Signed) BAN Ki-moon
