Letter dated 14 April 2011 from the President of the Security Council
addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 12 April 2011
(S/2011/247) concerning a temporary reconfiguration of the police component of the
United Nations Operation in Côte d’Ivoire has been brought to the attention of the
members of the Council. They take note of the recommendation contained in your
letter and approve the course of action outlined therein.
(Signed) Néstor Osorio
President of the Security Council
