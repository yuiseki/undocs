Letter dated 28 January 2011 from the President of the
Security Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 25 January 2011
(S/2011/38) concerning your intention to appoint a selection panel, in accordance
with article 2, paragraph 5 (d), of the annex to resolution 1757 (2007), has been
brought to the attention of the members of the Security Council. They take note of
the intention and information contained in your letter.
(Signed) Ivan Barbalić
President of the Security Council
