Official communiqué of the 5310th (closed) meeting of the
Security Council
Held in private at Headquarters, New York, on Monday,
28 November 2005, at 11.10 a.m.
In accordance with rule 55 of the provisional rules of procedure of the Security
Council, the following communiqué was issued through the Secretary-General in
place of a verbatim record:
On 28 November 2005, the Security Council, pursuant to annex II, sections A
and B of resolution 1353 (2001), held its 5310th meeting in private with the troopcontributing countries to the United Nations Operation in Burundi (ONUB).
The Security Council and the troop-contributing countries heard briefings
under rule 39 of its provisional rules of procedure by Ms. Carolyn McAskie, Special
Representative of the Secretary-General for Burundi, and Major-General Derrick
Mbuyiselo Mgwebi, Force Commander of the United Nations Operation in Burundi.
Members of the Council, Ms. McAskie, Major-General Mgwebi and
representatives of participating troop-contributing countries had a constructive
exchange of views.
