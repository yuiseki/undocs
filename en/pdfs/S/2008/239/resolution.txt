Letter dated 11 April 2008 from the President of the
Security Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 8 April 2008
(S/2008/238), concerning the appointment of Gennady P. Tarasov of the Russian
Federation as your High-level Coordinator pursuant to paragraph 14 of resolution
1284 (1999), has been brought to the attention of the members of the Security
Council. They take note of the appointment indicated in your letter.
(Signed) Dumisani Shadrack Kumalo
President of the Security Council
