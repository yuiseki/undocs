Letter dated 7 March 2008 from the President of the
Security Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 6 March 2008
(S/2008/165) concerning your intention to appoint Kai Eide (Norway) as your
Special Representative for Afghanistan and Head of the United Nations Assistance
Mission in Afghanistan has been brought to the attention of the members of the
Security Council. They take note of the intention expressed in your letter.
(Signed) Vitaly I. Churkin
President of the Security Council
