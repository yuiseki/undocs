Letter dated 1 August 2008 from the President of the
Security Council to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 30 July 2008
(S/2008/518) concerning your intention to appoint Johan Verbeke (Belgium) as your
Special Representative for Georgia and Head of the United Nations Observer
Mission in Georgia has been brought to the attention of the members of the Security
Council. They take note of the intention expressed in your letter.
(Signed) Jan Grauls
President of the Security Council
