Letter dated 31 October 2008 from the President of the
Security Council to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 31 October 2008
(S/2008/681) concerning your intention to reappoint Lieutenant General Babacar
Gaye (Senegal) as Interim Force Commander of the United Nations Organization
Mission in the Democratic Republic of the Congo (MONUC) has been brought to
the attention of the members of the Security Council. They take note of the intention
expressed in your letter.
(Signed) Zhang Yesui
President of the Security Council
