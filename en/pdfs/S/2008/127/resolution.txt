Letter dated 21 February 2008 from the Secretary-General
addressed to the President of the Security Council
I have the honour to refer to the letter from the President of the Security
Council dated 21 December 2007 (S/2007/754) in which the Council approved the
extension of the mandate of the United Nations Office for West Africa (UNOWA)
for three years, until 31 December 2010, and recognized the critical role played by
that office and by my Special Representative in promoting an integrated approach
towards peace and progress in that subregion.
In that connection, I wish to inform you of my intention to appoint Said
Djinnit of Algeria as my new Special Representative for West Africa and head of
UNOWA. Mr. Djinnit, who is currently serving as Commissioner for Peace and
Security of the African Union Commission, will take up his functions on 1 April
2008. He will replace General Lamine Cissé, who has been serving as Officer-inCharge of UNOWA since September 2007 when Ahmedou Ould-Abdallah was
reassigned as my Special Representative for Somalia.
I would like to take this opportunity to express my appreciation to
Messrs. Ould-Abdallah and Cissé for their dedicated contributions to United Nations
efforts for peace and progress in West Africa.
I should be grateful if you would bring the present letter to the attention of the
members of the Security Council.
(Signed) Ban Ki-moon
