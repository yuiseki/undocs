Letter dated 30 July 2008 from the Secretary-General to the
President of the Security Council
I have the honour to refer to the United Nations Observer Mission in Georgia
(UNOMIG).
Following the usual consultations, I would like to inform the Security Council
of my intention to appoint Johan Verbeke of Belgium as my Special Representative
for Georgia and Head of UNOMIG. Mr. Verbeke will replace Jean Arnault.
I should be grateful if you would bring this letter to the attention of the
members of the Security Council.
(Signed) Ban Ki-moon
