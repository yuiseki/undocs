Letter dated 29 December 2008 from the President of the
Security Council to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 23 December 2008
(S/2008/826), concerning your recommendation that the mandate of the temporary
Liaison Office of your Special Envoy for the Lord’s Resistance Army (LRA)affected areas, which expires on 31 December 2008, be upgraded to a special
political mission for the Special Envoy for the LRA-affected areas in Uganda and be
extended through 31 December 2009, has been brought to the attention of the
members of the Security Council. They take note of the information and
recommendation contained in your letter.
(Signed) Neven Jurica
President of the Security Council
