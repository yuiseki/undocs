Letter dated 30 April 2008 from the President of the Security
Council to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 28 April 2008
(S/2008/285) concerning your intention to appoint Miroslav Jenča (Slovakia) as
your Special Representative and Head of the United Nations Regional Centre for
Preventive Diplomacy for Central Asia in Ashgabat has been brought to the
attention of the members of the Security Council. They take note of the intention
expressed in your letter.
(Signed) Dumisani Shadrack Kumalo
President of the Security Council
