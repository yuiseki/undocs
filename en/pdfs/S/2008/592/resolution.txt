Letter dated 3 September 2008 from the President of the
Security Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 2 September 2008
(S/2008/591), concerning your intention to appoint General Vicente Diaz de Villegas
(Spain) as Force Commander of the United Nations Organization Mission in the
Democratic Republic of the Congo, has been brought to the attention of the
members of the Security Council. They take note of the intention expressed in your
letter.
(Signed) Michel Kafando
