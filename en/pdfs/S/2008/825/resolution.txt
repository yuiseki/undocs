Letter dated 29 December 2008 from the President of the
Security Council to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 18 December 2008
(S/2008/824) concerning your decision, in accordance with article 19 (2) of the
annex to Security Council resolution 1757 (2007), to commence the Special
Tribunal for Lebanon on 1 March 2009 has been brought to the attention of the
members of the Security Council. They take note of the decision and information
conveyed in your letter.
(Signed) Neven Jurica
President of the Security Council
