Letter dated 20 October 2008 from the President of the
Security Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 16 October 2008
(S/2008/663) concerning your intention to appoint Major General Kim Moon Hwa
(Republic of Korea) as Chief Military Observer of the United Nations Military
Observer Group in India and Pakistan has been brought to the attention of the
members of the Security Council. They take note of the intention expressed in your
letter.
(Signed) Zhang Yesui
President of the Security Council
