Letter dated 14 April 2008 from the President of the
Security Council to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 9 April 2008
(S/2008/244) concerning your intention to appoint Tayé-Brook Zerihoun (Ethiopia)
as your Special Representative in Cyprus and head of the United Nations
Peacekeeping Force in Cyprus has been brought to the attention of the members of
the Security Council. They take note of the intention expressed in your letter.
(Signed) Dumisani Shadrack Kumalo
President of the Security Council
