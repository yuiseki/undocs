Letter dated 26 February 2008 from the President of the Security
Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 21 February 2008
(S/2008/127) concerning your intention to appoint Said Djinnit of Algeria as your
Special Representative for West Africa and Head of the United Nations Office for
West Africa has been brought to the attention of the members of the Security
Council. They take note of the intention expressed in your letter.
(Signed) Ricardo Alberto Arias
President of the Security Council
