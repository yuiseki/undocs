Letter dated 23 December 2008 from the President of the
Security Council to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that the recommendation contained in
paragraph 50 of your report dated 26 November 2008 (S/2008/733) to extend the
mandate of the United Nations Peacebuilding Support Office in the Central African
Republic for an additional year, from 1 January to 31 December 2009, has been
brought to the attention of the members of the Security Council. They take note of
that recommendation.
Council members also acknowledge the need to establish an integrated United
Nations office in the Central African Republic, as emphasized in the conclusions
made by a delegation of the Peacebuilding Commission following its recent visit to
the country. They look forward to your recommendations in this regard and would
appreciate receiving them by 28 February 2009.
(Signed) Neven Jurica
President of the Security Council
