Letter dated 1 August 2008 from the President of the
Security Council to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 30 July 2008
(S/2008/516) concerning your intention to appoint Michael C. Williams (United
Kingdom of Great Britain and Northern Ireland) as the United Nations Special
Coordinator for Lebanon has been brought to the attention of the members of the
Security Council. They take note of the information and intention contained in your
letter.
(Signed) Jan Grauls
President of the Security Council
