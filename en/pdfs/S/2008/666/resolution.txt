Letter dated 20 October 2008 from the President of the
Security Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 16 October 2008
(S/2008/665) concerning your intention to appoint Lieutenant General A. T. M.
Zahirul Alam (Bangladesh) as Force Commander of the United Nations Mission in
Liberia has been brought to the attention of the members of the Security Council.
They take note of the intention expressed in your letter.
(Signed) Zhang Yesui
President of the Security Council
