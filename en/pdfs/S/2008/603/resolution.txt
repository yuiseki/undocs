Annex
On behalf of the Government of the Islamic Republic of Afghanistan, I have
the honour to express my appreciation for the role played by the International
Security Assistance Force (ISAF), authorized under Security Council resolution
1386 (2001) of 20 December 2001 and subsequent resolutions, for the wider
contribution that the United Nations is making towards security and stability in
Afghanistan.
The International Security Assistance Force, led by NATO since August 2003,
has been crucial in improving security initially in Kabul. Its programme of phased
expansion in the north, west, south and east of the country, which is very important,
has played a key role in this regard.
The Islamic Republic of Afghanistan welcomes the proposal that ISAF should
continue to operate in Afghanistan until the national security forces are fully able to
provide security to our nation. The Government of Afghanistan is grateful to all
those nations contributing resources to ISAF and to those nations that have assumed
a leadership role.
I hope the Security Council will continue to reflect in its resolutions the
paramount importance of fulfilling the ISAF mandate.
(Signed) Rangin Dadfar Spanta
Minister for Foreign Affairs
