EXPRESSION OF THANKS TO THE RETIRING PRESIDENT .

The PRESIDENT: As this is the first meeting of the Security Council
for the month of July, I should like to take this opportunity to pay a
tribute, on behalf of the Council, to His Excellency Mr. Paul Noterdaeme,
Permanent Representative of Belgium to the United Nations, for his service as
President of the Security Council for the month of June 1992. I am sure that
I speak for all members of the Security Council in expressing deep
appreciation to Ambassador Noterdaeme for his untiring efforts and dedication
and for the great diplomatic skill and unfailing courtesy with which he
conducted the Council's business last month. '

ADMISSION OF NEW MEMBERS

LETTER DATED 6 MAY 1992 FROM THE PRESIDENT OF THE STATE COUNCIL OF THE
REPUBLIC OF GEORGIA ADDRESSED TO THE SECRETARY-GENERAL (S/24116)

The PRESIDENT: The Security Council will now begin its
consideration of the item on its agenda. Ina letter dated 6 May 1992
addressed to the Secretary-General, the President of the State Council of the
Republic of Georgia submitted the application of his country for membership in
the United Nations. That letter has been circulated in document S/24116.

Under the provisions of rule 59 of the provisional rules of procedure of
the Security Council, unless the Council decides otherwise, an application for

membership shall be referred by the President of the Council to the Committee
