Official communiqué of the 8800th (closed) meeting of the
Security Council
Held in private at Headquarters, New York, on Friday,
18 June 2021, at 10.30 a.m.
In accordance with rule 55 of the provisional rules of procedure of the Security
Council, the following communiqué was issued through the Secretary-General in
place of a verbatim record:
“At its 8800th meeting, held in private on 18 June 2021, the Securit y
Council considered the item entitled “The situation in Myanmar”.
“The President invited the following briefers to participate in the meeting
in accordance with rule 39 of the Council’s provisional rules of procedure:
Ms. Christine Schraner Burgener, Special Envoy of the Secretary-General for
Myanmar; and His Excellency Dato Erywan Pehin Yusof, Foreign Minister II of
Brunei Darussalam, in his capacity as Chair of the Association of Southeast
Asian Nations.
“The Council heard briefings by Ms. Schraner Burgener and His
Excellency Dato Pehin Yusof.
“Statements were made by the representatives of the United Kingdom,
Ireland, the United States, France, Kenya, Mexico, Norway, the Russian
Federation, India, Niger, Viet Nam, Tunisia, Saint Vincent and the Grena dines,
China, and by the President, speaking in his capacity as the representative of
Estonia.
“Ms. Schraner Burgener and His Excellency Dato Pehin Yusof responded
to comments and questions raised.”
