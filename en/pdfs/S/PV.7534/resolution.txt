The question concerning Haiti
Report of the Secretary-General on the
United Nations Stabilization Mission in
Haiti (S/2015/667)
The President (spoke in Spanish): In accordance
with rule 37 of the Council’s provisional rules of
procedure, I invite the representatives of Argentina,
Brazil, Canada, Colombia, Guatemala, Peru and
Uruguay to participate in this meeting.
The Security Council will now begin its consideration
of the item on its agenda.
Members of the Council have before them document
S/2015/775, which contains the text of a draft resolution
submitted by Angola, Argentina, Brazil, Canada,
Chile Colombia, France, Guatemala, Jordan, Malaysia,
New Zealand, Peru, Spain, the United Kingdom of

Great Britain and Northern Ireland, the United States
of America, Uruguay and the Bolivarian Republic of
Venezuela.
Members of the Council also have before them
document S/2015/667, which contains the report of the
Secretary-General on the United Nations Stabilization
Mission in Haiti.
It is my understanding that the Council is ready to
proceed to the vote on the draft resolution before it. I
shall put the draft resolution to the vote now.
A vote was taken by show of hands.
In favour:
Angola, Chad, Chile, China, France, Jordan,
Lithuania, Malaysia, New Zealand, Nigeria,
Russian Federation, Spain, United Kingdom of
Great Britain and Northern Ireland, United States
of America and Venezuela (Bolivarian Republic of)
The President (spoke in Spanish): The draft
resolution received 15 votes in favour. The draft
resolution has been adopted as resolution 2243 (2015).
The meeting rose at 10.15 a.m.
