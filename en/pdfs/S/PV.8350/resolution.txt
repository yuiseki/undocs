The situation in Libya
Report of the Secretary-General on the
United Nations Support Mission in Libya
(S/2018/780)
The President: In accordance with rule 37 of the
Council’s provisional rules of procedure, I invite the
representative of Libya to participate in this meeting.
The Security Council will now begin its consideration
of the item on its agenda.
Members of the Council have before them document
S/2018/836, which contains the text of a draft resolution
submitted by the United Kingdom of Great Britain and
Northern Ireland.

2/2

13/09/2018

I wish to draw the attention of Council members
to document S/2018/780, which contains the report of
the Secretary-General on the United Nations Support
Mission in Libya .
The Council is ready to proceed to the vote on the
draft resolution before it. I shall put the draft resolution
to the vote now.
A vote was taken by show of hands.
In favour:
Bolivia (Plurinational State of), China, Côte
d’Ivoire, Equatorial Guinea, Ethiopia, France,
Kazakhstan, Kuwait, Netherlands, Peru, Poland,
Russian Federation, Sweden, United Kingdom of
Great Britain and Northern Ireland, United States
of America
The President: The draft resolution received 15
votes in favour. The draft resolution has been adopted
unanimously as resolution 2434 (2018).
The meeting rose at 10.05 a.m.
