Letter dated 27 August 2020 from the Secretary-General
addressed to the President of the Security Council
I have the honour to refer to Security Council resolution 2524 (2020) of 3 June
2020, in which the Council requested that I report every 90 days on the
implementation of the mandate of the United Nations Integrated Transition Assistance
Mission in the Sudan (UNITAMS) and include in the first report clear and measurable
core and contextual benchmarks and indicators to track progress made by the Mission
against its strategic objectives and enable early planning for a future reconfiguration
of the United Nations presence in the Sudan.
The planning process for the establishment of UNITAMS has been ongoing,
notably through a visit to the Sudan by the planning team that took place from 26 July
to 11 August 2020. The team experienced positive engagement with the Government
and other stakeholders, which facilitated substantive progr ess in the planning process.
Nonetheless, the challenges linked to the coronavirus disease (COVID -19) pandemic
and the attendant delays, which I had referred to in my previous letter on this subject
(S/2020/749), have been compounded by the absence of a Head of Mission for
UNITAMS.
I therefore seek an extension of two weeks for the submission of the first
quarterly report. The report will be focused on the situation in the Sudan and the
planning process for the establishment of UNITAMS. The submission of the
suggested structure and geographical deployment of UNITAMS, as well as the
quarterly report on the African Union-United Nations Hybrid Operation in Darfur,
will be annexed to that report. My recommendations on core and contextual
benchmarks and indicators to track progress of UNITAMS against its strategic
objectives will be contained in the subsequent quarterly report.
I should be grateful if you would bring the present letter to the attention of the
members of the Security Council.
(Signed) António Guterres
