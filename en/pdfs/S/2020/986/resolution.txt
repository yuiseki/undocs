Letter dated 6 October 2020 from the Secretary-General
addressed to the President of the Security Council
I have the honour to refer to paragraph 3 of Security Council resolution 2541
(2020), adopted on 31 August 2020, by which the Council decided to extend the
mandate of the Panel of Experts on Mali until 30 September 2021 and requested that
I take the necessary administrative measures to that effect.
Accordingly, I wish to inform you that, after consulting with the Security
Council Committee established pursuant to resolution 2374 (2017) concerning Mali,
I have appointed the following experts:
Mr. Albert Kwokwo Barume, regional (Democratic Republic of the Congo)
Ms. Sanaa Elhakim, humanitarian affairs (Egypt)
Mr. Ruben de Koning, finance (Netherlands)
Mr. Aurélien Llorca, armed groups (France)
I have designated Mr. Barume to serve as the Coordinator of the Panel.
I should be grateful if you would bring this matter to the attention of the
members of the Security Council.
(Signed) António Guterres
