Letter dated 23 December 2020 from the President of the
Security Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that the Security Council has approved the
proposal in your letter dated 22 December 2020 (S/2020/1290) concerning the
temporary redeployment, for a two-month period, of two infantry companies and two
military utility helicopters from the United Nations Mission in South Sudan
(UNMISS) to assist the United Nations Multidimensional Integrated Stabilization
Mission in the Central African Republic (MINUSCA) in reinforcing security in
critical areas, while maintaining the security of Bangui. Any forces temporarily
redeployed shall continue to be counted against the authorized ceiling on military and
civilian personnel of UNMISS and shall not count against the ceiling of MINUSCA.
The Council views the adequate resourcing and staffin g of UNMISS as vital to the
implementation of the peace accords for South Sudan. The members of the Council
wish to highlight that this is an approval for a temporary request brought about by
emergency circumstances.
(Signed) Jerry Matthews Matjila
President of the Security Council
