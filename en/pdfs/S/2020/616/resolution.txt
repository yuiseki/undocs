Letter dated 26 June 2020 from the Secretary-General addressed
to the President of the Security Council
I have the honour to refer to your letter dated 25 June 2020 ( S/2020/581) taking
note of my proposal to reappoint the 25 judges of the International Residual
Mechanism for Criminal Tribunals.
I have received a corresponding letter from the President of the General
Assembly (see annex I). Accordingly, I am proceeding with the reappointment of the
25 judges of the Mechanism. A list of the judges is set out in annex II to the present
letter.
In accordance with paragraph 2 of Security Council resolution 2269 (2016) of
29 February 2016, the judges will be reappointed for a two -year term commencing on
1 July 2020 and expiring on 30 June 2022.
I will also proceed to reappoint Judge Carmel Agius (Malta) as President of the
Mechanism for a new term of office from 1 July 2020 to 30 June 2022.
I should be grateful if you would bring the present letter to the attention of the
members of the Security Council.
(Signed) António Guterres

I have the honour to refer to your letter dated 19 June 2020 ( S/2020/580)
concerning the reappointment of 25 judges of the International Residual Mechani sm
for Criminal Tribunals.
Pursuant to article 10, paragraph 3, of the statute of the Mechanism, I am pleased
to inform you that I hereby concur with the proposed reappointment of the 25 judges
listed in the annex to your letter, for a term of two years, from 1 July 2020 until
30 June 2022, which would be in line with the upcoming period of operation of the
Mechanism.
