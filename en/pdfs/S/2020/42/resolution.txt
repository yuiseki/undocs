Letter dated 13 January 2020 from the Secretary-General
addressed to the President of the Security Council
I have the honour to refer to paragraph 29 of Security Council resolution 2498
(2019), by which the Council renewed the mandate of the Panel of Experts on Somalia
until 15 December 2020.
In my letter dated 16 December 2019 (S/2019/977), I appointed five members
of the Panel and mentioned that a sixth expert (humanitarian affair s) would be
appointed as soon as possible.
Accordingly, I wish to inform you that, after consulting the Security Council
Committee pursuant to resolution 751 (1992) concerning Somalia, I have appointed
Ms. Irene Raciti (Italy).
I should be grateful if you would bring the present letter to the attention of the
members of the Security Council.
(Signed) António Guterres
