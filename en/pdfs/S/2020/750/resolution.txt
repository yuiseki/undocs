Letter dated 28 July 2020 from the President of the
Security Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 23 July 2020 (S/2020/749)
with reference to Security Council resolution 2524 (2020), in which the Council
requested you to provide a suggested structure and geographical deployment for the
United Nations Integrated Transition Assistance Mission in the Sudan (UNITAMS),
within 60 days of the adoption of the resolution, has been brought to the attention of
the members of the Security Council.
The members of the Council have taken note of the request contained in your
letter that the time frame for the submission of the suggested structure and
geographical deployment for UNITAMS be extended for one month.
(Signed) Christoph Heusgen
President of the Security Council
