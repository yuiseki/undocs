Letter dated 2 September 2020 from the President of the
Security Council addressed to the Permanent Representatives of
the members of the Security Council
The Niger assumes the presidency of the Security Council on 1 September 2020,
in a context still marked by the constraints linked to the persistence of the coronavirus
disease (COVID-19) pandemic, in particular the restrictions imposed by the
authorities of the State and the City of New York.
Following the evolution of the pandemic and taking into account the advice of
the health service of the United Nations Secretariat, the Nigerien presidency would
like to encourage the resumption of in-person meetings in the Economic and Social
Council Chamber while respecting the necessary measures that are required under
current circumstances, in particular the health measures of 7 August 2020 and
subsequent ones.
In this regard, virtual meetings will continue to be the rule and in-person
meetings the exception, in strict compliance with appropriate precautionary and
distancing measures.
The members of the Security Council agree that the decision to convene
meetings of the Council on United Nations Headquarters premises rests with the
Council.
The members of the Council will continue to discuss and decide by consensus
on the possibility of convening meetings at United Nations Headquarters in September
2020, taking into account the regulations and measures taken by the host country and
the United Nations Secretariat and the general situation in New York City.
Our presidency also intends to encourage general compliance with certain good
practices and with document S/2017/507 of 30 August 2017 and the eight notes by the
President of the Security Council of 27 December 2019 (S/2019/990 to S/2019/997).
Therefore, after consultation with the United Nations Secretariat and all
members of the Council, the Nigerien presidency will continue the working methods
of the former presidencies, as outlined in the letter dated 7 May 2020 from the
President of the Council (S/2020/372), as well as the modalities of the Council
Chamber meetings enshrined in the letter dated 1 July 2020 from the President of the
Council (S/2020/639), as well as the letter dated 4 August 2020 from the President of
the Council (S/2020/778) and its additional note.
During its presidency, the Niger intends to conduct the business of the Council
and deliver its national statements in its official language. The English versions of
such statements will be made available to Council members in advance. In this regard,
