Letter dated 10 June 2020 from the Secretary-General addressed
to the President of the Security Council
I have the honour to refer to the United Nations Interim Security Force for Abyei
(UNISFA), established by the Security Council in its resolution 1990 (2011) of
27 June 2011 and recently extended under resolution 2519 (2020) of 14 May 2020.
I should like to inform you of my intention to appoint Major General Kefyalew
Amde Tessema (Ethiopia) as Force Commander of UNISFA. He succeeds Mehari
Zewde Gebremariam (Ethiopia), who will complete his assignment on 7 July 2020. I
should like to take this opportunity to express my gratitude to Major General
Gebremariam for his dedication and effective leadership of UNISFA.
I should be grateful if you would bring the present letter to the attention of the
members of the Security Council.
(Signed) António Guterres
