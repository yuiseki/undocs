Letter dated 11 December 2020 from the Secretary-General
addressed to the President of the Security Council
I have the honour to refer to Security Council resolution 2551 (2020), adopted
on 12 November 2020, by which the Council decided to extend the manda te of the
Panel of Experts on Somalia until 15 December 2021.
Accordingly, I wish to inform you that, after consulting the Security Council
Committee pursuant to resolution 751 (1992) concerning Somalia, I have appointed
the following experts:
Mr. John Peter Hazenberg, armed groups/natural resources (United States of
America)
Mr. Ahmed Himmiche, armed groups (Morocco)
Ms. Natascha Hryckow, maritime/regional (Australia)
Ms. Irene Raciti, humanitarian (Italy)
Mr. Matthew Rosbottom, finance (United Kingdom of Great Britain and
Northern Ireland)
Mr. Richard Zabot, arms (France)
I have designated Ms. Hryckow to serve as Coordinator of the Panel.
I would be grateful if you could bring this matter to the attention of the members
of the Security Council.
(Signed) António Guterres
