Letter dated 12 June 2020 from the President of the
Security Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 10 June 2020 ( S/2020/539)
concerning your intention to appoint Major General Kefyalew Amde Tessema
(Ethiopia) as Force Commander of the United Nations Interim Security Force for
Abyei has been brought to the attention of the members of the Security Council. They
take note of the intention expressed therein.
(Signed) Nicolas de Rivière
President of the Security Council
