Letter dated 2 November 2020 from the President of the
Security Council addressed to the Permanent Representatives of
the members of the Security Council
Saint Vincent and the Grenadines assumes the presidency of the Security
Council for November 2020 against the backdrop of the coronavirus disease
(COVID-19) pandemic, restrictions imposed by the authorities of the State and City
of New York and a recent move by the Council to return to the Security Council
Chamber for quite a number of meetings.
Saint Vincent and the Grenadines will approach the issue of in-person, including
in-Council, meetings with agility, flexibility, responsiveness and a keen awareness of
the material circumstances, and, above all, we will seek consensus among Council
members while ensuring that the views of key stakeholders are also taken into
account.
Accordingly, following consultations with the Secretariat an d all the members
of the Council, the presidency will extend and implement the working methods set
out in the letter dated 7 May 2020 from the President of the Security Council
addressed to the Permanent Representatives of the members of the Security Counc il
(S/2020/372) for Council work conducted virtually. For in-person meetings in the
Security Council Chamber, Saint Vincent and the Grenadines will extend and
implement the measures laid out in the annex to the letter from the President of the
Security Council for October 2020 on working methods ( S/2020/966), with particular
emphasis on the critical importance of wearing a mask at all times while in the
Chamber, including while delivering remarks. Furthermore, the members of the
Council are in agreement that, during November, for meetings in the Chamber, all
participants will wear a mask. Use of the Economic and Social Council Chamber for
in-person meetings remains an option to provide flexibility in November.
The Security Council will continue to optimize its working methods towards the
maintenance of international peace and security in a number of ways. Council
members agree that multilingualism will be prioritized for greater effectiveness and
involvement at meetings. In addition, the Council will continue to endeavour to
conduct its work effectively, efficiently and transparently, as the situation demands.
The Council remains committed to further enhancing interaction with and seeking the
views of non-Council members during the current extraordinary circumstances.
Saint Vincent and the Grenadines recalls all the letters from the President of the
Security Council issued during the COVID-19 pandemic from March to October 2020
on the issue of working methods (S/2020/253, S/2020/273, S/2020/372, S/2020/490,

Security Council.
