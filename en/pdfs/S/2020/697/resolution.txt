Letter dated 11 July 2020 from the President of the
Security Council addressed to the Secretary-General and
the Permanent Representatives of the members of the
Security Council
I have the honour to inform you that the Security Council has concluded the
voting procedure on the amendment contained in document S/2020/692, proposed by
China, to the draft resolution contained in documen t S/2020/684, submitted by
Belgium and Germany in connection, with the agenda item “The situation in the
Middle East”. The voting was carried out taking into account the extraordinary current
circumstances relating to the Council’s practice and working methods and in
accordance with the procedure set out in the letter dated 27 March 2020 by the
President of the Security Council addressed to the Permanent Representatives of the
members of the Security Council (S/2020/253), which was agreed upon in the light
of the extraordinary circumstances caused by the coronavirus disease (COVID -19)
pandemic.
The Director of the Security Council Affairs Division has receive d letters from
all 15 members of the Security Council indicating their national position on the
proposed amendment contained in document S/2020/692, to the draft resolution
contained in document S/2020/684. The result of the vote is as follows:
In favour:
China, Indonesia, the Niger, the Russian Federation, Saint Vincent and the
Grenadines, South Africa, Tunisia and Viet Nam.
Against:
Belgium, Estonia, France, Germany, the United Kingdom of Great Britain
and Northern Ireland and the United States of America.
Abstaining:
The Dominican Republic.
The proposal received eight votes in favour, six against and one abstention. The
proposal was not carried, having failed to obtain the required number of votes.
(Signed) Christoph Heusgen
President of the Security Council
