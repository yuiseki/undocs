Letter dated 18 November 2020 from the President of the
Security Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 13 November 2020
(S/2020/1124) with reference to paragraph 4 of Security Council resolution 2542
(2020), in which the Council requested an assessment of the steps required to reach a
lasting ceasefire in Libya and the possible role of the United Nations Support Mission
in Libya in providing scalable ceasefire support alongside an interim report on the
proposals for effective ceasefire monitoring under the auspices of the United Nations,
has been brought to the attention of the members of the Council.
The members of the Council have taken note that the interim report will be
delayed and look forward to its issuance no later than 30 Dece mber 2020. In the
meantime, they should be grateful for an interim briefing on progress given no later
than 7 December.
(Signed) I. Rhonda King
President of the Security Council
