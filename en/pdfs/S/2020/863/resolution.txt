Letter dated 31 August 2020 from the President of the
Security Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 25 August 2020
concerning the financing of the Residual Special Court for Sierra Leone for 2021
(S/2020/862) has been brought to the attention of the members of the Security
Council.
I wish to inform you that the members of the Council have taken note of the
intentions expressed in your letter, with the understanding that the requested
subvention will be subsequently reimbursed from the voluntary contributions
received by the Court and that the voluntary nature of the funding arrangement of the
Court will not be changed.
(Signed) Dian Triansyah Djani
President of the Security Council
