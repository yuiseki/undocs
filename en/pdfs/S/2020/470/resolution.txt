Letter dated 29 May 2020 from the Secretary-General addressed
to the President of the Security Council
I have the honour to refer to Security Council resolution 2498 (2019), adopted
on 15 November 2019, concerning Somalia. By paragraph 29 of that resolution, the
Council decided to renew until 15 December 2020 the Panel of Experts on Somalia.
Accordingly, I wish to inform you that, in view of the resignation of the armed
groups expert, and after consulting with the Security Council Committee pursuant to
resolution 751 (1992) concerning Somalia, I have appointed the following expert:
Mr. Patrick Loots, armed groups (South Africa).
I should be grateful if you would bring the present letter to the attention of the
members of the Security Council.
(Signed) António Guterres
