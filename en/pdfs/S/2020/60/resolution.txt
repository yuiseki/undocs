Letter dated 21 January 2020 from the President of the
Security Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 17 January 2020
(S/2020/59) concerning your intention to appoint Lieutenant General Daniel Sidiki
Traoré (Burkina Faso) as Force Commander of the United Nations Multidimensional
Integrated Stabilization Mission in the Central African Republic has been brought to
the attention of the members of the Security Council. They take note of the intention
expressed in your letter.
(Signed) Dang Dinh Quy
President of the Security Council
