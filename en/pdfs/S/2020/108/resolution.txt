Letter dated 6 February 2020 from the Secretary-General
addressed to the President of the Security Council
I have the honour to refer to the letter dated 27 December 2019 addressed to me
by the President of the Security Council (S/2019/1000), in which the President
concurred with my proposal to appoint Lord Iain Bonomy (United Kingdom of Great
Britain and Northern Ireland) as a judge of the International Residual Mechanism for
Criminal Tribunals to replace Judge Ben Emmerson, who resigned with effect from
19 July 2019.
I have received a corresponding letter from the President of the Gener al
Assembly (see annex). Accordingly, I am proceeding with the appointment of Lord
Bonomy as a judge of the Mechanism with immediate effect.
In accordance with article 10, paragraph 2, of the Statute of the Mechanism, the
appointment of Lord Bonomy is effective for the remainder of the term of office that
Judge Emmerson would have served, that is, until 30 June 2020.
I should be grateful if you would bring the present letter and its annex to the
attention of the members of the Security Council.
(Signed) António Guterres

Annex
Letter dated 20 December 2019 from the President of the
General Assembly addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to refer to your letter dated 11 December 2019 ( S/2019/999)
concerning the nomination of Lord Iain Bonomy of the United Kingdom of Great
Britain and Northern Ireland as a judge of the International Residual Mechanism for
Criminal Tribunals to replace Judge Ben Emmerson (United Kingdom of Great
Britain and Northern Ireland) for the remainder of his term of office, which ends on
30 June 2020.
Pursuant to article 10, paragraph 2, of the statute of the Mechanism and in
accordance with article 9, paragraph 1, of the same statute, as well as the established
practice regarding the appointment of a judge to fill a vacancy of the Mechanism in
similar circumstances, I have reviewed the qualifications of Lord Bonomy and I am
pleased to inform you that I hereby concur with the proposed appointment.
(Signed) Tijjani Muhammad-Bande
