Letter dated 2 June 2020 from the President of the
Security Council addressed to the Permanent Representatives
of the members of the Security Council
As France assumes the presidency of the Security Council on 1 June, the
restrictions imposed by the Governor of New York State in relation to coronavirus
disease (COVID-19) are still in place, and the measures issued by the SecretaryGeneral aimed at limiting the presence of United Nations staff within the United
Nations premises are in place until 30 June.
Accordingly, after consultation with the Secretariat and all members of the
Council, the French presidency will continue to implement the gu idelines agreed upon
under previous presidencies, which are set out in the letter dated 7 May 2020 from
the President of the Council addressed to the Permanent Representatives of the
members of the Council (S/2020/372).
The guidelines are a response to the current exceptional and unprecedented
circumstances.
The guidelines will be assessed at the end of June and may be reviewed, adjusted
or discontinued, subject to the agreement of all members.
(Signed) Nicolas de Rivière
President of the Security Council
