Letter dated 27 February 2020 from the Secretary-General
addressed to the President of the Security Council
I have the honour to refer to Security Council resolution 2507 (2020), adopted
on 31 January 2020, by which the Council decided to extend the mandate of the Panel
of Experts on the Central African Republic until 31 August 2020.
I wish to inform you that, after consulting the Security Council Committee
established pursuant to resolution 2127 (2013) concerning the Central African
Republic, I have appointed the following experts:
Mr. Zobel Behalal, finance/natural resources (Cameroon)
Mr. Romain Esmenjaud, regional (France)
Ms. Fiona Mangan, arms (Ireland)
Ms. Anna Osborne, humanitarian (United Kingdom of Great Britain and
Northern Ireland)
Mr. Ilyas Oussedik, armed groups (Algeria)
I have designated Mr. Esmenjaud to serve as Coordinator of the Panel of
Experts.
I should be grateful if you would bring this matter to the attention of the
members of the Security Council.
(Signed) António Guterres
