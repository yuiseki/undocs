Letter dated 31 August 2020 from the President of the
Security Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 27 August 2020
(S/2020/868) with reference to Security Council resolution 2524 (2020), in which the
Council requested that you report every 90 days on the implementation of the mandate
of the United Nations Integrated Transition Assistance Mission in the Sudan
(UNITAMS) and include in the first report clear and measurable core and contextual
benchmarks and indicators to track progress made by the Mission against the strategic
objectives and enable early planning for a future reconfiguration of the United
Nations presence in the Sudan, has been brought to the attention of the members of
the Council.
The members of the Council have taken note of the request contained in your
letter that the time frame for the submission of the first quarterly report be extended
for two weeks and that the submission of the suggested structure and geographical
deployment of UNITAMS, as well as the quarterly report on the African Union-United
Nations Hybrid Operation in Darfur, be annexed to that report.
(Signed) Dian Triansyah Djani
President of the Security Council
