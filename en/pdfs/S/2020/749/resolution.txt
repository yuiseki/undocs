Letter dated 23 July 2020 from the Secretary-General addressed to
the President of the Security Council
I have the honour to refer to Security Council resolution 2524 (2020) of 3 June
2020, in which the Council requested that I provide, for its information, a suggested
structure and geographical deployment for the United Nations Integrated Transition
Assistance Mission in the Sudan (UNITAMS), within 60 days of the adoption of the
resolution.
While the planning process for the establishment of UNITAMS was swiftly
initiated and important progress has been made, there have also been considerable
challenges linked to the coronavirus disease (COVID-19) pandemic. The planning
team is expected to travel to the Sudan in late July, circumstances permitting, to hold
in-depth discussions with Sudanese stakeholders and visit relevant locations in the
Sudan, with a view to developing a suggested structure and geographical deployment
for the Mission.
I therefore seek an extension of one month for the submission of the suggested
structure and geographical deployment, which I propose to annex to my first report
on the implementation of the UNITAMS mandate.
I should be grateful if you would bring the present letter to the attention of the
members of the Security Council.
(Signed) António Guterres
