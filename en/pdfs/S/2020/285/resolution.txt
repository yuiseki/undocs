Enclosure
[Original: French]

Report No. 27 on the operational support provided by French forces
to the United Nations Multidimensional Integrated Stabilization
Mission in Mali from 15 December 2019 to 1 March 2020
References:
• Security Council resolution 2480 (2019), of 28 June 2019 (paras. 42 and 64)
• Technical cooperation and assistance arrangement between the United Nations
Department of Peacekeeping Operations and the French Ministry of Armed
Forces of 26 March 2015
This twenty-seventh report summarizes the support that the French forc es
provided the United Nations Multidimensional Integrated Stabilization Mission in
Mali (MINUSMA) from 15 December 2019 to 1 March 2020.
The Barkhane force provided MINUSMA with the following operational
support during the period covered by this report:
1.

Operations
None.

2.

Intelligence
The Barkhane force provided weekly updates and some 40 intelligence
documents every month.
The force provided intelligence support, including by disseminating documents
concerning improvised explosive device disposal from the Counter-IED Exploitation
Laboratory (CIEL), J2 analysis products and lessons learned from operations.

3.

Engineering
Aeronautical engineering
The French aeronautical engineering detachment (25th Air Engineer Regiment),
under a MINUSMA letter of assist, maintains the Gao airfield in operational
condition.
The French aeronautical engineering detachment (25th Air Engineer Regiment)
mentors the MINUSMA detachment in Tessalit in relation to the maintenance of
unpaved airfields.
The aeronautical engineering officer at the engineering branch of the joint forces
headquarters is overseeing a project to construct a runway in Kidal, Mali. The 25th
Air Engineer Regiment has provided a team of explosive ordnance disposal specialists
for the explosives analysis phase and the clearing of the site of the future runway.
Countering improvised explosive devices
The Mine Action Service and the Barkhane officer responsible for efforts to
counter improvised explosive devices continue to exchange information on a neardaily basis (detailed reports on all incidents concerning improvised explosive devices,
weekly updates on improvised explosive device events in Mali, and an ad hoc
exchange of analyses).

The Weapons Intelligence Team and CIEL participate in a weekly information
exchange meeting with the MINUSMA improvised explosive device specialists in
Gao, including the German teams and the Mine Action Service. Strong dialogue has
led to the exchange of information and resources concerning the various cases
handled jointly or separately by the teams.
4.

Logistics
Since 15 December 2019, the Barkhane force has transported 741 persons and
3,082 kg of goods for MINUSMA, which involved 277 flight hours.

5.

Health
The Barkhane force role 1 and 2 medical services are able to support MINUSMA
in the event of an influx of injuries or under exceptional circumstances. Bilateral
agreements allow MINUSMA personnel of certain nationalities to have access to
routine and emergency consultations, paramedical care, dental care and surgical
procedures. Such services were rendered exclusively by the role 2 medical service in
Gao and comprised:
• 1 surgical procedure (visceral surgery)
• 4 cases of treatment of war wounded (including three Belgians injured by
improvised explosive devices in two incidents)
• 2 hospitalizations
• 486 instances of paramedical care
• 12 specialized consultations
• 24 radiographic examinations
• 5 biological tests
• 25 dental consultations
