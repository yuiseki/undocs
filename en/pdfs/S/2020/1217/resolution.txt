Letter dated 19 November 2020 from the Secretary-General
addressed to the President of the Security Council
I have the honour to refer to Security Council resolution 2542 (2020), adopted
on 15 September 2020, by which the Council extended the mandate o f the United
Nations Support Mission in Libya (UNSMIL) until 15 September 2021 and requested
me to appoint a special envoy on Libya to assume overall leadership of the Mission,
with a particular focus on good offices and mediation with Libyan and internati onal
actors to end the conflict in Libya.
I should like to inform you of my intention to appoint Mr. Nickolay Mladenov
(Bulgaria) as my Special Envoy on Libya and Head of UNSMIL. He succeeds
Ms. Stephanie T. Williams (United States of America), who has served as my Acting
Special Representative since 16 March 2020.
I should be grateful if you would bring this matter to the attention of the
members of the Security Council.
(Signed) António Guterres
