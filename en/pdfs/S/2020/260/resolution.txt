Letter dated 31 March 2020 from the Secretary-General addressed
to the President of the Security Council
I have the honour to refer to Security Council resolution 2511 (2020), adopted
on 25 February 2020, by which the Council decided to extend the mandate of the
Panel of Experts on Yemen until 28 March 2021.
Accordingly, I wish to inform you that, after consultin g the Security Council
Committee established pursuant to resolution 2140 (2014), I have appointed the
following experts:
Mr. Mourad Baly, finance (Tunisia)
Ms. Dakshinie Ruwanthika Gunaratne, armed groups (Sri Lanka)
Mr. Wolf-Christian Paes, arms (Germany)
Mr. Henry Thompson, regional issues (United Kingdom of Great Britain and
Northern Ireland)
Ms. Marie-Louise Tougas, international humanitarian law (Canada)
I have designated Ms. Gunaratne to serve as Coordinator of the Panel.
I should be grateful if you would bring this matter to the attention of the
members of the Security Council.
(Signed) António Guterres
