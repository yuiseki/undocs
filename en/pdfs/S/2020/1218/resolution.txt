Letter dated 15 December 2020 from the President of the
Security Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 19 November 2020
(S/2020/1217) concerning your intention to appoint Mr. Nickolay Mladenov
(Bulgaria) as your Special Envoy on Libya and Head of the United Nations Support
Mission in Libya has been brought to the attention of the members of the Security
Council. They take note of the intention expressed in your letter.
(Signed) Jerry Mathews Matjila
President of the Security Council
