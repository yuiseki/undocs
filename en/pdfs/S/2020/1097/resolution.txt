Letter dated 9 November 2020 from the President of the
Security Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 5 November 2020
(S/2020/1096) with reference to Security Council resolution 2525 (2020), in which
the Council requested an African Union-United Nations joint special report with
recommendations on the appropriate course of action for the African Union -United
Nations Hybrid Operation in Darfur (UNAMID) beyond the end of its current
mandate on 31 December 2020, has been brought to the attention of the members of
the Council.
The members of the Security Council have taken note that the publication of the
joint special report will be slightly delayed. They look forward to its publication by
13 November 2020.
(Signed) Inga Rhonda King
President of the Security Council
