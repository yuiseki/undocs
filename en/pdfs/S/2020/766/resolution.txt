Letter dated 23 July 2020 from the President of the Security Council
addressed to the Secretary-General
In a statement by the President of the Security Council (S/PRST/2017/13), the
Council requested that the Secretary-General identify options for enhancing United
Nations involvement in Burundi and developing mutual confidence -building
measures between the Government of Burundi and the United Nations. In the same
statement, the Council stressed the importance of coordinating efforts between the
African Union, the East African Community, the International Conference on the
Great Lakes Region and the United Nations, including the Special Envoy of the
Secretary-General, to continue to seek solutions to the crisis in Burundi. It also
expressed its intention to review its request for a quarterly report on Burundi on the
basis of the evolution of the situation on the ground.
The elections in Burundi of 20 May, together with the evolution of the peace
and security situation in the country, necessitate a review of United Nations
engagement with Burundi. In his inaugural address of 18 June, the President of
Burundi, Evariste Ndayishimiye, committed himself to strengthening relations with
African countries; regional organizations, such as the African Union and the East
African Community; and international organizations. In view of these elements and
the continued need for the engagement and support of international partners to foster
reconciliation, peace and security in Burundi, the Security Council requests the
Secretary-General to conduct and provide the Security Council, no later than
31 October 2020, a written report with recommendations assessing the challenges
facing Burundi and the scope and modalities of United Nations engagement in the
country, alongside the African Union and the region, to support Burundi in achieving
sustainable peace, reconciliation and development.
(Signed) Christoph Heusgen
President of the Security Council
