Letter dated 31 December 2019 from the Secretary-General
addressed to the President of the Security Council
I have the honour to refer to Security Council resolution 2495 (2019), in which
the Council requested the Secretary-General and the Chairperson of the African Union
Commission to provide a special report, no later than 31 January 2020, with
recommendations regarding the drawdown of the African Union-United Nations
Hybrid Operation in Darfur (UNAMID) and options for a follow-on presence to
UNAMID, based on the views and the needs of the Government of the Sudan.
We have since engaged with the African Union at the headquarters level, as well
as with the Sudanese transitional authorities to ascertain their priorities, including
through a visit by a multidisciplinary team to Khartoum from 12 to 16 December
2019. On the basis of those discussions, it is our collective assessment that more time
is required for the Sudanese interlocutors to articulate their preferences, especially in
relation to peace implementation. The conclusion of the ongoing talks in Juba
between the transitional authorities and the Sudanese armed groups, including those
from Darfur, Blue Nile and Southern Kordofan (the Two Areas) and other regions,
has been extended from December 2019 to February 2020. It would therefore be
prudent to solidify consultations with the Sudanese interlocutor s on the details of the
focus, architecture and operating modalities of the post -UNAMID mechanism in line
with the progress of the peace talks.
We therefore wish to request a one-month extension, to 28 February 2020, for
the submission of the special report. That adjustment would allow us to continue
consultations at a high level with the African Union and the Sudanese authorities in
January 2020.
I should be grateful if you would bring the present letter to the attention of the
members of the Security Council.
(Signed) António Guterres
