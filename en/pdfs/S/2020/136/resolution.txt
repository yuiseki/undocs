Letter dated 10 December 2019 from the Secretary-General
addressed to the President of the Security Council
I have the honour to refer to the United Nations Disengagement Observer Force
(UNDOF), which was established by the Security Council in its resolution 350 (1974).
Its mandate was most recently extended in resolution 2477 (2019).
I should like to inform you of my intention to appoint Major General Ishwar
Hamal (Nepal) as Head of Mission and Force Commander of UNDOF. I should also
like to take this opportunity to express my gratitude to Major General Shivaram
Kharel (Nepal), who was the acting Head of Mission and Force Commander of
UNDOF from 9 June to 24 October 2019.
I should be grateful if you would bring this matter to the attention of the
members of the Security Council.
(Signed) António Guterres
