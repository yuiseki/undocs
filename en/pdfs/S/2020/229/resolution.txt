Letter dated 24 March 2020 from the President of the
Security Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 21 February 2020
(S/2020/228) concerning your intention to appoint Ms. Deborah Lyons (Canada) as
your Special Representative for Afghanistan and Head of the United Nations
Assistance Mission in Afghanistan has been brought to the attention of the members
of the Security Council. They take note of the intention expressed in your letter.
(Signed) Zhang Jun
President of the Security Council
