Enclosure
Report No. 28 on the operational support provided by French
forces to the United Nations Multidimensional Integrated
Stabilization Mission in Mali from 1 March to 31 May 2020
References
• Security Council resolution 2480 (2019), of 28 June 2019 (paras. 42 and 64)
• Technical cooperation and assistance arrangement between the United Nations
Department of Peacekeeping Operations and the French Ministry of Armed
Forces of 26 March 2015
The Barkhane force provided the United Nations Multidimensional Integrated
Stabilization Mission in Mali (MINUSMA) with the following operational support
during the period covered by this report:
Operations
• None
Intelligence
• The Barkhane force provided weekly updates and some 40 intelligence
documents every month.
• The force provided intelligence support, including by disseminating documents
concerning improvised explosive device disposal from the Counter-IED
Exploitation Laboratory (CIEL), J2 analysis products and lessons learned from
operations.
Engineering
Aeronautical engineering
• The French aeronautical engineering detachment (25th Air Engineer Regiment),
under a MINUSMA letter of assist, maintains the Gao airfield in operational
condition.
• The French aeronautical engineering detachment (25th Air Engineer Regiment)
mentors the MINUSMA detachment in Tessalit in relation to the maintenance of
unpaved airfields.
• The aeronautical engineering officer at the engineering branch of the joint forces
headquarters is overseeing a project to construct a runway in Kidal, Mali. The
25th Air Engineer Regiment has provided a team of explosive ordnance disposal
specialists for the explosives analysis phase and the clearing of the site of the
future runway and a team to build the runway.
• The force provided logistics support for the construction of the camp in
Labézanga for the Malian armed forces.
Countering improvised explosive devices
• The Barkhane engineers carried out two IED sweeps in Camp Castor (a
MINUSMA camp) for the German detachment in May 2020.
• Counter-IED teams share information, expertise and data on a daily basis as part
of the effort to combat explosive devices. After the Ménaka camp was hit b y

Logistics
Since 1 March 2020, the Bharkane force has transported 91 persons and no
goods for MINUSMA, which involved 40 hours and 40 minutes of flight time.
Health
The Barkhane force role 1 and 2 medical services are able to support MINUSMA
in the event of an influx of injuries or under exceptional circumstances. Bilateral
agreements allow MINUSMA personnel of certain nationalities to have access to
routine and emergency consultations, paramedical care, dental care and surgical
procedures. Such services were rendered exclusively by the role 2 medical service in
Gao and comprised:
• 2 surgical procedures (visceral surgery)
• 2 cases of treatment of war wounded
• 2 hospitalizations
• 414 instances of paramedical care
• 48 specialized consultations
• 31 radiographic examinations
• 36 biological tests
• 16 dental consultations
