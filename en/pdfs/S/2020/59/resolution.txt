Letter dated 17 January 2020 from the Secretary-General
addressed to the President of the Security Council
I have the honour to refer to the United Nations Multidimensional Integrated
Stabilization Mission in the Central African Republic (MINUSCA), which was
established by the Security Council in its resolution 2149 (2014). Its mandate was
most recently extended, until 15 November 2020, by the Council in its resolution
2499 (2019).
I should like to inform you of my intention to appoint Lieutenant General Daniel
Sidiki Traoré (Burkina Faso) as Force Commander of MINUSCA. Lieutenant General
Traoré will succeed Lieutenant General Balla Keita (Senegal), who will complete his
assignment at the end of February 2020. I should like to take this opportunity to
reiterate my appreciation for the contribution of Lieutenant General Keita through his
dedication and effective leadership of MINUSCA.
I should be grateful if you would bring this matter to the attention of the
members of the Security Council.
(Signed) António Guterres
