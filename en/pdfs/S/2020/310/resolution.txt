Letter dated 15 April 2020 from the Secretary-General addressed
to the President of the Security Council
I have the honour to transmit herewith the first report by the Organisation for
the Prohibition of Chemical Weapons (OPCW) Investigation and Identification Team
pursuant to paragraph 10 of decision C-SS-4/DEC.3 (“Addressing the Threat from
Chemical Weapons Use”) Ltamenah (Syrian Arab Republic), 24, 25 and 30 March
2017, which I received from the Director-General of OPCW on 8 April 2020 (see
annex).
The issue of the use of chemical weapons in the Syrian Arab Republic is one
that the Security Council has been seized of since 2013. As any use of chemical
weapons poses a serious threat to international peace and security, I believe it is
appropriate that the report be shared with the members of the Council.
I should be grateful if you would bring this matter to the attention of the
members of the Security Council.
(Signed) António Guterres

I have the honour to provide to you, for your consideration, the attached report
entitled “First report of the Technical Secretariat pursuant to paragraph 10 of decision
C-SS-4/DEC.3 (dated 27 June 2018)” (see enclosure). The report was prepared in
accordance with paragraph 10 of decision C-SS-4/DEC.3, dated 27 June 2018, which
was adopted by the Conference of the States Parties of the Organisation for the
Prohibition of Chemical Weapons (hereinafter referred to as “OPCW”). Consistent
with paragraph 10 of this decision, the report has already been provided to the
Executive Council of OPCW, for its consideration.
I also wish to inform you that some of the annexes of the report contain
confidential information classified as “OPCW highly protected” and will be provided
to you through appropriate means. Consistent with the Agreement concerning the
Relationship between the United Nations and the Organisation for the Prohibition of
Chemical Weapons, I would kindly ask that the United Nations ensure the appropriate
protection in respect of such confidential information, in accordance with its
constituent instrument and policies on confidentiality.
Owing to the current situation arising from the C OVID-19 pandemic outbreak
and the ensuing restrictions on convening meetings that are, at the moment, applicable
at OPCW, the report will be considered by the Executive Council of OPCW when the
situation will allow.
(Signed) Fernando Arias
