Letter dated 29 January 2007 from the Secretary-General
addressed to the President of the Security Council
I have the honour to refer to Security Council resolution 1731 (2006)
concerning Liberia, adopted on 20 December 2006, by which the Council decided to
extend the mandate of the Panel of Experts appointed pursuant to resolution 1689
(2006) for a further period, until 20 June 2007. In paragraph 5 of resolution 1731
(2006) the Council also requested me to reappoint the members of the Panel of
Experts and to make the necessary financial and security arrangements to support
the work of the Panel.
Accordingly, I wish to inform you that I have reappointed the following
persons to serve on the Panel of Experts for a period ending on 20 June 2007.

I have designated Mr. Blundell to serve as the Chairman of the Panel of
Experts.
I should be grateful if you could bring this information to the attention of the
members of the Security Council.
(Signed) Ban Ki-moon
