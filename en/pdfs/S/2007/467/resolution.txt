Letter dated 30 July 2007 from the President of the
Security Council to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 26 July 2007
(S/2007/466) concerning your intention to appoint Hédi Annabi (Tunisia) as your
Special Representative and Head of the United Nations Stabilization Mission in
Haiti (MINUSTAH) has been brought to the attention of the members of the
Security Council. They take note of the intention expressed in your letter.
(Signed) Wang Guangya
President of the Security Council
