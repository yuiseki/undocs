Letter dated 12 December 2007 from the Secretary-General to the
President of the Security Council
I received a letter today from the Prime Minister of Lebanon, Mr. Fouad
Siniora (see annex), in which he requested technical assistance for the Government
of Lebanon’s efforts to investigate the murder of Brigadier-General François Hajj.
The Government requested that the International Independent Investigation
Commission be in touch with the relevant Lebanese authorities for that purpose.
Since the Commission reports to the Security Council, I am transmitting this
request to you, and through you to the other members of the Council, in order for
members to take appropriate action.
(Signed) Ban Ki-moon

Annex
Letter dated 12 December 2007 from the Prime Minister of
Lebanon addressed to the Secretary-General
I write to you of the heinous terrorist act which took the life of BrigadierGeneral François Hajj this morning in Baabda, east of Beirut. This horrific
assassination comes after a temporary halt in the series of crimes Lebanon has been
witnessing for over three years now. However, this attack is the first against a high
ranking officer in the Lebanese army, the symbol of national unity, and is clearly an
attempt to intimidate this institution in the exercise of its duties. Furthermore, the
timing of this terrorist act undermines the process to elect a new President of the
Republic.
Based on the previous agreement between the International Independent
Investigation Commission and the Lebanese Government, we hereby request
technical assistance in the effort to investigate the murder of Brigadier-General Hajj.
We request that the International Independent Investigation Commission be in
touch with the relevant Lebanese authorities for that purpose.
(Signed) Fuad Siniora
