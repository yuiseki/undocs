Letter dated 17 April 2007 from the Secretary-General to the
President of the Security Council
On 9 February 2007, pursuant to Security Council resolution 1727 (2006), I
took the necessary administrative measures to extend the mandate of the Group of
Experts on Côte d’Ivoire and appointed the following five experts to serve on the
Group:
Grégoire Bafouatika, Congo (aviation)
Lipika Majumdar Roy Choudhury, India (finance)
Abdoul Wahab Diakhaby, Guinea (diamonds)
André Thorsen, Canada (arms)
Oumar Dieye Sidi, Niger (customs)
I have subsequently been informed that one of the experts, André Thorsen, was
unable to assume his functions. Taking into account the short period of time
remaining in the mandate of the Group of Experts, and in consultation with the
Security Council Committee established pursuant to resolution 1572 (2004), I wish
to inform you that I have decided not to appoint a new expert to replace
Mr. Thorsen.
I should be grateful if you would bring this letter to the attention of the
members of the Security Council.
(Signed) Ban Ki-moon
