Letter dated 12 January 2007 from the President of the
Security Council to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to refer to Security Council resolution 1646 (2005), adopted
on 20 December 2005, in which the Council, in keeping with its resolution 1645
(2005), adopted on the same date, decided that the permanent members listed in
article 23, paragraph 1, of the United Nations Charter should be members of the
Organizational Committee of the Peacebuilding Commission and that, in addition,
the Council should select annually two of its elected members to participate in the
Organizational Committee.
I therefore have the honour to inform you that following informal
consultations on 11 January 2007, members of the Security Council agreed on the
selection of Panama and South Africa as the two elected members of the Council to
participate in the Organizational Committee for a term of one year, until the end of
2007.
(Signed) Vitaly I. Churkin
President of the Security Council
