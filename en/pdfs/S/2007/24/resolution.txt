Letter dated 16 January 2007 from the Secretary-General
addressed to the President of the Security Council
I have the honour to refer to the command of the United Nations
Disengagement Observer Force (UNDOF).
Following the usual consultations, it is my intention to appoint
Major-General Wolfgang Jilke of Austria as the Force Commander of UNDOF.
Major-General Jilke will replace Major-General Sharma, who will relinquish the
post on 17 January 2007.
I would be grateful if you would bring this matter to the attention of the
members of the Security Council.
(Signed) Ban Ki-moon
