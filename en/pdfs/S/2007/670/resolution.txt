Letter dated 14 November 2007 from the President of the
Security Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 12 November 2007
(S/2007/669) concerning your intention to appoint Daniel Bellemare, of Canada, as
the Commissioner of the United Nations International Independent Investigation
Commission established following the assassination of the former Prime Minister of
Lebanon, Rafik Hariri, in accordance with Security Council resolution 1595 (2005),
has been brought to the attention of the members of the Security Council. They take
note of the intention expressed in your letter.
(Signed) R. M. Marty M. Natalegawa
President of the Security Council
