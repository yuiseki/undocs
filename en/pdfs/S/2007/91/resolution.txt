Letter dated 15 February 2007 from the President of the
Security Council to the Secretary-General
The members of the Security Council have carefully considered your letter
dated 13 February 2007 (S/2007/90), regarding the request of the Government of
Lebanon for technical assistance from the International Independent Investigation
Commission (IIIC) in the effort to investigate the bombing near the town of Bikfaya
on 13 February 2007 which, according to reports, killed 3 people and injured 22.
Determined to continue to assist the Government of Lebanon in the search for the
truth and to bring to justice perpetrators, organizers and sponsors of that terrorist
attack, as well as of other terrorist attacks and assassinations committed in Lebanon
since October 2004 (see press statement SC/8954), we refer to resolutions 1644
(2005) and 1686 (2006), invite the Commission to extend appropriate technical
assistance to the Lebanese authorities in the investigation and invite the SecretaryGeneral to so inform the Government of Lebanon and the Commissioner of IIIC.
(Signed) Peter Burian
President of the Security Council
