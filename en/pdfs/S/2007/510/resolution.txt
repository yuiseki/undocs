Letter dated 27 August 2007 from the President of the
Security Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 22 August 2007
(S/2007/509), in which you stated your intention to appoint Division General Zhao
Jingmin (China) to the post of Force Commander of the United Nations Mission for
the Referendum in Western Sahara, has been brought to the attention of the
members of the Council and that they have taken note of it.
(Signed) Pascal Gayama
President of the Security Council
