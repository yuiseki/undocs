Letter dated 28 November 2007 from the Secretary-General to the
President of the Security Council
I have the honour to refer to Security Council resolution 1782 (2007)
concerning Côte d’Ivoire. By paragraph 8 of the resolution, the Council decided to
extend the mandate of the Group of Experts until 31 October 2008 and requested me
to take the necessary administrative measures.
Accordingly, I wish to inform you that, after consulting with the Security
Council Committee established pursuant to resolution 1572 (2004) concerning
Côte d’Ivoire, I have appointed the following experts:
Grégoire Bafouatika, Congo (aviation)
Lipika Majumdar Roy Choudhury, India (finance)
Agim de Bruycker, Belgium (diamonds)
Claudio Gramizzi, Italy (arms)
Vernon Paul Kulyk, Canada (customs)
I have designated Ms. Lipika Majumdar Roy Choudhury to serve as
Coordinator of the Group of Experts.
I should be grateful if you would bring this information to the attention of the
members of the Security Council.
(Signed) Ban Ki-moon
