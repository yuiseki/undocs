Letter dated 7 September 2007 from the President of the
Security Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 3 September 2007
(S/2007/533) concerning your intention to appoint Staffan de Mistura (Sweden) as
your Special Representative and Head of the United Nations Assistance Mission for
Iraq has been brought to the attention of the members of the Security Council. They
take note of the intention contained in your letter.
(Signed) Jean-Marie Ripert
President of the Security Council
