Note by the President of the Security Council
Following consultations among the members of the Security Council, it has
been agreed that the Ad Hoc Working Group on Conflict Prevention and Resolution
in Africa, initially established on 1 March 2002 (see S/2002/207) for a period of one
year, will continue its work until 31 December 2007.
