Letter dated 29 August 2007 from the Secretary-General
addressed to the President of the Security Council
I have the honour to refer to the Counter-Terrorism Committee Executive
Directorate, which was established pursuant to Security Council resolution 1535
(2004).
Following the usual consultations, I would like to inform you that I intend to
appoint Mike Smith (Australia) as Executive Director of the Counter-Terrorism
Committee Executive Directorate. Mr. Smith will replace Javier Ruperez (Spain),
who stepped down at the end of June. I would like to take this opportunity to
express my appreciation to Mr. Ruperez for his dedication and effective leadership.
I should be grateful if you would bring the present letter to the attention of the
members of the Security Council.
(Signed) Ban Ki-moon
