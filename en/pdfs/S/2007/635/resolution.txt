Note by the President of the Security Council
Pursuant to paragraph 4 (b) of the note by the President of the Security
Council dated 30 October 1998 (S/1998/1016), and after consultations among the
members of the Council, the members of the Council agreed to elect the Chairmen
and Vice-Chairman of the following subsidiary bodies for the period ending
31 December 2007:
Security Council Committee established pursuant to resolution 918 (1994)
concerning Rwanda
Chairman:
R. M. Marty M. Natalegawa (Indonesia)
Italy will continue to serve as Vice-Chairman for the period.
Working Group on Peacekeeping Operations
Chairman:
R. M. Marty M. Natalegawa (Indonesia)
