Letter dated 31 July 2007 from the Secretary-General addressed
to the President of the Security Council
By its resolution 1505 (2003) of 4 September 2003, the Security Council
appointed Hassan Bubacar Jallow as Prosecutor of the International Criminal
Tribunal for Rwanda. In accordance with the statute of the International Criminal
Tribunal, the term of office of Mr. Jallow will come to an end on 14 September
2007.
In accordance with article 15, paragraph 4, of the statute of the International
Criminal Tribunal for Rwanda, the Prosecutor is appointed by the Security Council
on nomination by the Secretary-General and shall serve a four-year term.
In accordance with my responsibilities under that provision, I hereby submit to
you and, through you, to the Security Council the name of Hassan Bubacar Jallow as
my nominee and would ask the Council to reappoint him as Prosecutor of the
International Criminal Tribunal for Rwanda for a four-year term, effective
15 September 2007, subject to the following paragraph.
At the present time, the expectation is that the International Tribunal shall
complete its mandate before the expiry of Mr. Jallow’s four-year term. Therefore the
actual duration of Mr. Jallow’s appointment shall be determined by the completion
of the work of the International Criminal Tribunal for Rwanda.
(Signed) Ban Ki-Moon
