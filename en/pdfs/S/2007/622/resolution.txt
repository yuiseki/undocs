Letter dated 17 October 2007 from the Secretary-General
addressed to the President of the Security Council
I have the honour to refer to the United Nations Operation in Côte d’Ivoire
(UNOCI), established on 27 February 2004 pursuant to Security Council resolution
1528 (2004), the United Nations Organization Mission in the Democratic Republic
of the Congo (MONUC), established on 30 November 1999 pursuant to Security
Council resolution 1279 (1999), and the United Nations Mission in Liberia
(UNMIL), established on 19 September 2003 pursuant to Security Council
resolution 1509 (2003).
Following the usual consultations, it is my intention to appoint:
• Ellen Margrethe Løj (Denmark) as my Special Representative for Liberia.
Ms. Løj will replace Alan Doss. Ms. Løj has previously served as Permanent
Representative of Denmark to the United Nations and as Representative of
Denmark on the Security Council, as well as Chairperson of its Sanctions
Committee on Liberia
• Alan Doss (United Kingdom of Great Britain and Northern Ireland) as my
Special Representative for the Democratic Republic of the Congo. Mr. Doss,
currently the Special Representative for Liberia, will replace William Lacy
Swing (United States of America)
• Choi Young-Jin (Republic of Korea) as my Special Representative for Côte
d’Ivoire. Mr. Choi, who has previously served as Permanent Representative of
the Republic of Korea to the United Nations, as well as Vice Foreign Minister
of his country, has also served in the United Nations as Assistant SecretaryGeneral for Peacekeeping Operations.
The precise timing of their assignments will be finalized taking into account
relevant factors in each case.
I should like to take this opportunity to express my appreciation to Mr. Schori,
Mr. Swing and Mr. Doss for their remarkable contributions to the United Nations
peace efforts in Côte d’Ivoire, the Democratic Republic of the Congo and Liberia,
respectively.
I would be grateful if you would bring this matter to the attention of the
members of the Security Council.
(Signed) Ban Ki-moon
