Report of the Secretary-General and the Chairperson

Addendum
1.
Further to the report on the hybrid operation in Darfur (S/2007/307/Rev.1)
dated 5 June, I wish to inform the Security Council that, based on preliminary
estimates of requirements, the financial implications of the African Union-United
Nations hybrid operation in Darfur, as they correspond to options I and II, which are
set out in paragraphs 79 and 80 of that report, are estimated on a full-cost basis for a
12-month period at some $2.6 billion and $2.5 billion, respectively.
2.
The preliminary projection of $2,625.8 million for the hybrid operation in
Darfur under the first option provides for the full deployment of 19,555 military
personnel, including 240 military observers and up to 120 liaison officers. The
projection also provides for the full emplacement of 3,772 police officers and 2,660
formed police (19 formed police units); and the full deployment of 5,009 civilian
personnel.
3.
The preliminary projection of $2,494.6 million for the hybrid operation in
Darfur under the second option provides for the full deployment of 17,605 military
personnel, including 240 military observers and up to 120 liaison officers. The
projection also provides for the full emplacement of 3,772 police officers and 2,660
formed police (19 formed police units); and the full deployment of 4,941 civilian
personnel.
4.
The above preliminary projections include requirements of some $840 million
for capital expenditures and construction services, which are non-recurrent.
5.
An indicative preliminary breakdown of the financial implications of the two
options by main categories of expenditure is provided in the annex to the present
addendum, for information purposes only.
6.
Subject to the Security Council’s decision regarding the proposal on the
hybrid operation in Darfur as contained in S/2007/307/Rev.1, the additional
resources will be sought from the General Assembly.
