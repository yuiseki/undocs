Letter dated 7 September 2007 from the President of the
Security Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 3 September 2007
(S/2007/531) concerning your intention to appoint Mr. Ashraf Jehangir Qazi
(Pakistan) as your Special Representative for the Sudan has been brought to the
attention of the members of the Security Council. They take note of the intention
contained in your letter.
(Signed) Jean-Marie Ripert
President of the Security Council
