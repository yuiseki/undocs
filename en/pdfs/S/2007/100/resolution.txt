Note by the President of the Security Council
At its 5612th meeting, held on 23 December 2006, in connection with the item
on non-proliferation, the Security Council adopted resolution 1737 (2006).
In paragraph 23 of the resolution, the Security Council requested within
60 days a report from the Director General of the International Atomic Energy
Agency (IAEA) on whether the Islamic Republic of Iran had established full and
sustained suspension of all activities mentioned in the resolution, as well as on the
process of Iranian compliance with all the steps required by the IAEA Board of
Governors and with the other provisions of the resolution, to the Board and in
parallel to the Council for its consideration.
Accordingly, the President hereby circulates the report received today from
IAEA (see annex).
