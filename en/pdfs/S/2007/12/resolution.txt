Letter dated 10 January 2007 from the President of the
Security Council to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 8 January 2007
(S/2007/11) concerning your intention to appoint Major General Carlos Alberto dos
Santos Cruz (Brazil) as Force Commander of the United Nations Stabilization
Mission in Haiti has been brought to the attention of the members of the Security
Council. They take note of the intention expressed in your letter.
(Signed) Vitaly I. Churkin
President of the Security Council
