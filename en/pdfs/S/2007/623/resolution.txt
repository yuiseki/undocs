Letter dated 19 October 2007 from the President of the
Security Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 17 October 2007
(S/2007/622) concerning your intention to appoint Ellen Margrethe Løj (Denmark)
as your Special Representative for Liberia, Alan Doss (United Kingdom of Great
Britain and Northern Ireland) as your Special Representative for the Democratic
Republic of the Congo and Mr. Choi Young-Jin (Republic of Korea) as your Special
Representative for Côte d’Ivoire has been brought to the attention of the members of
the Security Council. They take note of the intention expressed in your letter.
(Signed) Leslie Kojo Christian
President of the Security Council
