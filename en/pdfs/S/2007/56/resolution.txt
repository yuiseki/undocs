Letter dated 5 February 2007 from the President of the
Security Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 31 January 2007
(S/2007/55) concerning your intention to appoint Julian Harston (United Kingdom
of Great Britain and Northern Ireland) as your Special Representative for Western
Sahara and Head of the United Nations Mission for the Referendum in Western
Sahara (MINURSO) has been brought to the attention of the members of the
Security Council. They take note of the intention expressed in your letter.
(Signed) Peter Burian
President of the Security Council
