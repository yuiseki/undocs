Letter dated 31 August 2007 from the President of the Security
Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 27 August 2007
(S/2007/522) concerning your intentions to upgrade the level of the Head of the
United Nations Political Office for Somalia (UNPOS) to that of Under-SecretaryGeneral and to appoint Ahmedou Ould Abdallah as your Special Representative for
Somalia, to reassign François Lonseny Fall as Head of the United Nations
Peacebuilding Support Office in the Central African Republic (BONUCA) and to
temporarily reassign General Lamine Cisse as Officer-in-Charge of the United
Nations Office for West Africa (UNOWA) has been brought to the attention of the
members of the Security Council. They take note of the intentions and information
contained in your letter.
(Signed) Pascal Gayama
President of the Security Council
