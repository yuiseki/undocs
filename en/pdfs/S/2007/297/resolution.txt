Letter dated 18 May 2007 from the President of the
Security Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 15 May 2007
(S/2007/296) concerning your intention to phase down and close the United Nations
Tajikistan Office of Peacebuilding (UNTOP) and, in response to the request of the
Government of Tajikistan, to continue the activities of UNTOP for a further period
of two months, until 31 July 2007, has been brought to the attention of the members
of the Security Council. They take note of the information contained in your letter
and the intention expressed therein.
(Signed) Zalmay Khalilzad
President of the Security Council
