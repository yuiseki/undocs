Note by the President of the Security Council
Pursuant to paragraph 4 (b) of the note by the President of the Security
Council dated 30 October 1998 (S/1998/1016), and after consultations among the
members of the Council, it was agreed to elect the chairmen and vice-chairmen of
the following subsidiary bodies for the period ending 31 December 2007.
Security Council Committee established pursuant to resolution 751 (1992)
concerning Somalia
Chairman:
Dumisani Shadrack Kumalo (South Africa)
Vice-Chairmen: Ghana and Slovakia
Security Council Committee established pursuant to resolution 918 (1994)
concerning Rwanda
Chairman:
Rezlan Ishar Jenie (Indonesia)
Vice-Chairman: Italy
Security Council Committee established pursuant to resolution 1132 (1997)
concerning Sierra Leone
Chairman:
Nassir Abdulaziz Al-Nasser (Qatar)
Vice-Chairmen: Congo and Panama
Security Council Committee established pursuant to resolution 1267 (1999)
concerning Al-Qaida and the Taliban and associated individuals and entities
Chairman:
Johan C. Verbeke (Belgium)
Vice-Chairmen: Ghana and Russian Federation
