Letter dated 7 February 2007 from the President of the Security
Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 2 February 2007
(S/2007/61) concerning your intention to appoint Ian Martin (United Kingdom of
Great Britain and Northern Ireland) as your Special Representative in Nepal and
Head of the United Nations Mission in Nepal (UNMIN) has been brought to the
attention of the members of the Security Council. They take note of the intention
expressed in your letter.
(Signed) Peter Burian
President of the Security Council
