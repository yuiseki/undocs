Letter dated 16 October 2007 from the President of the
Security Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 10 October 2007
(S/2007/609) concerning your intention to appoint Judge Mohamed Amin El Mahdi
of Egypt and Judge Erik Møse of Norway as the two judges on the selection panel,
as well as Mr. Nicolas Michel, Under-Secretary-General for Legal Affairs, the Legal
Counsel, as your representative has been brought to the attention of the members of
the Security Council. They take note of the intention expressed in your letter.
(Signed) Leslie Kojo Christian
President of the Security Council
