Letter dated 13 February 2007 from the President of the
Security Council to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 8 February 2007
(S/2007/85) concerning your intention to appoint Geir Pedersen as your Special
Coordinator for Lebanon has been brought to the attention of the members of the
Security Council. They take note of the intention expressed in your letter.
(Signed) Peter Burian
President of the Security Council
