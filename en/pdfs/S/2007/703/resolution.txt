Letter dated 3 December 2007 from the President of the
Security Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 28 November 2007
(S/2007/702) concerning your recommendation that the mandate of the United
Nations Peacebuilding Support Office in the Central African Republic (BONUCA)
be extended for an additional year, from 1 January to 31 December 2008, has been
brought to the attention of the members of the Security Council. They take note of
the information and recommendation contained in your letter.
(Signed) Marcello Spatafora
President of the Security Council
