LETTER DATED 13 MAY 1998 FROM THE PRESIDENT OF THE SECURITY
COUNCIL ADDRESSED TO THE SECRETARY-GENERAL

I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 8 May 1998
(S/1998/388) concerning your intention to appoint Ms. Ann Hercus (New Zealand)
as Deputy Special Representative and Chief of Mission in Cyprus has been brought
to the attention of the members of the Council. They agree with the intention
contained in your letter.

(Signed) Njuguna M. MAHUGU, OGW
President of the Security Council
