LETTER DATED 15 JULY 1998 FROM THE PRESIDENT OF THE
SECURITY COUNCIL ADDRESSED TO THE SECRETARY-GENERAL

I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 8 July 1998
(S/1998/640) concerning the list of candidates to the International Criminal
Tribunal for Rwanda and your suggestion to extend the deadline for nominations
to the Tribunal until 4 August 1998 has been brought to the attention of the
Security Council. The Council noted the information contained in the letter and
agreed with the proposal contained therein.

(Signed) Sergey V. LAVROV
President of the Security Council
