LETTER DATED 17 JULY 1998 FROM THE SECRETARY-GENERAL
ADDRESSED TO THE PRESIDENT OF THE SECURITY COUNCIL

I have the honour to refer to the composition of the United Nations Truce
Supervision Organization (UNTSO).
Slovakia and Slovenia have expressed an interest in providing military
observers to UNTSO, and Austria has offered to reduce its contingent to make
that possible. This is in the framework of the cooperation between these States
in the field of peacekeeping.
Following the usual consultations, I propose that Slovakia and Slovenia be
added to the list of States providing military observers to UNTSO.
I should be grateful if you could bring this letter to the attention of the
members of the Security Council.
