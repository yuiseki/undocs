LETTER DATED 14 APRIL 1998 FROM THE PRESIDENT OF THE
SECURITY COUNCIL ADDRESSED TO THE SECRETARY-GENERAL

I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 8 April 1998
(S/1998/320) concerning your intention to appoint Brigadier-General
Barthélémy Ratanga (Gabon) as Force Commander of the United Nations Mission in
the Central African Republic has been brought to the attention of the members of
the Security Council. They agree with the intention contained in your letter.

(Signed) Hisashi OWADA
President of the Security Council
