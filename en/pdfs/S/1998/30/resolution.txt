LETTER DATED 13 JANUARY 1998 FROM THE PRESIDENT OF THE
SECURITY COUNCIL ADDRESSED TO THE SECRETARY-GENERAL

I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 8 January 1998
(S/1998/29), concerning the appointment of Mr. Souren Seraydarian (Syrian Arab
Republic) as the representative of the Secretary-General and head of the support
group and the United Nations Liaison Office in Zagreb has been brought to the
attention of the members of the Security Council. They agree with the intention
that you express therein.

(Signed)
