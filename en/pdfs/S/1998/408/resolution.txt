LETTER DATED 19 MAY 1998 FROM THE PRESIDENT OF THE
SECURITY COUNCIL ADDRESSED TO THE SECRETARY-GENERAL

I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 15 May 1998
(S/1998/407) concerning your intention to appoint Mr. Ján Kubiš (Slovakia) as
your Special Representative for Tajikistan has been brought to the attention of
the members of the Security Council. They agree with the intention contained in
your letter.

(Signed) Njuguna M. MAHUGU, OGW
