LETTER DATED 21 JULY 1998 FROM THE PRESIDENT OF THE
SECURITY COUNCIL ADDRESSED TO THE SECRETARY-GENERAL

I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 16 July 1998
(S/1998/673) concerning your proposal to include China, Egypt, India, Kenya,
Kyrgyzstan, New Zealand, Pakistan, the Russian Federation, the United Kingdom of
Great Britain and Northern Ireland and Zambia in the list of countries
contributing military personnel to the United Nations Observer Mission in Sierra
Leone (UNOMSIL) has been brought to the attention of the members of the Council.
They agree with the proposal contained in your letter. The members of the
Council also take note that Brigadier-General Subhash C. Joshi (India) will be
the Chief Military Observer of UNOMSIL.

(Signed) Sergey V. LAVROV
President of the Security Council
