LETTER DATED 1 MAY 1998 FROM THE PRESIDENT OF THE SECURITY
COUNCIL ADDRESSED TO THE SECRETARY-GENERAL

I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 28 April 1998
(S/1998/363) concerning your proposal to add Slovakia to the list of Member
States providing troops to the United Nations Disengagement Observer Force
(UNDOF) has been brought to the attention of the members of the Council. They
agree with the proposal contained in your letter.

(Signed) Njuguna M. MAHUGU, OGW
President of the Security Council
