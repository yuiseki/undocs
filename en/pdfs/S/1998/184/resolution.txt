LETTER DATED 2 MARCH 1998 FROM THE PRESIDENT OF THE SECURITY
COUNCIL ADDRESSED TO THE SECRETARY-GENERAL

I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 25 February 1998
(S/1998/183) regarding your intention to appoint Major General
Timothy Roger Ford of Australia to succeed Major General Rufus Kupolati
of Nigeria as Chief of Staff of the United Nations Truce Supervision
Organization (UNTSO) has been brought to the attention of the members of the
Council. They agree with the intention contained in the letter.

(Signed) Abdoulie Momodou SALLAH
President of the Security Council
