LETTER DATED 19 OCTOBER 1998 FROM THE PRESIDENT OF THE
SECURITY COUNCIL TO THE SECRETARY-GENERAL

I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 15 October 1998
(S/1998/968) concerning your decision to appoint Ambassador
Ayité Jean-Claude Kpakpo (Benin) as Senior United Nations Adviser to the
Facilitator of the Burundi peace process, His Excellency Mwalimu Julius Nyerere,
has been brought to the attention of the members of the Security Council. They
take note of the decision contained in your letter.

(Signed) Jeremy GREENSTOCK
President of the Security Council
