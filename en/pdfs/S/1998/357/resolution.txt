LETTER DATED 30 APRIL 1998 FROM THE PRESIDENT OF THE
SECURITY COUNCIL ADDRESSED TO THE SECRETARY-GENERAL

I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 31 March 1998
(S/1998/356) concerning your intention to add Sweden to the list of countries
contributing military personnel to the United Nations Mission for the Referendum
in Western Sahara (MINURSO) has been brought to the attention of the members of
the Security Council. They agree with the intention contained in your letter.

(Signed) Hisashi OWADA
President of the Security Council
