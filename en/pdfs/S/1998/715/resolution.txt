LETTER DATED 5 AUGUST 1998 FROM THE PRESIDENT OF THE
SECURITY COUNCIL ADDRESSED TO THE SECRETARY-GENERAL

I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 3 August 1998
(S/1998/714) concerning the establishment of security arrangements for United
Nations personnel with the Chairman of the Economic Community of West African
States (ECOWAS) and the conclusion of the status of mission agreement with the
Government of Sierra Leone has been brought to the attention of the members of
the Security Council. They take note of the information regarding the
implementation of paragraphs 9 and 10 of resolution 1181 (1998) contained in
your letter.

(Signed) Danilo TÜRK
