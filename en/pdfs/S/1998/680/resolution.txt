LETTER DATED 22 JULY 1998 FROM THE PRESIDENT OF THE
SECURITY COUNCIL ADDRESSED TO THE SECRETARY-GENERAL

I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 17 July 1998
(S/1998/679) concerning your proposal to add Slovakia and Slovenia to the list
of States providing military observers to the United Nations Truce Supervision
Organization (UNTSO) has been brought to the attention of the members of the
Council. They agree with the proposal contained in your letter.

(Signed) Sergey V. LAVROV
President of the Security Council
