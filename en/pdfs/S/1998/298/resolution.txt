LETTER DATED 3 APRIL 1998 FROM THE PRESIDENT OF THE
SECURITY COUNCIL ADDRESSED TO THE SECRETARY-GENERAL

I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 31 March 1998
(S/1998/297) concerning your intention to appoint Mr. Oluyemi Adeniji (Nigeria)
Special Representative of the Secretary-General for the Central African Republic
and Head of the United Nations Mission in the Central African Republic (MINURCA)
has been brought to the attention of the members of the Council. They agree
with the intention contained in your letter.

(Signed)
Hisashi OWADA
President of the Security Council
