LETTER DATED 7 AUGUST 1998 FROM THE SECRETARY-GENERAL
ADDRESSED TO THE PRESIDENT OF THE SECURITY COUNCIL

I have the honour to refer to my letter dated 8 July 1998 (document
S/1998/640) by which I forwarded to the Security Council, further to Paragraph 3
of Article 12 of the Statute of the International Tribunal for Rwanda, the
nominations for judges of the Trial Chambers of that Tribunal which had been
received from States Members of the United Nations within the thirty-day period
specified in Article 12 (3) (b) of its Statute.
I also have the honour to refer to the letter, dated 15 July 1998 (document
S/1998/646), of the former President of the Security Council,
Mr. Sergey V. Lavrov, in which he informed me of the decision of the Security
Council which was taken at its 3908th meeting on 15 July 1998 to agree to the
suggestion which I had made in my letter of 8 July 1998 that the Council extend
the deadline for nominations for judges of the Trial Chambers of the
International Tribunal for Rwanda until 4 August 1998.
Pursuant to Paragraph 3 (c) of the Statute of the International Tribunal
for Rwanda, I have the honour to forward to the Security Council the fourteen
nominations for judges of the Trial Chambers of the Tribunal which have been
received from States Members of the United Nations within the period specified
in Paragraph 3 (b) of Article 12 of that Statute, as extended by the Security
Council in the decision taken at its 3908th meeting. The list of candidates,*
in alphabetical order, is annexed to this letter, together with the curricula
vitae which have been provided in connection with their nominations.
In this connection, I would draw the attention of the Security Council to
Paragraph 3 (c) of Article 12 of the Tribunal’s Statute, which provides in
relevant part as follows: "From the nominations received, the Security Council
shall establish a list of not less than eighteen and not more than twenty-seven
candidates, taking due account of the adequate representation on the
International Tribunal for Rwanda of the principal legal systems of the world".
