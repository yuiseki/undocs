Appointment of the Prosecutor
The President: The Security Council will now begin
its consideration of the item on its agenda. The Security
Council is meeting in accordance with the understanding
reached in its prior consultations.

Let me say, as President of the Council, that I am
very pleased that we are able to provide a seamless
transition for the important work of the war crimes
Tribunals. I think that this is a great step forward and that
we should all be proud.

Members of the Council have before them document
S/1996/139, which contains the text of a draft resolution
prepared in the course of the Council’s prior consultations.

The Security Council has thus concluded the present
stage of its consideration of the item on its agenda. The
Security Council will remain seized of the matter.

It is my understanding that the Council is ready to
proceed to the vote on the draft resolution (S/1996/139)
