OPENING STATEMENT BY THE PRESIDENT

The PRESIDENT (interpretation from French): At the beginning of 1990, on
the threshold of the last decade of the twentieth century, I should like to extend
my best wishes to all the members of the Council and other representatives and to
the Secretary-General and his staff. Céte d'Ivoire began its term of office as a
member of the Security Council on 1 January 1990. At the same time it assumed the
presidency of this important organ of the United Nations. In shouldering this
great responsibility, Céte d'Ivoire is mindful of the need to respect the ideals of
the, Charter of the United Nations.

EXPRESSION OF WELCOME TO THE NEW MEMBERS OF THE SECURITY COUNCIL AND OF THANKS TO
THE OUTGOING MEMBERS OF THE SECURITY COUNCIL

The PRESIDENT (interpretation from French): In presiding over this first
meeting of the Security Council in 1990, I am happy to welcome the other new
members of the Council, Cuba, Democratic Yemen, Romania and Zaire. The Council is
fortunate to be able to draw upon the knowledge and experience of their
representatives.

I also take this opportunity to pay tribute to the outgoing members of the
Council - Algeria, Brazil, Nepal, Senegal and Yugoslavia. On behalf of the Council
I express heartfelt gratitude to them for their valuable contributions to the work
of the Council over the past two years. The representatives of those States have
spared no effort to find positive solutions to the complex problems before the
Council, in a spirit of understanding and sincere collaboration. I express our
deep appreciation to the delegations of those States, and especially to their
Permanent Representatives to the United Nations, Their Excellencies
Ambassadors Hocine Djoudi, Paulo Nogueira-Batista, Jai Pratap Rana,

Absa Claude Diallo and Dragoslav Pejic. I wish them every success in their future

activities, and I hope that the Council will be able to call on them when necessary.

EXPRESSION OF THANKS TO THE RETIRING PRESIDENT

The PRESIDENT (interpretation from French): I should also like to take
this opportunity to pay a well-deserved tribute, on behalf of the Council, to
His Excellency Dr. Enrique Pefialosa, Permanent Representative of Colombia to the
United Nations, for the competence he displayed as President of the Security
Council for the month of December 1989. TI take pleasure in mentioning here his
great generosity in providing his successor with all the logistical support
necessary to ensure the success of our deliberations. On behalf of the members of
the Council, I express our gratitude to Ambassador Pefialosa.
