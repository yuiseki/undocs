Official communiqué of the 5934th (closed) meeting of the
Security Council
Held in private at Headquarters, New York, on Wednesday,
16 July 2008, at 10 a.m.
In accordance with rule 55 of the provisional rules of procedure of the Security
Council, the following communiqué was issued through the Secretary-General in
place of a verbatim record:
“On 16 July 2008, the Security Council, pursuant to resolution 1353
(2001), annex II, sections A and B, held its 5934th meeting in private with the
troop-contributing countries to the African Union-United Nations Hybrid
Operation in Darfur.
“The Security Council and the troop-contributing countries heard a
briefing under rule 39 of its provisional rules of procedure by Major General
Per-Arne Five, Acting Military Adviser of the Department of Peacekeeping
Operations.
“Members of the Council, Major General Five and representatives of
participating troop-contributing countries had an exchange of views.”
