LETTER DATED 29 JUNE 1999 FROM THE SECRETARY-GENERAL
ADDRESSED TO THE PRESIDENT OF THE SECURITY COUNCIL

I have the honour to refer to your letter of yesterday’s date (S/1999/728)
concurring with my proposal to appoint Ms. Patricia McGowan Wald as a judge of
the International Tribunal for the Former Yugoslavia. I have received a
corresponding letter from the President of the General Assembly. Accordingly, I
have appointed Ms. Wald as a judge of the International Tribunal, effective
17 November 1999, for the remainder of the term of office of Judge McDonald,
which term will expire on 16 November 2001.

(Signed)
