LETTER DATED 2 NOVEMBER 1999 FROM THE SECRETARY-GENERAL
ADDRESSED TO THE PRESIDENT OF THE SECURITY COUNCIL

As you are aware, as my Special Envoy in Africa, Mohammed Sahnoun has been
actively engaged, in collaboration with the Organization of African Unity (OAU)
and the United States of America, in efforts to help resolve the conflict
between Eritrea and Ethiopia. Those efforts helped to facilitate the conclusion
of the OAU Framework Agreement and the modalities for its implementation, both
of which were accepted by the two parties. The Technical Arrangements, which
were prepared in Algiers by OAU, the United States of America and the United
Nations with Mr. Sahnoun’s active participation, to assist further the
implementation of the Framework Agreement were accepted by Eritrea.
Clarification was provided to the questions raised by Ethiopia on some of the
provisions of the Technical Arrangements and efforts are continuing to obtain
Ethiopia’s acceptance. Mr. Sahnoun is fully involved in those efforts.
Moreover, I have decided to ask Mr. Sahnoun also to follow developments in
other countries in the Horn of Africa subregion, especially Somalia and the
Sudan, and to represent me in meetings on the two countries of the
Intergovernmental Authority on Development (IEAD) and the IEAD Partners Forum.
For these reasons, I have decided to extend the appointment of Mr. Sahnoun
as my Adviser on Africa until 31 December 2000.
I should be grateful if you would bring the present letter to the attention
of the members of the Security Council.

(Signed)
