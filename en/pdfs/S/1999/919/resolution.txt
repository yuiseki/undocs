LETTER DATED 27 AUGUST 1999 FROM THE PRESIDENT OF THE
SECURITY COUNCIL ADDRESSED TO THE SECRETARY-GENERAL

I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 24 August 1999
(S/1999/918) concerning your proposal to add Croatia, the Czech Republic,
Denmark, France, Indonesia, Nepal, Norway, Sweden, Thailand, the United Republic
of Tanzania and Uruguay to the list of Member States contributing military
personnel to the United Nations Observer Mission in Sierra Leone has been
brought to the attention of the members of the Council. They agree with the
proposal contained in your letter.

(Signed) Martin ANDJABA
President of the Security Council
