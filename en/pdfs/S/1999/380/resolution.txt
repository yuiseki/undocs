LETTER DATED 5 APRIL 1999 FROM THE PRESIDENT OF THE
SECURITY COUNCIL ADDRESSED TO THE SECRETARY-GENERAL

I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 1 April 1999
(S/1999/379) concerning your decision to appoint Mr. Moustapha Niasse (Senegal)
as your Special Envoy for the Democratic Republic of the Congo Peace Process has
been brought to the attention of the members of the Security Council. They
agree with the decision contained in your letter and take note of the
information contained therein.

(Signed) Alain DEJAMMET
President of the Security Council
