LETTER DATED 21 JUNE 1999 FROM THE SECRETARY-GENERAL ADDRESSED TO
THE PRESIDENT OF THE SECURITY COUNCIL

I have the honour to refer to the military liaison component of the United
Nations Mission in East Timor (UNAMET).
It is my intention to appoint Brigadier Rezaqui Haider of Bangladesh to the
post of Chief Military Liaison Officer of UNAMET.
I should be grateful if you would bring this matter to the attention of the
members of the Security Council.

(Signed)
