LETTER DATED 25 MAY 1999 FROM THE PRESIDENT OF THE
SECURITY COUNCIL ADDRESSED TO THE SECRETARY-GENERAL

I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 21 May 1999
(S/1999/602) concerning your decision to appoint Mr. Ian Martin (United Kingdom)
as your Special Representative for the East Timor Popular Consultation has been
brought to the attention of the members of the Council. They have taken note of
the decision.

(Signed) Denis DANGUE RÉWAKA
