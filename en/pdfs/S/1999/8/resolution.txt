NOTE BY THE PRESIDENT OF THE SECURITY COUNCIL

1.
Pursuant to paragraph 4 (b) of the note by the President of the Security
Council dated 30 October 1998 (S/1998/1016), and following consultations among
the members of the Council under the no-objection procedure, the members of the
Council agreed to elect the Chairmen and Vice-Chairmen of the following
Sanctions Committees for a period running until 31 December 1999:

concerning the situation between Iraq and Kuwait

Vice-Chairmen:

Argentina and Gabon

concerning the Libyan Arab Jamahiriya

concerning Somalia

Security Council Committee established pursuant to resolution 864 (1993)
concerning Angola

Robert R. Fowler (Canada)
