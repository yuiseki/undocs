LETTER DATED 23 DECEMBER 1999 FROM THE PRESIDENT OF THE
SECURITY COUNCIL ADDRESSED TO THE SECRETARY-GENERAL

I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 21 December 1999
(S/1999/1286), concerning your decision to appoint Mr. Matthew Nimetz (United
States of America) to replace Mr. Cyrus Vance as your Personal Envoy, has been
brought to the attention of the members of the Security Council. They take note
of the decision contained in your letter.

(Signed) Jeremy GREENSTOCK
President of the Security Council
