NOTE BY THE PRESIDENT OF THE SECURITY COUNCIL

Pursuant to paragraph 4 (b) of the note by the President of the Security
Council dated 30 October 1998 (S/1998/1016) and further to the note by the
President dated 5 January 1999 (S/1999/8), following consultations among the
members of the Council under the no-objection procedure, the members of the
Council agreed to elect Mr. Gelson Fonseca Jr. as Chairman of the Security
Council Committee established pursuant to resolution 1160 (1998), to succeed
Mr. Celso L. N. Amorim, for a term ending on 31 December 1999.
