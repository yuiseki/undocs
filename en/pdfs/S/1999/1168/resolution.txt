LETTER DATED 15 NOVEMBER 1999 FROM THE PRESIDENT OF THE
SECURITY COUNCIL ADDRESSED TO THE SECRETARY-GENERAL

I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 9 November 1999
(S/1999/1167) regarding your intention to appoint Major General Seth Kofi Obeng
of Ghana to the post of Force Commander of the United Nations Interim Force in
Lebanon (UNIFIL) has been brought to the attention of the members of the
Security Council. They agree with the intention contained in your letter.

(Signed) Danilo TÜRK
