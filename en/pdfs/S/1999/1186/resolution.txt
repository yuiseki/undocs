LETTER DATED 16 NOVEMBER 1999 FROM THE SECRETARY-GENERAL
ADDRESSED TO THE PRESIDENT OF THE SECURITY COUNCIL

I have the honour to inform you that, having completed the usual
consultations, it is my intention to appoint Mr. Oluyemi Adeniji (Nigeria) as my
Special Representative for Sierra Leone and Head of the United Nations Mission
in Sierra Leone (UNAMSIL).
It is envisaged that Mr. Adeniji will take up his responsibilities shortly.
I wish to take this opportunity to place on record my deep appreciation to my
former Special Representative, Mr. Francis Okelo, for his dedication in carrying
out his duties in Sierra Leone under extremely difficult circumstances.
I should be grateful if you would bring this matter to the attention of the
members of the Security Council.

(Signed)
