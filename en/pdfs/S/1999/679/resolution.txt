LETTER DATED 11 JUNE 1999 FROM THE SECRETARY-GENERAL
ADDRESSED TO THE PRESIDENT OF THE SECURITY COUNCIL

I have the honour to refer to the civilian police component of the United
Nations Mission in East Timor (UNAMET).
It is my intention to appoint Alan James Mills of Australia to the post of
Civilian Police Commissioner of UNAMET.
I should be grateful if you would bring this matter to the attention of the
members of the Security Council.

(Signed)
