LETTER DATED 26 OCTOBER 1999 FROM THE PRESIDENT OF THE
SECURITY COUNCIL ADDRESSED TO THE SECRETARY-GENERAL

I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 25 October 1999
(S/1999/1093) concerning your intention to appoint Sergio Vieira de Mello as
your Special Representative and Head of the United Nations Transitional
Administration in East Timor (UNTAET) has been brought to the attention of the
members of the Security Council. They take note of the intention contained in
your letter.

(Signed) Sergey V. LAVROV
President of the Security Council
