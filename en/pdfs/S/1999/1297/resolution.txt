LETTER DATED 30 DECEMBER 1999 FROM THE PRESIDENT OF THE
SECURITY COUNCIL ADDRESSED TO THE SECRETARY-GENERAL

I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 29 December 1999
(S/1999/1296) concerning your decision to appoint Mr. Berhanu Dinka as your
Special Representative for the Great Lakes Region has been brought to the
attention of the members of the Security Council. They take note of the
decision contained in your letter.

(Signed) Jeremy GREENSTOCK
President of the Security Council
