LETTER DATED 10 NOVEMBER 1999 FROM THE PRESIDENT OF THE
SECURITY COUNCIL ADDRESSED TO THE SECRETARY-GENERAL

I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 5 November 1999
(S/1999/1154) regarding your intention to appoint General John Augustine Vize of
Ireland as the next Force Commander of the United Nations Iraq-Kuwait
Observation Mission (UNIKOM) has been brought to the attention of the members of
the Security Council. They agree with the intention contained in your letter.

(Signed) Danilo Türk
President of the Security Council
