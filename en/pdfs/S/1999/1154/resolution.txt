LETTER DATED 5 NOVEMBER 1999 FROM THE SECRETARY-GENERAL
ADDRESSED TO THE PRESIDENT OF THE SECURITY COUNCIL

I have the honour to refer to the United Nations Iraq-Kuwait Observation
Mission (UNIKOM).
Major-General Esa Karlevo Tarvainen (Finland), who has been the Force
Commander of UNIKOM since 1 December 1997, will be relinquishing his post on
30 November 1999. Following consultations with the parties concerned, it is my
intention to appoint General John Augustine Vize of Ireland as the next Force
Commander of UNIKOM.
I should be grateful if you would bring this matter to the attention of the
members of the Security Council.

(Signed)
