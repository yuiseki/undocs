LETTER DATED 9 NOVEMBER 1999 FROM THE SECRETARY-GENERAL
ADDRESSED TO THE PRESIDENT OF THE SECURITY COUNCIL

I have the honour to refer to the command of the United Nations Interim
Force in Lebanon (UNIFIL).
Following the usual consultations, it is my intention to appoint
Major General Seth Kofi Obeng of Ghana to the post of Force Commander of UNIFIL.
He will replace Major General Jioje Konousi Konrote of Fiji, who completed his
tour of duty on 30 September 1999.
I should be grateful if you would bring this matter to the attention of the
members of the Security Council.

(Signed)
