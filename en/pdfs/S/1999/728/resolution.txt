LETTER DATED 28 JUNE 1999 FROM THE PRESIDENT OF THE
SECURITY COUNCIL ADDRESSED TO THE SECRETARY-GENERAL

I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 21 June 1999
(S/1999/727) concerning the composition of the International Tribunal for the
Prosecution of Persons Responsible for Serious Violations of International
Humanitarian Law Committed in the Territory of the Former Yugoslavia since 1991
has been brought to the attention of the members of the Security Council.
Having consulted those members, I support your intention to appoint
Ms. Patricia McGowan Wald as a judge of the Chambers of the International
Tribunal.
(Signed)
