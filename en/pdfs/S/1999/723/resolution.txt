LETTER DATED 25 JUNE 1999 FROM THE PRESIDENT OF THE
SECURITY COUNCIL ADDRESSED TO THE SECRETARY-GENERAL

I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 20 June 1999
(S/1999/722) concerning your intention to appoint Ms. Ann Hercus as your
resident Special Representative and Chief of the United Nations Operation in
Cyprus, with effect from 1 July 1999, has been brought to the attention of the
members of the Security Council. They take note of the intention contained in
your letter.

(Signed)
