LETTER DATED 8 DECEMBER 1999 FROM THE PRESIDENT OF THE
SECURITY COUNCIL ADDRESSED TO THE SECRETARY-GENERAL

I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 9 November 1999,
concerning the Office of the United Nations Special Coordinator for the Middle
East Peace Process and your Personal Representative to the Palestine Liberation
Organization and the Palestinian Authority, has been brought to the attention of
the members of the Security Council. They take note of your request to the
Special Coordinator to reconfigure the existing United Nations office based in
Gaza, bearing in mind the resources likely to be required.

(Signed) Jeremy GREENSTOCK
President of the Security Council
