LETTER DATED 6 DECEMBER 1999 FROM THE SECRETARY-GENERAL
ADDRESSED TO THE PRESIDENT OF THE SECURITY COUNCIL

I have the honour to refer to the United Nations Peacekeeping Force in
Cyprus (UNFICYP).
Major General Evergisto Arturo de Vergara (Argentina), who has been the
Commander of UNFICYP since 1 March 1997, will relinquish his post on
15 December 1999, and I have decided to appoint Major General Victory Rana
(Nepal) as the next Commander of UNFICYP.
I should be grateful if you would convey this information to the members of
the Security Council.

(Signed)
