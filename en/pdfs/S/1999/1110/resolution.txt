LETTER DATED 1 NOVEMBER 1999 FROM THE PRESIDENT OF THE
SECURITY COUNCIL ADDRESSED TO THE SECRETARY-GENERAL

I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 28 October 1999
(S/1999/1109) regarding your intention to appoint Brigadier-General Claude Buze
(Belgium) to succeed Brigadier-General Bernd S. Lubenik (Austria) as the Force
Commander of the United Nations Mission for the Referendum in Western Sahara
(MINURSO) has been brought to the attention of the members of the Council. They
agree with the intention contained in your letter.

(Signed) Danilo TÜRK
President of the Security Council
