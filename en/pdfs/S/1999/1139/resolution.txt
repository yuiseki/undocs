LETTER DATED 5 NOVEMBER 1999 FROM THE PRESIDENT OF
THE SECURITY COUNCIL TO THE SECRETARY-GENERAL

I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 2 November 1999
(S/1999/1138) concerning your decision to extend the appointment of
Ayité Jean-Claude Kpakpo (Benin) as Senior United Nations Adviser to the
Facilitator of the Burundi peace process until June 2000 has been brought to the
attention of the members of the Security Council. They take note of the
decision contained in your letter.

(Signed) Danilo TÜRK
President of the Security Council
