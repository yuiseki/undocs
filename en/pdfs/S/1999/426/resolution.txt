LETTER DATED 15 APRIL 1999 FROM THE PRESIDENT OF THE
SECURITY COUNCIL ADDRESSED TO THE SECRETARY-GENERAL

I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 12 April 1999
(S/1999/425) concerning your decision to upgrade the level of the United Nations
Office in Burundi by appointing Mr. Cheikh Tidiane Sy, the current head of the
Office, as your Representative in Burundi has been brought to the attention of
the members of the Council. They take note of the decision contained in your
letter.

(Signed)
