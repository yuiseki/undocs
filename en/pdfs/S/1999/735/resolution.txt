LETTER DATED 25 JUNE 1999 FROM THE SECRETARY-GENERAL ADDRESSED
TO THE PRESIDENT OF THE SECURITY COUNCIL

I have the honour to refer to the civilian police component of the United
Nations Mission in East Timor (UNAMET).
Following the usual consultations, I propose that the civilian police
component of UNAMET be composed of the countries in the attached list (see
annex).
I should be grateful if you could bring this matter to the attention of the
members of the Security Council.

(Signed)
