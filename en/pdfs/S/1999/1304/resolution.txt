LETTER DATED 20 DECEMBER 1999 FROM THE SECRETARY-GENERAL
ADDRESSED TO THE PRESIDENT OF THE SECURITY COUNCIL

I have the honour to refer to article 13, paragraph 3, of the statute of
the International Tribunal for the Prosecution of Persons Responsible for
Serious Violations of International Humanitarian Law Committed in the Territory
of the Former Yugoslavia since 1991, which reads as follows:
"In the event of a vacancy in the Chambers, after consultation with
the Presidents of the Security Council and of the General Assembly, the
Secretary-General shall appoint a person meeting the qualifications of
paragraph 1 above, for the remainder of the term of office concerned."
In this connection, I should like to recall that Judge Antonio Cassese, a
national of Italy, has resigned as a judge of the International Tribunal,
effective 1 February 2000 or as soon thereafter as the Kupreskic and others
trial is completed.
The Government of Italy has presented the candidacy of Mr. Fausto Pocar to
replace Judge Cassese. Please find attached his curriculum vitae (see annex).
It is my view that Mr. Pocar meets the qualifications which are prescribed
in article 13, paragraph 1, of the statute of the International Tribunal. I
also consider that Mr. Pocar’s appointment would be well calculated to ensure
adequate representation on the Tribunal of the principal legal systems of the
world, as contemplated in article 13, paragraph 2 (c), of the statute of the
Tribunal.
I should therefore like to proceed to consultation with you on the
appointment of Mr. Pocar, in accordance with article 13, paragraph 3, of the
statute of the International Tribunal. I look forward to receiving your views
on the matter.
