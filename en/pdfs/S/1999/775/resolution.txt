LETTER DATED 12 JULY 1999 FROM THE PRESIDENT OF THE
SECURITY COUNCIL ADDRESSED TO THE SECRETARY-GENERAL

I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 8 July 1999
(S/1999/774) concerning your intention to appoint Jacques Paul Klein (United
States of America) as your Special Representative and Coordinator of United
Nations Operations in Bosnia and Herzegovina has been brought to the attention
of the members of the Council. They agree with the intention contained in your
letter. They also take this opportunity to join you in expressing deep
appreciation for the exemplary way in which Elisabeth Rehn has carried out her
important assignment.

(Signed) HASMY Agam
President of the Security Council
