LETTER DATED 16 SEPTEMBER 1999 FROM THE PRESIDENT
OF THE SECURITY COUNCIL TO THE SECRETARY-GENERAL

I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 10 September 1999
(S/1999/983) concerning your intention to appoint Mr. Terje Roed-Larsen (Norway)
as United Nations Special Coordinator for the Middle East Peace Process and your
Personal Representative to the Palestine Liberation Organization and the
Palestinian Authority has been brought to the attention of the members of the
Security Council. They take note of the intention contained in your letter.

(Signed) Peter van WALSUM
President of the Security Council
