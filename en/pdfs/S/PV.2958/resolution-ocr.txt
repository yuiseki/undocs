OFFICIAL COMMUNIQUE OF THE 2958TH MEETING OF THE
SECURITY COUNCIL

Held in private in the Security Council Chamber at Headquarters,
on Friday, 23 November 1990, at 12 noon

; In accordance with rule 55 of the provisional rules of procedure of the
Security Council, the following communiqué was issued by the Secretary-General and
is circulated in place of a verbatim record.

"At its 2958th meeting, held in private on 23 November 19930, the Security
Council considered its draft report to the General Assembly covering the

period from 16 June 1989 to 15 June 1990. The Security Council adopted the
draft report unanimously."
