Official communiqué of the 5506th (closed) meeting
of the Security Council
Held in private at Headquarters, New York, on Tuesday,
8 August 2006, at 10 a.m.
In accordance with rule 55 of the provisional rules of procedure of the Security
Council, the following communiqué was issued through the Secretary-General in
place of a verbatim record:
“On 8 August 2006, the Security Council, pursuant to annex II, sections
A and B of resolution 1353 (2001), held its 5506th meeting in private with the
troop-contributing countries to the United Nations Stabilization Mission in
Haiti (MINUSTAH).
“The Security Council and the troop-contributing countries heard a
briefing under rule 39 of its provisional rules of procedure by Mr. Edmond
Mulet, Special Representative of the Secretary-General and Head of the United
Nations Stabilization Mission in Haiti.
“Members of the Council, Mr. Mulet and representatives of participating
troop-contributing countries had a constructive exchange of views.”
