The meeting was called to order at 10.15 a.m.
Adoption of the agenda
The agenda was adopted.

It is my understanding that the Security Council
is ready to proceed to the vote on the draft resolution
before it. Unless I hear any objection, I shall put the
draft resolution to the vote now.
There being no objection, it is so decided.

The situation concerning the Democratic Republic of
the Congo
The President: The Security Council will now
begin its consideration of the item on its agenda. The
Security Council is meeting in accordance with the
understanding reached in its prior consultations.
Members of the Council have before them
document S/2010/597, which contains the text of a
draft resolution submitted by France.
I wish to draw the attention of members to the
text of a letter dated 15 November 2010 from the Chair
of the Security Council Committee established
pursuant to resolution 1533 (2004) concerning the
Democratic Republic of the Congo, transmitting the
final report of the Group of Experts on the Democratic
Republic of the Congo established pursuant to
resolution 1533 (2004), which will be issued as
document S/2010/596.

2

A vote was taken by show of hands.
In favour:
Austria, Bosnia and Herzegovina, Brazil, China,
France, Gabon, Japan, Lebanon, Mexico, Nigeria,
Russian Federation, Turkey, Uganda, United
Kingdom of Great Britain and Northern Ireland,
United States of America
The President: The draft resolution received
15 votes in favour. The draft resolution has been
adopted unanimously as resolution 1952 (2010).
The Security Council has thus concluded the
present stage of its consideration of the item on its
agenda. The Security Council will remain seized of the
matter.
The meeting rose at 10.20 a.m.
