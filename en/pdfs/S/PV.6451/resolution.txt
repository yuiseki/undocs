The meeting was called to order at 10.20 a.m.
Adoption of the agenda
The agenda was adopted.

It is my understanding that the Council is ready
to proceed to the vote on the draft resolution before it.
Unless I hear any objection, I shall put the draft
resolution to the vote now.
There being no objection, it is so decided.

The situation in Burundi
Seventh report of the Secretary-General on the
United Nations Integrated Office in Burundi
(S/2010/608)
The President: Members of the Council have
before them document S/2010/640, containing the text
of a draft resolution submitted by France, Gabon,
Japan, Lebanon, the United Kingdom of Great Britain
and Northern Ireland, and the United States of
America.
Members of the Council also have before them
document S/2010/608, containing the seventh report of
the Secretary-General on the United Nations Integrated
Office in Burundi.

A vote was taken by show of hands.
In favour:
Austria, Bosnia and Herzegovina, Brazil, China,
France, Gabon, Japan, Lebanon, Mexico, Nigeria,
Russian Federation, Turkey, Uganda, United
Kingdom of Great Britain and Northern Ireland,
United States of America
The President: There were 15 votes in favour.
The draft resolution has been adopted unanimously as
resolution 1959 (2010).
The Security Council has thus concluded the
present stage of its consideration of the item on its
agenda.
The meeting rose at 10.25 a.m.
