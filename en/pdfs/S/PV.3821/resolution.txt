The situation concerning Western Sahara
A vote was taken by show of hands.
Report of the Secretary-General on the situation
concerning Western Sahara (S/1997/742 and Add.1)
The President: The Security Council will now begin
its consideration of the item on its agenda. The Security
Council is meeting in accordance with the understanding
reached in its prior consultations.
Members of the Council have before them the report
of the Secretary-General on the situation concerning
Western Sahara, documents S/1997/742 and addendum 1.
Members of the Council also have before them
document S/1997/751, which contains the text of a draft
resolution prepared in the course of the Council’s prior
consultations.

In favour:
Chile, China, Costa Rica, Egypt, France, GuineaBissau, Japan, Kenya, Poland, Portugal, Republic of
Korea, Russian Federation, Sweden, United
Kingdom of Great Britain and Northern Ireland,
United States of America
The President: There were 15 votes in favour. The
draft resolution has been adopted unanimously as
resolution 1131 (1997).
The Security Council has thus concluded the present
stage of its consideration of the item on its agenda.
The meeting rose at 12.50 p.m.
