NOTE BY THE PRESIDENT OF THE SECURITY COUNCIL
At the 2667th meeting of the Security Council, held on 21 March 1986, in
connection with the consideration of the item entitled "The situation between Iran
and Iraq", the President of lhe Security Council made the following statementr
"On behalf of the members of the Security council, I am authorized to
make the following declaration:
'The members of the security Council, seized with the ~ontinuing
conflict between Iran and Iraq, have considered the report of the mission
of specialists dispatched by the Secretary-General to investigate
alleqations of the use of chemical weapons in the conflict between the
tslamic Republic of Iran and Iraq (S/17911 and Add.l).
'Profoundly concerned by the unanimous conclusion of the specialists
that chemical weapons on many occasions have been .used by Iraqi forces
against Iranian forces, most recently in the course of the present
Iranian offensive into Iraqi territory, the members of the Council
strongly condemn this continued use of chemical weapons in clear
violation of the Geneva Protocol of 1925 which prohihits the use in war
of chemical weapons.
'They recall the statements by the President of the Security Council
of 30 March 1984 (S/16454) and 2~ April 1985 (S/17130), and demand again
that the provisions of the Geneva Protocol be strictly observed.
'At the same time the members of the Council condemn the
prolongation of the conflict which continues to take a heavy toll of
human lives and to cause considerable material damage as well as to
endanger peace and security in the region.
'They express concern over the risk of an ~xtension of the conflict
to other States in the reqion and call upon the two sides to respect the
territorial integrity of all States, including those that are not parties
to the hostilities.
