Letter dated 13 December 2006 from the President of the
Security Council to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 8 December 2006
(S/2006/974) concerning your recommendation that the mandate of the United
Nations Peacebuilding Support Office in Guinea-Bissau be extended to
31 December 2007 has been brought to the attention of the members of the Security
Council. They take note of the information and proposal contained in your letter.
(Signed) Nassir Abdulaziz Al-Nasser
President of the Security Council
