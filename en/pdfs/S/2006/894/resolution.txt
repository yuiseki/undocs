Letter dated 10 November 2006 from the Secretary-General
addressed to the President of the Security Council
I have the honour to refer to the command of the United Nations Truce
Supervision Organization (UNTSO).
Following the usual consultations, it is now my intention to appoint Major
General Ian Campbell Gordon of Australia as the Chief of Staff of UNTSO. General
Gordon will replace Major General Clive Lilley, who will relinquish the post on
30 November 2006.
I should be grateful if you would bring this matter to the attention of the
members of the Security Council.
(Signed) Kofi A. Annan
