Letter dated 17 May 2006 from the Secretary-General
addressed to the President of the Security Council
I have the honour to refer to resolution 1665 (2006) concerning the Sudan,
adopted by the Security Council on 29 March 2006. The Council decided in
paragraph 1 of that resolution to extend until 29 September 2006 the mandate of the
Panel of Experts appointed pursuant to resolution 1591 (2005) and requested me to
take the necessary administrative measures.
I therefore wish to inform you that I have appointed the following four experts
until 29 September 2006:
Thomas W. Bifwoli (Kenya)
Debi P. Dash (India)
Marc Lavergne (France)
Bernard S. Saunders (Canada)
I should be grateful if you would bring this information to the attention of the
members of the Security Council.
(Signed) Kofi A. Annan
