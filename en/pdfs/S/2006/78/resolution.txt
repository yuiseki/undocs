Note by the President of the Security Council
In order to familiarize all Member States with efforts made by the Security
Council in recent years to increase the effectiveness and transparency of its
operations, the members of the Council asked the Secretariat to update the 2002
descriptive index of notes and statements by the President of the Security Council
relating to documentation and procedure (S/2002/1000, annex). Accordingly, the
Secretariat has prepared an updated index which is reproduced in the annex to the
present note.
