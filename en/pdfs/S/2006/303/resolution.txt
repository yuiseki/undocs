Letter dated 15 May 2006 from the Secretary-General
addressed to the President of the Security Council
I have the honour to refer to the United Nations Stabilization Mission in Haiti
(MINUSTAH) established on 1 June 2004 pursuant to Security Council resolution
1542 (2004).
Following the usual consultations, it is my intention to appoint Mr. Edmond
Mulet (Guatemala) as my Special Representative in Haiti and Head of MINUSTAH.
He will replace Mr. Juan Valdés (Chile), who will complete his assignment on
31 May 2006. I would like to take this opportunity to express my appreciation to
Mr. Valdés for his dedication and effective leadership of MINUSTAH.
I should be grateful if you would bring this matter to the attention of the
members of the Security Council.
(Signed) Kofi A. Annan
