Letter dated 23 June 2006 from the Secretary-General
addressed to the President of the Security Council
I have the honour to refer to Security Council resolution 1689 (2006)
concerning Liberia. In paragraph 5 of the resolution, the Council requested me to
renew the mandate of the Panel of Experts re-established according to paragraph 9
of resolution 1647 (2005) for an additional six months.
Accordingly, I wish to inform you that I have re-appointed the following
persons to serve on the Panel of Experts for a period ending 21 December 2006:
1.

Mr. Arthur Gregory Blundell (Canada)

2.

Mr. Damien Callamand (France)

3.

Mr. Caspar Fithen (United Kingdom of Great Britain and Northern
Ireland)

4.

Mr. Tommy Garnett (Sierra Leone)

5.

Mr. Rajiva Bhushan Sinha (India)

I have also designated Mr. Arthur Blundell to serve as the Chairman of the
Panel of Experts.
I should be grateful if you would bring this information to the attention of the
members of the Security Council.
(Signed) Kofi A. Annan
