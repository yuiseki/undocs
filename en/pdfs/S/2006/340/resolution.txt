Letter dated 26 May 2006 from the President of the
Security Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that the letter dated 23 May 2006
(S/2006/339), whereby you announce your intention to appoint Mr. Francis C.
Record to the post of commissioner of the United Nations Monitoring, Verification
and Inspection Commission, has been brought to the attention of the members of the
Security Council, who have taken note of the intention expressed in your letter.
(Signed) Basile Ikouebe
President of the Security Council
