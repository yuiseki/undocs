Letter dated 19 December from the President of the Security
Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 14 December 2006
(S/2006/998) concerning your intention to extend until 15 June 2007 the
appointment of Mr. Serge Brammertz as the Commissioner of the United Nations
International Independent Investigation Commission into the assassination of former
Lebanese Prime Minister Rafik Hariri established pursuant to resolution 1595
(2005) has been brought to the attention of the members of the Security Council.
They take note of the intention expressed in your letter.
(Signed) Nassir Abdulaziz Al-Nasser
President of the Security Council
