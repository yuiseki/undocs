Letter dated 29 November 2006 from the President of the
Security Council to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 30 October 2006
(S/2006/923) concerning your intention to appoint Mr. Atul Khare (India) as your
Special Representative for Timor-Leste and Head of the United Nations Integrated
Mission in Timor-Leste (UNMIT) has been brought to the attention of the members
of the Security Council. They take note of the information and intention contained
in your letter.
(Signed) Jorge Voto-Bernales
President of the Security Council
