Letter dated 6 November 2006 from the President of the
Security Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 1 November 2006
(S/2006/866), concerning your intention to extend the deployment of the remaining
two infantry battalions and supporting military units of the United Nations
Operation in Burundi for a few weeks, until 31 December 2006, has been brought to
the attention of the members of the Security Council. They take note of the
information and intention contained in your letter.
(Signed) Jorge Voto-Bernales
President of the Security Council
