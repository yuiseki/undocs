Letter dated 11 April 2006 from the President of the
Security Council to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 7 April 2006
(S/2006/235) concerning your intention to appoint Major-General Mohammad Taisir
Masadeh (Jordan) as Force Commander of the United Nations Mission in Ethiopia
and Eritrea (UNMEE) has been brought to the attention of the members of the
Security Council. They take note of the intention expressed in your letter.
(Signed) Wang Guangya
President of the Security Council
