Letter dated 13 April 2006 from the President of the
Security Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 11 April 2006
(S/2006/242) concerning your intention, as requested by the Security Council in its
resolution 1603 (2005), to appoint Gérard Stoudmann (Switzerland) to the post of
High Representative for the elections in Côte d’Ivoire has been brought to the
attention of the members of the Council. They take note of the intention expressed
in your letter.
(Signed) Wang Guangya
President of the Security Council
