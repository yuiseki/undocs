Letter dated 9 February 2006 from the President of the
Security Council to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 6 February 2006
(S/2006/91) concerning your intention to appoint Major General Rafael José Barni
(Argentina) as Force Commander of the United Nations Peacekeeping Force in
Cyprus (UNFICYP) has been brought to the attention of the members of the
Security Council. They take note of the intention expressed in your letter.
(Signed) John R. Bolton
President of the Security Council
