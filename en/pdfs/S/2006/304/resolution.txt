Letter dated 18 May 2006 from the President of the
Security Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 15 May 2006
(S/2006/303) concerning your proposal to appoint Mr. Edmond Mulet (Guatemala)
as Special Representative of the Secretary-General and Head of the United Nations
Stabilization Mission in Haiti has been brought to the attention of the members of
the Security Council, who have taken due note of the proposal.
(Signed) Basile Ikouebe
