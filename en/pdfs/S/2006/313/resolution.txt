Letter dated 22 May 2006 from the Secretary-General addressed
to the President of the Security Council
I have the honour to draw your attention to resolution 1676 (2006) concerning
Somalia, adopted by the Security Council on 10 May 2006. In paragraph 3 of that
resolution, the Council requested me, in consultation with the Committee
established pursuant to resolution 751 (1992) on the situation in Somalia, to
re-establish within thirty days from the date of the adoption of resolution 1676
(2006), and for a period of six months, the Monitoring Group referred to in
paragraph 3 of resolution 1558 (2004), with the mandate to carry out the tasks set
forth in paragraphs 3 (a) to 3 (i) of resolution 1676 (2006).
I should like to inform you, therefore, that, following consultations with the
Committee, I have appointed the following experts:
Bruno Schiemsky (Belgium)
Melvin E. Holt, Jr. (United States of America)
Harjit Singh Kelley (Kenya)
Joel Salek (Colombia)
I have also appointed Mr. Bruno Schiemsky Chairman of the Monitoring
Group.
I should be grateful if you would bring this information to the attention of the
members of the Security Council.
(Signed) Kofi A. Annan
