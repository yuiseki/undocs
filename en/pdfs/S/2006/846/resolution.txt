Note by the President of the Security Council
Following consultations among the members of the Security Council, the
members agreed to elect the Chairman of the following committee for the period
ending 31 December 2006:
Security Council Committee established pursuant to resolution 1533 (2004)
concerning the Democratic Republic of the Congo
Jorge Voto-Bernales (Peru)
Japan will continue to serve as Vice-Chairman for the said period.
