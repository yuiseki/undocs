Letter dated 20 January 2006 from the President of the
Security Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 18 January 2006
(S/2006/32) concerning your intention to appoint Lieutenant General José Elito
Carvalho de Siqueira (Brazil) as Force Commander of the United Nations
Stabilization Mission in Haiti (MINUSTAH) has been brought to the attention of the
members of the Security Council. They take note of the intention expressed in your
letter.
(Signed) Augustine P. Mahiga
President of the Security Council
