Letter dated 5 October 2006 from the President of the
Security Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 2 October 2006
(S/2006/790) concerning your intention to designate Shola Omoregie (Nigeria) as
your Representative in Guinea-Bissau and Head of the United Nations
Peacebuilding Support Office in Guinea-Bissau (UNOGBIS) has been brought to the
attention of the members of the Security Council. They take note of the intention
expressed in your letter.
(Signed) Kenzo Oshima
President of the Security Council
