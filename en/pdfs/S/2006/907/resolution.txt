Letter dated 17 November 2006 from the Secretary-General
addressed to the President of the Security Council
I have the honour to refer to Security Council resolution 1284 (1999), which
requires me to consult the Council and the Executive Chairman of the United
Nations Monitoring, Verification and Inspection Commission (UNMOVIC) on the
appointment of suitably qualified experts as UNMOVIC Commissioners.
Mr. Francis C. Record has resigned from the College of Commissioners and I
would like to propose the appointment of Mr. Robert Witajewski.
I should be grateful if you would bring this matter to the attention of the
members of the Security Council.
(Signed) Kofi A. Annan
