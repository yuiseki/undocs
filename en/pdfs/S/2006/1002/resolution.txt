Letter dated 15 December 2006 from the Secretary-General
addressed to the President of the Security Council
As the Security Council prepares to undertake the comprehensive review of
the Counter-Terrorism Committee Executive Directorate foreseen in its presidential
statement of 21 December 2005 (S/PRST/2005/64), I wish to follow up on my letter
to the Security Council of 13 December 2005 (S/2005/817) in which I indicated my
intention to review the reporting lines of the Counter-Terrorism Committee
Executive Directorate.
This effort is aimed at better reflecting the spirit of Security Council resolution
1535 (2004) and the above-mentioned presidential statement, in which “the Security
Council recalled that the mandate of the Counter-Terrorism Committee Executive
Directorate flows from that of the Counter-Terrorism Committee and reaffirmed that
the Committee has the sole responsibility for providing policy guidance to the
Counter-Terrorism Committee Executive Directorate”. At the same time, this
exercise also reaffirms that responsibility for the managerial and administrative
oversight of the Executive Directorate remains vested in me, to be discharged
through customary Secretariat channels.
Since the Council is due to complete its comprehensive review by
31 December 2006, I should like to address the process whereby the Executive
Directorate submits its semi-annual comprehensive reports and its programmes of
work to the Counter-Terrorism Committee through me, in accordance with the report
of the Committee on its revitalization (see S/2004/124, annex), subsequently
endorsed by the Security Council in its resolution 1535 (2004). Since the past and
projected activities of the Executive Directorate must necessarily reflect the policy
guidance provided by the Committee itself, the Security Council might wish to
consider adopting an approach whereby the Executive Directorate submits those
documents directly to the Counter-Terrorism Committee.
(Signed) Kofi A. Annan
