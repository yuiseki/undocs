Letter dated 13 February 2006 from the President of the
Security Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 8 February 2006
(S/2006/100) concerning your intention to add India to the list of countries that have
agreed to provide military personnel to the United Nations Disengagement Observer
Force has been brought to the attention of the members of the Security Council.
They take note of the intention expressed in your letter.
(Signed) John R. Bolton
President of the Security Council
