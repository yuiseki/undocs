Letter dated 18 April 2006 from the President of the
Security Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 13 April 2006
(S/2006/245) concerning your intention to add China to the list of countries that
have agreed to provide military personnel to the United Nations Interim Force in
Lebanon has been brought to the attention of the members of the Security Council.
They take note of the intention expressed in your letter.
(Signed) Wang Guangya
President of the Security Council
