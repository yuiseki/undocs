Note by the President of the Security Council
At the 5403rd meeting of the Security Council held on 29 March 2006, in
connection with the item “Non-proliferation”, the President of the Council made a
statement on behalf of the Council (S/PRST/2006/15).
In the last paragraph of the statement, the Security Council “requests in 30
days a report from the Director General of the IAEA on the process of Iranian
compliance with the steps required by the IAEA Board, to the IAEA Board of
Governors and in parallel to the Security Council for its consideration”.
Accordingly, the President hereby circulates the report received today from the
International Atomic Energy Agency (IAEA) (see annex).

Letter dated 28 April 2006 from the Director General of

As requested in the statement by the President of the Security Council of
29 March 2006 (S/PRST/2006/15), I enclose herewith my report on the process of
Iranian compliance with the steps required by the Board of Governors of the
International Atomic Energy Agency in connection with the implementation of the
Safeguards Agreement of the Treaty on the Non-Proliferation of Nuclear Weapons
in the Islamic Republic of Iran (see enclosure).
I should be grateful if you would bring the present letter and its annex to the
attention of all members of the Security Council.
(Signed) Mohamed ElBaradei
