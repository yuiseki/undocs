Letter dated 4 July 2006 from the Permanent Representative
of Japan to the United Nations addressed to the President of
the Security Council
Upon instructions from my Government, I have the honour to request an
immediate meeting of the Security Council to consider the launch of ballistic
missiles or unidentified flying vehicles by the Democratic People’s Republic of
Korea.
(Signed) Kenzo Oshima
Permanent Representative of Japan to the United Nations
