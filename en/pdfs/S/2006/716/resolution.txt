Letter dated 6 September 2006 from the President of the
Security Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 30 August 2006
(S/2006/715) concerning your intention to appoint Major General Fernand Marcel
Amoussou (Benin) as the Force Commander of the United Nations Operation in
Côte d’Ivoire has been brought to the attention of the members of the Security
Council. They take note of the intention expressed in your letter.
(Signed) Adamantios Th. Vassilakis
President of the Security Council
