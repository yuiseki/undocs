Letter dated 24 August 2015 from the President of the
Security Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 20 August 2015
(S/2015/660) concerning your request for the United Nations Interim Force in
Lebanon demining team to remain in Cyprus under the current arrangement until
December 2015 has been brought to the attention of the members of the Security
Council. They take note of the information contained in your letter and the request
expressed therein.
(Signed) U. Joy Ogwu
President of the Security Council
