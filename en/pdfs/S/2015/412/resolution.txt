Letter dated 5 June 2015 from the President of the Security
Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 3 June 2015
(S/2015/411) concerning your intention to appoint Major General Didier L’Hôte
(France) as Force Commander of the United Nations Operation in Côte d’Ivoire has
been brought to the attention of the members of the Security Council. They take
note of the intention expressed in your letter.
(Signed) Ramlan Bin Ibrahim
President of the Security Council
