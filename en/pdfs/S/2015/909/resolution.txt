Letter dated 20 November 2015 from the Secretary-General
addressed to the President of the Security Council
The Security Council, in the presidential statement of 14 January 2015
(S/PRST/2015/2), requested me to provide a briefing by December 2015 on further
United Nations peacebuilding efforts in the aftermath of conf lict, including progress
towards increasing the participation of women in peacebuilding, taking into
consideration the views of the Peacebuilding Commission.
I had intended to brief the Security Council in December, after the conclusion
of the 2015 review of the peacebuilding architecture. It has, however, become clear
that the intergovernmental phase of the review, which is being co -facilitated by the
Permanent Representative of Angola to the United Nations, Ismael Abraão Gaspar
Martins, and the Permanent Representative of Australia to the United Nations,
Gillian Bird, will take longer than originally anticipated.
I would, therefore, like to request a postponement of the consideration of this
item in the programme of work of the Security Council. The Pe acebuilding Support
Office will work closely with the members of the Council to reschedule the briefing
as soon as the review has been concluded.
I thank you for your consideration.
(Signed) BAN Ki-moon
