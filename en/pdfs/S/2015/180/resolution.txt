Letter dated 12 March 2015 from the Secretary-General addressed
to the President of the Security Council
I have the honour to refer to Security Council resolution 2200 (2015), adopted
on 12 February 2015, by which the Council decided to extend unti l 12 March 2016
the mandate of the Panel of Experts originally appointed pursuant to resolution 1591
(2005), and requested that I should take the necessary administrative measures.
Accordingly, I wish to inform you that, after consulting with the Security
Council Committee established pursuant to resolution 1591 (2005) concerning the
Sudan, I have appointed the following experts:
– Ms. Dakshinie Ruwanthika Gunaratne, international humanitarian law
(Sri Lanka)
– Mr. Issa Maraut, regional issues (France)
– Mr. Guido Potters, aviation (the Netherlands)
– Mr. Abhai Srivastav, finance (India)
– Mr. Adrian Wilkinson, weapons (United Kingdom of Great Britain and
Northern Ireland).
I have designated Mr. Srivastav to serve as Coordinator of the Panel of
Experts.
I should be grateful if you would bring this letter to the attention of the
members of the Security Council.
(Signed) BAN Ki-moon
