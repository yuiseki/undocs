Letter dated 6 March 2015 from the President of the
Security Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 4 March 2015
(S/2015/166) concerning your intention to appoint Major General Michael
Lollesgaard (Denmark) as Force Commander of the United Nations
Multidimensional Integrated Stabilization Mission in Mali has been brought to the
attention of the members of the Security Council. They take note of t he intention
expressed in your letter.
(Signed) François Delattre
President of the Security Council
