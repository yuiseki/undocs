Letter dated 3 February 2015 from the President of the
Security Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 30 January 2015
(S/2015/88) concerning your intention to appoint Mr. Nickolay Mladenov (Bulgaria)
as the United Nations Special Coordinator for the Middle East Peace Process and
your Personal Representative to the Palestine Liberation Organization and the
Palestinian Authority has been brought to the attention of the members of the
Security Council. They take note of the intention expressed in your letter.
(Signed) Liu Jieyi
President of the Security Council
