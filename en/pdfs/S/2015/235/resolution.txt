Letter dated 2 April 2015 from the President of the
Security Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 31 March 2015
(S/2015/234) concerning your intention to increase the strength of the guard unit in
Mogadishu to a total of 530 personnel has been brought to the attention of the
members of the Security Council. They take note of the information contained in
your letter and the intention expressed therein.
(Signed) Dina Kawar
President of the Security Council
