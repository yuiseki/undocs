Letter dated 21 July 2015 from the President of the Security
Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 16 July 2015
(S/2015/554) concerning your recommendation to extend the mandate of the United
Nations Regional Office for Central Africa for a period of three years, fr om
31 August 2015 until 31 August 2018, has been brought to the attention of the
members of the Security Council. They take note of the information contained in
your letter and the recommendation expressed therein.
(Signed) Gerard Jacobus van Bohemen
President of the Security Council
