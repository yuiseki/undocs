Letter dated 26 October 2015 from the President of the
Security Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 22 October 2015
(S/2015/817) concerning your intention, as well as that of the Chair person of the
African Union Commission Nkosazana Dlamini-Zuma, to appoint Martin Ihoeghian
Uhomoibhi (Nigeria) as your Joint Special Representative for Darfur and Head of
the African Union-United Nations Hybrid Operation in Darfur (UNAMID) has been
brought to the attention of the members of the Security Council. They take note o f
the intention expressed in your letter.
(Signed) Román Oyarzun Marchesi
President of the Security Council
