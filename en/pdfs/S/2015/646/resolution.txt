Letter dated 14 August 2015 from the Secretary-General
addressed to the President of the Security Council
I have the honour to refer to the United Nations Interim Administration Mission
in Kosovo (UNMIK), established by Security Council resolution 1244 (1999).
Following the usual consultations, I would like to inform you of my intention
to appoint Mr. Zahir Tanin (Afghanistan) as my Special Representative in Kosovo
and Head of UNMIK. Mr. Tanin will succeed Mr. Farid Zarif (Afghanistan), who
will complete his assignment on 31 August 2015. I would like to take this
opportunity to express my gratitude to Mr. Zarif for his dedication and effective
leadership of UNMIK.
I would be grateful if you would bring the present letter to the attention of the
members of the Security Council.
(Signed) BAN Ki-moon
