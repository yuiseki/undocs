Letter dated 25 April 2015 from the President of the Security Council
addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 23 April 2015
(S/2015/283) concerning your intention to appoint Ismail Ould Cheikh Ahmed
(Mauritania) as your Special Envoy for Yemen at the level of Under-SecretaryGeneral has been brought to the attention of the members of the Security Council.
They take note of the intention expressed in your letter.
(Signed) Dina Kawar
President of the Security Council
