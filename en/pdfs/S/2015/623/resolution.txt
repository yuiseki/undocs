Letter dated 12 August 2015 from the President of the
Security Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 10 August 2015
(S/2015/622) concerning your intention to appoint Farid Zarif (Afghanistan) as your
Special Representative for Liberia and Head of the United Nation s Mission in
Liberia has been brought to the attention of the members of the Security Council.
They take note of the intention expressed in your letter.
(Signed) U. Joy Ogwu
President of the Security Council
