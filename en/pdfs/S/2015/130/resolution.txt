Letter dated 23 February 2015 from the President of the
Security Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 19 February 2015
(S/2015/129) concerning your intention to appoint Mr. Ján Kubiš (Slovakia) as your
Special Representative for Iraq and Head of the United Nations Assistance Mission
for Iraq has been brought to the attention of the members of the Security Council.
They take note of the intention expressed in your letter.
(Signed) Liu Jieyi
President of the Security Council
