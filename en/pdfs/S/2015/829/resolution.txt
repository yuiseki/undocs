Letter dated 28 October 2015 from the Secretary-General
addressed to the President of the Security Council
I have the honour to refer to the United Nations Support Mission in Libya
(UNSMIL), established by Security Council resolution 2009 (2011), and extended
through resolutions 2040 (2012), 2095 (2013), 2144 (2014), 2208 (2015), 2213
(2015) and 2238 (2015).
I would like to inform you of my intention to appoint Martin Kobler
(Germany) as my new Special Representative and Head of UNSMIL. He will
succeed Bernardino León (Spain), who has served as my Special Representative
since September 2014. I would like to take this opportunity to express my deep
gratitude to Mr. León for his dedication and effective leadership of UNSMIL.
I would be grateful if you could bring this matter to the attention of the
members of the Security Council.
(Signed) BAN Ki-moon
