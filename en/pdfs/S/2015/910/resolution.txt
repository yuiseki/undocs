Letter dated 24 November 2015 from the President of the
Security Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 20 November 2015
(S/2015/909) concerning your request for a postponement of your briefing to the
Security Council on further United Nations peacebuilding eff orts in the aftermath of
conflict, including progress towards increasing the participation of women in
peacebuilding, taking into consideration the views of the Peacebuilding Commission
has been brought to the attention of the members of the Council. They take note of
the information contained in your letter and the request for postponement made
therein.
(Signed) Matthew Rycroft
President of the Security Council
