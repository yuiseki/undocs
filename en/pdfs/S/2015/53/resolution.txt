Letter dated 26 January 2015 from the President of the
Security Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 21 January 2015
(S/2015/52) concerning your intention to appoint Mr. Haile Tilahun Gebremariam
(Ethiopia) as your Head of Mission for the United Nations Interim Security Force
for Abyei (UNISFA) has been brought to the attention of the members of the
Security Council. They take note of the intention expressed in your letter.
(Signed) Cristián Barros Melet
President of the Security Council
