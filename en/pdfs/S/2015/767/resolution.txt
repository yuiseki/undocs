Letter dated 7 October 2015 from the President of the
Security Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 5 October 2015
(S/2015/766) concerning your intention to appoint Maman Sambo Sidikou (Niger)
as your Special Representative for the Democratic Republic of the Congo and Head
of the United Nations Organization Stabilization Mission in the Democratic
Republic of the Congo (MONUSCO) has been brought to the attention of the
members of the Security Council. They take note of the intention expressed in your
letter.
(Signed) Román Oyarzun Marchesi
President of the Security Council
