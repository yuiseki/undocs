Letter dated 13 January 2015 from the Secretary-General
addressed to the President of the Security Council
I have the honour to refer to the United Nations Disengagement Observer
Force (UNDOF), which was established by the Security Council in its r esolution
350 (1974) and most recently extended by the Council in its resolution 2192 (2014).
Following the usual consultations, I should like to inform you of my intention
to appoint Major General Purna Chandra Thapa (Nepal) as the Head of Mission and
Force Commander of UNDOF. He succeeds Lieutenant General Iqbal Singh Singha
(India), who completed his assignment on 2 January 2015. I should like to take this
opportunity to express my gratitude to Lieutenant General Singha for his dedication
and effective leadership of UNDOF.
I should be grateful if you would bring the present letter to the attention of the
members of the Security Council.
(Signed) BAN Ki-moon
