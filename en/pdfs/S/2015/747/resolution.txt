Letter dated 30 September 2015 from the President of the
Security Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 28 September 2015
(S/2015/746) concerning your intention to appoint Lieutenant-General Ajax Porto
Pinheiro (Brazil) as Force Commander of the United Nations Stabilization Mission
in Haiti (MINUSTAH) has been brought to the attention of the members of the
Security Council. They take note of the intention expressed in your letter.
(Signed) Vitaly Churkin
President of the Security Council
