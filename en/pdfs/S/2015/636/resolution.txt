Letter dated 14 August 2015 from the President of the
Security Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 13 August 2015
(S/2015/635) concerning your intention to appoint Parfait Onanga -Anyanga
(Gabon) as your Acting Special Representative and Head of the United Nations
Multidimensional Integrated Stabilization Mission in the Central African Republic
has been brought to the attention of the members of the Security Council. They take
note of the intention expressed in your letter.
(Signed) U. Joy Ogwu
President of the Security Council
