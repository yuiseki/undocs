Letter dated 11 December 2015 from the Secretary-General
addressed to the President of the Security Council
I have the honour to refer to the United Nations Multidimensional Integrated
Stabilization Mission in Mali (MINUSMA), established by the Security Council in
resolution 2100 (2013) of 25 April 2013 and most recently extended by the Council
in resolution 2227 (2015) of 29 June 2015.
Following the usual consultations, I would like to inform you of my intention
to appoint Mr. Mahamat Saleh Annadif (Chad) as my Special Representative for
Mali and Head of MINUSMA. Mr. Annadif succeeds Mr. Mongi Hamdi (Tunisia),
who will complete his assignment on 14 January 2016. I would like to take this
opportunity to express my gratitude to Mr. Hamdi for his dedication and effective
leadership of MINUSMA.
I would be grateful if you could bring the present letter to the atten tion of the
members of the Security Council.
(Signed) BAN Ki-moon
