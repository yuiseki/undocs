Letter dated 17 March 2015 from the President of the
Security Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 13 March 2015
(S/2015/188) concerning your intention to appoint Mr. Petko Draganov (Bulgaria)
as your Special Representative and Head of the United Nations Regional Centre for
Preventive Diplomacy for Central Asia in Ashgabat has been brought to the
attention of the members of the Security Council. They take note of the intention
expressed in your letter.
(Signed) François Delattre
President of the Security Council
