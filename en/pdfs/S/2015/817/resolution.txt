Letter dated 22 October 2015 from the Secretary-General
addressed to the President of the Security Council
I have the honour to refer to the African Union-United Nations Hybrid
Operation in Darfur (UNAMID). Following the usual consultations, it is my
intention, as well as that of the Chairperson of the African Union Commission,
Nkosazana Dlamini-Zuma, to appoint Martin Ihoeghian Uhomoibhi (Nigeria) as the
African Union-United Nations Joint Special Representative for Darfur and Head of
UNAMID, with responsibilities including those of African Union -United Nations
Joint Chief Mediator.
Mr. Uhomoibhi succeeds Abiodun Oluremi Bashua (Nigeria), to whom
Ms. Dlamini-Zuma and I wish to reiterate our deep appreciation for his dedicated
service during his tenure as Acting Joint Special Representative and Head of
UNAMID and Joint Chief Mediator ad interim.
I would be grateful if you could bring this matter to the attention of the
members of the Security Council.
(Signed) BAN Ki-moon
