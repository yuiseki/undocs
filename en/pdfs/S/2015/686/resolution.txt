Letter dated 3 September 2015 from the President of the
Security Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 1 September 2015
(S/2015/685) concerning your intention to appoint Major General Muhammad
Tayyab Azam (Pakistan) as Force Commander of the United Nations Miss ion for
the Referendum in Western Sahara (MINURSO) has been brought to the attention of
the members of the Security Council. They take note of the intention expressed in
your letter.
(Signed) Vitaly Churkin
President of the Security Council
