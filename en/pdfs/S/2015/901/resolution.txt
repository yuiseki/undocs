Letter dated 20 November 2015 from the President of the
Security Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 18 November 2015
(S/2015/900) concerning your intention to appoint Michael Keating (United
Kingdom of Great Britain and Northern Ireland) as your Special R epresentative and
Head of the United Nations Assistance Mission in Somalia (UNSOM) has been
brought to the attention of the members of the Security Council. They take note of
the intention expressed in your letter.
(Signed) Matthew Rycroft
President of the Security Council
