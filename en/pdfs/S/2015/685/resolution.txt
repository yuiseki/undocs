Letter dated 1 September 2015 from the Secretary-General
addressed to the President of the Security Council
I have the honour to refer to the United Nations Mission for the Referendum
in Western Sahara (MINURSO), established by the Security Council in resolution
690 (1991) of 29 April 1991 and most recently extended by the Council in
resolution 2218 (2015) of 28 April 2015.
Following the usual consultations, I would like to inform you of my intention
to appoint Major General Muhammad Tayyab Azam (Pakistan) as Force
Commander of MINURSO. He succeeds Major General Imam Edy Mulyono
(Indonesia), who will complete his assignment on 17 September 2015. I would like
to take this opportunity to express my gratitude to Major General Mulyono for his
dedication and effective leadership of the MINURSO military component.
I should be grateful if you would bring this matter to the attention of the
members of the Security Council.
(Signed) BAN Ki-moon
