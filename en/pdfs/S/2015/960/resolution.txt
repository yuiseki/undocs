Letter dated 11 December 2015 from the President of the
Security Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 9 December 2015
(S/2015/959) concerning your intention, as well as that of the African Union
Commission Chairperson, Nkosazana Dlamini-Zuma, to appoint Lieutenant General
Frank Mushyo Kamanzi (Rwanda) as Force Commander of the African Union United Nations Hybrid Operation in Darfur has been brought to the attention of the
members of the Security Council. They take note of the intention expressed in your
letter.
(Signed) Samantha Power
President of the Security Council
