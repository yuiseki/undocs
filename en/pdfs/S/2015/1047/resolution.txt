Letter dated 29 December 2015 from the President of the
Security Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 24 December 2015
(S/2015/1046) concerning your intention to appoint Lieutenant General Derick
Mbuyiselo Mgwebi (South Africa) as Force Commander of the Unite d Nations
Organization Stabilization Mission in the Democratic Republic of the Congo has
been brought to the attention of the members of the Security Council. They take
note of the intention expressed in your letter.
(Signed) Samantha Power
President of the Security Council
