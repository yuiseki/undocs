Letter dated 27 July 2015 from the Secretary-General addressed to
the President of the Security Council
I refer to Security Council resolution 2171 (2014) of 21 August 2014, in which
the Council requested me to submit to it a report on actions I have taken to promote
and strengthen conflict prevention tools within the United Nations system, including
through cooperation with regional and subregional organizations, by 31 August
2015.
On 31 October 2014, I announced the appointment of a High -level
Independent Panel on Peace Operations to comprehensively assess the state of
United Nations peace operations. The Panel issued its report on 16 June 2015, and
made a number of specific recommendations on conflict prevention, calling for
prevention and mediation to be “brought back to the fore” of the United Nations
work. I am currently preparing my response to the Panel’s recomm endations. In
view of this, and to ensure consistency between my reports, I would like to request
an extension, until 30 September 2015, for the submission of my report on conflict
prevention to the Security Council.
(Signed) BAN Ki-moon
