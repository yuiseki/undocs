Letter dated 18 August 2015 from the President of the
Security Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 14 August 2015
(S/2015/646) concerning your intention to appoint Mr. Zahir Tanin (Afghanistan) as
your Special Representative in Kosovo and Head of the United Nations Interim
Administration Mission in Kosovo (UNMIK) has been brought to the attention of
the members of the Security Council. They take note of the intention expressed in
your letter.
(Signed) U. Joy Ogwu
President of the Security Council
