Letter dated 30 January 2015 from the Secretary-General
addressed to the President of the Security Council
Following the usual consultations, I wish to inform you of my intention to
appoint Mr. Nickolay Mladenov (Bulgaria) as the United Nations Spe cial
Coordinator for the Middle East Peace Process and my Personal Representative to
the Palestine Liberation Organization and the Palestinian Authority. He will succeed
Mr. Robert H. Serry (Netherlands), who will complete his assignment on
31 March 2015.
Mr. Mladenov currently serves as my Special Representative for Iraq and Head
of the United Nations Assistance Mission for Iraq. In his capacity as Special
Coordinator for the Middle East Peace Process, he will represent me in all meetings
and structures involving the parties and the international community, and provide
political guidance to the United Nations system. He will also coordinate all United
Nations activities on the ground relating to the Middle East peace process, with the
aim of ensuring that the contribution of the United Nations is fully integrated and
coordinated.
I should be grateful if you would bring the present letter to the attention of the
members of the Security Council.
(Signed) BAN Ki-moon
