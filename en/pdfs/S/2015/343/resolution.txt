Letter dated 15 May 2015 from the Secretary-General addressed
to the President of the Security Council
I refer to the exchange of letters dated 31 March and 2 April 2015 ( S/2015/234
and S/2015/235) with the President of the Security Council pertaining to the
expansion of the United Nations guard unit in Mogadishu.
I am pleased to inform you of the progress made in implementing the decision
of the Security Council. Uganda has agreed to contribute, from newly generated
troops, the additional 120 personnel required for the guard unit, bringing the total
number of personnel deployed to 530. They are expected to begin their deployment
during the course of the current month.
I should be grateful if you would bring the present letter to the attention of the
members of the Security Council.
(Signed) BAN Ki-moon
