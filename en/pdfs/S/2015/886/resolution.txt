Enclosure
Report to the United Nations on the operations of the Kosovo Force
1.
The present report on the Kosovo Force (KFOR) is provided in accordance
with Security Council resolution 1244 (1999) and covers the period from 1 April to
30 September 2015.
2.
As of 30 September 2015, the total number of KFOR troops in theatre
remained approximately 4,600.
3.
The main event during the reporting period was the progress achieved in the
dialogue between Belgrade and Pristina facilitated by the European Un ion, which
resulted in the signing of a significant package of agreements on 25 August
regarding, inter alia, the establishment of the Association/Community of Serb
municipalities in Kosovo.
Security situation and operations
4.
During the reporting period, the security situation in theatre remained calm in
the southern part of Kosovo and reasonably stable in the northern part of Kosovo.
The implementation of the European Union-facilitated First Agreement of Principles
governing the Normalization of Relations, reached by Belgrade and Pristina on
19 April 2013, continues to have a positive effect on the security situation, and
freedom of movement remains unimpeded Kosovo-wide.
5.
Against this positive background, the KFOR mission to contribute to a safe
and secure environment and freedom of movement has continued as part of a
comprehensive international effort.
6.
During the reporting period, KFOR continued to work in close coordination
with the Kosovo police and the European Rule of Law Mission (EULEX). K FOR
remained ready to provide immediate intervention as a third responder if required
and also maintained full situational awareness. However, there was no requirement
for direct intervention by KFOR.
7.
Two significant events occurred without security incidents. The remains of
lsa Boletini were paraded peacefully and finally laid to rest in his hometown of
Boletin/Boljetin. The Serbian celebration of Vidovdan and the 626th anniversary of
the battle of Kosovo were marked with a commemorative service at the Gazimestan
memorial and a religious ceremony at the Graçanicë/Gračanica Monastery. The
Kosovo police were in charge of security for these events and no EULEX or KFOR
intervention was required.
Summary
8.
During the reporting period, KFOR continued to contribute to both
maintaining a safe and secure environment and ensuring freedom of movement, in
close coordination with EULEX and the Kosovo police. The close coordination on
the ground between KFOR, in its capacity as third responder, EULEX and the
Kosovo police continues to be highly effective.
