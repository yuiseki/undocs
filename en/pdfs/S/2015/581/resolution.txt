Letter dated 30 July 2015 from the President of the Security
Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 27 July 2015
(S/2015/580) concerning your report pursuant to resolution 2171 (2014) has been
brought to the attention of the members of the Security Council.
The members of the Council have taken note of the request contained in your
letter that the time frame for the issuance of the report be extended, and they look
forward to the submission of the report by 30 September 2015.
(Signed) Gerard Jacobus van Bohemen
President of the Security Council
