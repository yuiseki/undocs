Letter dated 10 August 2015 from the Secretary-General
addressed to the President of the Security Council
I have the honour to refer to the United Nations Mission in Liberia (UNMIL),
which was established by the Security Council in its resolution 1509 (2003) and
most recently extended by the Council in its resolution 2190 (2014).
Following the usual consultations, I should like to inform you of my intention
to appoint Farid Zarif (Afghanistan) as my Special Representative for Liberia and
Head of UNMIL. He will replace Karin Landgren (Sweden), who completed her
assignment in July 2015. I should like to take this opportunity to express my
gratitude to Ms. Landgren for her contribution to UNMIL and Liberia.
I should be grateful if you would bring this matter to the attention of the
members of the Security Council.
(Signed) BAN Ki-moon
