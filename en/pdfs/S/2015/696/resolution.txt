Letter dated 9 September 2015 from the Secretary-General
addressed to the President of the Security Council
In view of the deliberations by the members of the Security Council with
regard to my letter of 27 August 2015 (S/2015/669) conveying my recommendations
and elements of the terms of reference for an Organization for the Prohibition of
Chemical Weapons-United Nations Joint Investigative Mechanism, pursuant to
Security Council resolution 2235 (2015), I wish to reaffirm the foll owing:
One of the steps to be taken will be to expeditiously consult with the Syrian
Arab Republic regarding a status of mission agreement, which is necessary for the
proper implementation of the mandated activities of the Joint Investigative
Mechanism under resolution 2235 (2015) in the Syrian Arab Republic, including
with respect to privileges, immunities and security.
The Joint Investigative Mechanism shall take due care in making its
determinations regarding whether there are reasonable grounds to bel ieve that
access is justified in accordance with paragraphs 5 and 7 of resolution 2235 (2015),
which also provide that the Syrian Arab Republic and all parties shall provide full
access to all locations, individuals and materials in the Syrian Arab Republ ic that
the Mechanism deems relevant to its investigation.
The trust fund to be established in support of the activities of the Joint
Investigative Mechanism shall be administered in accordance with the
administrative and financial regulations of the United Nations. Voluntary
contributions to the trust fund will be used for the material and technical needs of
the Mechanism.
I should be grateful if you would bring the present letter to the attention of the
members of the Security Council.
(Signed) BAN Ki-moon
