Letter dated 29 May 2015 from the Secretary-General addressed
to the President of the Security Council
I have the honour to refer to Security Council resolution 2219 (2015), by
which the Council decided to extend the mandate of the Group of Expert s on Côte
d’Ivoire until 30 May 2016 and requested me to take the necessary administrative
measures.
Accordingly, I wish to inform you that, after consulting with the Security
Council Committee established pursuant to resolution 1572 (2004) concerning Cô te
d’Ivoire, I have appointed the following experts:
Mr. Roman Brühwiler, customs/transport (Switzerland)
Mr. Raymond Debelle, arms (Belgium)
Ms. Lipika Majumdar Roy Choudhury, finance (India)
Mr. Roberto Sollazzo, diamonds/natural resources (Italy)
Mr. Manuel Vázquez-Boidard, regional issues (Spain)
I have also designated Mr. Vázquez-Boidard to serve as Coordinator of the
Group of Experts.
(Signed) BAN Ki-moon
