Letter dated 13 March 2015 from the Secretary-General addressed
to the President of the Security Council
Following the usual consultations, I wish to inform you of my intention to
appoint Mr. Petko Draganov (Bulgaria) as my Special Representative a nd Head of
the United Nations Regional Centre for Preventive Diplomacy for Central Asia in
Ashgabat.
Mr. Draganov will succeed Mr. Miroslav Jenča (Slovakia).
Mr. Draganov is currently Deputy Secretary-General of the United Nations
Conference on Trade and Development in Geneva. In his new capacity, he will have
overall authority over the activities of the Regional Centre and, in that regard, will
provide leadership, political guidance and high-level operational management. He
will build on the achievements of his predecessor and on the regional and
international efforts of the United Nations.
I should be grateful if you would bring the present letter to the attention of the
members of the Security Council.
(Signed) BAN Ki-moon
