Letter dated 30 January 2015 from the Secretary-General
addressed to the President of the Security Council
I have the honour to inform you that Ms. Jacqueline W. Shire (United States of
America), whom I appointed on 2 July 2014 to serve as nuclear issues/technology
expert on the Panel of Experts established pursuant to resolution 1929 (2010),
resigned from the Panel on 18 December 2014.
Accordingly, in consultation with the Security Council Committee established
pursuant to resolution 1737 (2006), I have appointed Mr. John Christian Kessler
(United States of America) to serve as nuclear issues/technology expert.
I should be grateful if you would bring the present letter to the attention of the
members of the Security Council.
(Signed) BAN Ki-moon
