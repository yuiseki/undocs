Letter dated 8 January 2015 from the President of the
Security Council to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 6 January 2015
(S/2015/12), concerning your intention to appoint Major General Salihu Zaway Uba
(Nigeria) as Force Commander of the United Nations Mission in Liberia (UNMIL),
has been brought to the attention of the members of the Security Council. They take
note of the intention expressed in your letter.
(Signed) Cristián Barros Melet
President of the Security Council
