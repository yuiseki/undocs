Letter dated 27 April 2015 from the Secretary-General addressed
to the President of the Security Council
I have the honour to refer to Security Council resolution 2206 (2015), adopted
on 3 March 2015, by which the Council decided to create the Pane l of Experts on
South Sudan for an initial period of 13 months.
Accordingly, I wish to inform you that, after consulting with the Security
Council Committee established pursuant to resolution 2206 (2015) concerning
South Sudan, I have appointed the follo wing experts:
Mr. Andrews Atta-Asamoah, natural resources/finance (Ghana)
Mr. Payton Knopf, regional issues (United States of America)
Ms. Anna Oosterlinck, humanitarian affairs (Belgium)
Mr. Lucas van de Vondervoort, arms (Netherlands)
Mr. Vladimir Zhagora, armed groups (Belarus).
I have designated Mr. Vladimir Zhagora to serve as Coordinator of the Panel
of Experts.
I should be grateful if you would bring this matter to the attention of the
members of the Security Council.
(Signed) BAN Ki-moon
