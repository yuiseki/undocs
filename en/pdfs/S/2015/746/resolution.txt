Letter dated 28 September 2015 from the Secretary-General
addressed to the President of the Security Council
I have the honour to refer to the United Nations Stabilization Mission in Haiti
(MINUSTAH), established by Security Council resolution 1542 (2004) of 30 April
2004 and most recently extended by resolution 2180 (2014) of 14 October 2014.
Following the usual consultations, I would like to inform you of my intention
to appoint Lieutenant-General Ajax Porto Pinheiro (Brazil) as Force Commander of
MINUSTAH. He succeeds Lieutenant-General José Luiz Jaborandy Junior (Brazil),
who passed away on 30 August 2015. I would like to take this opportunity to
express my appreciation for the contribution made by Lieutenant -General Jaborandy
Junior to the work of the United Nations for peace, including in Haiti.
I should be grateful if you would bring this matter to the a ttention of the
members of the Security Council.
(Signed) BAN Ki-moon
