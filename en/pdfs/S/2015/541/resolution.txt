Letter dated 14 July 2015 from the Secretary-General addressed to
the President of the Security Council
I have the honour to refer to the United Nations Truce Supervision
Organization (UNTSO), established by Security Council resolution 50 (1948).
Following the usual consultations, I would like to inform you of my intention
to appoint Major General Arthur David Gawn (New Zealand) as Head of Mission
and Chief of Staff of UNTSO. Major General Gawn succeeds Major General
Michael Finn (Ireland), who will complete his assignment on 31 July 2015. I would
like to take this opportunity to express my gratitude to Maj or General Finn for his
dedication and effective leadership of UNTSO.
I would be grateful if you could bring this letter to the attention of the
members of the Security Council.
(Signed) BAN Ki-moon
