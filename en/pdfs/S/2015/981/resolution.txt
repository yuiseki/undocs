Letter dated 15 December 2015 from the President of the Security
Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 11 December 2015
(S/2015/980) concerning your intention to appoint Mr. Mahamat Saleh Annadif
(Chad) as your Special Representative for Mali and Head of the United Nations
Multidimensional Integrated Stabilization Mission in Mali (MINUSMA) has been
brought to the attention of the members of the Security Council. They take note of
the intention expressed in your letter.
(Signed) Samantha Power
President of the Security Council
