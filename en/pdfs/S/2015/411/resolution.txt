Letter dated 3 June 2015 from the Secretary-General addressed to
the President of the Security Council
I have the honour to refer to the United Nations Operation in Côte d’Ivoire
(UNOCI), established pursuant to Security Council resolution 1528 (20 04) and most
recently extended by the Council in resolution 2162 (2014).
Following the usual consultations, I would like to inform you of my intention
to appoint Major General Didier L’Hôte (France) as Force Commander of UNOCI.
Major General L’Hôte will replace Major General Hafiz Masroor Ahmed (Pakistan),
who will complete his tour of duty on 30 June 2015. I would like to take this
opportunity to express my gratitude to Major General Hafiz Masroor Ahmed for his
contribution to UNOCI.
I should be grateful if you would bring the present letter to the attention of the
members of the Security Council.
(Signed) BAN Ki-moon
