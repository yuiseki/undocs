Official communiqué of the 6044th (closed) meeting of the
Security Council
Held in private at Headquarters, New York, on Monday,
15 December 2008, at 2 p.m.
In accordance with rule 55 of the provisional rules of procedure of the Security
Council, the following communiqué was issued through the Secretary-General in
place of a verbatim record:
“At its 6044th meeting, held in private on 15 December 2008, the
Security Council considered the item entitled “Peace and security in Africa”.
“The meeting was presided over by His Excellency Mr. Ivo Sanader,
Prime Minister of the Republic of Croatia, in his capacity as the President of
the Security Council.
“The Secretary-General, His Excellency Mr. Ban Ki-moon, made a
statement.
“His Excellency The Right Honourable Mr. David Miliband, M.P.,
Secretary of State for Foreign and Commonwealth Affairs of the United
Kingdom of Great Britain and Northern Ireland, Her Excellency
Ms. Condoleezza Rice, Secretary of State of the United States of America, and
other members of the Council had an exchange of views.”
