Official Communique of the 4210th meeting of the
Security Council
Held in private in the Security Council Chamber at Headquarters,
New York, on Thursday, 26 October 2000, at 3.30 p.m.
At its 4210th meeting, held in private on 26 October 2000, the Security
Council considered the item entitled “The situation concerning Western Sahara”, for
the purpose of a briefing by the Minister for Foreign Affairs and Cooperation of
Morocco.
In accordance with the understanding reached in the Council’s prior
consultations, and with the consent of the Council, the President extended an
invitation to His Excellency Mr. Mohamed Benaissa, Minister for Foreign Affairs
and Cooperation of Morocco.
The members of the Council and His Excellency Mr. Mohamed Benaissa had a
frank discussion.
