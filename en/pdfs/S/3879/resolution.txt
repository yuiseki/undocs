ELECTION OF FIVE MEMBERS OF THE INTElmATIONAL COURT OF JUSTICE
LIST OF' CANDIDAfES NOMINATED BY THE NATIONAL GROUPS
Note by the Secretary-General
In accordance with Article 7 of the Statute of the International Court of
Justice, the Secretary-General has the honour to submit to the General Assembly
and to the Security Council an alphabetical list of the candidates nominated by
national ~roups for the election to be held during the forthcoming regular session
of the General Assembly in order to fill the five vacancies which will occur on
5 February 1958, the date of expiry of the terms of office of Judges Abdel Hamik
Badawi (Egypt), Bohdan Winiarski (Poland), Milovan Zori~ic (Yugoslavia),
John E. Read (Canada) and V.K. Wellington Koo (China).
Curricula vitae of the candidates will be circulated subsequently.
