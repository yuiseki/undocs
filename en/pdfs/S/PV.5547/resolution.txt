Official communiqué of the 5547th (closed) meeting
of the Security Council
Held in private at Headquarters, New York, on Monday,
9 October 2006, at 9.55 a.m.
In accordance with rule 55 of the provisional rules of procedure of the Security
Council, the following communiqué was issued through the Secretary-General in
place of a verbatim record:
“At its 5547th meeting, held in private on 9 October 2006, the Security
Council considered the question of the recommendation for the appointment of
the Secretary-General of the United Nations.
“The Security Council adopted by acclamation the following resolution:
‘The Security Council,
‘Having considered the question of the recommendation for the
appointment of the Secretary-General of the United Nations,
‘Recommends to the General Assembly that Mr. Ban Ki-moon be
appointed Secretary-General of the United Nations for a term of office
from 1 January 2007 to 31 December 2011.’”
