NOTE
Symbols of United Nations documents are composed of capital letters combined with figures. Mention of such a symbol indicates a reference to a United
Nations document.
Documents of the Security Council (symbol S/ . . .) are normally published in
quarterly Supplements of the Official Records of the Security Council. The date
of the document indicates the supplement in which it appears or in which information about it is given.
The resolutions of the Security Council, numbered in accordance with a
system adopted in 1964, are published in yearly volumes of Resolutions and
Decisions of the Security Council. The new system, which has been applied
retroactively to resolutions adopted before 1 January 1965, became fully operative
on that date.

2. The PRESIDENT:
Since our last meeting, members of the Security Council have learned with great
sorrow of the recent passing away of the President of
Botswana, Sir Seretse Khama, whose distinguished
leadership had served the people and the Government of Botswana both before and after that country’s
attainment
of independence.
As a strong supporter
of the United Nations, Sir Seretse Khama is deeply
mourned by the international community.
On behalf of
the Council, I wish to express profound condolences
to the Government
and people of Botswana, and
sincere sympathy to the bereaved family.
