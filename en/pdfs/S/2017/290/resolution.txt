Letter dated 3 April 2017 from the Secretary-General addressed to
the President of the Security Council
I have the honour to refer to the United Nations Mission in the Republic of
South Sudan (UNMISS), established by the Security Council in its resolution 1996
(2011) of 8 July 2011 and most recently extended by the Council in its resolution
2327 (2016) of 16 December 2016.
The post of Force Commander of UNMISS has been vacant since
11 November 2016 and requires urgent replacement. Following the necessary
consultations, I wish to inform you that it is my intention to reassign Lieutenant
General Frank Mushyo Kamanzi of Rwanda, currently the Force Commander of the
African Union-United Nations Hybrid Operation in Darfur, as the Force
Commander of UNMISS.
I should be grateful if you would bring this matter to the attention of the
members of the Security Council.
(Signed) António Guterres
