Letter dated 26 December 2017 from the President of the
Security Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 21 December 2017
(S/2017/1111) concerning your intention to appoint Ms. Leila Zerrougui (Algeria) as
your Special Representative for the Democratic Republic of the C ongo and Head of
the United Nations Organization Stabilization Mission in the Democratic Republic of
the Congo has been brought to the attention of the members of the Security Council.
They take note of the intention expressed therein.
(Signed) Koro Bessho
President of the Security Council
