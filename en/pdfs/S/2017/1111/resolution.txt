Letter dated 21 December 2017 from the Secretary-General
addressed to the President of the Security Council
I have the honour to refer to the United Nations Organization Stabilization
Mission in the Democratic Republic of the Congo (MONUSCO), which was
established by the Security Council in its resolution 1925 (2010) and whose mandate
was most recently extended in resolution 2348 (2017).
Following the usual consultations, I should like to inform you of my intention
to appoint Ms. Leila Zerrougui (Algeria) as my Special Representative for the
Democratic Republic of the Congo and Head of MONUSCO. She succeeds
Mr. Maman Sambo Sidikou (Niger), who will complete his assignment on 31 January
2018. I should like to take this opportunity to express my gratitude to Mr. Sidikou for
his dedication and leadership of MONUSCO.
I should be grateful if you would bring this matter to the attention of the
members of the Security Council.
(Signed) António Guterres
