Letter dated 22 November 2017 from the Secretary-General
addressed to the President of the Security Council
I have the honour to refer to the United Nations Mission for the Referendum in
Western Sahara (MINURSO), which was established by the Security Council in its
resolution 690 (1991) of 29 April 1991 and most recently extended by resolution
2351 (2017) of 28 April 2017.
Following the usual consultations, I would like to inform you of my intention
to appoint Mr. Colin Stewart (Canada) as my Special Representative for Western
Sahara and Head of MINURSO. He succeeds Ms. Kim Bolduc (Canada), who
completes her assignment on 22 November 2017. I would like to take this
opportunity to express my gratitude to Ms. Bolduc for her dedication and effective
leadership of MINURSO.
I should be grateful if you would bring the present letter to the attention of the
members of the Security Council.
(Signed) António Guterres
