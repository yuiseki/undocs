Enclosure
Report to the United Nations on the operations of the Kosovo Force
1.
The present report is submitted in accordance with Security Council resolution
1244 (1999) and covers the activities of the Kosovo Force (KFOR) during the
period from 1 July to 30 September 2017.
2.
As at 15 September 2017, the total number of KFOR troops in theatre remains
approximately 4,100, from 21 North Atlantic Treaty Organization (NATO) and
8 non-NATO troop-contributing countries.
3.
There were no major security incidents during this reporting period and no
requirement for KFOR intervention.
Security situation update and operations
4.
The reporting period brought no significant changes to the overall security
situation in Kosovo, which remains generally stable. Continued political progress is
required to sustain this, in particular through the European Union -facilitated
dialogue between Belgrade and Pristina.
5.
During this reporting period, KFOR conducted normal framework operations
across the area of operations with joint regional detachments maintaining close
contact with the population. With regard to the Austerlitz Bridge refurbishment
works in Mitrovica North, which resumed in mid-July 2017, KFOR maintains its
presence to ensure adequate security measures in the bridge area.
6.
KFOR continues its close cooperation with both the Kosovo Police and the
European Union Rule of Law Mission (EULEX). One example is a multi -faceted
medical evacuation exercise in the area of Mitrovica North in July 2017, organized
by KFOR in close cooperation with EULEX. The exercise successfully tested the
interoperability and operational procedures of KFOR and EULEX in emergency
situations requiring immediate medical evacuation of multiple personnel from an
isolated location.
Summary
7.
KFOR continues to contribute both to maintaining a safe and secure
environment and to ensuring freedom of movement as part of a comprehensive
international effort. The close coordination on the ground between KFOR in its
capacity as third responder, EULEX as second responder and the Kosovo Police as
first responder continues to be effective.
