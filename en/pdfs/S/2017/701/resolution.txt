Letter dated 10 August 2017 from the President of the
Security Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 8 August 2017
(S/2017/700) concerning your intention to appoint Michèle Coninsx (Belgium) as
the new Executive Director of the Counter-Terrorism Committee Executive
Directorate has been brought to the attention of the members of the Security
Council. They take note of the intention expressed in your letter.
(Signed) Amr Abdellatif Aboulatta
President of the Security Council
