Note by the Secretary-General
Addendum

Withdrawal of a candidate nominated by national groups
By a letter dated 14 August 2017, the Permanent Mission of Zambia to the
United Nations informed the Secretary-General of the decision taken by the national
group of Zambia in the Permanent Court of Arbitration, dated 11 August 2017, to
withdraw its nomination of Mr. Chaloka Beyani (Zambia) as a candidate in the
regular election of members of the International Court of Justice to be held during
the seventy-second session of the General Assembly.
