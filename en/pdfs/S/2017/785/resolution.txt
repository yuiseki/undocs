Letter dated 13 September 2017 from the Secretary-General
addressed to the President of the Security Council
I have the honour to refer to the United Nations Regional Centre for
Preventive Diplomacy for Central Asia, mandated through an exchange of letters
(S/2007/279 and S/2007/280) between the Secretary-General and the President of
the Security Council dated 7 May 2007 and 15 May 2007, respectively.
Following the usual consultations, I would like to inform you of my intention
to appoint Ms. Natalia Gherman (Republic of Moldova) as my Special Representative
and Head of the United Nations Regional Centre for Preventive Diplomacy for
Central Asia. She succeeds Mr. Petko Draganov (Bulgaria), who completed his
assignment on 4 September 2017. I would like to take this opportunity to expre ss
my gratitude to Mr. Draganov for his dedication to and effective leadership of the
Centre.
I should be grateful if you would bring this matter to the attention of the
members of the Security Council.
(Signed) António Guterres
