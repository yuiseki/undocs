Letter dated 20 June 2017 from the President of the
Security Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 16 June 2017
(S/2017/527) concerning your intention to appoint Mr. Ghassan Salamé (Lebanon)
as Special Representative for Libya and Head of the United Nations Support
Mission in Libya has been brought to the attention of the members of the Security
Council. They take note of the intention expressed in your letter.
(Signed) Sacha Sergio Llorentty Solíz
President of the Security Council
