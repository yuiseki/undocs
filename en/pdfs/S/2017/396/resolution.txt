Letter dated 3 May 2017 from the Secretary-General addressed to
the President of the Security Council
Following the usual consultations, I wish to inform you of my intention to
appoint Mr. Michel Kafando (Burkina Faso) to serve as my Special Envoy, at the
Under-Secretary-General level, to lead and coordinate the political efforts of the
United Nations in Burundi.
Mr. Kafando will lead and coordinate United Nations support to Burundi, in
particular for the promotion of peace and sustainable development, and to the
political dialogue process under the auspices of the East African Community.
He will succeed Mr. Jamal Benomar (United Kingdom of Great Britain and
Northern Ireland), who has completed his assignment, in undertaking the functions
carried out by the Special Adviser on Conflict Prevention, including in Burundi, as
mandated by the Security Council in its resolutions 2248 (2015), 2279 (2016) and
2303 (2016). I should like to take this opportunity to express my appreciation to
Mr. Benomar for his dedication and commitment in discharging his responsibilities.
I should be grateful if you would bring the present letter to the attention of the
members of the Security Council.
(Signed) António Guterres
