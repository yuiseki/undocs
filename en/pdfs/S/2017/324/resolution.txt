Letter dated 12 April 2017 from the Secretary-General addressed
to the President of the Security Council
I have the honour to refer to Security Council resolution 2342 (2017), adopted
on 23 February 2017, concerning Yemen. By paragraph 5 of that resolution, the
Council decided to extend the mandate of the Panel of Experts on Yemen until
28 March 2018.
Accordingly, I wish to inform you that, after consulting with the Security
Council Committee established pursuant to resolution 2140 (2014), I have appointed
the following armed groups expert:
Mr. Fernando Rosenfeld Carvajal (Mexico)
(Signed) António Guterres
