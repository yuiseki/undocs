Enclosure
Report to the United Nations on Kosovo Force operations
1.
The present report on the Kosovo Force (KFOR) is submitted in
accordance with Security Council resolution 1244 (1999) and covers the period
from 1 October to 31 December 2016.
2.
As at 31 December 2016, the total number of KFOR troops in theatre was
4,150. During the reporting period, there were no major security incidents, and
there was no requirement for direct intervention by KFOR.
Security situation and operations
3.
During the reporting period, there were no major changes to the overall
security situation in Kosovo, which remained stable on the whole. In general,
the trend is gradually growing more positive, as Serbia and Kosovo remain
committed to the dialogue process under the auspices of the European Union.
4.
KFOR continued to work in close cooperation and coordination with the
Kosovo Police and the European Union Rule of Law Mission in Kosovo
(EULEX). One example is the recent Silver Sabre 2016 exercise, which was
jointly executed with other security providers in Kosovo, namely, the Kosovo
Police, EULEX and the Kosovo Security Forces, in Pristina and its vicinity in
November.
5.
As part of its efforts to maintain an effective force posture in all sensitive
areas and to respond immediately if required, KFOR continued to conduct
training and performed operational rehearsal activities in November and
December to verify the readiness of the operational reserve forces.
6.
During the first week of December, KFOR removed defences established
in 2004 at the entrance of the Dević Monastery in the Skenderaj municipality,
following an official request by the nuns living in the monastery. This event is a
clear signal of the improving security situation and the ongoing normalization of
relations between the Serb Orthodox Church and the institutions in Kosovo.
Summary
7.
During the reporting period, KFOR continued to contribute to maintaining
both a safe and secure environment and ensuring freedom of movement as part
of a comprehensive international effort. The close coordination on the ground
between KFOR, in its capacity as third responder, EULEX and the Kosovo
Police continues to be highly effective.
