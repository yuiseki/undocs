Letter dated 10 October 2017 from the Secretary-General
addressed to the President of the Security Council
I have the honour to refer to the United Nations Disengagement Observer
Force (UNDOF), which was established by the Security Council in its resolution
350 (1974) and most recently extended by the Council in its resolution 2361 (2017).
Following the necessary consultations, I would like to inform you of my
intention to appoint Major General Francis Vib-Sanziri (Ghana) as the Head of
Mission and Force Commander of UNDOF. He will succeed Major General Jai
Shanker Menon (India), who completed his assignment on 30 September 2017. I
would like to take this opportunity to express my gratitude to Major General Menon
for his dedication and effective leadership of UNDOF.
I should be grateful if you would bring the present letter to the attenti on of the
members of the Security Council.
(Signed) António Guterres
