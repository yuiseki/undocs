Letter dated 21 April 2017 from the Secretary-General addressed
to the President of the Security Council
I have the honour to refer to the exchange of letters dated 11 September 2015
(S/2015/709) and 15 September 2015 (S/2015/710) between the Secretary-General
and the President of the Security Council on the appointment of Virginia Gamba
(Argentina) as head of the independent, three-person leadership panel of the
Organization for the Prohibition of Chemical Weapons-United Nations Joint
Investigative Mechanism. I have further the honour to refer to Security Council
resolution 2319 (2016), by which the Council decided to renew the mandate of the
Mechanism, as set out in resolution 2235 (2015), for an additional period of one
year.
Further to the appointment of Ms. Gamba as my Special Representative for
Children and Armed Conflict, effective 1 May 2017, following the usual
consultations, and in coordination with the Director -General of the Organization for
the Prohibition of Chemical Weapons, I should like to inform you of my intention to
appoint Mr. Edmond Mulet (Guatemala) as head of the leadership panel of the Joint
Investigative Mechanism. He will assume overall responsibility, with two deputies
responsible for the political and investigative components, respectively. Mr. Mulet
served most recently as Chef de Cabinet to Secretary-General Ban Ki moon.
(Signed) António Guterres
