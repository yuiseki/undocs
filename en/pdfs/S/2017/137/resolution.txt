Letter dated 10 February 2017 from the Secretary-General
addressed to the President of the Security Council
I have the honour to refer to the United Nations Regional Office for Central
Africa (UNOCA), established following an exchange of letters between the
Secretary-General and the President of the Security Council in 2009 and 2010
(S/2009/697 and S/2010/457), the mandate of which was most recently renewed and
amended in 2015 (S/2015/554 and S/2015/555).
I would like to inform you of my intention to appoint Mr. François Louncény
Fall (Guinea) as my Special Representative for Central Africa and Head of UNOCA
for a period of one year, until 28 February 2018. As you are aware, Mr. Fall has
been acting temporarily as my Special Representative for Central Africa and Head
of UNOCA.
I should be grateful if you would bring this matter to the attention of the
members of the Security Council.
(Signed) António Guterres
