Letter dated 31 October 2017 from the President of the
Security Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter of 26 October 2017
(S/2017/919) in which you stated your intention to appoint Ms. Susan D. Page
(United States of America) as your Special Representative and Head of the United
Nations Mission for Justice Support in Haiti (MINUJUSTH) has been brought to the
attention of the members of the Security Council, who have taken due note of it.
The members of the Council have also recalled Article 101, paragraph 3, of the
Charter of the United Nations.
(Signed) François Delattre
President of the Security Council
