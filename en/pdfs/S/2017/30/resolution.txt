Letter dated 6 January 2017 from the Secretary-General addressed
to the President of the Security Council
I have the honour to refer to the African Union-United Nations Hybrid
Operation in Darfur (UNAMID). Following the usual consultations, it is my
intention, as well as that of the Chairperson of the African Union Commission,
Nkosazana Dlamini-Zuma, to appoint Jeremiah Nyamane Kingsley Mamabolo
(South Africa) as Acting Joint Special Representative for Darfur and Head of
UNAMID, with responsibilities including those of African Union -United Nations
Joint Chief Mediator, for an initial period of three months.
Mr. Mamabolo succeeds Martin Ihoeghian Uhomoibhi (Nigeria), to whom
Ms. Dlamini-Zuma and I wish to reiterate our deep appreciation for his service
during his tenure.
I would be grateful if you could bring this matter to the attention of the
members of the Security Council.
(Signed) António Guterres
