Letter dated 4 December 2017 from the President of the
Security Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 30 November 2017
(S/2017/1017) concerning your intention to submit the report on the future funding
of the African Union Mission in Somalia by the end of February 2018, has been
brought to the attention of the members of the Security Council. They take note of
the information contained in your letter and the intention expressed therein.
(Signed) Koro Bessho
President of the Security Council
