Annex
Letter dated 14 December 2017 from the Chair of the Working
Group of the Security Council on Children and Armed Conflict
addressed to the Secretary-General
At its 67th meeting, on 3 August 2017, the Working Group on Children and
Armed Conflict examined the first report of the Secretary-General on children and
armed conflict in Nigeria (S/2017/304), covering the period from January 2013 to
December 2016. At its 68th meeting, on 15 November 2017, the Working Group
adopted its conclusions on children and armed conflict in Nigeria ( S/AC.51/2017/5).
In follow-up to the recommendations of the Working Group and subject to and
consistent with applicable international law and relevant Security Council
resolutions, including resolutions 1612 (2005), 1882 (2009), 1998 (2011), 2068
(2012), 2143 (2014) and 2255 (2015), I am entrusted, in my capacity as Chair of the
Working Group, with:
(a) Inviting you to ensure that the United Nations country task force on
monitoring and reporting strengthens its monitoring and reporting activities;
(b) Requesting you to ensure that the United Nations country task force on
monitoring and reporting continues its ongoing work and advocacy for the release
and reintegration of children associated with armed groups and armed forces and
continues its engagement with the Civilian Joint Task Force to develop and sign an
action plan to end and prevent the recruitment and use of children.
(Signed) Olof Skoog
Chair
Security Council Working Group
