Letter dated 15 September 2017 from the President of the
Security Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 13 September 2017
(S/2017/785) concerning your intention to appoint Ms. Natalia Gherman (Republic
of Moldova) as the new Special Representative and Head of the United Nations
Regional Centre for Preventive Diplomacy for Central Asia has been brought to the
attention of the members of the Security Council. They take note of the intention
expressed in your letter.
(Signed) Tekeda Alemu
President of the Security Council
