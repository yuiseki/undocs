Letter dated 9 June 2017 from the President of the Security Council
addressed to the Secretary-General
The identical letters from the Permanent Representative of Colombia to the
President of the Security Council and the Secretary-General dated 7 June 2017
(S/2017/481) have been brought to the attention of the members of the Security
Council.
The Security Council respectfully requests the Secretary-General to provide
recommendations on how the Security Council could best meet the request of the
Colombian parties for a second special political mission in Colombia.
In the light of the urgency of the request from the Colombian parties, the
Security Council would welcome initial recommendations by 23 June, in
consultation with the parties as necessary.
(Signed) Sacha Sergio Llorentty Solíz
President of the Security Council
