Letter dated 30 May 2017 from the President of the Security Council
addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 25 May 2017
(S/2017/462) concerning your intention to appoint Horst Köhler (Germany) to serve
as your Personal Envoy for Western Sahara has been brought to the attention of the
members of the Security Council. They take note of the intention expressed in your
letter.
(Signed) Elbio Rosselli
President of the Security Council
