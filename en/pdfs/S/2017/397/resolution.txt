Letter dated 4 May 2017 from the President of the Security Council
addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 3 May 2017
(S/2017/396) concerning your intention to appoint Mr. Michel Kafando (Burkina
Faso) to serve as your Special Envoy, at the Under-Secretary-General level, to lead
and coordinate the political efforts of the United Nations in Burundi has been
brought to the attention of the members of the Security Council. They take note of
the intention expressed in your letter.
(Signed) Elbio Rosselli
President of the Security Council
