Letter dated 10 January 2017 from the President of the
Security Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 6 January 2017
(S/2017/30) concerning your intention, as well as that of the African Union
Commission Chairperson, Nkosazana Dlamini-Zuma, to appoint Jeremiah Nyamane
Kingsley Mamabolo (South Africa) as Acting Joint Special Representative for
Darfur and Head of the African Union-United Nations Hybrid Operation in Darfur
(UNAMID), with responsibilities including those of African -Union-United Nations
Joint Chief Mediator, for an initial period of three months, has been brought to the
attention of the members of the Security Council. They take note of the intention
expressed in your letter.
(Signed) Olof Skoog
President of the Security Council
