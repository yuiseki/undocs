Letter dated 5 January 2017 from the President of the
Security Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I am hereby transmitting to you a letter dated 30 December 2016 from the
Chair, for the period ending 31 December 2016, of the Working Group on Children
and Armed Conflict established pursuant to Security Council resolution 1612
(2005), based on the Working Group’s conclusions, adopted on 14 December 2016
(S/AC.51/2016/3) (see annex).
(Signed) Olof Skoog
President of the Security Council

Annex
At its 58th formal meeting, on 17 May 2016, the Working Group on Children
and Armed Conflict examined the third report of the Secretary -General on children
and armed conflict in the Central African Republic ( S/2016/133), covering the
period from January 2011 to December 2015. At its 60th formal meeting, on
14 December 2016, the Working Group adopted its conclusions on children and
armed conflict in the Central African Republic (S/AC.51/2016/3).
In follow-up to the recommendations of the Working Group and subject to and
consistent with applicable international law and relevant Security Council
resolutions, including resolutions 1612 (2005), 1882 (2009), 1998 (2011), 2068
(2012), 2143 (2014) and 2255 (2015), I am entrusted, in my capacity as Chair of the
Working Group, to:
(a) Request you to ensure that the country task force on monitoring and
reporting in the Central African Republic and other relevant United Nations
agencies continue their engagement and support to the Government of the Central
African Republic to address violations and abuses against children in armed
conflict;
(b) Request you to ensure that the country task force o n monitoring and
reporting continues its engagement with parties to conflict, in line with resolution
1612 (2005), to advocate the release and reintegration of children and facilitate the
adoption of action plans to end and prevent violations and abuses against children,
and to encourage the country task force to continue its ongoing dialogue on the
adoption of action plans with Unité du peuple centrafricain (UPC), Rassemblement
patriotique pour le renouveau de la Centrafrique (RPRC) and Front populaire pour
la renaissance de la Centrafrique (FPRC) factions of the ex-Séléka;
(c) Request you to continue ensuring the effectiveness of the monitoring and
reporting mechanism on children and armed conflict in the Central African Republic
and of the child protection component of the United Nations Multidimensional
Integrated Stabilization Mission in the Central African Republic (MINUSCA),
including by ensuring that sufficient child protection capacities are allocat ed to the
Mission;
(d) Welcome the directive issued by the Special Representative of the
Secretary-General to the Central African Republic to the MINUSCA Force
Commander and the Police Commissioner prohibiting the use of schools during the
conduct of their activities and calling for the full implementation of the directive;
(e) Express deep concern over the continuing and serious allegations of
sexual exploitation and abuse by United Nations peacekeepers in MINUSCA and by
non-United Nations forces and request you to ensure the implementation of
resolution 2272 (2016) and the full implementation of the United Nations zero tolerance policy on sexual exploitation and abuse;
(f) Request you to ensure that the matter of children and armed conflict is
included as a specific element in all country-specific reports on the Central African
Republic;
