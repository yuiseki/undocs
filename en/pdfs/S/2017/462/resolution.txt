Letter dated 25 May 2017 from the Secretary-General addressed
to the President of the Security Council
I have the honour to refer to the recent Security Council resolutions on the
question of Western Sahara, in which the Council called on the parties to engage in
negotiations under the auspices of the Secretary-General.
Following the usual consultations, I intend to appoint Horst Köhler of
Germany as my Personal Envoy for Western Sahara. Mr. Köhler will work with the
parties and the neighbouring States, on the basis of Security Council resolution
2351 (2017) and earlier resolutions and taking into account the progress made to
date, in order to achieve a just, durable and mutually acceptable political solution
which will provide for the self-determination of the people of Western Sahara.
I should also like to take this opportunity to thank Christopher Ross, who
served as Personal Envoy of the Secretary-General for Western Sahara from 2009 to
2017. I wish to express my appreciation to him for his dedication as one of my
principal advisers on this important issue.
I should be grateful if you would bring the present letter to the attention of the
members of the Security Council.
(Signed) António Guterres
