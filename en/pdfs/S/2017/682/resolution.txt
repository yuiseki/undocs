Letter dated 4 August 2017 from the President of the
Security Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 2 August 2017
(S/2017/681) concerning your intention, as well as that of the Chairperson of the
African Union Commission, Moussa Faki Mahamat, to appoint Lieutenant General
Leonard Muriuki Ngondi (Kenya) as Force Commander of the African Union United Nations Hybrid Operation in Darfur has been brought to the attention of the
members of the Security Council. They take note of the intention expressed in your
letter.
(Signed) Amr Abdellatif Aboulatta
President of the Security Council
