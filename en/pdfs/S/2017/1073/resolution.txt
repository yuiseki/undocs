Letter dated 18 December 2017 from the President of the
Security Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 13 December 2017
(S/2017/1072) concerning the submission of the terms of reference requested in
paragraph 4 of Security Council resolution 2379 (2017) has been brought to the
attention of the members of the Council.
The members of the Security Council have taken note of the request contained
in your letter that the time frame for the submission of the terms of reference be
extended. They look forward to the submission of the terms of referen ce by
21 December 2017.
(Signed) Koro Bessho
President of the Security Council
