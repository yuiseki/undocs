Letter dated 14 February 2017 from the President of the
Security Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 10 February 2017
(S/2017/137) concerning your intention to appoint Mr. François Louncény Fall
(Guinea) as your Special Representative for Central Africa and Head of th e United
Nations Regional Office for Central Africa for a period of one year, until
28 February 2018, has been brought to the attention of the members of the Security
Council. They take note of the intention expressed in your letter.
(Signed) Volodymyr Yelchenko
President of the Security Council
