Letter dated 11 July 2017 from the Secretary-General addressed to
the President of the Security Council
I have the honour to refer to Security Council resolution 2353 (2017), adopted
by the Council on 24 May 2017, by which the Council decided to extend until
30 June 2018 the mandate of the Panel of Experts as set out in paragraph 12 of
resolution 2290 (2016).
Accordingly, I wish to inform you that, after consulting with the Security
Council Committee established pursuant to resolution 2206 (2015) concerning
South Sudan, I have appointed the following experts:
• Mr. Andrews Atta-Asamoah, regional issues (Ghana)
• Mr. Andrei Kolmakov, natural resources/finance (Russian Federation)
• Ms. Anna Oosterlinck, humanitarian affairs (Belgium)
• Mr. Klem Ryan, arms (New Zealand)
• Mr. Colin Thomas-Jensen, armed groups (United States of America)
I have designated Mr. Klem Ryan to serve as Coordinator of the Panel of
Experts.
I would be grateful if you could bring this matter to the attention of the
members of the Security Council.
(Signed) António Guterres
