Letter dated 2 August 2017 from the Secretary-General addressed
to the President of the Security Council
I have the honour to refer to the African Union-United Nations Hybrid
Operation in Darfur (UNAMID), which was established by the Security Council in
its resolution 1769 (2007). Its mandate was most recently extended in resolution
2363 (2017).
Following the usual consultations, I would like to inform you that it is my
intention, as well as that of the Chairperson of the Afric an Union Commission,
Moussa Faki Mahamat, to appoint Lieutenant General Leonard Muriuki Ngondi
(Kenya) as Force Commander of UNAMID.
Lieutenant General Ngondi succeeds Lieutenant General Frank Mushyo
Kamanzi (Rwanda), who has been appointed as Force Commander of the United
Nations Mission in South Sudan. Mr. Mahamat and I would like to take this
opportunity to express our gratitude to Lieutenant General Kamanzi for his
dedication and effective leadership during his tenure in UNAMID.
I should be grateful if you would bring this matter to the attention of the
members of the Security Council.
(Signed) António Guterres
