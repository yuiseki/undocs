Letter dated 5 April 2017 from the President of the Security Council
addressed to the Secretary-General
In my capacity as President of the Security Council, I am forwarding to you a
letter dated 9 March 2017 from the Chair of the Working Group on Children and
Armed Conflict established pursuant to Security Council resolution 1612 (2005),
based on the Working Group’s conclusions adopted on 14 February 2017
(S/AC.51/2017/1) (see annex).
(Signed) Nikki R. Haley

Annex
At its 61st formal meeting, on 9 January 2017, the Working Group on Children
and Armed Conflict examined the third report of the Secretary -General on children
and armed conflict in Colombia (S/2016/837), covering the period from September
2011 to June 2016. At its 62nd formal meeting, on 14 February 2017, the Working
Group adopted its conclusions on children and armed conflict in Colombia
(S/AC.51/2017/1).
In follow-up to the recommendations of the Working Group and subject to and
consistent with applicable international law and relevant Security Council
resolutions, including resolutions 1612 (2005), 1882 (2009), 1998 (2011), 2068
(2012), 2143 (2014) and 2255 (2015), I am entrusted, in my capacity as Chair of the
Working Group, to:
(a) Request you to ensure the continued effectiveness of the monitoring and
reporting mechanism on children and armed conflict in Colombia, i ncluding for the
full and expeditious implementation of the agreement between the Government and
the Revolutionary Armed Forces of Colombia-People’s Army (FARC-EP) for the
separation and reintegration of children, signed on 15 May 2016;
(b) Request you to encourage the United Nations country task force on
monitoring and reporting and other relevant United Nations entities to continue their
engagement and efforts to support the Government in addressing violations and
abuses committed against children affected by armed conflict, in particular with a
view to engaging the National Liberation Army (ELN) to end and prevent the
recruitment and use of children in violation of international law.
(Signed) Olof Skoog
Chair
Working Group on Children and Armed Conflict
