Letter dated 16 June 2017 from the Secretary-General addressed
to the President of the Security Council
I have the honour to refer to the United Nations Support Mission in Libya
(UNSMIL), which was established by the Security Council in its resolution 2009
(2011). The mandate of UNSMIL has since been extended and modified in
resolutions 2040 (2012), 2095 (2013), 2144 (2014), 2208 (2015), 2213 (2015), 2273
(2016), 2291 (2016) and, most recently, 2323 (2016), by which it was extended until
15 September 2017.
Following the usual consultations, I wish to inform you of my intention to
appoint Mr. Ghassan Salamé (Lebanon) as my Special Representative for Li bya and
Head of UNSMIL. He succeeds Mr. Martin Kobler (Germany), who has served as
Special Representative since 1 November 2015. I should like to take this
opportunity to express my appreciation to Mr. Kobler for his dedication and
effective leadership of UNSMIL.
I should be grateful if you would bring this matter to the attention of the
members of the Security Council.
(Signed) António Guterres
