First report of the Secretary-General pursuant to
paragraph 4 of Security Council resolution 2335 (2016)
I. Introduction
1.
The present report is submitted pursuant to paragraph 4 of Security Council
resolution 2335 (2016), in which the Council requested the Secretary-General to
report on the implementation of the resolution no later than 30 March 2017, with a
final report three months after the transfer of any remaining funds, pursuant to
paragraph 2 of the resolution, to the Government of Iraq, unless otherwise
authorized by the Council.
2.
Pursuant to paragraph 2 of resolution 2335 (2016), the Secretariat continues to
maintain the escrow accounts authorized in paragraphs 3, 4 and 5 of Security
Council resolution 1958 (2010).

II. Administrative escrow account
3.
I should like to inform the members of the Security Council that the activities
undertaken and funded as outlined in paragraph 4 of resolution 1958 (2010) from
1 January to 31 December 2016 have led to expenditure amounting to $657,514 for
the activities related to the orderly termination of the residual activities of the oil for-food programme.

III. Indemnification escrow account
4.
I should like to inform the members of the Security Council that no
expenditures have been incurred to date in respect of the amount referred to in
paragraph 5 of resolution 1958 (2010).
5.
In a letter dated 29 December 2016 addressed to the President of the Security
Council (S/2016/1126) and his fifth report to the Council pursuant to paragraph 8 of
resolution 1958 (2010) (S/2016/497), the Secretary-General indicated that the
United Nations and the Government of Iraq had reached certain understandings on
the indemnification arrangements to be concluded in accordance with paragraph 7
of resolution 1958 (2010). I wish to express my appreciation for the continuous and
