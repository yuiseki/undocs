Letter dated 22 November 2017 from the President of the
Security Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 20 November 2017
(S/2017/983) concerning your intention to appoint Ms. Pernille Dahler Kardel
(Denmark) as Acting United Nations Special Coordinator for Lebanon has been
brought to the attention of the members of the Security Council. They take note of
the intention expressed in your letter.
(Signed) Sebastiano Cardi
President of the Security Council
