Letter dated 30 November 2017 from the President of the
Security Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 22 November 2017
(S/2017/1003) concerning your intention to appoint Mr. Colin Stewart (Canada) as
your Special Representative for Western Sahara and Head of the United Nations
Mission for the Referendum in Western Sahara (MINURSO) has been brought to the
attention of the members of the Security Council. They take note of the intention
expressed in your letter.
Furthermore, the members of the Security Council recalled Article 101,
paragraph 3, of the Charter of the United Nations.
(Signed) Sebastiano Cardi
President of the Security Council
