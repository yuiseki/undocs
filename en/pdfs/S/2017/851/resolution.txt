Letter dated 6 October 2017 from the President of the
Security Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 3 October 2017
(S/2017/850), referring to paragraph 7 of Security Council resolution 2367 (2017),
in which the Council called upon you to conduct, by 15 October 2017, an
independent, external assessment of the structure and staffi ng of the United Nations
Assistance Mission for Iraq, has been brought to the attention of the members of the
Security Council.
The members of the Security Council have taken note of the recommendation
in your letter that the deadline for the completion of the mandated assessment
should be extended, and they look forward to the submission of the report by
15 November 2017.
(Signed) François Delattre
President of the Security Council
