Letter dated 26 October 2017 from the Secretary-General
addressed to the President of the Security Council
I have the honour to refer to the United Nations Mission for Justice Support in
Haiti (MINUJUSTH) established pursuant to Security Council resolution 2350
(2017) on 13 April 2017.
Following the usual consultations, I should like to inform you that I intend to
appoint Ms. Susan D. Page (United States of America) Special Representative and
Head of MINUJUSTH.
I would be grateful if you could bring the present letter to the attention of the
members of the Security Council.
(Signed) António Guterres
