Letter dated 28 February 2017 from the Secretary-General
addressed to the President of the Security Council
I have the honour to refer to the United Nations Multidimensional Integrated
Stabilization Mission in Mali (MINUSMA), established by Security Council
resolution 2100 (2013) of 25 April 2013, and most recently extended by resolution
2295 (2016) of 29 June 2016.
Following the usual consultations, I would like to inform you of my intention
to appoint Major General Jean-Paul Deconinck (Belgium) as Force Commander of
MINUSMA. Major General Deconinck will succeed Major General Michael
Lollesgaard (Denmark), who completed his tour of duty on 31 December 2016.
I should be grateful if you would bring the present letter to the atten tion of the
members of the Security Council.
(Signed) António Guterres
