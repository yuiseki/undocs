Letter dated 25 April 2017 from the President of the Security Council
addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 21 April 2017
(S/2017/354) concerning your intention to appoint Edmond Mulet (Guatemala) as
head of the leadership panel of the Organization for the Prohibition of Chemical
Weapons-United Nations Joint Investigative Mechanism has been brought to the
attention of the members of the Security Council. They take note of the intention
expressed in your letter.
(Signed) Nikki R. Haley
President of the Security Council
