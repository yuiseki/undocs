Letter dated 4 October 2017 from the Secretary-General
addressed to the President of the Security Council
Pursuant to Security Council resolution 2364 (2017), I have the honour to
transmit a note verbale from the Permanent Mission of France to the United
Nations, dated 29 September 2017, conveying a report to the Security Council on
the implementation of the mandate of French troops in Mali.
I should be grateful if you would bring the present letter and its annex to the
attention of the members of the Security Council.
(Signed) António Guterres

Annex
The Permanent Mission of France to the United Nations presents its
compliments to the United Nations Secretariat, Office of the Secretary -General, and
has the honour to convey the information below.
Pursuant to paragraphs 37 and 47 of Security Council resol ution 2364 (2017),
France has the honour to enclose herewith the report on actions undertaken between
1 June and 1 September 2017 by French forces in support of the United Nations
Multidimensional Integrated Stabilization Mission in Mali.
The Permanent Mission of France to the United Nations would be grateful if
the Secretary-General would bring the present report to the attention of the members
of the Security Council.

Enclosure
Seventeenth report on the support provided by French forces
to the United Nations Multidimensional Integrated Stabilization
Mission in Mali between 1 June and 1 September 2017
References:
– Security Council resolution 2364 (2016) of 29 June 2016 (paragraphs 37 and 47)
– Technical cooperation and assistance arrangement between the United Nations
Department of Peacekeeping Operations and the French Ministry of Defence
of 26 March 2015
This seventeenth report since the establishment of the United Nations
Multidimensional Integrated Stabilization Mission in Mali (MINUSMA)
summarizes the activities conducted between 1 June and 1 September 2017 by
French forces in support of MINUSMA.
Since 1 June 2017, no combat action has been conducted in response to an
imminent and serious threat.
