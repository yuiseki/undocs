Letter dated 12 December 2017 from the Secretary-General
addressed to the President of the Security Council
I have the honour to refer to Security Council resolution 2374 (2017), adopted
on 5 September 2017, concerning the situation in Mali. By paragraph 11 of the
resolution, the Council requested me to create, in consultation with the Security
Council Committee established pursuant to resolution 2374 (2017) concerning Mali,
a group of up to five experts (“Panel of Experts”) on Mali, under the direction of the
Committee, for an initial period of 13 months.
Accordingly, I wish to inform you that, after consulting the Committee, I have
appointed the following experts:
Mr. Marc-André Boisvert, regional (Canada)
Mr. Ruben de Koning, finance (Netherlands)
Mr. Aurélien Llorca, armed groups (France)
Ms. Holo Makwaia, humanitarian affairs (United Republic of Tanzania)
I have also designated Mr. de Koning to serve as Coordinator of the Panel of
Experts.
I should be grateful if you would bring this matter to the att ention of the
members of the Security Council.
(Signed) António Guterres
