Final report of the Secretary-General pursuant to
paragraph 4 of Security Council resolution 2335 (2016)
I. Introduction
1.
The present report is submitted pursuant to paragraph 4 of Security Council
resolution 2335 (2016), in which the Council requested the Secretary-General to
provide a final report three months after the transfer, pursuant to paragraph 2 of the
resolution, of any funds remaining in the escrow accounts authorized in
paragraphs 3, 4 and 5 of resolution 1958 (2010) to the Government of Iraq, unless
otherwise authorized by the Council.

II. Administrative escrow account
2.
The activities undertaken and funded as outlined in paragraph 4 of resolution
1958 (2010) from 1 January to 30 September 2017 have led to expenditure
amounting to $591,208 for the activities relating to the orderly termination of the
residual activities of the oil-for-food programme.
3.
In accordance with paragraph 2 of resolution 2335 (2016), the remaining funds
in the administrative escrow account, in the amount of $14,283,565, have been
transferred to the Government of Iraq.

III. Indemnification escrow account
4.
No expenditure has been incurred in respect of the amount referred to in
paragraph 5 of resolution 1958 (2010).
5.
In accordance with paragraph 2 of resolution 2335 (2016), the remaining funds
in the indemnification escrow account, in the amount of $138,424,160, including
interest, have been transferred to the Government of Iraq.

IV. Conclusion of implementing arrangements
6.
By paragraph 7 of resolution 1958 (2010), the Secretary-General was
requested to enter into all implementing arrangements with the Government of Iraq
necessary to provide, in particular, appropriate indemnification for the benefit of the
United Nations, its representatives, agents and independent contractors, with regard
to all activities in connection with the oil-for-food programme since its inception. In
