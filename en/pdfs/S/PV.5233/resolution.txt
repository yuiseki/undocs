Official communiqué of the 5233rd (closed) meeting of the
Security Council
Held in private at Headquarters, New York, on Monday,
25 July 2005, at 3 p.m.
In accordance with rule 55 of the provisional rules of procedure of the Security
Council, the following communiqué was issued through the Secretary-General in
place of a verbatim record:
“On 25 July 2005, the Security Council, pursuant to annex II, sections A
and B of resolution 1353 (2001), held its 5233rd meeting in private with the
troop-contributing countries to the United Nations Interim Force in Lebanon
(UNIFIL).
“The Security Council and the troop-contributing countries heard a
statement by Ms. Lisa Buttenheim, Director of the Asia and Middle East
Division of the Department of Peacekeeping Operations, and a briefing by
Major General Alain Pellegrini, Force Commander of the United Nations
Interim Force in Lebanon, under rule 39 of the Council’s provisional rules of
procedure.
“Members of the Council, Ms. Buttenheim, Major General Pellegrini and
representatives of participating troop-contributing countries had a constructive
exchange of views.”
