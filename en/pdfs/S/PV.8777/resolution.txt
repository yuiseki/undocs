The situation in Mali
The President: The Security Council will now
begin its consideration of the item on its agenda.
At this meeting, we will hear a briefing by
Ambassador José Singer Weisinger, Special Envoy of
the Dominican Republic to the Security Council, in his
capacity as Chair of the Security Council Committee
established pursuant to resolution 2374 (2017)
concerning Mali.
I now give the floor to Mr. Singer Weisinger.
Mr. Singer Weisinger (Dominican Republic)
(spoke in Spanish): I have the honour to brief the
members of the Council in my capacity as Chair of
the Security Council Committee established pursuant
to resolution 2374 (2017) concerning Mali. In my
statement, I intend to reflect on my role as Chair since
1 January 2020 to the present, and in so doing highlight
the work of the Committee and the Panel of Experts
since the beginning of the year.
Since the beginning of this year, the Committee
has met three times. On 12 February, the Committee
held informal consultations to consider the midterm
report of the Panel of Experts on Mali, submitted to
the Security Council in accordance with paragraph 4 of
resolution 2484 (2019).

17/11/2020

This year, on 2 March, the members of the
Committee met with the representative of Mali, as
well as representatives of the countries of the region,
including Algeria, Burkina Faso, Chad, Côte d’Ivoire,
Guinea, Mauritania, Morocco and the Niger, pursuant
to paragraph 9 (e) of resolution 2374 (2017) and
paragraph 2 (a) (v) of the guidelines of the Committee
for the conduct of its work.
In the light of the challenges posed by the
coronavirus disease pandemic to the Committee’s
usual working procedures, including the limitations on
holding in-person meetings, and to ensure the continuity
of its work, the members of the Committee agreed, on an
exceptional basis, to hold informal virtual meetings in
the form of closed video-teleconferences. The first such
meeting was held on 5 August. During that meeting, the
Committee heard a presentation on the final report of
the Panel of Experts on Mali (S/2019/636), submitted in
accordance with paragraph 4 of resolution 2432 (2018).
Since the establishment of the Committee to date,
there have been eight individuals on the resolution 2374
(2017) sanctions list.
Finally, as mandated by the Security Council, the
Panel of Experts submitted to the Security Council its
final report on 7 August.
The President: I thank Ambassador Singer
Weisinger for his briefing.
The meeting rose at 10.05 a.m.
