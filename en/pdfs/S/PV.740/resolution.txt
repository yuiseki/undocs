The Security Council held its 739th meeting on 9

of the item: "Situation created by the unilateral action
of the Egyptian Government in bringing to an end the
system of international operation of the Suez Canal.
which Was confirmed and completed by the Suez Canal
Convention of 1888 (5/3654)". The Council decided
to hold its next meeting in private on 11 October 1956
at 3.30 p.m.
