Official communiqué of the 5707th (closed) meeting of the
Security Council
Held in private at Headquarters, New York, on Thursday, 28 June
2007, at 10 a.m.
In accordance with rule 55 of the provisional rules of procedure of the Security
Council, the following communiqué was issued through the Secretary-General in
place of a verbatim record:
“At its 5707th meeting, held in private on 28 June 2007, the Security
Council considered the item entitled “The situation in Somalia”.
“The President, with the consent of the Council, invited the
representative of Somalia to participate in the consideration of the item
without the right to vote, in accordance with the relevant provisions of the
Charter and rule 37 of the Council’s provisional rules of procedure.
“Members of the Council and His Excellency Mr. Ali Mohamed Gedi,
Prime Minister of the Transitional Federal Government of Somalia, had an
exchange of views.”
