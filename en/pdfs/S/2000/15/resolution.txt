LETTER DATED 6 JANUARY 2000 FROM THE SECRETARY-GENERAL
ADDRESSED TO THE PRESIDENT OF THE SECURITY COUNCIL

I have the honour to refer to the appointment of the Chief Military
Observer of the United Nations Observer Mission in Georgia (UNOMIG).
The Chief Military Observer of UNOMIG, Major-General Tariq Waseem Ghazi,
completed his assignment on 31 December 1999 and has returned to the service of
his Government. I would like to put on record my appreciation for General
Ghazi’s dedication to the cause of peace and for his exemplary work in UNOMIG
over the past 15 months.
Following the usual consultations, it is my intention to appoint MajorGeneral Anis Ahmed Bajwa of Pakistan to succeed General Ghazi. General Bajwa is
expected to take up his functions in mid-January 2000.
I should be grateful if you would bring this matter to the attention of the
members of the Security Council.

(Signed)
