Letter dated 24 October 2000 from the President of the Security
Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 20 October 2000
(S/2000/1018) concerning the addition of countries to the list of States contributing
military personnel to the United Nations Mission in Ethiopia and Eritrea (UNMEE)
has been brought to the attention of the members of the Council. They take note of
the proposal contained in your letter.
(Signed) Martin Andjaba
President of the Security Council
