Letter dated 8 August 2000 from the President of the Security
Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 4 August 2000
(S/2000/778) concerning your decision to appoint Rolf G. Knutsson as your
Personal Representative for Southern Lebanon has been brought to the attention of
the members of the Security Council. They take note of the decision contained in
your letter.
(Signed) Hasmy Agam
President of the Security Council
