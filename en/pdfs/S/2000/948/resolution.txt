Letter dated 3 October 2000 from the President of the Security
Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 29 September 2000
concerning the appointment of Legwaila Joseph Legwaila (Botswana) as your
Special Representative for Ethiopia and Eritrea (S/2000/947) has been brought to the
attention of the members of the Security Council. They take note of the intention
contained therein.
(Signed) Martin Andjaba
President of the Security Council
