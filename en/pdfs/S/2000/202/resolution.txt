LETTER DATED 10 MARCH 2000 FROM THE PRESIDENT OF THE
SECURITY COUNCIL TO THE SECRETARY-GENERAL

I have the honour to inform you that, as requested, your letter dated
3 March 2000 (S/2000/201) concerning your proposal to extend the mandate of the
United Nations Peace-building Support Office in Guinea-Bissau (UNOGBIS) for one
year after the current one expires on 31 March 2000 has been brought to the
attention of the members of the Security Council. They take note of the
intention contained in your letter.

(Signed) Anwarul Karim CHOWDHURY
President of the Security Council
