Letter dated 5 July 2000 from the President of the Security
Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 30 June 2000
(S/2000/650) concerning your decision to extend the appointment of Mr. Ayite Jean
Claude Kpakpo (Benin) as Senior United Nations Adviser to the Facilitator of the
Burundi peace process until 31 October 2000 has been brought to the attention of
the members of the Security Council. They take note of the decision contained in
your letter.
(Signed) Patricia Durrant
President of the Security Council
