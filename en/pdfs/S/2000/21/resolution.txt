LETTER DATED 14 JANUARY 2000 FROM THE PRESIDENT OF THE
SECURITY COUNCIL ADDRESSED TO THE SECRETARY-GENERAL

I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 12 January 2000
(A/54/706-S/2000/20) concerning your decision to appoint Mr. Francesc Vendrell

Council. They take note of the decision contained in your letter.
