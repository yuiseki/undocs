Letter dated 19 June 2000 from the President of the Security
Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 14 June 2000
(S/2000/598), concerning your proposal to add Ukraine and Sweden to the list of
States providing contingents to the United Nations Interim Force in Lebanon
(UNIFIL), has been brought to the attention of the members of the Council. They
have taken note of the proposal contained therein.
(Signed) Jean-David Levitte
President of the Security Council
