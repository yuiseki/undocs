Letter dated 3 October 2000 from the President of the Security
Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that, as requested, your letter dated
28 September 2000 concerning your proposal to extend the mandate of the United
Nations Peace-Building Support Office in Liberia (UNOL) until 31 December 2001
(S/2000/945), has been brought to the attention of the members of the Security
Council. They take note of the proposal contained in your letter.
(Signed) Martin Andjaba
President of the Security Council
