Letter dated 2 August 2000 from the President of the Security
Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 31 July 2000
(S/2000/760) concerning your decision to appoint Mr. Mussagy Jeichande
(Mozambique) as your Representative and Head of the United Nations Office in
Angola (UNOA) has been brought to the attention of the members of the Security
Council. They take note of the decision contained in your letter.
(Signed) Hasmy Agam
President of the Security Council
