LETTER DATED 2 MARCH 2000 FROM THE PRESIDENT OF THE SECURITY
COUNCIL ADDRESSED TO THE SECRETARY-GENERAL

I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 28 February 2000
(S/2000/172) concerning your intention to appoint Major General Mountaga Diallo
(Senegal) as Force Commander of the United Nations Organization Mission in the
Democratic Republic of the Congo (MONUC) has been brought to the attention of
the members of the Security Council. They take note of the intention contained
in your letter.

(Signed) Anwarul Karim CHOWDHURY
President of the Security Council
