Letter dated 11 July 2000 from the Secretary-General addressed to
the President of the Security Council
I have the honour to refer to resolution 1295 (2000), adopted by the Security
Council on 18 April 2000, concerning Angola. In paragraph 3 of that resolution the
Council requested that, after consultation with the Security Council Committee
established pursuant to resolution 864 (1993), I appoint experts to serve on a
monitoring mechanism composed of up to five members, for a period of six months
from its effective entry into operation, to collect additional relevant information and
investigate relevant leads relating to any allegations of violations of the measures
contained in resolutions 864 (1993), 1127 (1997) and 1173 (1998).
Accordingly, following consultation with the Committee, I wish to inform you
that I have appointed the following five experts:
Christine Gordon (United Kingdom of Great Britain and Northern Ireland)
Juan Larraín (Chile)
James Manzou (Zimbabwe)
Ismaila Seck (Senegal)
Lena Sundh (Sweden)
I have also selected Ambassador Juan Larraín to chair the monitoring
mechanism.
(Signed) Kofi A. Annan
