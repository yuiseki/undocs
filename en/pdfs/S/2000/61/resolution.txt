LETTER DATED 27 JANUARY 2000 FROM THE PRESIDENT OF THE
SECURITY COUNCIL ADDRESSED TO THE SECRETARY-GENERAL

In reference to your letters dated 17 and 26 January 2000 (S/2000/60), I
have the honour to inform you that your nomination of Dr. Hans Blix of Sweden as
Executive Chairman of the United Nations Monitoring, Verification and Inspection
Commission (UNMOVIC), in accordance with paragraph 5 of resolution 1284 (1999),
has been brought to the attention of the members of the Security Council. They
approve the appointment. Dr. Blix should take up his mandated tasks as soon as
possible.

(Signed) Richard C. HOLBROOKE
President of the Security Council
