LETTER DATED 25 JANUARY 2000 FROM THE SECRETARY-GENERAL
ADDRESSED TO THE PRESIDENT OF THE SECURITY COUNCIL

I have the honour to refer to the military component of the United Nations
Transitional Administration in East Timor (UNTAET).
Following the usual consultations, I propose that the military component of
UNTAET be composed of the countries in the attached list (see annex).
I should be grateful if you could bring this matter to the attention of the
members of the Security Council.
