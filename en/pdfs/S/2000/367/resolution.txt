Letter dated 1 May 2000 from the President of the Security
Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 26 April 2000
(S/2000/366) concerning your intention to appoint Mr. Cheikh Tidiane Sy (Senegal)
as your Representative in the Central African Republic and head of the United
Nations Peace-Building Support Office in the Central African Republic (BONUCA)
has been brought to the attention of the members of the Security Council. They take
note of the intention contained in your letter.
(Signed) Wang Yingfan
President of the Security Council
