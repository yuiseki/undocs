Letter dated 7 November 2000 from the Secretary-General
addressed to the President of the Security Council
As you are aware, my Adviser for Special Assignments in Africa, Mr. Ibrahim
Gambari has been actively engaged in efforts to promote the peaceful resolution of
conflicts in Africa with particular attention to Angola. Those efforts have led to the
improvements in the relationship between the Government of Angola and the United
Nations and to the despatch to Luanda of Mr. Mussagy Jeichande as my
Representative and Head of the United Nations Office in Angola (UNOA).
There are still many issues that need to be addressed before the war in Angola
can be ended in the context of the Lusaka Protocol. It should be noted that President
dos Santos has confirmed the validity of the Lusaka Protocol as a framework for the
peace process in Angola. Moreover, the Angolan war has spilled over to
neighbouring countries of Zambia, Namibia and the Democratic Republic of the
Congo with negative consequences for peace and security there. It would be
desirable for Mr. Gambari to continue to monitor developments in the subregion and
to remain engaged in the efforts to promote the peace process.
For these reasons, I have decided to extend the appointment of Mr. Gambari as
my Adviser for Special Assignments in Africa until 28 February 2002.
I should be grateful if you would bring the present letter to the attention of
members of the Security Council.
(Signed) Kofi A. Annan
