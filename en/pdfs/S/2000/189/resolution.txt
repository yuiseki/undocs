LETTER DATED 3 MARCH 2000 FROM THE PRESIDENT OF THE SECURITY
COUNCIL ADDRESSED TO THE SECRETARY-GENERAL

I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 23 February 2000
(S/2000/188) concerning the composition of the International Tribunal for the
Prosecution of Persons Responsible for Serious Violations of International
Humanitarian Law Committed in the Territory of the Former Yugoslavia since 1991
has been brought to the attention of the members of the Security Council.
Having consulted those members, I support your intention to appoint
Mr. Liu Daqun as a judge of the Chambers of the International Tribunal.

(Signed) Anwarul Karim CHOWDHURY
President of the Security Council
