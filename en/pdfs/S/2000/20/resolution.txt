Identical letters dated 12 January 2000 from the
Secretary-General to the President of the General
Assembly and the President of the Security Council

I have the honour to refer to my annual report on Afghanistan (A/54/536S/1999/1145) submitted on 16 November 1999 to the General Assembly and the
Security Council. In the observations and conclusions (sect. VIII) of the
report, I stated:
"Following a careful review of recent developments, my Special Envoy
and I have reached the conclusion that given the lack of progress achieved
so far, his activities should be ‘frozen’ until such time as circumstances
change to justify his renewed intervention.
"The Special Mission, on the other hand, will assume the primary role
in conducting United Nations peacemaking activities in Afghanistan. It is
my intention to appoint a substantive Head of the Mission at the Assistant
Secretary-General level."
In its resolution 54/189 A, adopted on 17 December 1999, the General
Assembly endorsed my observations and recommendations in the report and
expressed support for my intention to "strengthen the United Nations Special
Mission to Afghanistan with a view to assuring its primary role in conducting
United Nations peacemaking activities in Afghanistan, in particular by
appointing a new Head of Mission, by progressively moving its head office to
Kabul and by increasing its presence in neighbouring countries".
I remain convinced of the need to strengthen the United Nations Special
Mission to Afghanistan, which is now playing the primary role in conducting the
United Nations peacemaking activities in Afghanistan. I have therefore decided
