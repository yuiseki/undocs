Letter dated 28 November 2000 from the Secretary-General
addressed to the President of the Security Council
I have the honour to refer to my mission of good offices in Cyprus.
You will recall that in its resolution 1250 (1999) of 29 June 1999 the Security
Council requested me to invite the leaders of the two sides to negotiations in the
autumn of 1999.
On 29 October 1999, I informed the Security Council of my intention to
designate Alvaro de Soto as my Special Adviser on Cyprus at Headquarters as from
1 November 1999 (S/1999/1111). In December 1999, Mr. Clerides and Mr. Denktash
accepted my invitation to proximity talks to prepare the ground for meaningful
negotiations leading to a comprehensive settlement. The first round of talks was
held in New York from 3 to 14 December 1999. This was followed by another round
at Geneva, from 31 January to 8 February 2000.
On 10 May 2000, I informed the Council that, in order to give priority to the
good offices efforts under way, I had asked Mr. de Soto to remain based at
Headquarters (S/2000/431). Further rounds of proximity talks were held at Geneva
(from 5 to 12 July and from 24 July to 4 August 2000), in New York (from 12 to
26 September 2000) and again at Geneva (from 1 to 10 November 2000).
In furtherance of the mission entrusted to me, which includes consultations in
the region as well as with various capitals as required, Mr. de Soto has been assisted
by a small team consisting of staff of the Secretariat and of the United Nations
Peacekeeping Force in Cyprus (UNFICYP), a legal adviser and international
consultants as required. I expect that efforts connected with my mission of good
offices in Cyprus will continue from January until at least June 2001, at which point
the resource requirements in support of this mandate could be incorporated into the
next budget of UNFICYP, on which I shall report separately.
I should be grateful if you would bring this letter to the attention of the
members of the Security Council and alert them to the importance of continuing to
support my efforts and those of my Special Adviser in discharging my mission in
Cyprus.
(Signed) Kofi A. Annan
