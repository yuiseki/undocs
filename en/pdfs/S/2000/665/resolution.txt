Letter dated 10 July 2000 from the President of the Security
Council to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 6 July 2000
(S/2000/664) regarding your intention to appoint Major General Bo Wranker of
Sweden to the post of Force Commander of the United Nations Disengagement
Observer Force (UNDOF) has been brought to the attention of the members of the
Security Council. They take note of the intention contained in your letter.
(Signed) Patricia Durrant
President of the Security Council
