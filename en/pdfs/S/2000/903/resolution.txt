Letter dated 26 September 2000 from the President of the
Security Council addressed to the Secretary-General
Further to my letter of 21 September to you concerning the sending of a
Security Council mission to Sierra Leone (S/2000/886), I have the honour to inform
you that the composition of the mission will be as follows:
1.

Ambassador Jeremy Greenstock (United Kingdom, Head of the mission)

2.

Ambassador Anwarul Karim Chowdhury (Bangladesh)

3.

Ambassador Paul Heinbecker (Canada)

4.

Ambassador Wang Yingfan (China)

5.

Ambassador Patricia Durrant (Jamaica)

6.

Ambassador Moctar Ouane (Mali)

7.

Ambassador A. Peter van Walsum (Netherlands)

8.

Ambassador Andrei Granovsky (Russian Federation)

9.

Ambassador Volodymyr Yel’chenko (Ukraine)

10.

Ambassador James B. Cunningham (United States of America)

I should be grateful if the necessary arrangements could be made to provide
support for the mission.
(Signed) Moctar Ouane
President of the Security Council
