LETTER DATED 7 MARCH 2000 FROM THE SECRETARY-GENERAL
ADDRESSED TO THE PRESIDENT OF THE SECURITY COUNCIL

I have the honour to refer to your letter of 3 March 2000 (S/2000/189)
concurring with my proposal to appoint Mr. Liu Daqun as a judge of the
International Tribunal for the Former Yugoslavia. I have received a
corresponding letter from the President of the General Assembly. Accordingly, I
have appointed Mr. Liu as a judge of the International Tribunal, effective
1 April 2000, for the remainder of the term of office of Judge Wang, which term
will expire on 16 November 2001.

(Signed)
