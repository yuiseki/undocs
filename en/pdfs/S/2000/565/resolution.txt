Letter dated 12 June 2000 from the Secretary-General addressed
to the President of the Security Council
Further to my letter dated 12 January 2000 (S/2000/18) regarding the approval
of the distribution plan submitted by the Government of Iraq for phase VII of the
humanitarian programme pursuant to Security Council resolution 986 (1995) of
14 April 1995, I have the honour to inform you that the Government of Iraq was
informed today that I have approved the proposals made by the Government for the
inclusion of a housing sector as part IX of the distribution plans for phases VI and
VII. The Government of Iraq has also been informed that the requirements for
housing have been approved on the understanding that the implementation of this
part of the plan would be governed by resolutions 986 (1995), 1281 (1999) and 1284
(1999) and by the Memorandum of Understanding concluded on 20 May 1996
between the United Nations Secretariat and the Government of Iraq (S/1996/356),
and would be without prejudice to the procedures followed by the Security Council
Committee established by resolution 661 (1990) of 2 August 1990. The letter dated
12 June 2000 addressed to the Permanent Representative of Iraq to the United
Nations from the Executive Director of the Iraq Programme is attached.
A copy of the list of supplies and goods contained in the distribution plans
concerned will be made available to the Security Council Committee. The list has
been scrutinized by experts of the United Nations Monitoring, Verification and
Inspection Commission, who concluded that, on the basis of the limited information
given in the annexes, no prohibited items could be identified. They will keep the
matter under review and provide a further assessment in the light of any additional
information that may become available.
(Signed) Kofi A. Annan

Letter dated 12 June 2000 from the Executive Director of the
Office of the Iraq Programme addressed to the Permanent
Representative of Iraq to the United Nations
With reference to your letter dated 6 June 2000 and previous letters dated 3, 10
and 22 May 2000 concerning allocations to the housing sector under phase VI and
phase VII of the humanitarian programme pursuant to Security Council resolution
986 (1995) of 14 April 1995, I should like to recall that in my letter of 12 January
2000, I informed you that the Secretary-General would await the outcome of the
joint technical review before taking a decision on the proposals made by the
Government of Iraq regarding the housing sector. I have now been authorized by the
Secretary-General to convey the following.
Having examined the findings of the joint technical review, submitted to your
Government under cover of my letter dated 12 April 2000, and having received the
agreement of your Government conveyed in your letter dated 22 May 2000, the
Secretary-General has concluded that the revised plan concerning housing
requirements, if properly implemented, should assist in meeting essential civilian
needs for housing. The housing sector requirement is therefore approved for the
inclusion as part IX of the distribution plans for phase VI and VII, with the
following understandings.
In regard to the budgetary allocations for phases VI and VII of the
humanitarian programme, while this is endorsed for the purpose of planning, it may
be necessary to adjust expenditures in the light of the final revenues generated
during the respective phases.
The implementation of the plan for the housing sector is governed by Security
Council resolution 986 (1995) and all other relevant resolutions, and the
Memorandum of Understanding concluded on 20 May 1996 between the Secretariat
of the United Nations and the Government of Iraq (S/1996/356). In case of
inconsistency between the particular provisions of the revised plan on the one hand,
and the relevant resolutions and the Memorandum of Understanding on the other,
the provisions of the latter documents shall prevail.
The approval of the housing sector is without prejudice to actions that might
be taken by the Security Council Committee established by resolution 661 (1990)
regarding the applications for export of particular items contained in the list
submitted for the Committee’s consideration, in accordance with its procedures.
The housing sector is approved in accordance with the findings and
conclusions of the joint technical review referred to above. I also note that, as
explained in your letter dated 22 May 2000, additional requirements for housing will
be submitted, as amendments, to the distribution plans for phase VI and phase VII,
by the Ministry of Housing and Construction and the Ministry of Industry and
Minerals. On receipt of the said amendments, these will be reviewed and
incorporated, as appropriate, in the distribution plans concerned, within the
framework for the equitable distribution of goods established by the joint technical
review.

In his letter dated 12 June 2000 addressed to the President of the Security
Council, the Secretary-General stated that, as part of his endorsement of the housing
sector, United Nations observers will be deployed to Iraq to fulfil the observation
requirements pursuant to Security Council resolution 986 (1995) and the
Memorandum of Understanding of 1996. Accordingly, I have requested the United
Nations Humanitarian Coordinator in Iraq, in conjunction with the United Nations
Centre for Human Settlements (Habitat), to ensure that appropriate observation
procedures are put in place immediately and to ensure that United Nations observers
undertake observation of housing settlement supplies delivered and utilized under
the relevant resolutions.
The Office of the Iraq Programme will assist in identifying item codes for all
commodities that fall within the housing sector, as well as in amending the relevant
distribution plans, as appropriate. Contracts circulated and approved to date against
the original codes in annex I to the distribution plan for phase VII will be considered
as funded against the allocation for the housing sector, thereby freeing resources for
other items required for the food-handling subsector. In this context, I should like to
note that the additional $100 million allocation for the food-handling subsector
requested under cover of your letter, dated 10 May 2000, may now be assigned.
However, should your Government wish to fund housing sector commodities from
the additional $100 million allocation, as indicated in your letter dated 4 May 2000,
this sum may be added to the new sectoral allocation.
I should like to assure you that the observations of the relevant Iraqi
authorities conveyed to me in your letter dated 22 May 2000 are appreciated and
will be dealt with at the technical level by my colleagues in Iraq.
(Signed) Benon V. Sevan
Executive Director
