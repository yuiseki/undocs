Letter dated 15 November 2000 from the President of the Security
Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 9 November 2000
(S/2000/1098) concerning your decision to extend the appointment of Mr. Ayite J. C.
Kpakpo, Senior Adviser to the Facilitator of the Arusha Peace Process in Burundi,
until 31 January 2001 has been brought to the attention of the members of the
Security Council. They take note of the decision contained in your letter.
(Signed) Peter van Walsum
President of the Security Council
