Letter dated 10 November 2000 from the President of the
Security Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 7 November 2000
concerning your decision to extend the appointment of Mr. Ibrahim Gambari as your
Adviser for Special Assignments in Africa until 28 February 2002 (S/2000/1082)
has been brought to the attention of the members of the Security Council. They take
note of the decision contained in your letter.
(Signed) Peter van Walsum
President of the Security Council
