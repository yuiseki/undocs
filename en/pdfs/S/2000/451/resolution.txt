Letter dated 18 May 2000 from the President of the Security
Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 18 May 2000
(S/2000/450), concerning the request of the Indonesian Authorities for emergency
assistance by the United Nations Transitional Administration in East Timor
(UNTAET), has been brought to the attention of the members of the Security
Council. They take note of your proposal to accede to the request.
(Signed) Wang Yingfan
President of the Security Council
