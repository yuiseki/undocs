Letter dated 15 November 2000 from the President of the Security
Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 10 November 2000
(S/2000/1096) concerning the United Nations Office in Burundi has been brought to
the attention of the members of the Security Council. They take note of the
information contained in your letter and agree with your recommendation that the
mandate of the United Nations Office in Burundi be extended until 31 December
2001.
(Signed) Peter van Walsum
President of the Security Council
