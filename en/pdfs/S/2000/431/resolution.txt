Letter dated 10 May 2000 from the Secretary-General addressed
to the President of the Security Council
With reference to my letter of 1 November 1999 (S/1999/1111), I wish to
inform you that I have asked Alvaro de Soto, my Special Adviser on Cyprus, to
remain based at Headquarters for the time being in order to give priority to the good
offices effort under way.
Further, I wish to inform you that the term of James Holger as Acting Special
Representative and Chief of Mission, the United Nations Peacekeeping Force in
Cyprus (UNFICYP), comes to an end on 31 May 2000. In this connection, I have
appointed Zbigniew Wlosowicz (Poland) to succeed Mr. Holger as Acting Special
Representative and Chief of Mission, UNFICYP, effective 1 June 2000.
(Signed) Kofi A Annan
