Letter dated 1 June 2000 from the President of the Security
Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that the letter dated 26 May 2000
(S/2000/518) in which you proposed to establish a United Nations Peace-building
Support Office in Tajikistan for an initial period of one year starting on 1 June 2000
has been brought to the attention of the members of the Security Council, who took
note of it with appreciation.
(Signed) Jean-David Levitte
President of the Security Council
