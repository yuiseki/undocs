Letter dated 12 June 2000 from the Secretary-General addressed
to the President of the Security Council
I have the honour to refer to the United Nations Military Observer Group in
India and Pakistan (UNMOGIP).
Major-General Joszef Bali (Hungary), who had been the Chief Military
Observer of UNMOGIP since 4 March 1999, left the mission on 3 March 2000.
Following consultations with the parties concerned, it is my intention to appoint
Major-General Manuel Saavedra of Uruguay as the next Chief Military Observer of
UNMOGIP.
I should be grateful if you would bring this matter to the attention of the
members of the Security Council.
(Signed) Kofi A. Annan
