Letter dated 15 May 2000 from the President of the Security
Council addressed to the Secretary-General
I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 10 May 2000
(S/2000/431), concerning your appointment of Zbigniew Wlosowicz (Poland) as
your Acting Special Representative and Chief of Mission of the United Nations
Peacekeeping Force in Cyprus effective 1 June 2000, has been brought to the
attention of the members of the Security Council. They take note of the
appointment.
(Signed) Wang Yingfan
President of the Security Council
