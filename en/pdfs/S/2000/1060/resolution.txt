Letter dated 30 October 2000 from the Secretary-General
addressed to the President of the Security Council
I have the honour to refer to the command of the United Nations Mission in
Sierra Leone (UNAMSIL).
Following the usual consultations, it is my intention to appoint LieutenantGeneral Daniel Ishmael Opande of Kenya as Force Commander of UNAMSIL.
I should be grateful if you would bring this matter to the attention of the
members of the Security Council.
(Signed) Kofi A. Annan
