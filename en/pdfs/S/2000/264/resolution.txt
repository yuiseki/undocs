Letter dated 24 March 2000 from the President of the Security
Council addressed to the Secretary-General
Referring to document S/2000/40 of 15 February 2000, entitled “Summary
statement by the Secretary-General on matters of which the Security Council is
seized and on the stage reached in their consideration”, and pursuant to the
procedure set out in the note by the President of the Security Council of 29 August
1996 (S/1996/704), I have the honour to notify you of the wish of the members of
the Security Council to have the item “Security Council working methods and
procedure” retained on the list of matters of which the Security Council is seized.
(Signed) Anwarul Karim Chowdhury
President of the Security Council
