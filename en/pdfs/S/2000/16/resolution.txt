LETTER DATED 12 JANUARY 2000 FROM THE PRESIDENT OF THE
SECURITY COUNCIL ADDRESSED TO THE SECRETARY-GENERAL

I have the honour to inform you that your letter dated 6 January 2000
(S/2000/15) concerning your intention to appoint Major-General Anis Ahmed Bajwa
of Pakistan to the post of Chief Military Observer of the United Nations
Observer Mission in Georgia has been brought to the attention of the members of
the Security Council. They take note of the intention contained in your letter.

(Signed) Richard C. HOLBROOKE
President of the Security Council
