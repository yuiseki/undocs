LETTER DATED 28 FEBRUARY 2000 FROM THE SECRETARY-GENERAL
ADDRESSED TO THE PRESIDENT OF THE SECURITY COUNCIL

I have the honour to inform you that, having completed the usual
consultations, it is my intention to appoint Major General Mountaga Diallo
(Senegal) as Force Commander of the United Nations Organization Mission in the
Democratic Republic of the Congo (MONUC).
It is envisaged that Major General Diallo will take up his responsibilities
shortly.
I should be grateful if you would bring this matter to the attention of the
members of the Security Council.

(Signed)
