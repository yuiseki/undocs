Statement by the President of the Security Council
At the 8887th meeting of the Security Council, held on 27 October 2021, in
connection with the Council’s consideration of the item entitled “Reports of the
Secretary-General on the Sudan and South Sudan”, the President of the Security
Council made the following statement on behalf of the Council:
“The Security Council reiterates its support for the people of South Sudan,
and reaffirms its strong commitment to the sovereignty, independence,
territorial integrity, and national unity of the Republic of Sout h Sudan.
“The Security Council reaffirms its support for the 2018 “Revitalised
Agreement on the Resolution of the Conflict in the Republic of South Sudan”
(the Revitalised Agreement). The Security Council welcomes the swearing -in of
members of the Transitional National Legislature as an important step in the
implementation of the Revitalised Agreement.
“The Security Council takes note of the Secretary -General’s 15 July 2021
letter (S/2021/661), presented pursuant to resolution 2567 (2021), and the
conclusion of the needs assessment mission that the “aspirations for peaceful
and credible elections in South Sudan are high, albeit tempered by ubiquitous
concerns over a fragile security, political and socioeconomic situation.” The
Security Council requests the Secretary-General to establish an integrated
electoral assistance team led by the United Nations Mission in South Sudan
(UNMISS), consistent with resolution 2567 (2021), to implement the electoral
assistance activities set forth in phase 1 of the Annex to his 15 July 2021 letter
(S/2021/661), in support of the elections roadmap detailed in the Revitalised
Agreement.
“The Security Council recognizes that conducting free and fair elections,
reflecting the will of all South Sudanese and with the full, equal and meaningful
participation of women, youths, people with disabilities, displaced persons,
refugees, and members of all political groups, will be critical for a transition
towards a stable, inclusive, democratic, and self-reliant state. The Security
Council underscores that elections will need to be preceded by an inclusive,
transparent constitution-drafting process, carried out in an environment that
respects freedom of expression, association and peaceful assembly, and
encourages civic engagement.
“The Security Council urges the South Sudanese authorities to make
progress on key milestones that include: the necessary security arrangements;
the establishment of the legal framework for elections, including the electoral
system, boundary delimitation, national census, special measures for internall y

“The Security Council expresses its appreciation for the leadership of the
Intergovernmental Authority on Development (IGAD) in advancing the peace

early and sustained support to the electoral processes in South Sudan.
“The Security Council expresses grave concern regarding the increased
violence between armed groups in some parts of South Sudan, which has killed
and displaced thousands, and condemns all acts of violence, human right s
violations and abuses, and violations of international humanitarian law by all
parties that have taken place in South Sudan. The Security Council calls on the
South Sudanese leaders to take immediate and effective measures to restore
stability throughout South Sudan to facilitate the preparation and conduct of free
and fair elections, as stipulated in the Revitalised Agreement. The Security
Council welcomes the ceasefire call by the Government of South Sudan.
“The Security Council commends the work of UNMISS and expresses its
deep appreciation for the actions taken by UNMISS peacekeepers and troop and police-contributing countries in implementing the UNMISS mandate in a
challenging environment.”
