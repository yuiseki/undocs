Statement by the President of the Security Council
On 29 January 2021, in connection with the Council’s consideration of the item
entitled “Cooperation between the United Nations and regional and subregional
organizations in maintaining international peace and security”, in accordance with the
procedure agreed in light of the extraordinary circumstances caused by the COVID -19
pandemic and set out in S/2020/372, the President of the Security Council issued the
following statement on behalf of the Council:
“The Security Council reaffirms its primary responsibility for the
maintenance of international peace and security.
“The Security Council recalls all its previous resolutions and presidential
statements on this agenda item.
“The Security Council recognizes and further encourages efforts by the
League of Arab States and its member States to contribute to collective
endeavours to settle conflicts in the Arab region by peaceful means, while
reaffirming its strong commitment to the sovereignty, independence, unity and
territorial integrity of Countries and to the purposes and principles of the Charter
of the United Nations.
“The Security Council expresses its appreciation for the briefings of Under
Secretary-General of the United Nations for Political and Peacebuilding Affairs,
Rosemary DiCarlo, and the Secretary-General of the League of Arab States,
Ahmed Aboul Gheit, on 18 January 2021, and reiterates that cooperation with
regional and sub-regional organisations in matters relating to the maintenance
of peace and security, and consistent with Chapter VIII of the Charter, is
significant to improve collective security.
“The Security Council welcomes the strong cooperation between the
United Nations and the League of Arab States and reiterates its intention to
consider further steps to promote closer cooperation between the two
Organisations in the fields of conflict early warning, prevention, peacekeeping,
peacebuilding, sustaining peace, addressing root causes of conflicts, and
counter-terrorism, as well as in other areas of common interest; and to promote
the coherence and effectiveness of their efforts.
“The Security Council takes note of the outcome of general meetings on
the cooperation between the Secretariats of the United Nations and the League
of Arab States and their specialized agencies. The Council looks forward to the
fifteenth general cooperation meeting between the United Nations and the
League, to be held in Geneva in December 2021, to advance a biennial

“The Security Council recognizes the efforts undertaken by both
Organisations and the commitment of their Secretaries-Generals in response to
the impact of the COVID-19 pandemic, especially in the conflict-affected
contexts, and reiterates its demand, in line with resolution 2532 (2020), for a
general and immediate cessation of hostilities and a humanitarian pause in all
conflict situations on its agenda, including in the Arab region.
“The Security Council further stresses the urgent need for the
intensification and acceleration of international and regional efforts to prevent
the escalation of tensions, encourage multilateral dialogue, advance the political
settlement of disputes and promote just and lasting peace in all con flict
situations in the region.
“Cognizant of the dire humanitarian consequences of conflicts, the
Security Council emphasizes the urgent need for the funding of the humanitarian
response in the Arab region.
“The Security Council welcomes the efforts of the League of Arab States
to implement resolution 1325 (2000) and relevant subsequent resolutions,
including through the development of a Regional Action Plan for Women,
Peace, and Security (WPS), and encourages the United Nations and the Arab
League to further promote the implementation of the WPS and the Youth, Peace,
and Security agendas.
“The Security Council supports the holding of an annual briefing by the
Secretary-General of the League of Arab States, to further promote the
cooperation between the Security Council and the League of Arab States on
matters relating to the maintenance of peace and security, especially at the
regional level, and consider ways to improve collective security.
“The Security Council encourages, in the same spirit, the holding of an
annual informal meeting between its members and the members of the Council
of the League of Arab States; and whenever possible, an informal meeting
between its members and Representatives of the Arab Summit Troika and the
Secretary-General of the League of Arab States, on the margins of the General
Assembly high level segment.
“The Security Council emphasizes the importance of intensifying the
coordination between the League of Arab States and the United Nations Special
Envoys and Representatives, including through the arrangement of periodic
meetings, with a view to reaching a more comprehensive understanding of
different crisis situations in the region, and developing effective solutions
through joint action where appropriate.
“The Security Council reiterates the importance of trilateral cooperation
and coordination between the United Nations, the African Union and the League
of Arab States on cross-regional peace and security issues, as well as with other
relevant regional organizations on situations of common interest.
“The Security Council welcomes the opening of the United Nations
Liaison Office to the League of Arab States in Cairo in June 2019, and
encourages the United Nations Secretariat and the Secretariat General of the
League of Arab States to promote its role toward strengthening cooperation
between the two Organisations. The Security Council looks forward to filling
the vacancy in the leadership of the Office at the earliest op portunity, and notes
