Official communiqué of the 7456th (closed) meeting of the
Security Council
Held in private at Headquarters, New York, on Thursday,
4 June 2015, at 10 a.m.
In accordance with rule 55 of the provisional rules of procedure of the
Security Council, the following communiqué was issued through the SecretaryGeneral in place of a verbatim record:
“On 4 June 2015, the Security Council, pursuant to annex II, sections A and B
of resolution 1353 (2001), held its 7456th meeting in private with the troop - and
police-contributing countries to the African Union-United Nations Hybrid Operation
in Darfur.
“The President invited Ms. Daniela Kroslak, Darfur Integrated Operational
Team Leader, Department of Peacekeeping Operations, to participate in the meeting
in accordance with rule 39 of the Council’s provisional rules of procedure.
“The Security Council and the troop- and police-contributing countries heard a
briefing by Ms. Kroslak.
“Members of the Council, Ms. Kroslak and representatives of participating
contributing countries had an exchange of views.”
