Official communiqué of the 5345th (closed) meeting
of the Security Council
Held in private at Headquarters, New York, on Friday,
13 January 2006, at 11.30 a.m.
In accordance with rule 55 of the provisional rules of procedure of the Security
Council, the following communiqué was issued through the Secretary-General in
place of a verbatim record:
“At its 5345th meeting, held in private on 13 January 2006, the Security
Council considered the item entitled ‘Reports of the Secretary-General on the
Sudan’.
“In accordance with the decision taken at the 5344th meeting, the
President extended invitations under rule 39 of the Council’s provisional rules
of procedure to Mr. Jan Pronk, Special Representative of the Secretary-General
for the Sudan and Head of the United Nations Mission in the Sudan, and to
Mr. Salim A. Salim, Special Envoy of the African Union for the InterSudanese Peace Talks on the Conflict in Darfur.
“Members of the Council, Mr. Jan Pronk and Mr. Salim A. Salim had an
exchange of views.”
