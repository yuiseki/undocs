Official communiqué of the 8662nd (closed) meeting of the
Security Council
Held in private at Headquarters, New York, on Thursday,
7 November 2019, at 10 a.m.
In accordance with rule 55 of the provisional rules of procedure of the Security
Council, the following communiqué was issued through the Secretary-General in
place of a verbatim record:
“On 7 November 2019, the Security Council, pursuant to annex II, sections
A and B of resolution 1353 (2001), held its 8662nd meeting in private with the
troop- and police-contributing countries to the United Nations Multidimensional
Integrated Stabilization Mission in the Central African Republic.
“The President invited General Hugh Van Roosen, Deputy Military
Advisor, to participate in the meeting in accordance with rule 39 of the Council ’s
provisional rules of procedure.
“The Security Council and the troop- and police-contributing countries
heard a briefing by General Van Roosen.
“Members of the Council, General Van Roosen and the representatives of
participating contributing countries had an exchange of views. ”
